About The Seax

The Seax is a free, independent website for the people of Essex: the county's news, sport, weather, public services, community life and what's on, in one place and updated through the day.

How The Seax works

We watch hundreds of trusted sources across Essex and beyond: local newspapers and radio, hyperlocal and independent newsrooms, Essex Police, the fire and ambulance services, councils, the NHS, courts, charities, clubs and venues, plus national and specialist outlets when they report on Essex.

The Seax is produced with the help of AI. Automated research tools gather the latest reports each hour, and AI writes our headlines, summaries and short story pages in our own words, following our editorial rules. Every story names the outlet that reported it and links directly to the original, so you can always read the full report and support the journalists who did the work.

We do not copy articles, take photographs from other publishers, or publish anything we have not seen reported by a named source.

Our editorial rules

  • Facts only as the source states them. No invented quotes, figures, scores or details.
  • Crime and court reporting is limited to what police and courts have said. People who have been arrested or charged are presumed innocent, and we do not report anything that could prejudice a trial.
  • We never name victims of sexual offences or identify children involved in criminal cases.
  • We do not report suicide or self-harm, or individual inquests.
  • Advertising is always clearly labelled and never affects what we report.

Corrections and complaints

We work hard to be accurate, but if we get something wrong we want to put it right quickly. Email theseaxessex@gmail.com with a link to the page and what is wrong. We aim to review every correction request within 24 hours and will correct, clarify or remove content where it is inaccurate, unfair or should not have been published. Significant corrections are noted on the page.

If a story concerns you personally and you would like it reviewed or removed, contact us at the same address.

Contact

News tips, corrections, partnership ideas and advertising: theseaxessex@gmail.com.

If you run an Essex newsroom, magazine or community site and would like to be featured, or would prefer we did not summarise your work, please get in touch and we will respond promptly.

Advertising

The Seax offers Essex businesses brand-safe space alongside local news, sport and what's on, from county-wide takeovers to sponsorship of a single town or section. Email theseaxessex@gmail.com for rates.

Privacy

The Seax does not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites. We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies and collects no personal data. Our free morning newsletter is delivered by follow.it; if you sign up, your email address is used only to send it, and every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Why "The Seax"?

The seax is the short sword of the Saxons, and three seaxes appear on the arms of Essex: gules, three seaxes fesswise in pale argent, pommels and hilts or. Our badge follows that blazon.
