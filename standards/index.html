Editorial standards

The rules every story on The Seax is written to, and what to do if we fall short of them.

How our reporting is produced

The Seax is produced with AI. Automated tools read hundreds of Essex sources through the day, and AI writes our headlines, summaries and stories in our own words under the written rules on this page. No story is written from memory: every fact must come from a source read at the time of writing, and every story links to that source.

Some pages only point to another outlet's report, in one sentence with a link. Those pages say so plainly. Stories marked Council Watch are our own reporting from councils' public meeting papers. Stories described as analysis by The Seax are our own work on official open data, and always link to the data.

Sources

  • We prefer primary documents and official sources: council papers, police, fire and ambulance statements, courts, the NHS, regulators and government data.
  • We credit the outlet that reported a story first and link to it. We do not copy other publishers' sentences, quotes or pictures.
  • Social media is a tip-off, never a source, unless it is the official account of a public body.
  • Claims by politicians, unions, campaign groups and businesses are reported as their claims, with attribution.
  • We say what is not yet known.

Crime, courts and inquests

  • We report charges and court cases only from official sources or outlets that attended or reported them in full. A person charged is presumed innocent, and we do not report background that could prejudice a trial.
  • We never identify the complainant in a sexual offence case.
  • We do not name children in crime or inquest stories, except where an official body has named them, for example in a missing person appeal.
  • We do not report the method of a suicide, and we report deaths with care for the family.
  • Every crime, court and inquest story passes an automatic check against these rules before it is published. A story that fails is held back.

Data

When we publish figures we give the source, the date of the data and a link, say how we worked out anything we calculated, and say what the figures cannot show. A food hygiene rating, a contract award or a petition count is reported as the official record states it, on the date stated.

Fairness and independence

The Seax is independent of any political party, council, company or campaign. Every party and every MP is treated the same way. Where a story makes a serious claim about a person or organisation, we include their published response where there is one.

Corrections and complaints

If we get something wrong, email corrections@theseax.com with a link to the page and what is wrong. We aim to review every request within 24 hours, correct the story, and record significant corrections in our public corrections log. If you are named in a story and believe it is unfair or out of date, use the same address. More about how the site works is on the About page.
