How we do it

Seax Investigates goes to the primary source, such as a published review, an official register or a government list, and reports what it means for Essex. Seax Analysis works out official figures for the county and its towns, with our own charts and maps. Before anything we originate is published, every figure is recalculated from the source, the key facts are confirmed a second way, and the finished story is read back as a reader would read it. More on how we check our reporting.

We also read every Essex council's public papers twice a day: see Council Watch.

Know something we should look into? Email news@theseax.com. We never publish anything about a person from a tip alone.
