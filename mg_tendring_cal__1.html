Agenda

Miscellaneous Licensing Sub-Committee - Wednesday, 30th September, 2026 10.30 am

  • Attendance details
  • Agenda frontsheet PDF 172 KB
  • Agenda reports pack PDF 516 KB

Venue: Town Hall, Station Road, Clacton-on-Sea, CO15 1SE. View directions

Contact: Katie Moss Email: kkoppenaal@tendringdc.gov.uk or Telephone  01255 686585

Items
No. Item

1.

Apologies for Absence and Substitutions

The Committee is asked to note any apologies for absence and substitutions received from Members.

 

2.

Minutes of the Last Meeting PDF 92 KB

To confirm and sign as a correct record, the minutes of the last meeting of the Sub- Committee, held on Wednesday, 16 September 2026.

 

3.

Declarations of Interest

Councillors are invited to declare any Disclosable Pecuniary Interests, Other Registerable Interests of Non-Registerable Interests, and the nature of it, in relation to any item on the agenda.

 

4.

Exclusion of Press and Public

The Sub-Committee is requested to consider passing the following resolution:-

 

“That under Section 100A(4) of the Local Government Act 1972, the press and public be excluded from the meeting during the consideration of Agenda Items 5 to 8, on the grounds that they involve the likely disclosure of exempt information as defined in Paragraph 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 12A, as amended, by the Act.”

5.

Exempt Minutes PDF 156 KB

To confirm and sign as a correct record, the exempt minutes of the last meeting of the Sub-Committee, held on Wednesday, 16 September 2026.

6.

Report of the Corporate Director (Law and Governance) - B.1 - Application for the Renewal of a Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver's Licence

To present to Members the circumstances surrounding the renewal application submitted by the individual for a Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver Licence, and to request that the Sub?Committee determine whether they remain a fit and proper person to hold such a licence, given that their DVLA driving licence has been endorsed with six penalty points for exceeding the speed limit on a public road on two separate occasions.

7.

Report of the Corporate Director (Law and Governance) - B.2 - Review of a Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver and Hackney Carriage Vehicle Licence

This item was deferred from the meeting dated 16 September 2026 as the individual was unable to attend the meeting.

 

To present to Members a request to review the Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver Licence and Hackney Carriage Vehicle Licence currently held by the individual, and to seek the Sub-Committee’s determination as to whether they remain a fit and proper person to continue to hold such licences.

8.

Report of the Corporate Director (Law and Governance) - B.3 - Review of a Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver Licence

This item was deferred from the meeting dated 16 September 2026. Although the individual had confirmed his intention to attend, they did not attend the meeting.

 

To present to Members a request to review the Hackney Carriage/Private Hire Driver Licence currently held by the individual, and to seek the Sub-Committee’s determination as to whether they remain a fit and proper person to continue to hold such a licence.
