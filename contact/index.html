Contact The Seax

We want to hear from you. Pick the address that fits and it reaches the right desk.

  • News tips and storiesnews@theseax.comSomething happening in your town, street or village? Tell us what, where and when, and send a photo if you have one.
  • Sportsport@theseax.comResults, match reports, club news and fixtures from every level of Essex sport.
  • Events and what's onevents@theseax.comFestivals, shows, markets, fun runs and fireworks. Send the date, time, place, price and a link.
  • Charities and community groupscommunity@theseax.comAppeals, fundraisers, volunteers wanted and good news from the groups that hold Essex together.
  • Correctionscorrections@theseax.comSpotted a mistake? Send the link to the page and what is wrong. We aim to look at every request within 24 hours.
  • Complaints and removal requestseditor@theseax.comIf a story concerns you personally, or you want to make a complaint, write to the editorial team.
  • Everything elseinfo@theseax.comPartnerships, newsrooms and community sites that want to be featured, or anything not listed above.

Good to know

The Seax only publishes what we can check against an official source or a published report, so a tip may not appear straight away. We never publish anything about a person from a tip alone. For how we work and how we correct mistakes, see About The Seax.
