Minutes:

All four constitution review reports were considered under one item.

 

Constitution Review – Financial Procedure Rules

 

The report presented recommended the attached Financial Procedure Rules were considered and recommended for inclusion in the Council’s Constitution, subject to any comments and revisions as a result of the committee’s debate.

 

RESOLVED

 

For the reasons set out in this report, the Committee is recommended to recommend the Financial Procedure Rules in Appendix A to Full Council for approval.

 

Constitution Review – Scheme of Delegations

 

The report sought the committee’s approval to refer the draft revisions to the Constitution relating to the role and powers of officers and a revised Scheme of Delegation at Appendices 1 and 2 to, first, Cabinet for approval of the delegation of executive powers and then to Full Council for approval of the delegations in respect of non-executive powers and to adopt the scheme of delegation into the Council’s Constitution. By adopting an updated scheme of delegation, the Council meets a statutory direction imposed on the Council by the Secretary of State and moves the Council closer to meeting the directions overall.

 

RESOLVED

 

For the reasons set out in this report and its appendices, the Committee is recommended to agree:

 

2.1      The draft part 2.5 of the Constitution “Officers” relating to the role and powers of officers set out in Appendix 1.

2.2      The draft revised part 2.5A of the Constitution, the Scheme of Delegation set out at Appendix 2.

2.3      The monitoring officer will consult with all relevant officers on the scheme of delegation set out in the appendices as due diligence to ensure the proposals and council procedure and processes are aligned and consistent.

2.4      To delegate power to the monitoring officer to make minor changes to the Scheme of Delegation arising from the consultation process in 2.1 and, after doing to seek approval from.

2.4.1   Cabinet in respect of the delegation of executive powers; and if approved.

2.4.2   Full Council in respect of the delegation of non-executive powers and the adoption of the finalised scheme of delegation into the Council’s Constitution.

 

 

Constitutional Review: Contract Procedure Rules

 

The report sought the committee’s agreement to refer the draft revisions to the Council’s Contract Procedure Rules to Full Council for adoption into the Council’s Constitution.

 

RESOLVED

 

For the reasons set out in this report and its appendices, the Committee is recommended to agree:

 

2.1      The updated Contract Procedure Rules at Appendix 1 for inclusion in Part 4 of the Council’s Constitution.
