# Brentwood man charged with rape at Soho's Groucho Club

**Stefan-Pierre Tomlin, 36, is due at Southwark Crown Court on Monday. He also faces two counts of assault by penetration.**

## Key points

- Alleged incident: 13 November 2024
- On bail until the Crown Court hearing

## The story

A Brentwood man has been charged with rape over an alleged incident at the Groucho Club, the private members' club in Soho, central London, two years ago.

Stefan-Pierre Tomlin, 36, faces one count of rape and two counts of assault by penetration. The Crown Prosecution Service authorised the charges in September, after a Metropolitan Police investigation.

He first appeared at Southend Magistrates' Court on Monday 2 November and was granted bail. He is next due at Southwark Crown Court on Monday 30 November.

The Met began investigating after a woman reported that she had been raped inside the club, in Dean Street, on Wednesday 13 November 2024. Specialist officers supported her, and at the time the force said there was no suggestion that any of the club's staff were involved.

Westminster City Council suspended the club's licence later that month at the request of police. The club, long a haunt of writers, artists and stars of film and television, reopened in January 2025 under new conditions.

Mr Tomlin, a model, was widely reported in 2017 to be Tinder's most popular user.

By law, the woman is entitled to lifelong anonymity.
