# Saffron Walden to run free Christmas buses for two late-night events

**Saffron Walden Town Council is laying on free buses for late shopping and its Christmas fayre.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 10:06 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Carols in Market Square at 6pm on 4 December
- Paper timetables from the Tourist Information Centre
- Routes and operator not yet published

## The story

The free hop-on, hop-off service will run from 4pm to 8pm on Friday 27 November and again on Friday 4 December, carrying people to and from the town centre. Saffron Walden Town Council announced the buses on 23 November, after its regular free Tuesday shopper bus finished for 2026.

The first date is the late-night shopping evening led by the town's Business Improvement District, when several shops stay open until 8pm. The second is the Walden Christmas Fayre and Lantern Parade, which the town council hosts with the Saffron Walden Initiative. Stalls and entertainment run from 2pm to 8pm, with carols in Market Square at 6pm.

Timings are flexible, and the council asks passengers to check with the driver when the bus reaches Market Square. Paper timetables are available from the Tourist Information Centre. The council has not published the routes or named the operator. The Saffron Walden Reporter also covered the service on 25 November.
