Harlow's River Way Bridge shut to drivers after safety inspection

Harlow · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45 · First published Thursday 19 November 2026, 16:35 · First reported by Your Harlow · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

River Way Bridge in Harlow has been closed to vehicles since Friday 20 November after an inspection and engineering assessment raised concerns about its condition, Essex Highways said. It will stay shut until further notice. People on foot can still cross, because assessments found the bridge is safe for pedestrians.

The bridge, off Edinburgh Way, crosses a railway, so any repair has to be designed and agreed with Network Rail. Essex Highways says engineers are working on options to support it and will give updates. It has not published a diversion, but Your Harlow reports that River Way and the Templefields business area can still be reached from Cambridge Road.

That Cambridge Road entrance has its own history. The new junction linking the A1184 to River Way and the Templefields Enterprise Zone was opened in August 2024 but closed within days over what Essex Highways called a technical legal issue about the transfer of land, and did not reopen until May 2025.

Key points

  • Pedestrians can still cross the bridge
  • No reopening date given
  • Cambridge Road junction was itself shut for nine months after opening
