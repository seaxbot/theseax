# South Woodham Ferrers BP thefts: police identify man in CCTV appeal

**Officers thanked the public for sharing their appeal and say their enquiries are continuing.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: South Woodham Ferrers, Essex, England. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 15:38 (UK time). Updated: Monday 23 November 2026, 11:30. Section: News. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- Series of incidents at the Burnham Road garage shop in recent months
- Appeal images have now been withdrawn

## The story

Essex Police say they have identified the man they wanted to speak to in connection with a series of thefts from the BP garage shop in Burnham Road, South Woodham Ferrers.

Officers from the force's Chelmsford district had released CCTV images of the man on Sunday, saying there had been a number of incidents at the shop in recent months.

In an update on Monday, the force thanked everyone who shared the appeal and said its enquiries continue.
