# Saffron Walden families can drop in to make lanterns on Saturday

**No booking is needed for the session at the Jubilee Hub. It follows last week's Christmas lights switch-on in the town centre.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 06:21 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Drop-in session from 10am to 2pm
- Venue: The Jubilee Hub, CB10 1EH

## The story

Saffron Walden Town Council is running a lantern-making workshop at The Jubilee Hub, CB10 1EH, from 10am to 2pm on Saturday 28 November. Families can simply turn up, the council says. An earlier session took place on Friday afternoon.

It is part of the town's festive programme, which began on Friday 20 November when the Mayor, Cllr Jeanette Curtis, and Father Christmas switched on the lights. Stuart Vant of InXpress Cambridge hosted the evening and played music.

Cllr Curtis thanked everyone who came and backed her charity tombola. More events are listed on the Visit Saffron Walden website, and the council asks for feedback at events@saffronwalden.gov.uk.
