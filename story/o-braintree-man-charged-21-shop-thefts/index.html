Police charge man over string of shoplifting offences in Braintree

Braintree · Monday 16 November 2026, 18:00 · Reported by Essex Police

Essex Police said on Monday 16 November that a man has been charged with 21 counts of shop theft in Braintree. He has not been convicted of any offence.
