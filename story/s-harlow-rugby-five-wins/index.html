Harlow Rugby Club hold off Wanstead fightback for fifth win in a row

A 31-14 half-time lead at Latton Park was almost wiped out as Harlow finished with 13 men, but Liam Batty's late penalty settled it.

Harlow · Tuesday 3 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow Rugby Club made it five straight wins with a 41-35 home victory over Wanstead at Latton Park on Saturday, Your Harlow reports. Harlow led 31-14 at the break but had to dig in after a second-half comeback from the visitors.

Brad Etherington and Dale Stalley scored early tries as Harlow raced into a 17-0 lead, and Adam Nnochiri's try plus a second for Stalley secured the bonus point. Casey Baldock added a try after the break and Liam Batty kicked five conversions and two penalties. Wanstead scored five tries, including a penalty try, and cut the gap to three points as Harlow played the last 10 minutes with 13 men after two sin-bins.

Batty's late penalty restored a six-point margin. Harlow II also extended their unbeaten run to four with a 40-17 win over East London II, and the first team travel to Woodford on 14 November.

Key points

  • Harlow 41-35 Wanstead, half-time 31-14
  • Fifth win in a row
  • Liam Batty kicked 19 points
  • Next: away at Woodford on 14 November
