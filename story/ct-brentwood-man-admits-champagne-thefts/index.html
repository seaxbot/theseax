Brentwood man, 50, admits stealing champagne and meat from petrol station shops

Scott Allaker pleaded guilty to five shop thefts worth £1,117.55 and is due to be sentenced on November 25.

Brentwood & Wickford · Wednesday 28 October 2026, 10:21 · Reported by Basildon and Billericay Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scott Allaker, 50, of Beech Avenue, Brentwood, has pleaded guilty to five counts of shop theft committed between 8 August and 11 October.

Magistrates heard he took meat, wine, prosecco and champagne worth £1,117.55 in total from BP service stations including Herongate, the A13 at Orsett Cock and Hutton Mount, and from a Tesco in Brentwood. Roads policing officers stopped his car on London Road, Wickford, after a theft at the Hutton Mount site on 11 October and found eight bottles of champagne and one of prosecco.

Key points

  • Five thefts between 8 August and 11 October
  • Goods included champagne, prosecco, wine and meat
  • Bailed with a ban on entering several chains' stores in Essex and Suffolk
