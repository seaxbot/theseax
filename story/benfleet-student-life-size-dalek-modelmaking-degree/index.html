Benfleet student builds life-size Dalek for his degree

Benfleet · Saturday 21 November 2026, 06:32 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

He climbed inside the finished Dalek and sent it roaming the corridors of the University for the Creative Arts in Farnham, Surrey, for about an hour. It is based on the design used when Doctor Who returned in 2005.

The university supplied tools and much of the wooden casing, and he spent up to £400 of his own money. Its main weakness, he said, is uneven ground: "It gets defeated by very small inclines."

Key points

  • He graduated with a first-class degree
  • His next project is a portal gun from the video game Portal
