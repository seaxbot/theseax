Benfleet student builds life-size Dalek for his degree

Benfleet · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 21:05 · First published Saturday 21 November 2026, 06:32 · First reported by BBC News · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ben Dobinson, 27, from Benfleet, spent more than 300 hours building a full-size, working Dalek for his modelmaking degree at the University for the Creative Arts in Farnham, Surrey, BBC News reports.

In his final-year show, he says he set himself the brief of a production company such as the BBC commissioning an accurate replica prop for a new production. It is based on the design used when Doctor Who returned in 2005. The university supplied tools and much of the wooden casing, and he spent up to £400 of his own money. When it was finished he spent about an hour driving it around the campus from inside.

Its main weakness, he told the BBC, is uneven ground, which can stop it in its tracks. The Dalek was designed by Raymond Cusick for the BBC in 1963, and fan-built replicas have long been a pastime in sheds and garages across Britain.

Key points

  • Built for a modelmaking degree at UCA Farnham
  • Based on the 2005 Dalek design
  • Up to £400 of his own money
