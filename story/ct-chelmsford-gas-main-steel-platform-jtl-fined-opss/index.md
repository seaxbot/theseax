# Essex steel firm fined £12,000 over false papers for Chelmsford gas platform

**An Essex steel company supplied false safety paperwork for a platform holding up a Chelmsford gas main. It has been fined and must pay costs.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 07:53 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Prosecuted by the Office for Product Safety and Standards
- Costs of £12,048 plus a £2,000 victim surcharge

## The story

J.T.L. Steel Fabrications (U.K.) Ltd has been fined £12,000 at City of London Magistrates' Court, the government's Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) announced on Wednesday 25 November.

The company made a structural steel platform that supports a stepped-down high-pressure gas main and plant in Chelmsford. According to Construction Enquirer and UKPulse, it supplied the platform with falsified documents: a factory production control certificate, a declaration of performance and a declaration of conformity. That broke the Construction Products Regulations 2013, which require makers to prove structural products meet safety standards.

On top of the fine, the firm was ordered to pay £12,048 in costs and a £2,000 victim surcharge. Baroness Blake, the minister for building safety, said there "can be no shortcuts" on building safety. Neither report gives the date of the hearing or where in Essex the company is based.
