Harlow man admits drug-driving on Southern Way and is given interim ban

Perry Brown, 38, of Long Banks, Harlow, pleaded guilty at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court to driving with cannabis in his blood above the legal limit.

Harlow · Friday 13 November 2026, 15:34 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brown was driving a Vauxhall Astra on Southern Way, Harlow, on 8 April 2026 with at least 9.1 micrograms per litre of the cannabis compound THC in his blood, Your Harlow reports. Magistrates imposed an interim driving disqualification, and he is due back at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court on 8 February.
