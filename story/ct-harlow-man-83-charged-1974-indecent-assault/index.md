# Dover man, 83, charged with indecent assault in Harlow dating back to 1974

**An 83-year-old man from Dover in Kent will appear at Basildon Crown Court in December over an alleged offence in Harlow more than 50 years ago.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Friday 13 November 2026, 07:58 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Remanded on conditional bail
- The charge relates to a period of about four months in 1974

## The story

Brian Ponton, 83, of Wyndham Road, Dover, has been charged with indecently assaulting a girl under the age of 16 in Harlow between 29 June and 31 October 1974, Your Harlow reports. He has been released on conditional bail and is due at Basildon Crown Court on 4 December.
