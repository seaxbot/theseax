Weekly market to come back to Harlow's rebuilt Market Square in February

Harlow Council is asking traders to come forward to help shape a regular market selling fresh food and gifts in the town centre square.

Harlow, Market Square · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 08:17 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow Council leader Dan Swords said the weekly market will open in February in the redeveloped Market Square, Your Harlow reported on Tuesday 17 November. The council first promised its return in March, when it hoped the market would follow the end of building work in the summer.

Fresh bread, hot food, produce and goods from independent traders are planned. Days, opening times and the number of stalls have not yet been announced.

Key points

  • Traders can register interest with Harlow Council
  • Part of the council's Building Harlow's Future programme
