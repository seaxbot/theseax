# Chelmsford Local Plan hearings finish, with final day cancelled

**Public hearings on Chelmsford's plan for future homes and jobs have ended. Inspectors will now decide what changes it needs.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 11:47 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Property.

## Key points

- Design and monitoring policies will be settled in writing
- Changes the inspectors want will go out to public consultation
- No date yet for the inspectors' final report

## The story

The public hearings into Chelmsford's new Local Plan have closed, Chelmsford City Council said on Thursday 26 November. Planning inspectors have cancelled the last session, which had been due the following Tuesday.

A Local Plan sets out where new homes, jobs, roads and schools should go in the years ahead. Before a council can use it to judge planning applications, independent inspectors must decide that it is legally compliant and sound.

Hearings were held at Chelmsford Civic Centre over several sessions in November, [as The Seax reported when they opened](https://theseax.com/story/chelmsford-local-plan-examination-hearings-begin/). They covered housing, jobs, transport, infrastructure, the environment and the protection of valued landscapes and heritage. The inspectors decided the last two topics, design and monitoring policies, can be dealt with in writing instead. People who could not attend in person sent written comments, which count as evidence.

Councillor Richard Lee, the council's cabinet member for planning and place-shaping, said the plan "reflects our shared ambitions for Chelmsford" and thanked everyone who took part.

## What happens next

The inspectors will now weigh the evidence and say what main modifications, or changes, the plan needs to be sound. Chelmsford City Council will then publish those changes for public consultation. The inspectors will read the responses before writing their final report. The council has not given dates for any of these steps.
