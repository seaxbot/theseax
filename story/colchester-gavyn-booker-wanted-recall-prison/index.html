Colchester police seek Gavyn Booker, 34, wanted on recall to prison

Colchester · Saturday 21 November 2026, 14:53 · Reported by Essex Police Colchester
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police is asking for help to find Gavyn Booker, who is wanted on recall to prison.

Booker, 34, is about 5ft 9in tall, with long brown hair and a beard. He has connections to Colchester.

Anyone who sees him or knows where he is should contact Essex Police quoting reference 42/176658/26. Information can be given through the force's website or Live Chat, by calling 101, or anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Key points

  • About 5ft 9in tall
  • Quote reference 42/176658/26
  • Call 101 or Crimestoppers anonymously
