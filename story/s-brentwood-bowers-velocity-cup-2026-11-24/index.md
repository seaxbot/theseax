# Brentwood Town beat Bowers & Pitsea 4-2 in the Velocity Cup

**Bradley Sach scored twice inside ten minutes at The Arena. Brentwood are through to the fourth round.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 23:10 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Bowers hit the crossbar through a Connor Grace header
- Under-23 players Fenton Tucker and Ruben Kibrom started
- Next cup opponents: the winners of Southall v Newmarket Town

## The story

Brentwood Town beat Isthmian North side Bowers & Pitsea 4-2 at The Arena on Tuesday 24 November to reach the fourth round of the Velocity Cup. Sach struck in the seventh minute and again three minutes later. Ramsey McLachlan pulled one back, but an Ollie Peters free-kick and a Freddie Sears finish settled the tie. Danny Hanson scored a late penalty for Bowers.

Brentwood host Whitehawk in the league on Saturday.
