Broomfield Hospital gets £4.3m nuclear medicine suite for cancer scans

Broomfield, Chelmsford · Friday 20 November 2026, 16:00 · Reported by Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust
WhatsApp Facebook X

The suite is in the Nuclear Medicine department at Broomfield Hospital, the trust announced on Friday 20 November.

Doctors use these scans to plan cancer treatment as well as to diagnose the disease and check how it is responding. The machines are also used to investigate kidney and urinary conditions, the trust said.

Key points

  • Department: Nuclear Medicine
  • Also used for kidney and urinary checks
