Harlow's River Way Bridge shuts to vehicles until further notice

Harlow, River Way · Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:29 · Updated Saturday 21 November 2026, 00:41 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Highways closed the bridge off Edinburgh Way in Harlow to all traffic from Friday 20 November after an inspection raised concerns about its condition.

People on foot can still use the bridge, which crosses a railway line, because assessments found it safe to walk over, Your Harlow reports. Engineers are working on ways to support the structure, and any repair will need Network Rail's involvement. Harlow Council criticised the short notice given to the town. River Way can still be reached from Cambridge Road.

Key points

  • Any fix needs design, approval and building work
  • No diversion route had been published
