# Billericay Town hold Truro City to goalless draw in Cornwall

**Billericay Town stay sixth in the National League South after taking a point in wet and windy conditions against one of the division's front-runners.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Billericay, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 11:21 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Crowd of 1,460 at Truro City Stadium
- Khari Ranson kept a clean sheet in the Billericay goal
- Billericay are joint-second top scorers in the division

## The story

Billericay Town drew 0-0 at Truro City in the National League South on Tuesday 17 November 2026, watched by 1,460 at Truro City Stadium. Both defences had to clear shots off their own line.

Truro forward Luke Jephcott saw an eighth-minute effort scrambled away after a Christian Oxlade-Chamberlain long throw, and a Will Dean header from a set piece was also cleared on the line. After the break the home attack rarely troubled teenage Billericay goalkeeper Khari Ranson, and late on Jephcott tracked back to stop Jamie Mauge from shooting.

The result keeps Truro fourth, four points behind leaders Ebbsfleet United with a game in hand, The Post reported. Billericay host Slough Town on Saturday 21 November before a trip to Ebbsfleet on Tuesday 24 November.
