Braintree MP Cleverly targets Khan on crime and housing in mayor bid

Sir James Cleverly took 86% of London Conservative members' votes on 19 November. Braintree's Conservatives must now find a new parliamentary candidate.

Braintree · Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:04 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 07:42 · Reported by LBC
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sir James Cleverly, MP for Braintree since 2015, was chosen on Thursday 19 November as the Conservative candidate for the London mayoral election in May 2028. The former foreign secretary and home secretary told LBC he was "all in" for the contest.

He beat Westminster City councillor Festus Akinbusoye and Sara Gezdari, a former parliamentary candidate in Richmond Park, the BBC reports. Conservative leader Kemi Badenoch, his rival for the party leadership in 2024, congratulated him. Labour chair Bridget Phillipson said he should not be trusted with the capital.

After his selection, Sir James attacked Labour mayor Lord Khan's record on crime and housing. He said people are leaving London or choosing not to move there. Lord Khan has not said whether he will seek a fourth term. Reform UK has already named Laila Cunningham as its candidate.

  • Rivals: Festus Akinbusoye and Sara Gezdari
  • Kemi Badenoch congratulated him; Labour's Bridget Phillipson attacked his record
