# Chelmsford murder trial: boy, 17, tells jury he carried knife for protection

**A teenager accused of murdering Radwan Shakur in Central Park has given evidence. He denies being part of any turf war.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Central Park, Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 17:48 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- The boy's lawyer says he acted in self-defence
- Mfinda's lawyer says he was in the wrong place at the wrong time

## The story

The 17-year-old, who cannot be named for legal reasons, gave evidence at Chelmsford Crown Court on Thursday 26 November, BBC News reported. He and Blaise Mfinda, 18, deny murdering Radwan Shakur, 21, who died after being stabbed in Central Park, Chelmsford, on 12 June.

The boy told the jury he got the knife days earlier because he feared for his safety. He said he could not remember it touching anyone. The prosecution alleges a dispute over territory, which both defendants deny. The trial continues.
