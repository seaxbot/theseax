# Essex Poundland stores stay open for now as chain falls into administration

**Poundland's shops across Essex are still trading after the chain collapsed. Administrators are now looking for a buyer.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 16:31 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- AlixPartners appointed as administrators
- Gordon Brothers bought the chain for £1
- No store closures announced yet

## The story

Poundland went into administration on Wednesday 25 November, putting its shops in Essex towns including Southend, Chelmsford and Colchester at risk. The Press Association reported that specialists from the corporate finance firm AlixPartners have been appointed as administrators.

For now nothing changes for shoppers. The retailer will keep trading from its stores while the administrators search for a buyer, and no closures have been announced. Around 600 stores and some 11,000 jobs across the UK are at risk.

## How it got here

Gordon Brothers bought Poundland for £1 last year and began a sale process in September. Poundland first filed a notice of intention to appoint administrators on 29 October, which gave it 10 working days of protection from creditors. It filed a second notice on 12 November after the first expired, ITV News reported. MPs had accused the owner of cutting off funding.

Several bids fell through, including offers from Martin Property Group, Xyra Holdings and Zachert Private Equity. Tesco, Iceland and Lidl had been in talks over some of the shops. More than 100 Poundland stores had already shut during the owner's turnaround.

## What happens next

AlixPartners said it was "a deeply concerning time for all involved". The administrators will now try to sell all or part of the business. The Seax will report any decisions on individual Essex stores when they are announced.
