# Essex farmers raise fly-tipping costs at first county roundtable

**Farmers told Essex County Council that rural crime and the cost of clearing dumped waste hurt them. More meetings are planned.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 15:57 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Hosted by Essex County Council in November
- Roundtables every quarter for 18 months
- Water security and extreme weather also discussed

## The story

Fly-tipping on farmland and rural crime were the biggest worries raised at Essex County Council's first Farming and Rural Roundtable, held in November 2026. Farmers said clearing rubbish dumped on their land costs them money they cannot get back.

Essex County Council hosted the meeting. Farmers' groups attended, including Essex Young Farmers, the National Farmers' Union and the Countryside Land and Business Association. The Rural Community Council of Essex, the Farming and Wildlife Advisory Group, Natural England and Essex County Fire and Rescue Service also took part. Rising running costs, extreme weather and the security of water supplies were discussed too, the Bishop's Stortford Independent and Your Harlow reported.

Councillor Peter Harris, the Reform UK leader of Essex County Council, said agriculture "remains one of the foundations of our economy". Graham Harvey, who chairs the NFU in Essex, also welcomed the meeting.

The group agreed to meet every quarter for the next 18 months. Neither report gave dates for the next sessions or said what the council will do about fly-tipping on private land.
