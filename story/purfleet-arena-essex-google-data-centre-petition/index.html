Wildlife groups take 55,000-name petition to Google over data centre plan for Purfleet's old Arena Essex site

Buglife and Essex Wildlife Trust say the scheme would wipe out most of a protected wildlife site, while Google says it will regenerate the land and deliver a 10% biodiversity gain.

Purfleet, Thurrock · Thursday 5 November 2026, 14:06 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Campaigners have taken a petition with nearly 55,000 names to Google's London headquarters, urging the company to drop plans for a data centre on the former Arena Essex Raceway near Purfleet. The 52-hectare site was designated a Local Wildlife Site in 2006, and conservationists say it supports hundreds of species including rare bees, butterflies and nightingales.

Google's proposal is for up to four data centre buildings totalling 130,500 square metres, plus a separate office building.

Key points

  • Petition stood at 54,970 names
  • Up to four buildings totalling 130,500 square metres
  • 52-hectare Local Wildlife Site since 2006
