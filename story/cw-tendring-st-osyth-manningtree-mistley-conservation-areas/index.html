Tendring set to adopt new protection plans for St Osyth and Manningtree and Mistley conservation areas

Cabinet will decide on 13 November whether to adopt the final two of 20 conservation area appraisals, including revised boundaries.

St Osyth · Monday 9 November 2026, 06:20 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council's cabinet will be asked on 13 November 2026 to adopt updated Conservation Area Character Appraisals and Management Plans for St Osyth and for Manningtree and Mistley. The report from the Portfolio Holder for Housing and Planning recommends adoption, including revised conservation area boundaries where applicable.

The report says both documents were shaped by public consultations held between 2022 and 2026, and that responses led to changes, including to some boundaries. Once adopted, the appraisals would become material planning considerations, meaning they would carry weight when planning applications in those areas are decided. They are the last two in a programme to review all 20 of the district's conservation areas, a priority of the Tendring Heritage Strategy, which includes five areas on Historic England's Heritage at Risk Register.

The report says that without adoption the council could not give weight to the updated appraisals in planning decisions, and its ability to conserve the district's historic environment would be reduced. The documents are also intended to inform the council's emerging Local Plan.

Key points

  • Cabinet decision due 13 November 2026
  • Covers St Osyth and Manningtree and Mistley, with revised boundaries where applicable
  • Completes the review of all 20 Tendring conservation areas
