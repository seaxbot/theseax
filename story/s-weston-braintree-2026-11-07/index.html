Braintree Town beaten 4-1 by Weston-super-Mare in National League South

Luke Coulson struck twice before half-time for the hosts at the Optima Stadium, and Hubbard's late goal was only a consolation for the Iron.

Braintree · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:24 · Reported by Weston-super-Mare AFC
WhatsApp Facebook X

Braintree Town suffered a heavy 4-1 defeat away to Weston-super-Mare in the National League South on Saturday. The Iron were three goals down within eight minutes of the restart and could not find a way back.

Weston had a goal from Ollie Hulbert ruled out for offside before Luke Coulson opened the scoring from around 30 yards with a deflected effort on 33 minutes. Coulson then converted a penalty on the stroke of half-time after Will Jenkins Davies was tripped, Hulbert made it three on 53 minutes and Jenkins Davies added a fourth six minutes later.

Hubbard pulled a goal back for Braintree on 86 minutes, and Louis Britton dragged a late one-on-one chance over the bar for the home side. The game was watched by a crowd of 962.

Key points

  • Weston-super-Mare 4-1 Braintree Town, National League South
  • Weston scorers: Coulson 33 and 45 pen, Hulbert 53, Jenkins Davies 59
  • Hubbard scored Braintree's goal on 86 minutes
  • Attendance: 962
