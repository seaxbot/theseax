Colchester Local Plan heads for the inspector after more than 1,800 responses

Colchester City Council's draft Local Plan, which sets where new homes and jobs go across the city, could be sent for independent examination once a committee reviews the public's comments.

Colchester · Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

More than 1,800 responses came in from residents, businesses, community groups, developers and statutory bodies during the final Regulation 19 consultation on the plan, Colchester City Council said on Monday 16 November. The council's Local Plan Committee meets on 23 November to go through what people said and decide whether to recommend sending the plan to the Planning Inspectorate.

The council says the Government raised its housebuilding target for Colchester by 40% shortly after work on the plan began, and that the figure would climb by a further 20% if the city had no up-to-date plan in place. Councillor Martin Parsons, who chairs the Local Plan Committee, called it one of the most challenging local plan processes the council has had to undertake.

If the committee agrees, a government-appointed inspector is likely to examine the plan next year. Should the inspector find it sound, the final decision to adopt it rests with a meeting of the full council.

Key points

  • Local Plan Committee meets on Monday 23 November
  • Examination by the Planning Inspectorate likely in 2027
  • Full council makes the final adoption decision
