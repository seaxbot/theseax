Lung cancer screening arrives in Harlow, the first town in west Essex to get it

Current and former smokers in Harlow are being invited for free checks that can find the disease before any symptoms appear, NHS Essex says.

Harlow · Monday 16 November 2026, 14:46 · Reported by NHS Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Invitations began going out to eligible Harlow residents in November 2026, NHS Essex said on Monday 16 November, with Uttlesford and Epping Forest due to follow later. Each person first has a telephone consultation with a specialist nurse, and those judged to be at higher risk are offered a low-dose CT scan on a mobile unit, which is due to arrive in the town in December.

The NHS Lung Cancer Screening programme is open to people aged 55 to 74, but only those whose smoking history is recorded with their GP practice will be invited. Around 450 lung cancers have been found through the programme across Essex so far, and about three in four were caught at stage 1 or 2.

Dr Sara Astbury, a GP and clinical lead for cancer at NHS Essex, said symptoms are often missed or mistaken for less serious conditions, and that most people screened will get a normal result. Lung cancer causes more cancer deaths than any other cancer in the UK. The Harlow launch falls during Lung Cancer Awareness Month, and invitations will keep going out over several months.

Key points

  • Open to people aged 55 to 74 whose smoking history is on their GP record
  • About three quarters of the 450 or so cancers found in Essex were at an early stage
  • Uttlesford and Epping Forest are next in line
