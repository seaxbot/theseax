Autumn staycations close to home, from Mersea huts to woodland lodges

Glamping on Mersea Island, coastal cottages, vineyard stays, spa breaks and woodland lodges all make Essex Life's autumn escape list.

East Mersea · Reported by Essex Life (Great British Life)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Life has rounded up places to stay in the county this autumn for every taste and age, from glamping and coastal cottages to vineyard retreats, spa breaks and woodland lodges.

Mersea Barns Glamping in East Mersea has four luxury units on five acres of rural Mersea Island. Managing director Anthea Sanderson explains that its shepherd huts open all year and sleep two, with full-size double beds, built-in kitchens and shower rooms.

Elsewhere on the list, another featured stay has revamped two of its most secluded lodges for 2026 as couples-only retreats.

Key points

  • Four glamping units on five acres at East Mersea
  • Shepherd huts open all year, sleeping two
  • Vineyard, spa and woodland options also featured
