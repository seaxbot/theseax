Billericay community awards draw more than 20,000 public votes

Billericay · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 13:22 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cloud 9 Espresso Bar, the High Street Post Office and volunteer Mark Thomas were among 10 winners named at Billericay's second community awards.

The Your Billericay Business Improvement District handed out its 2026 awards at Cristi's Bar on Billericay High Street on Tuesday 17 November, with Great British Bake Off contestant Andy Ryan as host, Your Thurrock reports.

Votes rose on the first year of the awards in 2025, which drew more than 15,000. Other winners included K Barbers Emporium, Creatures Great & Small for customer care and The Olive Branch for its window display.

Key points

  • Each winner received a trophy and a £100 voucher to spend in the town
  • The 2025 awards drew more than 15,000 votes
