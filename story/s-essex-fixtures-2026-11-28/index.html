Essex fixtures: Colchester host York City on Saturday

Essex · Saturday 28 November 2026, 07:20 · Reported by Non League Matters: Isthmian North Division
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend United's trip to Hartlepool kicks off late, and Takeley against Bowers & Pitsea is one of two all-Essex derbies in the Isthmian North.

Colchester United lead the Essex fixtures on Saturday 28 November with a League Two home game against York City at the JobServe Community Stadium, kick-off 3pm. Southend United are away at Hartlepool United in the National League, with a later start of 5.30pm.

Southend defender Ben Goodliffe missed Tuesday's 2-0 win at Forest Green Rovers with an injury, and the club has not said if he is fit. Loan forward Omari Patrick can play again after sitting out the game against his parent club. Colchester's latest written team news, before the Tranmere game, had Kylian Kouassi out with a hamstring injury.

In the National League South, Braintree Town host AFC Totton at Cressing Road and Billericay Town are at home to Horsham. Chelmsford City make the long trip to Torquay United at Plainmoor. All three games kick off at 3pm.

In the Isthmian Premier Division, Aveley go to AFC Whyteleafe, Stanway Rovers visit Dulwich Hamlet, Brentwood Town host Whitehawk and Maldon & Tiptree welcome St Albans City.

Two Isthmian North Division games are all-Essex derbies: Takeley against Bowers & Pitsea, and Little Oakley against Canvey Island. Concord Rangers host Felixstowe & Walton United, Grays Athletic play Fakenham Town and Witham Town take on Gorleston. Tilbury go to Lowestoft Town, Buckhurst Hill visit Walthamstow and Brightlingsea Regent travel to Mulbarton Wanderers. Every Isthmian game kicks off at 3pm.

Away from football, Southend Saxons meet Westcliff in a rugby union derby at Warners Bridge at 2pm. Chelmsford Chieftains go to Romford Buccaneers in the ice hockey Challenge Cup at 5.15pm, then host Invicta Dynamos at Riverside on Sunday at 6pm.

Key points

  • Hartlepool have won their last two league games
  • Canvey Island are back in action at Redbridge on Monday night
  • Grays Athletic host Witham Town on Wednesday 2 December
