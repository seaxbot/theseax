Southend tip's Havens Hospices reuse shop sells 100 tonnes of goods

Southend-on-Sea · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 21:05 · First published Saturday 21 November 2026, 13:38 · First reported by Your Thurrock · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sales at the shop, run with SUEZ and Southend-on-Sea City Council, have passed £127,000 since it opened in May 2025.

The Havens Hospices charity shop at the Stock Road recycling centre in Southend has sold 100 tonnes of pre-loved goods, Your Thurrock reports. The charity says the money raised pays for more than 4,000 hours of care at Little Havens Children's Hospice in Thundersley or Fair Havens Adult Hospice in Prittlewell.

The shop opened in May 2025 as a partnership between the charity, Southend-on-Sea City Council and SUEZ, which runs the city's recycling centres; it was the 34th reuse shop in SUEZ's network. In its first year its sales paid for 1,750 hours of specialist hospice care, and the council has since marked its 10,000th sale.

People can donate furniture, electrical items, televisions, glassware, crockery and bric-a-brac as they leave the tip, or buy them, at Stock Road, SS2 5QF. Some items, including child car seats, motorcycle helmets, stair gates and upholstered furniture without a fire safety label, cannot be taken. The charity is looking for volunteers on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays.

Key points

  • Pays for 4,000+ hours of hospice care
  • First year paid for 1,750 hours of care
  • Volunteers wanted on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays
