# Felsted School meets every standard in October inspection

**Inspectors praised Felsted School's safeguarding culture and its curriculum for the youngest pupils.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Felsted, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 15:13 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Education.

## Key points

- Inspection carried out by the Independent Schools Inspectorate in October 2026
- Five areas checked, from leadership to pupils' wellbeing
- Earlier full reports from 2023 and 2019 are also online

## The story

Felsted School has met all of the Independent School Standards after an inspection in October 2026, according to the inspectors' report, which the school published in November.

The Independent Schools Inspectorate (ISI) carried out the visit. It is appointed by the Department for Education to inspect schools that belong to the independent school associations in England.

Inspectors looked at five areas: leadership, management and governance; the quality of education, training and recreation; physical and mental health and emotional wellbeing; social and economic education and contribution to society; and safeguarding. According to the school, they judged it to be meeting the required standards in all of them and commented positively on every part of its provision.

On safeguarding, the report describes a 'strong safeguarding culture where pupils feel safe and staff are well trained'. Inspectors called the curriculum for the youngest learners 'ambitious, coherent and responsive to children's interests'.

Felsted's head is Lara Pechard. Its earlier full ISI reports, from 2023 and 2019, sit on the school's website beside the new one. The Dunmow Broadcast first reported the outcome on Wednesday 25 November.
