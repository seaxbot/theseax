Police ask for dash cam footage after man in his 80s dies following Mount Bures crash on Hall Road

A woman in her 70s is still being treated in hospital after a car struck both pedestrians on Sunday afternoon.

Mount Bures, Colchester · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 17:32 · Updated Wednesday 11 November 2026, 16:48 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

The pair were hit on Hall Road in Mount Bures at about 1pm on Sunday 8 November, the BBC reports, and the man died the following day.

Essex Police said no arrests had been made and asked anyone with information, CCTV or dash cam footage to come forward.

Key points

  • The man died on Monday, a day after the collision
  • No arrests have been made, police told the BBC
