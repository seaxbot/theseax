Southend Airport hunts first Chief Christmas Officer with Lapland prize

Southend Airport, Rochford · Updated Monday 23 November 2026, 21:13 · First published Monday 23 November 2026, 10:45 · Reported by Breaking Travel News
WhatsApp Facebook X

London Southend Airport is looking for its first Chief Christmas Officer, and the winner and a guest fly to Lapland for the day on 22 December.

There is no CV and no interview. To apply, people post a short video on Instagram or TikTok saying why they deserve the job, using the hashtag #LSAChiefChristmasOfficer. Breaking Travel News reported that entries opened on Friday 20 November.

London Southend Airport is running the competition with tour operator The Lapland Experience. The best videos will be shared on both firms' social media, and the winner will be named on 11 December.

The prize

On the day, the winner and a guest fly from Southend to Rovaniemi in Finnish Lapland and are home the same evening. Their day includes huskies, reindeer, a one-to-one meeting with Santa and a visit to Santa Claus Village. The flight takes about three hours, according to the airport's own page.

Nigel Mayes, the airport's business development director, said the idea was to capture the excitement of Christmas and show off its Lapland flights.

Lapland from Southend

The Lapland Experience announced its Southend day trips in March. It said its earlier trips had flown from other airports.

Key points

  • Entries are videos on Instagram or TikTok
  • Winner named on Friday 11 December
  • Flight to Rovaniemi takes about three hours
