# Epping job fair draws more than 300 people hoping to work in tourism

**Epping Forest District Council says jobseekers met employers including Stansted Airport, Jet2 and the Epping Ongar Railway at its Civic Offices event.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Epping, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:31 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Run with the Department for Work and Pensions
- Jet2, Enterprise Mobility and Chigwell Tours also exhibited

## The story

More than 300 people went to Epping Forest District Council's Tourism and Hospitality Job Fair at the Civic Offices in Epping on Thursday 12 November, the council said on Wednesday 18 November. Visitors looking for work, a change of career, seasonal jobs or apprenticeships met employers face to face.

The council ran the fair with the Department for Work and Pensions. Exhibitors included Epping Forest District Museum, Princess Alexandra Hospital's catering service, Lee Valley Regional Park, the Royal Gunpowder Mills, Harlow College, Visit Essex and Delta Hotels by Marriott.

Councillor Holly Whitbread, the council's portfolio holder for economic development, said tourism and hospitality offer careers for people of all ages and backgrounds, and that the event helped bring businesses and jobseekers together.
