E-bike pods at Harwich station and on the harbour ferry aim to keep coastal visitors moving

Greater Anglia and Freeport East have launched the Harwich Haven Eezybike Connect network, with five pods at stations and on the Harwich Harbour Ferry.

Harwich · Saturday 7 November 2026, 08:45 · Reported by RailAdvent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rail and ferry passengers around Harwich can now pick up an electric bike for the last leg of their journey after Greater Anglia launched a new e-bike network on the Essex and Suffolk coast. The scheme, called Harwich Haven Eezybike Connect, puts five e-bike pods at railway stations, including Harwich Town, and on the Harwich Harbour Ferry.

The network was developed by Freeport East, the area's Industrial Strategy Zone, and received a £25,600 grant from Greater Anglia's Customer and Community Improvement Fund, which is backing 33 projects this year. Pod locations were chosen to line up with local hotels and Shotley Marina, with the aim of encouraging visitors to stay longer. Greater Anglia's partnerships manager Scott Dolling said the project makes it easier for customers to carry on their journeys beyond the railway.

Felixstowe station across the estuary in Suffolk is also part of the network. Greater Anglia says the bikes offer a low-carbon way to explore lesser-known corners of the harbour area, and the full list of pods and hire details is published by the operator.

Key points

  • Five e-bike pods at stations and on the Harwich Harbour Ferry
  • Harwich Town station among the sites
  • £25,600 grant from Greater Anglia's improvement fund
  • Developed by Freeport East
