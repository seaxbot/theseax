Volunteers wanted for Tesco winter food collection on 26 to 28 November

Volunteers are sought for three-hour shifts at collection points in large Tesco stores, supporting Trussell food banks and the Felix charity network.

Essex · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 13:24 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Foodbanks and food charities are looking for volunteers to stand at collection points in large Tesco stores for the supermarket's Winter Food Collection, which runs from 26 to 28 November. Volunteering takes about three hours, and the charities say having people in store significantly increases how much food is given.

This is the 14th year of the collection, run with charity partners Trussell and Felix, and last year shoppers donated almost 1.5 million meals' worth of food. One in five Trussell food parcels comes from Tesco and its customers, and more than half of the charities in the Felix network, which spans over 8,000 local groups, report rising demand over the past year.

People can sign up through the Trussell or Felix websites to help at their nearest large Tesco. Trussell's co-chief executive Matthew van Duyvenbode said volunteers were urgently needed to play their part.

Key points

  • Runs 26 to 28 November in all large Tesco stores
  • Shifts take about three hours
  • Last year's collection gave almost 1.5 million meals
