Colchester United drawn at home to Mansfield Town in FA Cup second round

The U's will host Mansfield for a place in the third round, with ties to be played over the weekend of Saturday 5 December.

Colchester · Monday 9 November 2026, 19:00 · Reported by The National League
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United, the only Essex club left in Monday's FA Cup second round draw, will face Mansfield Town at home, the Daily Gazette reports. Ties are due to be played over the weekend of Saturday 5 December, and the National League says winners at this stage collect £79,500 in prize money, with £21,200 for the beaten side.

Key points

  • Home tie against Mansfield Town
  • Ties on the weekend of 5 December
  • Winners get £79,500, losers £21,200
