Shoebury toddler Blake, 17 months, remembered as police inquiry continues

Shoeburyness · Updated Wednesday 18 November 2026, 19:39 · First published Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:07 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Blake Branch died after ambulance crews raised concerns for a child's welfare at a Shoeburyness home in October. Detectives investigating his death have two people on conditional bail.

Essex Police released the little boy's name and his family's tribute on Wednesday 18 November, five weeks after officers were called to an address in Eagle Way, Shoeburyness. Ambulance crews had raised concerns for a child's welfare there just before 8am on Monday 12 October, and detectives opened an investigation after Blake died.

"My precious little boy was loved more than words could ever explain," his family said. They said they longed for one more cuddle with him, and thanked everyone who has shown them kindness and support.

A 24-year-old man from Rayleigh and a 26-year-old woman from Shoeburyness were arrested as part of the investigation into Blake's death. Both are on conditional bail while enquiries continue, Essex Police said, and the family have asked for privacy while the investigation takes its course.
