Harlow's Water Gardens to host Mind in West Essex's fifth Merry Minds fun run

A festive 5km for walkers, joggers and runners in Harlow will raise money for a mental health charity whose funds all stay in west Essex.

Harlow, The Water Gardens · Monday 16 November 2026, 09:37 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mind in West Essex is holding its Merry Minds Festive Fun Run at The Water Gardens in Harlow on Sunday 29 November, with registration from 9am and the start at 10am, Your Harlow reports.

Fancy dress is encouraged, the route suits buggies, and dogs on leads can join in. Anyone fundraising for the charity through a sponsored walk or run gets a free place. Genna Roberts, the charity's community connector, said being active and having a laugh helps wellbeing in the darker months.

Key points

  • Fifth year of the event, the charity's biggest annual fundraiser
  • Every finisher gets a Merry Minds medal
  • Tickets for adults, children, families, groups and dogs
