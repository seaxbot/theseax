Brentwood residents earn the most in Essex and rank fourth in the UK for pay

Full-time workers living in Brentwood borough had median pay of £58,540 a year in April 2026, Office for National Statistics figures show, a rise of 16.9% on a year earlier.

Brentwood · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 16:53 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow sits at the other end of the Essex table at £37,622, according to the provisional Annual Survey of Hours and Earnings published on 22 October and analysed by Brentwood Online. The survey counts people where they live, so commuters from Shenfield, Hutton and Ingatestone are included. Only Westminster, St Albans and Wandsworth ranked higher among 346 council areas, and Uttlesford was second in Essex at £50,614.

Key points

  • Three in ten residents working full time earn over £78,100
  • Brentwood was 16th in England a year earlier
  • Essex County Council area median: £44,811
