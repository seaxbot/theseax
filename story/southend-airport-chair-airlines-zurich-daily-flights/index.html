Southend Airport wins daily Zurich flights with Swiss carrier Chair Airlines

Flights from London Southend Airport to Switzerland's largest city start on 27 May 2027 and run all year, with tickets on sale from 16 November 2026.

Southend-on-Sea · Monday 16 November 2026, 09:22 · Reported by London Southend Airport
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chair Airlines, a carrier based at Zurich Airport, will be the newest airline at London Southend Airport, the airport announced on Monday. The airport is selling the route both ways: a direct link for travellers from Essex, London and the South East heading for the city or the Swiss mountains, and an easier way for Swiss tourists and business travellers to reach Essex and London.

Sharmeen Divan, the airport's interim chief executive, said the daily service was another step in Southend's growth, giving passengers the choice of a European city break, a summer escape or a trip to explore Switzerland.

Key points

  • The airport says ski areas such as Flumserberg and Hoch-Ybrig are within reach of Zurich
  • Southend had the best on-time performance of any UK airport in the second quarter of 2026, the airport says
  • Tickets are sold through chair.ch
