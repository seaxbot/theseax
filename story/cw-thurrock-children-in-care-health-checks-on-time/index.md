# Three in four Thurrock children entering care now get their first health check on time, up from 36%

**Councillors scrutinising children's services heard that initial health assessments within the 20-day legal deadline have more than doubled.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Monday 9 November 2026, 06:20 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- 75% of initial health assessments now within 20 days, up from 36%
- Only one delay in the period was due to capacity
- Formal review of the nurse-led model due in December 2026

## The story

Thurrock Council's Children's Services Overview and Scrutiny Committee was told on 22 October 2026 that 75% of initial health assessments for children entering care are now completed within the statutory 20-day timescale. The figure compares with 36% previously, according to the minutes of the meeting.

Officers put the improvement down to closer joint working with Children's Services, more paediatric capacity and better oversight. They said late assessments were mainly caused by changes of placement, hospital admissions, missed appointments and occasional staff absence, and that only one delayed assessment in the reporting period was down to a lack of capacity.

The service is moving to a model led by specialist nurses working with paediatricians, and officers said a formal review in December 2026 will look at the views of children and families, the quality of assessments and timeliness. Councillors questioned whether enough paediatric capacity would remain, and officers said the aim was to use existing paediatric resources more effectively. The committee noted the update.
