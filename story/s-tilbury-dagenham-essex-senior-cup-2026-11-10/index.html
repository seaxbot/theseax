Ten-man Dagenham & Redbridge knock Tilbury out of the Essex Senior Cup

The Thurrock side, short of several players through cup-tie rules and absences, bowed out of the county competition in the fourth round.

Tilbury · Thursday 12 November 2026, 14:54 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tilbury lost 2-0 to National League South side Dagenham & Redbridge in the fourth round of the BBC Essex Senior Cup on Tuesday 10 November 2026. Freddie Ingram put the visitors ahead in the seventh minute after a defensive mistake, and Wiktor Makowski converted a 75th-minute penalty.

Dagenham played most of the tie a man down after Jack Bateson was shown a straight red card on 30 minutes for a last-man foul on Rasheed Salau.

Key points

  • The crowd was 336
  • Jake Brocklebank made his 50th Tilbury appearance
  • Tilbury travel to Felixstowe & Walton United in the Isthmian North on Saturday 14 November
