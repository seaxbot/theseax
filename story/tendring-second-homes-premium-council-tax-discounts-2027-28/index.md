# Tendring keeps 100% council tax premium on second homes

**Tendring District Council has kept its council tax discounts and extra charges for 2027/28. Working-age claimants could also get help backdated.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Clacton-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 00:39 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- Support scheme: up to 80% off for working-age claimants
- Second homes in Tendring fell by 186 in a year
- Final decision on backdating expected in January 2027

## The story

Second-home owners in Tendring will go on paying a 100 per cent council tax premium from 1 April 2027, which doubles their bill. Tendring District Council's full council agreed on Tuesday 24 November to leave its discounts and extra charges unchanged for 2027/28. Extra charges on homes left empty for a long time also stay at their current levels, and the existing exceptions remain.

The council said the number of second homes in the district fell by 186 in the past year. Cabinet has also agreed to widen the council's hardship policy, so it can consider help for people facing the extra charges on second homes and empty properties. Help for care leavers moving into their own homes carries on, alongside the council's other local discounts.

## Backdating for working-age claimants

Tendring's Local Council Tax Support Scheme gives eligible working-age residents up to 80 per cent off their bills, and that level of help stays the same next year. Council leader Mark Stephenson has backed plans to let working-age claimants have their support backdated by up to three months, which pension-age claimants can already do. Anyone asking for backdated help would have to show they were eligible during that time.

"When residents are struggling, a little help at the right time can stop things getting worse," Cllr Stephenson said. The council says the change is aimed at people who qualify but cannot apply straight away, for example because of difficult personal circumstances.

The backdating plan is still subject to consultation, [as The Seax reported when it was first proposed](https://theseax.com/story/cw-tendring-council-tax-support-backdating-working-age/). A draft scheme goes to Tendring's cabinet in December, and full council is expected to make the final decision in January 2027.
