# Uttlesford opens Warm Spaces with free soup in five towns this winter

**Uttlesford District Council is pointing residents to Warm Spaces in Saffron Walden, Great Dunmow, Thaxted, Stansted and Newport, plus a cost-of-living hub.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Great Dunmow, Thaxted, Stansted, Newport, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 17 November 2026, 10:49 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Council helpline: 01799 510 510
- Uttlesford Citizens Advice also offers help in person

## The story

Warm Spaces across Uttlesford offer a free bowl of homemade soup, company and advice from local organisations, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reported on Tuesday 17 November. Listings are on the council's warm spaces page, and its Cost-of-Living Support Hub covers benefits, debt, council tax support and help with heating costs.
