c2c trains to call at Purfleet, Rainham and Dagenham Dock again from Wednesday after Barking derailment repairs

Passenger services through the stretch shut since a freight train came off the tracks near Barking on 23 October are due back on Wednesday 11 November, ending almost three weeks of rail replacement buses for Purfleet commuters.

Purfleet, Thurrock · Monday 9 November 2026, 11:58 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 09:26 · Reported by c2c
WhatsApp Facebook X

c2c says passenger trains will resume calling at Dagenham Dock, Rainham and Purfleet from Wednesday 11 November, after the line near Barking was closed by the derailment of a GB Railfreight train on 23 October. The closure has left Purfleet and Rainham passengers relying on replacement buses to Grays and Upminster for nearly three weeks.

RailAdvent reports that engineers have repaired more than 1.2km of track, including rebuilding a damaged set of specially made points. Network Rail has confirmed the reopening, which also brings back London Overground trains between South Tottenham and Barking Riverside. Rob Mullen of GBR Anglia said he was pleased passenger services would return on Wednesday.

Until then, trains do not stop at the three stations and passengers are advised not to travel from them. Rail replacement buses run between Upminster and Grays via Rainham and Purfleet, and tickets are accepted on Greater Anglia and on TfL buses. Separately, London-bound trains are still not stopping at Limehouse while work continues there.

Key points

  • More than 1.2km of track and a set of points repaired
  • Replacement buses Upminster to Grays run until services resume
  • London-bound trains still not calling at Limehouse
