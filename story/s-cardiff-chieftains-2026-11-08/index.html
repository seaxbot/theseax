Chelmsford Chieftains hit nine to win 9-3 at Cardiff Fire

Tom Soar was man of the match as the Chieftains bounced back from Saturday's shootout loss at Swindon.

Chelmsford · Monday 9 November 2026, 23:14 · Reported by Chelmsford Chieftains
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford Chieftains beat Cardiff Fire 9-3 at Ice Arena Wales on Sunday night in NIHL South Division 1. The sides were level at 2-2 after the first period before Chelmsford scored five times in the second.

Tom Soar and Sasha Maltsev both scored twice, with Hallden Barnes-Garner, Grant Bartlett, Daniel Hitchings, Lane Paddison and Rio Grinell-Parke also on target.

Key points

  • Cardiff Fire 3-9 Chelmsford Chieftains
  • Five Chieftains goals in the second period
  • Tom Soar named man of the match
