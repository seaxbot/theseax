Southend: four masked men attack man who refused to hand over money

Southend, Clifftown Road · Wednesday 25 November 2026, 17:16 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man in his 30s was left with rib and neck injuries. Police want to hear from anyone who saw what happened.

A man was attacked by four men in Clifftown Road, Southend, after he refused to give them money, Essex Police said.

The man, who is in his 30s, was left with injuries to his ribs and neck. It happened at about 8.30pm on Tuesday 24 November, and police are treating it as a robbery.

The four men were dressed all in black and wore balaclavas.

Officers believe other people were in the area at the time and may have seen what happened. They want to hear from anyone with information, or with CCTV, dash cam or other footage.

Anyone who can help should quote crime reference 42/187896/26. Reports can be made on the Essex Police website or through its online Live Chat, which runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week. To stay anonymous, contact the independent charity Crimestoppers online or on 0800 555 111.

Key points

  • Attack in Clifftown Road at about 8.30pm on Tuesday
  • Four men dressed all in black, wearing balaclavas
  • Crime reference 42/187896/26
