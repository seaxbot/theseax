Harlow Market Square's first rebuild phase is finished

Harlow · Saturday 21 November 2026, 07:26 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Work now moves to the buildings around the square. The clock on Adams House is being restored and its ground floor turned into an all-day food and drink venue, while Market House becomes flexible office space.

The rebuild is part of Harlow Council's Building Harlow's Future programme, backed by the government's Towns Fund. Councillor Dan Swords, leader of Harlow Council, said: "Every great town has a heart."

Key points

  • A weekly market of independent traders returns in February 2027
  • The square also has space for stalls and events
