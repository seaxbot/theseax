# Harlow Market Square's first rebuild phase is finished

**A weekly market returns from February 2027, and the buildings around the square are next, including the restored clock on Adams House.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 07:26 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45. Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- New square has water jets, granite paving and rain gardens
- Market House becoming office space
- Paid for with £23.7m from the government's Towns Fund

## The story

Harlow Council has finished rebuilding Market Square from the ground up, the first phase of its plan to bring the heart of the town centre back to life, Your Harlow reports. The square now has a water jet feature at its centre, granite paving, planting and rain gardens, seating, new lighting and CCTV, with space set aside for market stalls and outdoor events.

A weekly market will return from February 2027, with independent traders selling fresh produce, artisan food and gifts. Work has moved on to the buildings around the square. The landmark clock on Adams House is being restored, and its ground floor is becoming an all-day food and drink venue with live entertainment and outdoor dining. Market House is becoming office space, and work is under way at The Rows.

The council appointed Hill Partnerships to carry out the rebuild, backed by £23.7m from the government's Towns Fund. It also bought Market House and Adams House, putting the square's clock under local control for the first time in decades. Building started in October 2025 and was first due to finish in spring 2026. Council leader Dan Swords said the new square was built to the standard of the best town centres.
