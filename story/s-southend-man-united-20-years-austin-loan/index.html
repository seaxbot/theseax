Southend mark 20 years since Eastwood sank Manchester United, as Sam Austin rejoins Kidderminster on loan

BBC Essex revisits the night Roots Hall beat Sir Alex Ferguson's side in the League Cup, while forward Sam Austin heads back to Aggborough for the rest of the season.

Southend-on-Sea · Saturday 7 November 2026, 06:22 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo Sports)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saturday marks 20 years since Southend United beat Manchester United 1-0 at Roots Hall in the fourth round of the League Cup, on 7 November 2006. Freddy Eastwood's 27th-minute free-kick settled the tie in front of 11,532 fans, with Southend then bottom of the Championship, and BBC Essex has released a four-part podcast in which those involved relive the win.

Sir Alex Ferguson made nine changes but still fielded ten internationals, including Wayne Rooney and Cristiano Ronaldo, who was twice denied by goalkeeper Darryl Flahavan. Then manager Steve Tilson recalled that the players feared the worst when they saw the teamsheet. Southend went on to take Tottenham to extra time in the quarter-final before losing to a Jermain Defoe goal.

In current squad news, the club confirmed on Thursday that Sam Austin has joined Kidderminster Harriers on loan, and Kidderminster say the move runs until the end of the season. Austin, who left Aggborough four years ago for Notts County, told the Harriers he had always kept a place in his heart for the club and felt the timing was right.

Key points

  • Southend 1-0 Manchester United, 7 November 2006
  • Eastwood free-kick, 27 minutes, crowd of 11,532
