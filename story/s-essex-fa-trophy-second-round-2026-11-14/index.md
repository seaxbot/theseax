# Billericay Town beat Waltham Abbey to stay in the FA Trophy

**Billericay are the last Essex club left in non-league's national knockout competition, after a weekend that ended the cup hopes of Braintree, Aveley and Chelmsford City.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Billericay, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 14 November 2026, 20:35 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 15 November 2026, 18:15. Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Braintree's crowd against Kettering was 407
- Fans voted Ben Isaacson Braintree's man of the match
- Saturday 21 November: Braintree host Torquay United, Aveley visit Carshalton Athletic

## The story

Billericay Town won the all-Essex FA Trophy second round tie against Waltham Abbey 3-1 at New Lodge on Saturday 14 November 2026. Elsewhere, Braintree Town lost 3-2 at home to Kettering Town and Aveley went down by the same score at Walton & Hersham.

At Cressing Road, Shaun Jeffers gave Kettering an early lead before Alfie Lewis levelled on 36 minutes. Lewis Coyle restored the visitors' advantage early in the second half and Ben Isaacson equalised again, but Jaiden White struck what proved the winner on 64 minutes. Isaacson and Kettering's Edward Panter were both forced off injured after a collision in the 82nd minute.

Chelmsford City also went out, losing 6-5 on penalties after a 1-1 draw at AFC Telford United. Braintree's own report notes the club has now exited all four of its cup competitions this season, leaving manager Steve Pitt's side to concentrate on the National League South.
