# Grays' Essex Fire Museum wins Best Museum at Essex Mums Awards

**The free museum at a working Grays fire station beat strong competition, including Essex Police's museum in Chelmsford.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 07:52 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Open on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10am to 3.30pm
- Christmas open day on Sunday 13 December

## The story

Essex Fire Museum was named Best Museum for 2026 at the Essex Mums Awards, Your Harlow reported on Tuesday 24 November. It shows vintage fire engines, uniforms and equipment from three centuries, and volunteers use a mock house to point out fire risks.

Dan Bailey, heritage and volunteering lead at Essex County Fire and Rescue Service, thanked the volunteers who host school, scout and family visits. Entry is free, but visits must be booked by email because the museum sits on an operational fire station.
