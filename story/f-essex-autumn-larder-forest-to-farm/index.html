From hedgerow to cheeseboard: what to eat in Essex this autumn

Essex Life asks a forager and an award-winning cheesemaker what the county's larder has to offer as the nights draw in.

Essex · Reported by Essex Life (Great British Life)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Apples, oysters, game, honey and cheese all hit their stride in autumn, and Essex Life has gone looking for the best of the county's seasonal larder, with a few foraged surprises along the way.

At The Foraging Chef, the season is treated as an adventure: wild mushrooms, and hedgerow fruits such as rosehips, crab apples and sloes, are at their peak. Muntjac also feature, with the case made that eating the deer puts a plentiful local food to use while helping to keep numbers in check.

Bradfields Farm Dairy talks through the cheeses locals keep coming back for, including its soft Beam and an Essex herb cheese. For a warm autumn day it suggests its Bourne Brook spread over a barbecue skewer with aubergines and marmande tomatoes from Sarah Green Organics and a drizzle of Essex rapeseed oil.

  • Muntjac on the menu at The Foraging Chef
  • Bradfields Farm Dairy's autumn cheese picks
