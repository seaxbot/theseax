Essex all-rounder Luc Benkenstein leaves for Durham

The leg-spinner has left by mutual agreement after 80 appearances across all formats since joining in 2021.

Chelmsford · Thursday 19 November 2026, 13:25 · Reported by Essex County Cricket Club
WhatsApp Facebook X

Luc Benkenstein has left Essex by mutual agreement and will join Durham as part of the deal, the club announced on Thursday 19 November.

The all-rounder arrived at Chelmsford in 2021 and made his debut that season in the One Day Cup, against Durham. He went on to play 80 times for Essex across all formats, and had signed a contract extension that ran until the end of the 2027 season.

His father, former South Africa international Dale Benkenstein, was appointed a batting consultant at Durham in March. Essex thanked Benkenstein for his efforts and commitment and wished him well for the future.

Key points

  • Debut came against his new county
  • Had been contracted until the end of 2027
