Step inside a painting: Simon Carter turns Firstsite into an Essex shoreline

The Frinton artist's biggest show yet fills Colchester's gallery with painted walls, sea-defence 'groynes' and sounds recorded on the coast.

Colchester · Reported by Essex Life (Great British Life)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Frinton-based painter Simon Carter has turned Firstsite in Colchester into a walk-through painting for From the Landscape, the largest exhibition of his career.

The show blends painting, architecture and sound. Carter has built monolithic walls modelled on the groynes that protect the Essex shoreline, and added a soundscape made from his own recordings of the coast. Firstsite director Sally Shaw says he has been quietly mapping the Essex landscape for more than forty years.

The exhibition is supported by the Firstsite Collectors' Group and Trevor Fenwick, and is listed to run until 17 January 2027.

Key points
