# Little Havens shows bereaved children's grief artwork in Thundersley

**Havens Hospices is displaying 'My Invisible Backpack', pictures made by children it supports, for Children's Grief Awareness Week.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Thundersley, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 08:42 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Horizons supports parents and carers whose child has died
- Exhibition ends Friday 20 November

## The story

The exhibition is on show at Little Havens children's hospice in Thundersley until Friday 20 November, Havens Hospices said on Wednesday 18 November. It was first shown at the Leigh Art Trail in July and then at Fair Havens' bistro, and explores the hidden feelings children carry when someone important has died.

The charity's children and young people's wellbeing team offers counselling, therapeutic play and memory boxes, plus Headspace, a group for 5 to 11-year-olds, and Griefside workshops for those aged 11 and over.
