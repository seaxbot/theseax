Lloyd Scott, Theydon Bois fundraiser who did a marathon in a diving suit, dies

Tributes have been paid to the former Leyton Orient goalkeeper and firefighter whose costumed marathons raised money for charity, the BBC reports.

Theydon Bois, Epping · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 12:42 · Updated Wednesday 18 November 2026, 15:39 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lloyd Scott, appointed an MBE in 2005 for services to charity, took six days to finish the 2002 London Marathon in a 130lb deep sea diving suit, BBC Essex reported on Tuesday 17 November. He later played Indiana Jones with a boulder and crawled the course as Brian the Snail.

He had leukaemia in 1987 and later sarcoma. Former England goalkeeper Peter Shilton said he was devastated to lose a great friend, and Dagenham & Redbridge, one of his old clubs, praised his generosity.

Key points

  • Ran his last marathon in 2023, aged 61, as Captain Scarlet
  • Also kept goal for Blackpool and Watford
