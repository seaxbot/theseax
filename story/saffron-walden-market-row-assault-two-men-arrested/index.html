Two men arrested after man suffers facial injuries in Saffron Walden's Market Row

Essex Police are appealing for information after a man in his 30s was taken to hospital following an assault in Saffron Walden town centre.

Saffron Walden, Market Row · Sunday 15 November 2026, 15:40 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Officers were called to Market Row in the early hours of Sunday 15 November, Greatest Hits Radio reports. The man's condition is not thought to be life-threatening.

Two men, aged 19 and 25, were arrested. Anyone with information is asked to contact Essex Police quoting reference 81 of 15 November, or Crimestoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.

Key points

  • Police were called at about 1.40am
  • Information to Essex Police, quoting reference 81
