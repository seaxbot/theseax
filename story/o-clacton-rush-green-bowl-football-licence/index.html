Clacton Town given temporary licence to keep playing at council-owned Rush Green Bowl

Tendring council says the short-term deal covers the rest of the 2026/27 season while it prepares an open tender for a longer lease on the ground.

Clacton-on-Sea · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council has granted Clacton Town Football Club Ltd a temporary licence to use the Rush Green Bowl on Rush Green Road, Clacton, for the rest of the 2026/27 season. The council says the licence formalises the club’s current occupation of the council-owned ground, with safeguards for the site, and keeps it open for football and community use.

The council says it has been working to resolve longstanding issues around the ground’s occupation. Councillor Peter Kotz, cabinet member for assets, said the licence keeps football going at the site and welcomed improvements the club has made to the ground.

Next, the council plans an open and fair tender for a longer-term lease. It says whoever wins the lease may be able to apply for Football Association funding.
