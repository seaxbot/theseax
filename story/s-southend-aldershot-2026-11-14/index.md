# Southend United fight back to beat ten-man Aldershot Town 2-1 at Roots Hall

**Second-half goals from Kyle McAdam and Tyreece Simpson kept Southend in the National League play-off places.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 14 November 2026, 18:54 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Southend are third in the National League on 32 points
- Leaders Boreham Wood visit Roots Hall on Friday 20 November, kick-off 7.45pm

## The story

Southend United came from behind to beat Aldershot Town 2-1 in the National League at Roots Hall, Southend-on-Sea, on Saturday 14 November 2026. Anisius Lewis gave the visitors a sixth-minute lead, and Aldershot played most of the game with ten men after John Gilbert was sent off on 26 minutes. McAdam equalised on 56 minutes and Simpson scored the winner on 66.
