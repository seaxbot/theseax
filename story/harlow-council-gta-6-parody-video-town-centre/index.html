Harlow Council spoofs GTA 6 with video tour of its town centre revamp

Harlow Council has made a jokey video in the style of Grand Theft Auto VI. It takes viewers round the town's roundabouts and rebuilt Market Square.

Harlow · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:54 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

The council put the video out on Wednesday 18 November, the day before the game went on sale, Your Harlow reports. It uses the game's look to promote new homes, rebuilt neighbourhoods and the multi-million-pound overhaul of the town centre. Council leader Dan Swords said Harlow deserved the 'blockbuster treatment' because so much is changing. He said people could see the changes just by stepping outside their front door.

Key points

  • GTA 6 went on sale at midnight on Thursday 19 November
  • Council leader Dan Swords fronted the launch
