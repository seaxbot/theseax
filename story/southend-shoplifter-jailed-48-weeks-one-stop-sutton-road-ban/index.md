# Southend shoplifter jailed for stealing from shop he was banned from

**Josh Love, 31, stole from the One Stop in Sutton Road five times in five days despite a court ban.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend, Sutton Road, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 17:04 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Courts.

## Key points

- Jailed for 48 weeks by Southend magistrates
- 16 shop thefts between 1 and 18 November
- More than £1,600 of goods taken

## The story

A Southend shoplifter who was banned from a city shop by the courts has been jailed for 48 weeks after stealing from it five times in five days, Essex Police said.

Josh Love, 31, had been barred from entering the One Stop Shop in Sutton Road for two years under a criminal behaviour order imposed in January, the Gazette reported. Breaking such an order is a criminal offence in its own right.

Southend magistrates heard he ignored the ban. He admitted 16 counts of shop theft committed between 1 and 18 November.

As well as the One Stop Shop, he stole groceries from Tesco and Sainsbury's in Southchurch Road. The goods he took were worth more than £1,600 in total.
