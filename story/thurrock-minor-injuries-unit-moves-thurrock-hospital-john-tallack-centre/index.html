Thurrock's minor injuries unit leaves Orsett for a bigger home at Thurrock Hospital

The Thurrock walk-in unit now has its own plaster room, a wound care room with a shower and X-ray next door, and is open 10am to 7.30pm every day.

Grays, Thurrock · Saturday 14 November 2026, 14:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock MP Jen Craft toured the minor injuries unit at the John Tallack Centre on the Thurrock Hospital site on Friday 13 November, after it moved from Orsett Hospital in the first half of November. NHS Essex secured £1 million in outside funding for the move, Your Thurrock reports.

Ms Craft said the unit should take pressure off the A&E at Basildon Hospital and that she would keep an eye on parking. NHS Essex wants to turn it into an urgent treatment centre that also sees minor illnesses within five years.

Key points

  • Run by North East London NHS Foundation Trust
  • Closes at 6.30pm on the last Thursday of each month
  • Larger waiting area with a children's space
