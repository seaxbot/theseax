Colchester asks residents where to save money in 2027/28 budget

Colchester · Updated Thursday 19 November 2026, 07:40 · First published Tuesday 17 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester City Council opened its consultation on the 2027/28 budget on Monday 16 November, asking residents where it should cut, spend more or work differently. It names rising costs, steady demand for services and the growing cost of supporting people facing homelessness as the main pressures.

Councillor William Sunnucks, portfolio holder for resources, said: "We need your views on where we can save money." The answers will shape priorities for services including bin collections, parks, leisure, housing and community safety. People can fill in the online survey or attend a Residents' Panel session at the Town Hall from 10am to noon on Tuesday 8 December, or from 7pm to 8.30pm on Thursday 10 December.

Cabinet looks at the first draft budget position on Wednesday 2 December, and the consultation closes at 9am on Monday 14 December. Full Council sets the final budget in February 2027.

Key points

  • Survey opened on 16 November
  • Residents' Panel sessions on 8 and 10 December
  • Final budget goes to Full Council in February 2027
