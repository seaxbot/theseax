Rochford warns Local Plan consultation may have to run over Christmas unless ministers lift their hold

The council says a Government holding direction in place since 2 October has blocked the final round of public consultation, with a 31 December submission deadline looming.

Rochford, Rayleigh · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Rochford District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rochford District Council has called on the Government to make a swift decision on its Local Plan, the document that will decide where new homes and infrastructure go across the district. A holding direction placed on the plan on 2 October stops the council starting the final round of public consultation until ministers lift it.

The council says it sent the evidence the Government asked for on 7 October and believes it is being reviewed. To meet the 31 December deadline for submitting the plan, it says a decision was needed by 5 November, leaving two weeks to prepare and six weeks for consultation; any later and the consultation would run over Christmas and close on the day the plan must be submitted. Council leader Cllr Adi Malviya said the authority had been on track before the holding direction and that a Local Plan is the best way to make sure new homes come with transport, schools and services.

The council had asked for a two-month extension, and Cllr Malviya said the Government has indicated the deadline could move to March, which he hopes will be offered when ministers respond. Whatever the timing, all comments would still go to an independent planning inspector, and the council says it will keep residents updated. Separately, the public inquiry into the Mount Bovers appeal on land west of Main Road, Hawkwell, resumes on 8 December.

Key points

  • Holding direction in place since 2 October
  • Plan must be submitted by 31 December
  • Council sent requested evidence on 7 October
  • Deadline could be extended to March, leader says
