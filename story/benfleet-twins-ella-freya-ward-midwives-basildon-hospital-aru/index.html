Benfleet twin sisters to work as midwives at Basildon Hospital

Benfleet, Basildon · Friday 27 November 2026, 20:38 · Reported by Anglia Ruskin University
WhatsApp Facebook X

Ella and Freya Ward, 21, graduated together in Chelmsford. Both trained on placement at Basildon Hospital and will now work there.

Identical twins Ella and Freya Ward from Benfleet are starting work as midwives at Basildon Hospital, after graduating from Anglia Ruskin University's midwifery course at Chelmsford Cathedral. The university told their story on Friday 27 November.

The sisters have stayed side by side since school. Both went to Appleton School in Benfleet, then studied health and social care at nearby USP College before applying to Anglia Ruskin University (ARU). Their hospital placements were at Basildon Hospital. They passed their driving tests three days apart and for a while shared a car to get to lectures and shifts.

Being identical has led to the odd mix-up on the delivery suite. Freya said a patient once asked whether she was tired after a long day, having mistaken her for Ella, who had just finished a day shift. Freya had only started her night shift a few hours earlier. She said the confusion "became a bit of a joke among the midwives".

Ella remembers helping to deliver her first baby in her first year of training. The family named the child Freya, and she told her sister straight away. The pair said they had different strengths: Ella was better at coursework and Freya at exams, so they shared revision notes. Freya said her mother first suggested midwifery when she was 15.

Joining the maternity unit

They join Basildon Hospital's maternity team after the trust stopped using gas and air there from Thursday 26 November over staff safety concerns, as The Seax has reported. ARU says 3,140 students are receiving degrees at its autumn ceremonies in Chelmsford, Cambridge and Peterborough.

Key points

  • Both went to Appleton School and USP College
  • Driving tests passed three days apart
  • A baby Ella helped deliver was named Freya
