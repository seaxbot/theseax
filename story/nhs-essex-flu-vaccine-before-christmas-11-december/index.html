NHS Essex urges flu jabs by 11 December for Christmas protection

Essex · Thursday 19 November 2026, 19:21 · Reported by NHS Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

NHS Essex, the integrated care board for the whole county, said on Thursday 19 November that people who miss that date should still have the vaccine, because flu keeps circulating long after Christmas. Older people, pregnant women and people with some long-term health conditions face the greatest risk of serious illness.

Eligible adults can book through the NHS website or NHS App, call 119 free if they cannot book online, or ask their GP practice or a participating pharmacy, some of which take walk-ins. Children who were two or three on 31 August 2026 can have the vaccine at their GP practice or a pharmacy, and from 1 December pupils from Reception to Year 11 who missed the school round can go to a participating pharmacy.

Dr Matthew Sweeting, executive medical director at NHS Essex, asked families to fit the jab into their Christmas plans, since gatherings make flu easier to spread. More than 2 million people in the East of England have had a flu jab so far this season, said Dr Eleanor Powers of NHS England's regional team.

Key points

  • Call 119 if you cannot book online
  • Pregnant women can ask their midwife
  • School-age catch-up jabs at pharmacies from 1 December
