Basildon councillors confirm refusal of 175 green belt homes at Little Burstead

Basildon Borough Council's Planning Committee agreed the formal grounds for turning down homes west of Tye Common Road, near Billericay, going against officers' advice to approve.

Billericay · Thursday 12 November 2026, 06:24 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Borough Council's Planning Committee agreed on Tuesday 3 November 2026 the formal reason for refusing outline permission for up to 175 homes on land west of Tye Common Road, Little Burstead, near Billericay. Councillors had resolved to refuse the scheme at their meeting on Wednesday 7 October 2026, and the wording was brought back for agreement. The vote was 5 in favour, none against and 2 abstentions, with two councillors who missed the earlier meeting taking no part.

The agreed reason says the site makes a strong contribution to the Green Belt's purpose of checking urban sprawl, so it does not count as grey belt, and the homes would cause substantial harm to the openness of the Green Belt. The committee was not satisfied that the benefits clearly outweighed that harm. Officers had recommended approval, pointing to 50% affordable housing, public open space, biodiversity gains and financial contributions.

The report notes that the risks of planning decisions relate to appeals, cost awards and judicial review. The applicant can appeal against the refusal to the Planning Inspectorate.

Key points

  • Outline plan for up to 175 homes refused on Green Belt grounds
  • Vote: 5 for, 0 against, 2 abstentions
  • Officers had recommended approval
  • Application reference 25/01214/OUT
