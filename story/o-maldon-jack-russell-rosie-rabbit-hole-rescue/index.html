Rosie the Jack Russell is dug out of a Maldon rabbit hole and learns absolutely nothing

Firefighters cleared trees and dug with great care to free the terrier, who bolted for the next burrow the moment she was out.

Maldon · Monday 16 November 2026, 15:53 · Updated Monday 16 November 2026, 17:50 · Reported by Essex County Fire and Rescue Service
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rosie, a Jack Russell terrier, was on an afternoon walk with her owner in Maldon on Monday 16 November when she spotted a rabbit hole, went in and could not get back out. Essex County Fire and Rescue Service was called at about 2.50pm.

Crews had to remove trees before they could dig her out, working very carefully to reach her, the service said. Its picture shows Rosie back above ground and getting a fuss from two of the firefighters who freed her.

Freedom did not last long. Once out, Rosie made straight for another rabbit hole, though this time she was on her lead. The service reminded people that its crews rescue animals as well as tackling fires, and asked anyone who finds an animal in trouble to call 999 and leave the rescue to them.

Key points

  • Rosie got stuck at about 2.50pm on Monday while out walking with her owner
  • Luckily she was on her lead for attempt number two
