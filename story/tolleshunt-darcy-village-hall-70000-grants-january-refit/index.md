# Tolleshunt D'Arcy village hall wins £70,000 for January refit

**Tolleshunt D'Arcy's century-old village hall has secured grants for its long-awaited repairs, years after a bid to replace the building outright fell through.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Tolleshunt D'Arcy, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 05:00 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Lottery bid for £870,000 failed
- A new build would have cost up to £1.1m
- Hall run by a charity trust since April 2019

## The story

Work on the floors, the stage and the storage areas of Tolleshunt D'Arcy Village Hall is due to begin in January, Essex County News reported on Tuesday 24 November. The grant money will pay for the next stage of a refurbishment that villagers have been planning for years.

## A long road

Tolleshunt D'Arcy Parish Council's project page tells the story. A parish survey by the Rural Community Council for Essex reported in 2018, and a charity trust with seven trustees took over running the hall in April 2019. Its first plan was a new building. The trustees bid for £870,000 from The National Lottery's Reaching Communities programme, but the bid failed.

Planning permission for a new hall was granted in August 2021. By October 2022 the estimated cost of a new build had risen to between £1.04m and £1.1m. The trustees then chose to refurbish the existing hall in stages instead.

Their plan has five phases, starting with the main hall, foyer and toilets. It includes insulating the floors and roofs, new windows and doors, and probable rewiring. Local help has played its part: D&D Engineering of Tollesbury refitted the kitchen free of charge, and volunteers from Ford UK painted the main hall and kitchen in 2022.
