Met Office fog warning for Essex ends at 11am after a misty Monday morning commute

The yellow fog warning that covered Essex from Sunday evening ended at 11am on Monday, with sunny intervals forecast for the rest of the day.

Essex · Sunday 8 November 2026, 16:40 · Updated Monday 9 November 2026, 11:21 · Reported by Met Office
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Met Office yellow weather warning for fog that took in Essex ended at 11am on Monday 9 November. It had run from 6pm on Sunday 8 November, covering the evening and overnight hours and the start of the working week.

The Met Office said the fog would affect parts of England and Wales from Sunday evening into Monday. Its forecast for Chelmsford early on Monday showed mist with a temperature of about 8C, gusts of only about 6mph and almost no chance of rain. The Daily Gazette reported that the warning stretched across Essex.

With the warning over, the Met Office is forecasting sunny intervals and a high of 10C in Chelmsford for the rest of Monday. Tuesday looks similar, before cloud and light rain are expected on Wednesday.

Key points

  • Yellow fog warning ran from 6pm Sunday 8 November to 11am Monday 9 November
  • Mist was reported in Chelmsford early on Monday
  • Sunny intervals and about 10C forecast for the rest of Monday
