Yellow fog warning now in force across Essex until 11am on Monday

Drivers face poor visibility tonight and into the Monday morning commute, with the Met Office alert running from 6pm on Sunday to 11am on Monday as mist settles over Chelmsford.

Essex · Sunday 8 November 2026, 16:40 · Reported by Met Office
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Met Office has issued a yellow weather warning for fog that takes in Essex, running from 6pm on Sunday 8 November until 11am on Monday 9 November. The alert covers the evening and overnight hours and the start of the working week, so people heading out on Sunday night or travelling on Monday morning are among those affected.

The Met Office said the fog would affect parts of England and Wales from Sunday evening. Its forecast for Chelmsford showed mist already settling on Sunday afternoon, with a temperature of about 8C, light west-northwesterly winds and very little chance of rain. Overnight the temperature is forecast to fall to around 3C. The Daily Gazette reports that the warning stretches across Essex.

Conditions are expected to improve once the warning ends, with the Met Office forecasting sunny intervals and a high of 10C in Chelmsford on Monday. Tuesday looks similar, before cloud and light rain are expected on Wednesday.

Key points

  • Yellow fog warning from 6pm Sunday 8 November to 11am Monday 9 November
  • Overnight low of about 3C forecast in Chelmsford
  • Sunny intervals and 10C expected on Monday after the fog clears
