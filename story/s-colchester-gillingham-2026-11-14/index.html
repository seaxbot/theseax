Colchester United end 10-game winless run by beating Gillingham 2-0

Two goals inside nine second-half minutes at the JobServe Community Stadium lifted the mood in Colchester after weeks without a win.

Colchester · Saturday 14 November 2026, 16:45 · Updated Saturday 14 November 2026, 17:00 · Reported by KentOnline
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United beat Gillingham 2-0 in League Two at the JobServe Community Stadium on Saturday 14 November 2026. Jack Payne converted a 61st-minute penalty after being fouled by Brophy, and Harry Anderson finished from close range around 74 minutes. A crowd of 5,548, including 1,255 from Kent, watched the U's end a run of 10 games without a win.

Key points

  • Visiting goalkeeper Ollie Wright saved from Anderson before half-time
  • Referee Lewis Sandoe waved away a Gillingham penalty appeal
  • Gillingham suffered their fifth league defeat of the season
