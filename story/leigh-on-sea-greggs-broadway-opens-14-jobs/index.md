# Leigh Broadway's Greggs opens despite residents' petition

**The bakery chain has opened on Leigh-on-Sea's Broadway. Nearly 2,000 people had signed a petition against it.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Leigh-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 10:05 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Unit is the former Factory Shop
- Sign redesigned for the conservation area

## The story

Greggs opened its shop at 25 The Broadway in Leigh-on-Sea on Thursday 26 November, creating 14 jobs, the Echo reported.

Your Southend reported in September that the plans for the former Factory Shop unit had been approved. A petition called Keep The Broadway Independent gathered more than 1,980 signatures, and the shop sign was changed to suit the Leigh Conservation Area.
