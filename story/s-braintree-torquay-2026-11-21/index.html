Braintree Town hold Torquay United to goalless draw at Cressing Road

Braintree · Saturday 21 November 2026, 19:12 · Reported by Torquay United
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Braintree side kept a clean sheet against a Torquay team that made most of the chances, according to the visitors' own report.

Braintree Town drew 0-0 with Torquay United in the National League South at Cressing Road on Saturday 21 November 2026.

Torquay's Jacob Beaumont-Clark hit the base of the post before half-time, and a Braintree defender cleared a Sonny Blu Lo-Everton shot off the line. Braintree's best chance came late on, when Harry Shield cleared Alex Bolovan's effort off the line.

Key points

  • Braintree are 19th in the National League South on 17 points
  • Torquay host Chesham United on Tuesday night
