# KFC applies to serve hot food until 3am on Brentwood High Street

**Residents have until 18 December to comment on the plan, which would run to 3am from Thursday to Saturday.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, High Street, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 17:10 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Up to 3am Thursday to Saturday
- Consultation runs for 28 days
- Applicant: Lexus Foods Limited

## The story

KFC on Brentwood High Street could serve hot food until 3am at weekends, under a licence application now with Brentwood Borough Council.

Lexus Foods Limited has applied for a new premises licence for late night refreshment indoors at KFC, 10 High Street. It would run from 11pm to 3am on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, to 1am on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and to midnight on Sundays and Mondays.

Late night refreshment is the legal term for selling hot food or hot drinks between 11pm and 5am, which needs a licence. The application covers indoor service only.

The council's 28-day consultation ends at midnight on Friday 18 December. Anyone who wants to support or object must write to the licensing team at licensing@brentwood.gov.uk or at the Town Hall, Ingrave Road, Brentwood CM15 8AY, quoting reference 26/00020/LAPRE.

Under the Licensing Act 2003, comments have to relate to the four licensing objectives: preventing crime and disorder, public safety, preventing public nuisance and protecting children from harm. If objections are made and not resolved, an application normally goes to a council licensing sub-committee hearing.

The application was first highlighted by The Brentwood Brief newsletter. The council's notice is dated 20 November.
