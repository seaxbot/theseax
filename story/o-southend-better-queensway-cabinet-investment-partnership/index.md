# Southend cabinet backs investment partnership for Better Queensway

**Southend-on-Sea City Council's long-running plan to rebuild the Queensway area moves on to its next stage.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Queensway, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 09:09 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## The story

The council's cabinet agreed to establish an investment partnership to take the Better Queensway regeneration forward, Southend-on-Sea City Council said on Wednesday 18 November. The Queensway area sits on the edge of Southend city centre.
