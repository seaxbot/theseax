Essex members given until Monday to renew at old price as Division One place is finally secured

With Leicestershire's appeal lost and top-flight status confirmed for 2027, Essex have held back their membership price rise so 2026 members can renew at the current rate until 9 November.

Chelmsford · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 15:09 · Updated Wednesday 4 November 2026, 16:22 · Reported by Essex Cricket
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex will play Division One cricket in 2027 after Leicestershire lost their appeal against the points penalty that sent them down. Essex finished 17 points above Leicestershire, and their own top-flight status had been in limbo for more than five weeks while the case was heard.

Leicestershire were stripped of the 19 points from their August win over Glamorgan after the pitch was rated poor, with a further deduction and a £5,000 fine. BBC Sport reports the appeals panel upheld the 19-point penalty, refused fresh expert evidence and said the club was the author of its own misfortune; chief executive Emma White said the club was hugely disappointed but would be ready for Division Two.

Essex said the outcome confirms its place in Division One and thanked members, supporters and sponsors for their backing during an uncertain period. The club has also held back its membership price rise, so 2026 members can renew at the current rate until 11.59pm on Monday 9 November, with prices going up the next day.
