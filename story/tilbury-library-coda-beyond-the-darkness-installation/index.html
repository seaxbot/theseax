Interactive artwork about living with MS and Parkinson's goes on show at Tilbury library

Thurrock dance charity CoDa has brought its Beyond the Darkness installation to Tilbury Library and Hub until Monday 7 December.

Tilbury · Thursday 12 November 2026, 08:02 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

CoDa Dance Company, a Thurrock dance charity, has opened its award-winning interactive installation Beyond the Darkness at Tilbury Library and Hub, Thurrock Nub News reported on Thursday 12 November 2026. It pairs a sensor screen that gets visitors moving with short dance films about life with multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's and acquired brain injury, made with neuro-disabled consultants.

Artistic director Nikki Watson founded the charity in 2013. The work was shown earlier at the Thameside in Grays.

Key points

  • Entry is free and the space is fully accessible to wheelchair users
  • CoDa also runs weekly Dance for Neurology sessions in hospitals
