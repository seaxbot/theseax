# Essex GP surgeries to offer 32,000 extra appointments this winter

**NHS Essex is paying for 8,000 more GP appointments a month until February. It is part of a county-wide plan for the busiest season.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 15:40 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Health.

## Key points

- NHS Essex has invested £1.1m
- Practices in Southend and Thurrock take part
- Separate national £40m scheme targets high-risk patients

## The story

NHS Essex, the county's integrated care board, has put £1.1m into the extra appointments, which run from November 2026 to February 2027. Groups of GP practices across Essex will offer them, including in Southend and Thurrock. Depending on what a patient needs, the appointment may be with a GP, a nurse, a physiotherapist or another member of the practice team.

Michelle Stapleton, winter lead director at NHS Essex, set out the plan in an article published on Thursday 26 November. She said the money should help more people get care close to home. She also warned that it does not mean every pressure on local services will disappear.

## One plan for the county

Ms Stapleton said hospitals, GP surgeries, ambulance crews, mental health teams, councils, social care and charities are working to a shared winter plan. Health bosses keep a daily view of demand and spare capacity so they can spot problems early. Services can call on each other for help seven days a week when pressure rises.

She asked families who can help a relative leave hospital to talk to the ward team about practical support. That could mean arranging a lift home and making sure keys, food and heating are ready. Getting patients home safely frees beds for others who need them.

## The national picture

The government announced a separate £40m programme on 9 November to help GP teams reach patients most at risk of becoming seriously unwell. According to the Department of Health and Social Care, it is aimed at people with heart disease, frailty and lung conditions such as COPD, up to 300,000 patients across England. Ms Stapleton said NHS Essex is still waiting for details of what it will mean locally.
