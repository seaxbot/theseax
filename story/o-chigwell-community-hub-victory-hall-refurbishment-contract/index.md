# Chigwell's Community Hub and Victory Hall to get major refurbishment

**Chigwell Parish Council has given Hartley Services Group the contract for a major refurbishment of the Community Hub and Victory Hall.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chigwell, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Announced by the parish council on 20 November 2026
- The parish holds its festive lights switch-on at Station Green on Saturday 5 December
