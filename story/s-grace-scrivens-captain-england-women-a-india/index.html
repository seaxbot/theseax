Essex skipper Grace Scrivens to lead England Women A in four-day match in Mumbai

The Essex Women's captain will take charge of England's second string for the red-ball game that closes their tour of India.

Chelmsford · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 16:03 · Reported by Essex County Cricket Club
WhatsApp Facebook X

Scrivens will captain the side at the Wankhede Stadium, Essex County Cricket Club announced on Tuesday 17 November. England Women A have split the captaincy across the three formats of the multi-format tour.

Surrey bowler Ryana MacDonald-Gay will lead the team in the three-match T20 series that opens the tour in Pune. Surrey wicketkeeper-batter Kira Chathli takes charge for the three 50-over matches in Mumbai, as she did in the home series last summer.

Injuries have forced two squad changes.

Key points

  • T20 series opens the tour in Pune
  • Issy Wong and Emma Lamb out injured
