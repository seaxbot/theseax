Braintree and County Hall leaders vow to resist Home Office bid to keep Wethersfield asylum site open after April 2027

The government has applied to extend its use of the former airbase near Braintree, where asylum seekers have been housed since July 2023, and both councils say they will look at ways to oppose it.

Wethersfield, Braintree · Monday 9 November 2026, 06:28 · Updated Monday 9 November 2026, 20:45 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News)
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Home Office has applied to keep housing asylum seekers at MDP Wethersfield, the former airbase near Braintree, after its current permission runs out in April 2027, BBC News reports. The site has been in use since July 2023.

Braintree District Council leader Tom Cunningham said the site was still unsuitable and should close as soon as possible, while county council leader Peter Harris said the authority would review the application and consider every option to challenge further expansion.

Key points

  • Current permission for the site expires in April 2027
  • The Home Office says such sites have round-the-clock security and CCTV
  • Essex Police's chief constable says crime linked to large asylum sites in the county is very low
