Almost 200 tonnes of glass collected from Tendring doorsteps in first month of new recycling rounds

Households across Tendring have taken to putting bottles and jars in their green boxes since the expanded Veolia service began.

Clacton-on-Sea, Tendring · Friday 13 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Glass recycling across Tendring rose by more than 115 tonnes in October compared with September, after Tendring District Council began collecting bottles and jars from the kerbside on Monday 5 October under its new waste contract with Veolia. Cartons and plastic pots, including yoghurt pots, joined the green box at the same time.

The council said the October glass figure includes bottle bank collections, and that early data suggests people are recycling more glass overall rather than simply switching from the banks to their green boxes. Collections of cartons, plastic bottles and steel cans also rose, though no tonnages were given.

Council leader Mark Stephenson called the early figures really encouraging and urged households that have not yet started to join in. Crews lift the boxes by hand, so the council asks residents not to put out broken glass.

Key points

  • Cartons and plastic pots also now collected
  • Glass figure includes bottle bank collections
  • Broken glass should not go in the box
