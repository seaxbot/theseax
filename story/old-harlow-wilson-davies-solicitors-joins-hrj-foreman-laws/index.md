# Wilson Davies solicitors in Old Harlow becomes part of HRJ Foreman Laws

**Old Harlow's longest-established law firm has joined a Hertfordshire practice. Clients keep their usual advisers, the firms say.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Old Harlow, Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 09:44 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Wilson Davies was founded in 1978
- Both are now trading names of Foreman Laws LLP
- HRJ Foreman Laws also has an office in Welwyn Garden City

## The story

Wilson Davies & Co Solicitors, which has served clients from Station Road in Old Harlow since 1978, has merged with HRJ Foreman Laws Solicitors, according to Your Harlow. HRJ Foreman Laws is based in Hitchin.

Emma Muldoon, a partner at Wilson Davies, said it would be business as usual for clients. They gain access to the wider firm's advice on trusts, inheritance tax and business law.
