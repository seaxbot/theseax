# Grays Athletic win 4-2 at Brightlingsea Regent in Isthmian North

**Tilbury, Witham Town and Buckhurst Hill also won on Saturday, while three Essex sides drew 2-2, including Takeley against Concord Rangers.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 18:22 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Gorleston lead the division on 38 points
- Concord Rangers are eighth, the highest Essex club
- Little Oakley host Canvey Island on Saturday 28 November

## The story

Grays Athletic won 4-2 at Brightlingsea Regent in an Essex derby in the Isthmian League North Division on Saturday 21 November 2026. Tilbury beat Wroxham 4-1 at home and Witham Town won 3-1 at AFC Sudbury.

Buckhurst Hill won 2-0 at Canvey Island in front of 321. Takeley drew 2-2 with Concord Rangers, Little Oakley drew 2-2 with Hashtag United and Bowers & Pitsea drew 2-2 with Felixstowe & Walton United.
