# Southend boss Dyer calls Hartlepool's 5.30pm kick-off a 'shambles'

**Southend United fans face another late start at the far end of the country. The manager says they will still travel in their hundreds.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 16:01 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Southend sit 16 points behind unbeaten leaders Boreham Wood
- Boreham Wood's chairman criticised a 5.30pm start for Carlisle fans in September

## The story

Southend United manager Kieron Dyer has criticised the 5.30pm kick-off for his side's National League game at Hartlepool United on Saturday 28 November, BBC Sport reported on Friday 27 November.

Dyer said it was the second time this season a late start had sent fans to the opposite end of the country. Southend are fourth, three points behind second-placed Harrogate Town. The National League and broadcaster DAZN were asked for comment.
