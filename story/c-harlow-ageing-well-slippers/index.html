Free slippers and falls advice for older people at Rainbow Services' Ageing Well drop-in in Harlow

Around 25 stallholders, speakers and seated exercise sessions feature at a free morning event aimed at cutting falls among Harlow's older residents.

Harlow · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 08:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow charity Rainbow Services is holding a free Ageing Well drop-in next week aimed at reducing falls among older people in the town. The event runs from 10.30am to 1pm on Wednesday 11 November at GPCA in Abercrombie Way, and everyone is welcome without booking.

Its Community Builder team has organised the morning with Princess Alexandra Hospital and other local groups, with about 25 stalls offering advice on staying safe at home and on local support.

Key points

  • Wednesday 11 November, 10.30am to 1pm
  • GPCA, Abercrombie Way, Harlow
  • About 25 advice stalls and seated exercise
