Police appeal after three men claiming to be officers targeted people in Tilbury before dawn

Police say a wallet and keys were taken in one of three incidents around St Chads Road and Calcutta Road early on Saturday 7 November.

Tilbury · Saturday 7 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police is appealing for witnesses after men claiming to be police officers approached people in Tilbury between 4am and 4.45am on Saturday 7 November. Police say the men were not in uniform and showed no identification, and nobody was hurt.

In one incident near the Tesco Express on Calcutta Road, two men got out of a white car and took a man's wallet and keys, police said. Officers say a taxi driver was also targeted on St Chads Road and another man was approached in the area. Police described three men, one in a high-visibility jacket and two in dark tracksuits, using a large white SUV-style car.

Grays CID is asking anyone who saw anything, or who was driving in the area with a dash cam, to come forward. Police say genuine officers will show their warrant card when asked and understand if people want to check their identity.

Key points

  • Police described the men as using a large white SUV-style car
  • Police advise asking any officer to show a warrant card
