Hanningfield among England's most depleted reservoirs as Essex hosepipe ban enters fourth month

Hanningfield and Abberton, which supply much of mid and south Essex, held 37% of their capacity on 10 November, below average for the time of year, Essex & Suffolk Water says.

Hanningfield · Thursday 12 November 2026, 06:22 · Updated Thursday 12 November 2026, 13:49 · Reported by Essex & Suffolk Water
WhatsApp Facebook X

In June, the company's total supply stood at 83%, the BBC reports. Katie Davies, Essex & Suffolk Water's head of water service planning, told the BBC the November level was "below where we'd expect to be at this time of year".

Essex & Suffolk Water brought in a Temporary Use Ban, or hosepipe ban, at 00:01 on Monday 3 August 2026 after a long hot and dry spell. It says the ban remains in force across its supply area.

The BBC reports that the Environment Agency still lists the East of England as in drought and names Hanningfield among eight reservoirs or reservoir groups in England with exceptionally low levels. The company says its advice to conserve water will stay in place until a considerable amount of rain has fallen.
