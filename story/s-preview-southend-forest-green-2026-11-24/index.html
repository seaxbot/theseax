Southend United visit struggling Forest Green Rovers on Tuesday

Southend-on-Sea · Tuesday 24 November 2026, 07:15 · Reported by Forest Green Rovers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kieron Dyer's Southend side, sixth in the National League, look to bounce back from their 3-2 loss to leaders Boreham Wood with a trip to Nailsworth.

Southend United play Forest Green Rovers in the National League at The New Lawn, Nailsworth, on Tuesday 24 November, with a 7.45pm kick-off. The Shrimpers are sixth on 32 points. That puts them level with Yeovil Town, Carlisle United and Worthing, and three behind second-placed Harrogate Town. Forest Green are 18th on 19 points but have three games in hand.

Both clubs lost at the weekend. Southend fought back from three goals down against Boreham Wood at Roots Hall on Friday but fell one short. A day later Forest Green conceded an injury-time winner at Yeovil and lost 3-2. Their own preview notes that Southend have won only one of their last five league games, while Rovers are unbeaten in three at home in all competitions. Southend won the last meeting 2-0 at Roots Hall in March.

Head coach Dyer, who replaced Kevin Maher in June, returned from a one-match touchline ban for the Boreham Wood game. Rico Richards scored on his debut off the bench that night, and Tyreece Simpson headed in from substitute Maxwell McKnight's cross. Leon Chambers-Parillon is top scorer with eight goals, while Jack Stone is away on a month's loan at Grays Athletic. Neither club had put injury news in writing before the game. DAZN, which shows the National League, lists the match in its live schedule for the week.

Key points

  • Leon Chambers-Parillon leads Southend's scoring with eight goals
  • Forest Green won 2-1 when the sides met at The New Lawn in November last year
  • Boreham Wood lead the table on 48 points
