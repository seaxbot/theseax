# Southend climb to fourth with 2-0 win at Forest Green

**Southend United won 2-0 at The New Lawn on Tuesday 24 November, bouncing back from their home defeat by leaders Boreham Wood four days earlier.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 00:33 (UK time). Updated: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 00:45. Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Cardwell and McAdam scored 20 minutes apart
- Two substitutes set up the second goal
- Boreham Wood lead the table by 13 points

## The story

Harry Cardwell and Kyle McAdam scored after a goalless first half. Nine minutes after the break a cleared corner fell to McAdam just outside the box. He drove it through a crowded area and Cardwell got the slightest of touches to put Southend ahead. Twenty minutes later two substitutes combined: Sahid Kamara ran at the defence and found Zavier Massiah-Edwards, who teed up McAdam to finish into the far corner.

Forest Green had chances of their own in front of 1,498. Southend goalkeeper Joe Duncan saved from Jayden Clarke at close range, and Clarke later saw a deflected shot hit the outside of the post. At the other end, Forest Green keeper Pagel kept out a header from McAdam and a shot from substitute Promise Omochere.

The win takes Southend to fourth in the National League on 35 points, level with Yeovil Town and Harrogate Town, after the night's other results on Sky Sports. Leaders Boreham Wood are 13 points clear. Southend's next home game is against Dartford at Roots Hall.
