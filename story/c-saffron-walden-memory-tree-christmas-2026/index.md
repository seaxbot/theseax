# Saffron Walden's Christmas Memory Tree invites messages for loved ones

**Wooden decorations for the town's Memory Tree cost a suggested £2. The money goes to the mayor's three chosen charities.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 11:39 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Decorations are sold at the Tourist Information Centre and Peasgood & Skeates
- Peasgood & Skeates sponsors the tree again

## The story

Saffron Walden Town Council is asking people to remember loved ones this Christmas by hanging a personalised decoration on the town's Memory Tree. Each wooden shape has room for a handwritten message.

Decorations are on sale at the Tourist Information Centre and at Peasgood & Skeates, which is sponsoring the tree again. Money raised goes to the Mayor's Appeal for Together in Sound, Garden Youth Music Project and Teenage Kicks Plantation Youth Football Club.
