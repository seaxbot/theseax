# Thurrock CVS invites would-be volunteers to drop in after Tilbury winter event

**Thurrock CVS says anyone curious about volunteering can get in touch. Its co-ordinator met many residents at a busy Tilbury winter event.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Tilbury, Grays, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 14:54 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Volunteering.

## Key points

- Volunteer centre: 01375 389853
- Drop-in appointments at the Beehive Centre

## The story

Thurrock CVS volunteer co-ordinator Eden went to Thurrock Cares' Warm Up for Winter event at Tilbury Community Association, where services and charities set up stalls for Tilbury residents. The charity said the hall was busy from start to finish.

Anyone who wants to volunteer, learn new skills or meet new people can call the volunteer centre on 01375 389853 or email volunteercentre@thurrockcvs.org. Appointments are also offered at the Beehive Centre in Grays and at local libraries and community hubs.
