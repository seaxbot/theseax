Deaf patient's feedback leads to communication boards at three Essex hospitals

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust has put the boards in emergency departments, children's wards, critical care and labour wards at Basildon, Southend and Broomfield hospitals.

Basildon, Southend-on-Sea, Broomfield · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

The boards let patients show staff how they feel, how much pain they are in and what they need from their care. The idea came from a deaf patient who struggled to talk to a care team when no interpreter was on hand, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reported on 17 November.

Georgina Fusedale, senior sister on the acute stroke unit at Broomfield Hospital, said they give patients who find speaking difficult another way to express their needs.

Key points

  • Paid for with £1,700 from Mid and South Essex Hospitals Charity
  • Also aimed at patients who may have had a stroke
