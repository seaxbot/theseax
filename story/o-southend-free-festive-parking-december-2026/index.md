# Free parking returns to selected Southend City Council car parks in December

**Southend-on-Sea City Council is waiving charges in nine of its own car parks on four December days to bring shoppers into local high streets.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 13:43 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Free on Saturday 5 December and Sundays 6, 13 and 20 December
- Car parks include Elm Road in Leigh, Seaway and University Square
- On-street bays are not included
