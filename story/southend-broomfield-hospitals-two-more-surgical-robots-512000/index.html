Southend and Broomfield hospitals get 2 more surgical robots

Southend-on-Sea, Broomfield, Chelmsford · Tuesday 24 November 2026, 07:26 · First reported by Basildon Today · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

A £512,000 charity gift pays for a six-month trial. Doctors hope more women and bowel cancer patients will benefit.

Mid and South Essex Hospitals Charity is paying £512,000 for two more robotic surgery systems at Southend Hospital and Broomfield Hospital in Chelmsford. Basildon Today reported the plan on 24 November. Each system has two consoles, so a trainee can work beside the operating surgeon during a six-month pilot in the hospitals' keyhole surgery theatres.

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust, which runs both hospitals, says the robots are open to every patient. It hopes they will make a real difference for women and for some cancer patients, including those with gynaecological and colorectal cancers. Robotic surgery is already well established for men with prostate cancer, but far fewer women have had it for complex operations.

Patients usually feel less pain after a robotic operation, the trust says, with fewer complications and a shorter stay. Some people having a hysterectomy or a prostate removal may be able to go home on the day of surgery.

How we got here

Both hospitals have used robotic surgery for about ten years. In September 2023 the trust put a second da Vinci Xi robot into Southend Hospital, saying it had carried out nearly 300 robotic prostate removals in the year before. Southend's robotic theatre was bought with the legacy of the hospital's Keyhole Cancer Appeal, which raised £600,000 in 2017.

What happens next

With two consoles on each machine, Southend can become a training centre for the trust's own surgeons and for others in the region. Miss Esther Fine, a consultant colorectal surgeon and divisional director of surgery at Southend Hospital, said: "We cannot thank Mid and South Essex Hospitals Charity and its supporters enough." No start or end date for the pilot has been published.
