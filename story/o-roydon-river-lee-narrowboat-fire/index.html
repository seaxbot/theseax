Roydon narrowboat gutted by fire on River Lee near Dobbs Weir Road

Roydon · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 11:25 · Reported by Essex County Fire and Rescue Service
WhatsApp Facebook X

About 20 firefighters tackled a fire that left a narrowboat fully alight on the River Lee at Roydon, with nobody on board.

A passer-by dialled 999 at 9.36pm on Tuesday 17 November after spotting a narrowboat on fire on the River Lee near Dobbs Weir Road in Roydon. By the time Essex County Fire and Rescue Service crews arrived, the boat was fully alight.

About 20 firefighters, some of them on-call firefighters who live or work within five minutes of their stations, worked to put the fire out. The fire service said no one was aboard the boat at the time.

Crews left the scene at 12.43am on Wednesday 18 November, and the fire service says an investigation will be carried out to establish the cause.

Key points

  • Crews were called at 9.36pm
  • Firefighters left at 12.43am
  • Cause still to be investigated
