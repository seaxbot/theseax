Former BT transformation director joins Harwich Haven Authority board

Harwich Haven Authority, the trust port that manages shipping channels into Harwich and Felixstowe, has named Clive Quantrill as a non-executive director.

Harwich · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 09:49 · Reported by Harwich Haven Authority
WhatsApp Facebook X

Clive Quantrill will join the board of Harwich Haven Authority on 1 January 2027, the authority said on Tuesday 17 November. He spent years in senior strategy and transformation roles at BT and also sits on the board of the Independent Office for Police Conduct.

He lives in Waldringfield on the River Deben and has family ties to the maritime trades of the River Stour. Nicola Hancock leaves the board at the end of 2026 after two terms as a non-executive director.

Key points

  • Starts 1 January 2027
  • Also a director of a multi-academy schools trust
