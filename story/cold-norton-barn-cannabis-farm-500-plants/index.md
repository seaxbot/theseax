# Cold Norton: police find £300,000 cannabis farm in barn

**Officers found more than 500 plants near Cold Norton after a tip-off. Police say grows like this can be linked to modern slavery.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Cold Norton, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 13:44 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- Barn searched on Sunday 22 November
- Tip-off came from UK Power Networks
- Report suspected drug activity at essex.police.uk

## The story

Maldon's neighbourhood policing team uncovered the cannabis grow in a barn in the Cold Norton area on Sunday 22 November, Essex Police said. Officers went there after a tip from UK Power Networks, the company that runs the local electricity network. The force estimates the plants are worth £300,000 on the street.

Pictures released by the force show rows of tall plants under hanging lamps, with ducting and cables strung across the roof. A body-worn camera image shows a narrow corridor between growing areas lit in yellow. A forensic investigator in a white suit is pictured at the scene.

Police have not said whether anyone has been arrested.

Sergeant Dalby said his team would keep targeting cannabis farms across the Maldon district. He warned that large grows like this 'can be linked to organised crime, modern slavery and forced labour'.

Cold Norton is a small village on the Dengie peninsula, south of Maldon. In June, officers from the Maldon and Chelmsford teams seized 681 cannabis plants, valued by police at £500,000, from an industrial unit off Latchingdon Road in the village.

Anyone who sees something they think may be linked to growing, making or dealing drugs can report it to Essex Police on its website.
