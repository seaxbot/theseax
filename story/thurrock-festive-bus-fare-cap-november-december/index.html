Bus journeys starting in Thurrock to cost no more than £2 from Monday under festive fare cap

The Department for Transport is paying for the scheme, which arrives ahead of a planned national cap at the same level from January.

Grays, Thurrock · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 17:53 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock Council has confirmed the Festive Fare Cap runs until 31 December and covers every single journey that starts in the borough.

Deputy leader Alan Benson, who holds the transport portfolio, said cheaper fares would help residents reach work, training, healthcare and local shops while living costs remain a worry.

Key points

  • Runs from 16 November to 31 December
  • The current national single fare cap is £3
  • The national change was announced by transport minister Lord Hendy in July
