Harlow's new bus lane and cycle corridor wins Partnership of the Year at the Essex Housing Awards

The Sustainable Transport Corridor along the A1019 from Burnt Mill to the town centre, finished in March with Homes England money, took the award for the Harlow and Gilston Garden Town partners.

Harlow · Monday 9 November 2026, 15:27 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Corridor runs from Burnt Mill junction to Harlow town centre
  • Adds bus lanes, walking and cycling paths, trees and lighting
  • Meant to link with infrastructure for 10,000 homes at Gilston
