Harlow employment rate rises to 82.9% in latest ONS figures

Harlow is among the five best-performing areas in the country for jobs growth, according to Your Harlow's report on official labour market data.

Harlow · Thursday 19 November 2026, 12:13 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

The share of working-age people in jobs in Harlow has climbed by 15% over two years, Your Harlow reports, drawing on Office for National Statistics labour market figures published on Tuesday 17 November 2026. Employment minister Andrew Western called the figures a vote of confidence in the town's people and businesses.

Key points

  • UK unemployment rate fell to 4.6%
  • Figures came out as the Government backed Alan Milburn's youth jobs review
