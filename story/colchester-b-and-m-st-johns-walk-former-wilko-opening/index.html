Colchester's old Wilko reopens as B&M on Saturday 28 November

Colchester · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 14:15 · First published Sunday 22 November 2026, 09:13 · Reported by Essex Magazine
WhatsApp Facebook X

It is the first big letting for the shopping centre's new owners, Nottingham developer ALB Group, who want its other empty units filled before Christmas.

B&M will open its first Colchester city centre store next Saturday in the former Wilko unit at St John's Walk shopping centre, the Daily Gazette reports. The shop, at Unit 1, 28 St John's Walk, has been empty since Wilko closed in October 2023 when the chain went into administration.

The discount retailer already trades from Turner Rise and Tollgate retail parks, and has been recruiting part-time supervisors at £12.89 an hour for the new branch.

ALB Group bought the 75,000 sq ft centre, home to Iceland, Poundstretcher and the 650-space St John's multi-storey car park, in September. Its letting agents, Cited, are marketing two long-vacant large units and creating smaller kiosks on permanent and flexible leases.

"Our immediate priority is to invest in the centre, attract new retailers and bring fresh energy back to the high street," said Arran Bailey, ALB's managing director.

Colchester City Council called the news a really positive development for the city centre when it emerged in September, adding that talks were ongoing about the future of the former Debenhams.

Key points

  • Ranges include Christmas goods, homeware, food, toys and DIY
  • B&M's third Colchester store, after Turner Rise and Tollgate
  • Two large units and new kiosks are being marketed
