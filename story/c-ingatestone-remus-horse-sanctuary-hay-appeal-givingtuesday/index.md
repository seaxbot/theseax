# Remus Horse Sanctuary asks for help with £5,400 a month hay bill

**Remus Horse Sanctuary near Ingatestone is asking supporters to back its hay appeal on GivingTuesday, Tuesday 1 December, as feed costs climb.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Ingatestone, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 08:00 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Raised more than £2,500 so far
- About 9 large bales needed each week
- No government funding

## The story

The sanctuary cares for more than 100 rescued horses, ponies and donkeys, and feeding them is now one of its biggest running costs. It gets through about nine large bales of hay every week, and hay costs it around £5,400 a month.

Remus Horse Sanctuary said the long summer drought made hay harder to find and pushed prices up considerably. Its hay appeal has already raised more than £2,500, but the charity receives no government funding and relies on donations to keep the animals fed.
