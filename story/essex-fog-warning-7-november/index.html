Dense fog warning covers all of Essex until 11am, with visibility down to 100 metres

A Met Office yellow warning for fog across the East of England and the South East runs until late morning, with slower journeys and possible bus, train and flight delays.

Essex · Friday 6 November 2026, 20:27 · Reported by Met Office
WhatsApp Facebook X

A yellow Met Office warning for fog is in force across Essex, Southend and Thurrock until 11am on Saturday 7 November. The warning, issued on Friday evening, covers a wide area of the East of England, London, the South East and the Midlands, and dense patches could make the morning drive hazardous.

The Met Office says visibility may fall to around 100 metres in places, with slower journeys and possible delays to bus and train services, and a chance of flight delays or cancellations. The AA advised drivers to cut their speed and use fog lights, while National Rail urged weekend travellers to check their journeys before setting out.

Forecasters expect the fog to lift to mist and haze during the morning, lingering longest where it was thickest early on, though the Met Office said fog could return in the evening. The rest of the weekend looks dry with sunny intervals across Essex.

Key points

  • Yellow fog warning until 11am Saturday
  • Visibility could fall to about 100 metres
  • Possible bus, train and flight delays
  • Fog may return on Saturday evening
