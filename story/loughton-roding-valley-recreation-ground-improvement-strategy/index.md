# Loughton's Roding Valley Recreation Ground to get new paths and meadows

**Epping Forest District Council has drawn up a long-term plan to improve Roding Valley Recreation Ground. Developers' money will pay for it.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Loughton, Buckhurst Hill, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:43 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Environment.

## Key points

- Projects will go ahead in phases as developer money arrives
- Covers Loughton, Buckhurst Hill and Theydon Bois users
- Natural England and Essex Wildlife Trust helped draw it up

## The story

Walkers, dog owners and families who use Roding Valley Recreation Ground could see better paths, new trees and wildflower meadows under a strategy that Epping Forest District Council expects to approve. The council set out the plan on Tuesday 24 November.

Its list of projects includes improved walking routes, tree planting, new meadow and wildlife habitat, upgraded entrances and signs, and information boards. The council says the aim is to make the green space easier to use and more welcoming, so more people get outdoors close to where they live.

The work will be paid for with section 106 money, the contributions developers make towards local services when they build. It will be done in phases as that money comes in, and the council has not given a timetable or a total cost.

There is a legal side too. According to the council, the strategy is part of its Local Plan approach to meeting its duty to protect the nearby Epping Forest Special Area of Conservation.

Councillor Ken Williamson, cabinet member for shaping the district, said the recreation ground became 'a lifeline for many residents' during the Covid pandemic. He called the strategy 'a clear and fundable plan' and welcomed new building paying for it.

Loughton Town Council, Buckhurst Hill Parish Council, Natural England and Essex Wildlife Trust helped shape the plan. A map of the areas marked for improvement is on the council's website.
