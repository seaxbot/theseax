Anglia Ruskin installs forensic scientist Sue Black as chancellor

Chelmsford · Friday 27 November 2026, 20:38 · Reported by Anglia Ruskin University
WhatsApp Facebook X

The forensic scientist who led war crimes work in Kosovo took up the role at Chelmsford graduations.

Professor Lady Sue Black was formally installed as Chancellor of Anglia Ruskin University (ARU) during graduation ceremonies in Chelmsford, the university said on Friday 27 November. She is one of the world's best-known forensic scientists.

She was the lead forensic anthropologist for the international war crimes investigations in Kosovo. Her work for the UK Government and international agencies has also taken her to Iraq, Sierra Leone and Grenada. She sits in the House of Lords as a crossbench peer and is President of St John's College, Oxford.

She was made a Dame in 2016, elected a Fellow of the Royal Society in 2023 and appointed to the Order of the Thistle in 2024. Before Oxford she was Pro Vice Chancellor for Engagement at Lancaster University. Her latest book, An Expert Witness, came out in July.

In her address she called the role "a tremendous honour". She promised to bring "a healthy dose of Scottish Highland determination" to it. The university said she has spoken of her commitment to widening opportunity through education.

A busy month for ARU

ARU says 3,140 students are receiving their degrees at its autumn ceremonies in Chelmsford, Cambridge and Peterborough. The university is also seeking voluntary redundancies to cut its staff bill, as The Seax reported.
