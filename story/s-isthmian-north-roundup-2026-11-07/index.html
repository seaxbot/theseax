Isthmian North: Buckhurst Hill win 3-0 at Bowers & Pitsea as Witham Town triumph at Redbridge

Two away wins for Essex clubs headlined Saturday in the Isthmian North, while Takeley and Little Oakley were both beaten 1-0 at home.

Buckhurst Hill · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:23 · Reported by Non League Matters
WhatsApp Facebook X

Buckhurst Hill produced the standout Essex result in the Isthmian North on Saturday, winning 3-0 away at Bowers & Pitsea. Witham Town also won on the road, beating Redbridge 2-1.

It was a frustrating afternoon at home for two other Essex sides. Little Oakley lost 1-0 to Walthamstow and Takeley went down by the same score to Felixstowe & Walton United.

Elsewhere, Tilbury and Concord Rangers drew 1-1 in an Essex derby, Grays Athletic shared a 1-1 draw away at Mulbarton Wanderers, and Brightlingsea Regent were held 0-0 at home by Fakenham Town. Canvey Island did not play.

Key points

  • Bowers & Pitsea 0-3 Buckhurst Hill
  • Redbridge 1-2 Witham Town
  • Tilbury 1-1 Concord Rangers in an Essex derby
  • Little Oakley and Takeley both lost 1-0 at home
