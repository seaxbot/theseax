Two teenagers deny murdering Radwan Shakur, 21, in Chelmsford's Central Park

Jurors in Chelmsford have been told the prosecution case is that the fatal stabbing in the city park in June grew out of a dispute between drug dealers over territory.

Chelmsford, Central Park · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 16:33 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Prosecutor Justin Rouse KC told the jury at Chelmsford Crown Court on Tuesday 17 November that Radwan Shakur was stabbed through the heart in the park on 12 June. Blaise Mfinda, 18, of Widford Chase, Chelmsford, and a 17-year-old boy who cannot be named for legal reasons deny murder, BBC News reported.

The 17-year-old admits having a knife in public but says he acted in self-defence. Mfinda's barrister told jurors there was no turf war and his client was in the wrong place at the wrong time.

Key points

  • Trial expected to last up to four weeks
  • Mfinda also denies having a knife in a public place
  • Both admit possessing cannabis with intent to supply
