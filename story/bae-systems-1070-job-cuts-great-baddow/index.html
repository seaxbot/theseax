BAE Systems, with Great Baddow base, plans up to 1,070 UK job cuts

The defence company says the cuts will fall on its UK head office and air sector, out of about 50,000 UK employees.

Great Baddow, Chelmsford · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 17:32 · Reported by Evening Standard
WhatsApp Facebook X

BAE Systems announced on Wednesday 18 November that it will seek the reductions through a voluntary process and work with trade unions, the Evening Standard reports. Shopfloor manufacturing, aircraft maintenance at RAF bases and early careers trainees are excluded.

Unite general secretary Sharon Graham blamed delays in defence spending and called for new contracts for British-built aircraft. The Echo reports that the company has not confirmed whether staff at Great Baddow, Chelmsford, are affected.

Key points

  • Roles sought through a voluntary process
  • Unite wants contracts for British-built Typhoons
