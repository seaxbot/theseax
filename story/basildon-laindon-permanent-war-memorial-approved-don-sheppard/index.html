Basildon councillors back permanent war memorial D-Day veteran sought

Basildon, Laindon · Friday 20 November 2026, 06:35 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Council approved the proposal unanimously on 17 November, BBC News reports. Mayor Sam Journet proposed it and Conservative leader Andy Barnes seconded it.

No site, design or cost has been chosen yet. The council will consult the Royal British Legion, the Commonwealth War Graves Commission, veterans and Mr Sheppard's family, and the plans are expected to include a tribute to him. The Royal Engineer, who landed on D-Day, died aged 104 in 2024.

Key points

  • Proposed by mayor Sam Journet
  • Site, design and funding still to be decided
  • Don Sheppard died in 2024, aged 104
