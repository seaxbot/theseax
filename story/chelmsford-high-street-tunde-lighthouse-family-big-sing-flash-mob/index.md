# Chelmsford gets Lifted as Lighthouse Family's Tunde hits the High Street

**Tunde Baiyewu marked his 58th birthday with a surprise lunchtime gig. Choir singers posing as shoppers joined in.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, High Street, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 22:45 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: What's On.

## Key points

- Six songs, from Ocean Drive to Lifted
- The choir revealed itself on the third song
- Lighthouse Family play Ipswich on 12 April

## The story

Lunchtime shoppers on Chelmsford High Street thought they were watching an ordinary busker on Wednesday 25 November. Singer Amber Prothero started Ocean Drive, the title track of Lighthouse Family's 1995 debut album, with a guitarist and choir leader Howard Francis beside her.

Then Tunde himself appeared and took over the song he made famous.

The pair went straight into High, the duo's biggest hit. It reached number four in the UK in 1998 and went to number one in Australia. By now a crowd was building, and many of the people in it were not who they seemed.

## The secret choir

The gig was organised by The BIG Sing, a community choir with groups across Essex. Members were told to come in their everyday clothes and stand "casually scattered around the High Street, looking like ordinary shoppers". They were asked not to bunch together, but to stop, watch and drift into the crowd like curious passers-by.

Singers were also told not to post about it or tell anyone outside the choir. The surprise was the whole point.

The reveal came on the third song. As Tunde started Rain To A Rainbow, a song from his solo work, the "shoppers" around him began to sing.

They stayed with him for the rest of the six-song set. Next came Bill Withers' Lean On Me, then (I Wish I Knew How It Would Feel to Be) Free / One, which went to number six in 2001. He finished with Lifted, the song that made Lighthouse Family's name.

Singer Rox Creeve also sang with him. Afterwards she posted a selfie with the star on Instagram, calling him "the absolute legend" and wishing him a happy birthday.

The Chelmsford For You page, which shared a video of the gig, said it had "not been your average hump day" in the city centre.

Each singer paid £10 towards the costs. A professional crew filmed the performance, and the organisers plan to share the footage on social media and send it to the national press in the hope it goes viral.

## The voice of Lighthouse Family

Tunde was born in London and grew up partly in Nigeria. He met keyboard player Paul Tucker while studying in Newcastle, and they formed Lighthouse Family in 1993. Ocean Drive went six times platinum in the UK, and the duo's albums have sold more than 15 million copies worldwide.

He already had links with the choir. In March he was billed as special guest at The BIG Sing's concert at Charter Hall in Colchester. That show had a 450-voice choir from Essex, Kent and the Midlands, including more than 100 Essex schoolchildren.

## The BIG Sing

Husband and wife Howard and Gemma Francis started The BIG Sing. Both used to sing with the London Community Gospel Choir. Howard, a keyboard player and songwriter, has toured with Westlife and Will Young and was musical director for Mis-Teeq.

The choir won over the judges on Britain's Got Talent in 2023 and won the Pride of Essex Award in 2017. It says it has raised more than £200,000 for good causes. Its Chelmsford choir rehearses on Wednesday evenings at Writtle Christian Centre, and money raised goes to its own charity, SOUL.

## See him again

Fans who missed the High Street gig will not have long to wait. Lighthouse Family are back on tour next year with Ocean Drive Live, celebrating their first album. The nearest dates are the London Palladium on 15 March and 6 April, and the Regent Theatre in Ipswich on 12 April.
