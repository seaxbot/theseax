# Harlow College students make consent films for 16 Days of Activism

**Media students in Harlow have made four short films on consent and abusive relationships. Essex Sexual Health Service will share them online.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 10:49 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Health.

## Key points

- Campaign runs until Thursday 10 December
- Service run by Provide Community for Essex County Council
- Advice and the films at eshs.org.uk

## The story

The videos, made by Harlow College's media and journalism students for a real brief, will run on Essex Sexual Health Service's social media and website from Wednesday 25 November to Thursday 10 December. Those dates mark the global 16 Days of Activism against gender-based violence.

The students pitched their own ideas before filming. The films look at the online 'manosphere' and its view of masculinity, the warning signs of domestic abuse, coercive control, and consent and peer pressure. Sophie Milner, head of outreach and education at the service, said involving young people keeps the messages 'relevant, relatable and meaningful'.
