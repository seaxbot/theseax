Thurrock bus journeys capped at £2 from Monday until New Year's Eve

Thurrock Council's festive fare cap means no single bus trip starting in the borough will cost more than £2, weeks before the national cap falls to the same level.

Grays, Thurrock · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Festive Fare Cap starts on Monday 16 November 2026 and runs until 31 December, covering any single bus journey that begins in Thurrock, Your Thurrock reported on 11 November. The council says the Department for Transport pays for it, so it costs the authority nothing extra.

From 1 January 2027 the national cap drops from £3 to £2 across England outside London. Thurrock deputy leader Alan Benson said cheaper fares would help residents reach jobs, training and healthcare.

Key points

  • The scheme is funded by the Department for Transport
  • The national cap is backed by an extra £400 million
