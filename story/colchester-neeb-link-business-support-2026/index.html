North Essex councils' joint Link scheme has backed 149 firms so far this year, 22 of them in Colchester

Most businesses helped had not used council-funded support in the previous year, Colchester City Council says, and a drop-in session for firms is planned in the city next month.

Colchester · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The North Essex Economic Board, a partnership of six councils, has supported 149 businesses so far this year through its Link programme, Colchester City Council said on Tuesday. It also ran 78 one-to-one advice sessions and drew 592 people to business masterclasses.

In Colchester, 22 businesses have been helped this year, including six one-to-one adviser sessions and four specialist time vouchers. Nearly eight in ten firms taking part had not used council-funded business support in the past 12 months. Councillor Alison Jay, Colchester's portfolio holder for economic growth, said the scheme showed the value of councils working together to support local firms.

Since it began in 2021 the programme has supported more than 2,428 businesses, held 237 events and invested over £2.35 million in economic growth, the council says. A business pop-in event is planned in Colchester on 2 December.

Key points

  • Six north Essex districts take part in the programme
  • 592 people have attended its masterclasses this year
  • Business pop-in event in Colchester on 2 December
