Multi-agency raids on more than 100 Essex premises seize £10,360 in cash and illicit cigarettes

Operation Machinize, coordinated by the National Crime Agency and carried out locally by Essex Police with partner agencies, targeted organised crime linked to businesses across the county.

Essex · Monday 9 November 2026, 11:45 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police, Trading Standards, HMRC, Border Force, the Home Office and the Fair Work Agency visited more than 100 premises across the county under Operation Machinize, a National Crime Agency-led crackdown on organised crime, Greatest Hits Radio reports.

Officers seized £10,360 in cash, nearly 10,000 illicit cigarettes valued at about £5,540, 1kg of tobacco worth £575 and nine illegal vapes. Detective Chief Inspector Terry Balding said such businesses can look legitimate while facilitating serious criminality.

Key points

  • More than 100 premises visited across Essex
  • £10,360 in cash seized
  • Nearly 10,000 illicit cigarettes taken off sale
