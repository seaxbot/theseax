Walk-in X-rays and new scans coming to St Margaret's diagnostic centre in Epping from mid-December

Blood tests move into the new community diagnostic centre at St Margaret's Hospital on 16 November, with X-ray, ultrasound and liver scans following on 14 December.

Epping · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 10:46 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Princess Alexandra Hospital NHS Trust is expanding the community diagnostic centre at St Margaret's Hospital in Epping. Blood testing moves there from outpatients on 16 November, with room for about 230 patients a day, and two walk-in X-ray rooms open on 14 December for GP-referred patients.

A fourth ultrasound room and FibroScan liver checks also start on 14 December, adding about 350 patients a day. Chief executive Thom Lafferty called it an important step forward for diagnostic care.

Key points

  • Endoscopy appointments due in early 2027
