# Braintree man, 38, charged with 21 shop thefts in Braintree and Great Notley

**Graham Pink, of Telford Road, Braintree, is due before Chelmsford Crown Court in December after appearing before magistrates.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Braintree, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 18:00 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Braintree's neighbourhood policing team led the inquiry
- Essex Police say shop thefts in the area fell by nearly 10% in a year

## The story

Graham Pink, 38, of Telford Road, Braintree, is charged with 21 counts of shop theft and four counts of breaching a Criminal Behaviour Order, relating to stores in Braintree and Great Notley between 26 August and 2 November 2026, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reports. He appeared at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court on 14 November and is due at Chelmsford Crown Court on 14 December 2026.
