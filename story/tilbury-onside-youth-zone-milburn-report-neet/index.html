Tilbury youth zone wants youth work in plan for young people out of work

OnSide Youth Zone Thames Gateway, due to open in Tilbury in 2027, has responded to Alan Milburn's report on the national crisis of young people not in education, employment or training.

Tilbury, Thurrock · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 14:07 · Updated Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:44 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock had 3.4% of 16 and 17-year-olds not in education, employment or training in July 2025, below the national figure of 5.8% but up from 1.8% in 2019/20, Your Thurrock reports. Bethanie Cundy, the youth zone's chief executive, said youth work was central to tackling the problem, and the charity is asking local businesses to support the project.

Key points

  • Planned activities include climbing, fitness, music production and creative arts
  • Local young people helping to shape it have toured Google and DP World sites
