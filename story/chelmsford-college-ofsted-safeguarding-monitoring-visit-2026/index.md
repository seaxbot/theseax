# Ofsted checks Chelmsford College after safeguarding concerns raised

**Inspectors found leaders had strengthened safeguarding, but some records of welfare checks were not updated promptly.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Moulsham Street, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 09:00 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Education.

## Key points

- Visit looked at safeguarding only
- Last graded Good in March 2024
- Learners say they feel safe

## The story

Ofsted inspectors visited Chelmsford College in October after concerns about safeguarding were raised with the inspectorate.

The focused visit on 15 October looked only at safeguarding, and its report was published on Thursday 26 November. It did not award grades. The college's last full inspection, in March 2024, rated it Good overall, with apprenticeships rated Requires Improvement.

Inspectors found that leaders and governors had responded quickly to weaknesses they had identified and had strengthened safeguarding and pastoral support. The report does not say what the original concerns were.

Dedicated personal tutors have improved how attendance is monitored, how worries are spotted early and how the college keeps in touch with students and parents, the report says. Staff carry out welfare checks when learners are absent.

Learners told inspectors they feel safe and are confident that concerns will be dealt with promptly.

In a very small minority of cases, staff had not updated the college's central systems promptly. Ofsted said this had not harmed learners, but it limits how well leaders and governors can oversee their work.

Inspectors asked the college to make sure welfare checks, attendance action and support are always recorded promptly, and that learner absences are reported up to leaders and governors.

The college, on Moulsham Street, had 2,747 young people on study programmes, 233 adult learners and 387 apprentices at the time of the visit. It has recently changed some senior leaders and has a new chair of governors.
