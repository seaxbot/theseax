Southend Council's 13th Christmas toy drive asks for new, unwrapped gifts by 15 December

Gifts for babies up to 18-year-olds can be left at the Civic Centre, the Forum or Southend Leisure and Tennis Centre, or bought online from two local toy shops.

Southend-on-Sea · Tuesday 27 October 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council's annual toy drive, now in its 13th year, is collecting new, unwrapped presents for vulnerable children and young people in the city. The council says past drives have reached more than 21,200 children.

Toys, vouchers, selection packs, stationery, colouring books and toiletry sets are all welcome, with batteries included for toys that need them. The council is especially keen on gifts for older children and teenagers, who tend to receive fewer donations.

Donations can be dropped at the Civic Centre, the Forum or Southend Leisure and Tennis Centre until Tuesday 15 December, or bought from Argosy Toys or Toys 'N' Tuck for council volunteers to collect. Bulk donors can call 01702 215841.

Key points

  • Runs until Tuesday 15 December
  • Drop-off at Civic Centre, the Forum and Southend Leisure and Tennis Centre
  • Gifts for older children and teens especially welcome
