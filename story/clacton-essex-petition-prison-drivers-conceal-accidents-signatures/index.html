Clacton 8th in UK for signing petition to jail drivers who hide crashes

Clacton · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 12:57 · First published Sunday 22 November 2026, 12:48 · Reported by UK Parliament Petitions, analysis by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

The petition wants mandatory prison for drivers who hide a crash or hold up a police investigation. Essex has under 3% of the UK's constituencies but about 7% of its signatures.

Drivers who knowingly avoid reporting a crash, even if they believed they had only hit an animal, would face a mandatory prison sentence under a petition to Parliament that is spreading fast in Essex.

The petition, started by Chloe Mefford, says some drivers stay silent so that alcohol or drug levels can fall below the legal limit, or to hide a victim. It also wants prison for drivers who pervert the course of justice or refuse to help an investigation, which it says causes great distress to families. It opened on 2 October and closes on 2 April 2027.

At present, failing to stop after a crash or to report it is dealt with by magistrates, with a maximum of six months in prison and an unlimited fine. Under sentencing guidelines, even the most serious cases start at a community order, with up to 26 weeks in custody possible.

By 5.33am on Sunday 22 November, 4,101 people in Essex had signed, out of 57,530 across the UK. Clacton has 537 signatures, eighth of all 650 constituencies and first in the county. Chelmsford (387), Maldon (386), Harlow (382) and Witham (330) come next. The fewest are in Thurrock (84), Southend West and Leigh (79) and Southend East and Rochford (69).

The petition passed 10,000 signatures on 27 October, so the government will publish a response. It had not done so by Sunday morning. If it reaches 100,000, Parliament will consider it for a debate. Signature counts show where a petition has been shared, not what everyone in an area thinks.

Key points

  • Current maximum for failing to stop or report: six months
  • The petition closes on 2 April 2027
  • At 100,000 signatures Parliament considers a debate
