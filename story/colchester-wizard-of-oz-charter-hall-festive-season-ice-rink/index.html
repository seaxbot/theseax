Jake Quickenden joins Wizard of Oz cast as Colchester lines up ice rink and Christmas shows

Colchester City Council's events company has set out a winter programme running from late November into the new year, from Charter Hall concerts to a skating rink in Culver Square.

Colchester · Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

TV personality Jake Quickenden will play the Scarecrow when The Wizard of Oz Christmas Show runs at Charter Hall, Colchester, from Saturday 12 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. The programme is run by Amphora Events, part of the council-owned Amphora Group, Colchester City Council said on Monday 16 November.

Charter Hall also stages Step Into Christmas on 22 November, The Snowman Live with Orchestra on 30 November, Live At Christmas on 1 December with Nina Conti and Milton Jones among the line-up, Christmas at the Movies on 2 December and Massaoke Christmas on 5 December. The Town Hall holds a Christmas market on 6 December and Candlelight: Christmas Classics on 20 December.

Amphora Events operations manager James Young said Christmas was a hugely important time for the city, and Amphora's interim chair Venessa Moffat said the board was pleased with the variety on offer. Tickets are sold through colchester-events.co.uk; the council's announcement did not give prices.

Key points

  • Ice rink in Culver Square from 5 December to 3 January
  • Santa Fun Run in Castle Park on 6 December
  • Ticket prices not yet published
