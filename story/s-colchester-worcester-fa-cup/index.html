Colchester United edge Worcester City 1-0 to reach the FA Cup second round

Danny Cowley's U's beat the non-league visitors at the JobServe Community Stadium on Saturday to stay in the competition.

Colchester · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:23 · Reported by Daily Gazette
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United are through to the FA Cup second round after a 1-0 win over Worcester City at the JobServe Community Stadium on Saturday 7 November. The Daily Gazette and the club both reported the win, which keeps the U's in the competition.

Before the first-round tie, midfielder Paul Digby, 31 and back in the Colchester side, told the Gazette the players knew how big the game was for Worcester. He has plenty of experience of the competition, including a third-round upset, and said he loves the FA Cup.

Key points

  • Colchester United 1 Worcester City 0
  • FA Cup first round, Saturday 7 November
  • Played at the JobServe Community Stadium
