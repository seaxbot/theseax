Digby relishes FA Cup as Colchester welcome Worcester City

The experienced midfielder, back in the U's side, says the players know how much the tie means to the visitors.

Colchester · Friday 6 November 2026, 15:07 · Reported by Colchester Gazette (via Yahoo Sports)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United are at home to Worcester City in the FA Cup this weekend, and midfielder Paul Digby says the U's must be ready for opponents who see the tie as a huge occasion. Digby, 31, is back in the Colchester side for the game.

Speaking to the Gazette, Digby said: "We know how big a game it is for them." He has plenty of experience of the competition, including a third-round upset, and said he loves the FA Cup.

The club has also opened bookings for supporters travelling to Grimsby. The result of the Worcester tie will appear in The Seax's results once it is confirmed.

  • FA Cup tie at home to Worcester City
  • Paul Digby, 31, back in the Colchester side
  • Digby says he loves the FA Cup
