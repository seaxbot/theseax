Shoeburyness picked for up to £20m from Pride in Place scheme

Shoeburyness · Thursday 19 November 2026, 14:01 · Reported by SAVS Southend
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shoeburyness is one of 399 wards in England chosen for Government money over 10 years through the Pride in Place scheme, SAVS Southend says.

Key points

  • The scheme targets areas ranked lowest on the English Index of Multiple Deprivation
  • It aims to improve pay, skills, productivity and health
