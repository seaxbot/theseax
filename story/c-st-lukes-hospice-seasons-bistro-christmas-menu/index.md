# St Luke's Hospice bistro launches its first Christmas menu

**SEASONS @ St Luke's, the hospice bistro created with MasterChef finalist Anurag Aggarwal, serves festive meals on weekdays from 1 to 24 December.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Thurrock, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 13:41 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Bookings by WhatsApp on 07738 273736

## The story

Two courses cost £19.95 and three cost £24.95, with drinks extra, from 11am on weekdays, Your Thurrock reports. The bistro opened in September, and diners support St Luke's Hospice by eating there.
