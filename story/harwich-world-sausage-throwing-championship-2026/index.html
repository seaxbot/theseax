Peter Swift wins World Sausage Throwing Championship on Harwich Green for third year running

The 29-year-old hurled an out-of-date banger 148.5ft to keep his title at the quirky contest run by Tendring Camra, while a Great Bromley butcher took the Champion Sausage of Tendring prize.

Harwich · Sunday 8 November 2026, 06:22 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Peter Swift has won the World Sausage Throwing Championship on Harwich Green for the third year in a row, BBC News reported. The 29-year-old's winning throw measured 148.5ft (45.26m), though the competition record of 171ft (52.12m), set by Todd Rothwell in 2019, still stands.

The event has been run since 2011 by the Tendring branch of the Campaign for Real Ale, and organiser Richard Oxborrow said it began in a pub as a quirky way to promote the local beer festival. Each competitor has two throws using out-of-date sausages. Swift's advice was to be gentle with the sausage, because crumpling it into a ball makes it burst, and he praised Harwich's community spirit and its pubs.

Among this year's field was Mark Rye, 47, from Cardiff, who was making his 70th world championship entry and persuaded organisers to adopt the World Sausage Throwing Championship name. Alongside the throwing, nine butchers competed for Champion Sausage of Tendring, which went to Becky's Butchers in Great Bromley for a sweet chilli sausage.

Key points

  • Winning throw of 148.5ft (45.26m)
  • Record of 171ft set in 2019 still stands
  • Run by Tendring Camra since 2011
  • Becky's Butchers, Great Bromley, won Champion Sausage of Tendring
