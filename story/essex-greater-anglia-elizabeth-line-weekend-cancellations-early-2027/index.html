Essex trains to London cut on seven weekend days in early 2027

Essex · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:30 · First published Sunday 22 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Network Rail
WhatsApp Facebook X

Network Rail is finishing work on the new track it is laying at Forest Gate over Christmas, so buses will replace trains to the Central line at Newbury Park.

Trains between Essex and London Liverpool Street will not run on seven weekend days in January and February 2027 while Network Rail carries out follow-up work on the Great Eastern Main Line, it announced on Tuesday 17 November. The cancellations, first reported by the Romford Recorder, hit Greater Anglia, the Elizabeth line and the Liberty line between Romford and Upminster.

When trains will not run

On Saturday 9 and Saturday 16 January, Greater Anglia will run no trains between Ingatestone and Liverpool Street. Trains from Norwich, Ipswich and Clacton will terminate at Shenfield, with replacement buses from Ingatestone to Newbury Park for the Central line. Southend Victoria and Southminster trains will run only as far as Shenfield, with buses from there to Newbury Park.

Five Sundays are affected: 10, 17, 24 and 31 January and 14 February. Greater Anglia's main line services will start and finish at Beaulieu Park, north of Chelmsford, with a coach link onward. On the Southend line, trains will reach Billericay on the first two of those Sundays; on the other three, the whole route from Southend Victoria towards the capital is bus-only, as is the branch from Southminster to Wickford.

On all seven days there will be no Elizabeth line trains between Liverpool Street and Shenfield, and the Liberty line will not run. Replacement buses will run from Stratford to Romford, and passengers for Gidea Park, Harold Wood, Brentwood and Shenfield should change at Chadwell Heath. Greater Anglia tickets will be accepted on the Central line between Newbury Park and Stratford or Liverpool Street.

What the work involves

Engineers will adjust six new sets of points at Forest Gate and two at Stratford, which are being installed during a 10-day closure from 27 December to 3 January, and refurbish points at Pudding Mill Lane and Shenfield. In Essex, rails will be renewed at Brentwood, Rayleigh and Hockley, overhead wires repaired between Romford and Chelmsford and between Wickford and Southminster, canopies repainted at Shenfield and repaired at Southminster and Hockley, and the footbridge at Wickford mended. Specialist trains will also clear leaves and clean the rails.

Martin Beable, chief operating officer of GBR Anglia, said the work was "very important to help maintain the safe and reliable running of the railway", and asked passengers to check before they travel and allow extra time. Greater Anglia lists the changes on its service alterations page, Elizabeth line and Liberty line passengers can use TfL's Journey Planner or the TfL Go app, and National Rail Enquiries has the latest advice.

Key points

  • No Greater Anglia trains Ingatestone to London on 9 and 16 January
  • Elizabeth line shut between Liverpool Street and Shenfield on all seven days
  • Buses to Newbury Park, with Greater Anglia tickets valid on the Central line
