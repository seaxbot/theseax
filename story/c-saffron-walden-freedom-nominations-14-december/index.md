# Saffron Walden opens nominations for Freedom of the town

**Saffron Walden Town Council is inviting nominations for residents who have made an exceptional, lasting contribution to the town's life, wellbeing, reputation or development.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 08:47 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Deadline: 9am on Monday 14 December
- Forms go to the Town Hall, Saffron Walden
- Decision due in January 2027

## The story

Nominations for the Freedom of Saffron Walden close at 9am on Monday 14 December 2026, under the town council's Freedom of Saffron Walden policy. The policy and nomination form are on the town council's website, and completed forms can be emailed or posted to the Town Hall.

Councillors will consider the nominations at their January 2027 meeting. Any successful nominees will be awarded the honour at the town council's February 2027 meeting.
