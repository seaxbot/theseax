Colchester neighbours raise alarm over hob fire in empty house

Colchester · Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:40 · Reported by Essex County Fire and Rescue Service
WhatsApp Facebook X

Neighbours heard smoke alarms going off in a Colchester house while the resident was out. Firefighters put out a fire on the hob.

Crews were called to the house in Colchester at 12.35pm on Tuesday 24 November and found the kitchen heavily smoke-logged, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. The fire was out by 1pm.

Firefighter Chris Simson, from Colchester Fire Station, said the neighbours "acted quickly" when they heard the alarms. Crews then knocked on doors nearby to give fire safety advice and carried out two home safety visits. The service fits smoke alarms free for people who need them: book at essex-fire.gov.uk/book or call 0300 303 0088.
