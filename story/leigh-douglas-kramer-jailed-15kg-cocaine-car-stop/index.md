# Leigh police stop finds £1m of cocaine as driver is jailed

**Officers found 8kg in a car boot, then 7kg more in a Southend storage unit. Douglas Kramer got six years and eight months.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Leigh-on-Sea, Bosworth Road, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 16:10 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Stopped in Bosworth Road on 21 September
- All 15 blocks were marked 'A-Y'
- Told police he was paying off a drug debt

## The story

Douglas Kramer, 42, of no fixed address, Canvey Island, was jailed for six years and eight months at Basildon Crown Court on Thursday 19 November, Essex Police said. He had pleaded guilty to possessing cocaine with intent to supply.

Officers on patrol stopped his blue Vauxhall Astra in Bosworth Road, Leigh-on-Sea, at about 1pm on Monday 21 September. Police said the car was potentially linked to drugs.

In a cardboard box in the boot they found eight 1kg blocks of cocaine. Their enquiries then led them to a storage unit in Southend, where they found seven more.

All 15 blocks carried the same 'A-Y' marking and tested positive for cocaine. Police put the street value of the 15kg at more than £1 million.

In interview, Kramer told officers he was moving the drugs to pay off a drug debt. He refused to say who he owed. The court ordered the drugs to be destroyed.

## 'Taken out of circulation'

Detective Sergeant Leo Fordham, of the force's Serious Crime Directorate, called it 'an excellent stop' by Operational Support Group officers.

'Finding eight kilograms in the vehicle was significant, but our officers continued to follow the evidence,' he said. 'Drugs on this scale cause significant harm to our communities and generate money for those involved in serious criminality.'
