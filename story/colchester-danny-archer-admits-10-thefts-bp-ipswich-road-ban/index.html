Colchester man admits 10 thefts from garage he was banned from

Colchester, Ipswich Road · Thursday 26 November 2026, 17:00 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Danny Archer, 40, took £550 of meat and other food from the BP in Ipswich Road. He has been remanded in custody.

A Colchester man has admitted ten shop thefts from a city petrol station he was banned from entering, Essex Police said.

Danny Archer, 40, was arrested by officers in Colchester on suspicion of stealing from the BP service station in Ipswich Road between 1 and 18 November. He admitted ten counts of shop theft when he appeared before magistrates on Wednesday 25 November. The goods were meat and other food worth £550.

The court heard he was already under a five-year criminal behaviour order, imposed a year ago. It bans him from the garage and from four Colchester shops: the Co-op in Tamarisk Way, Greenstead, Tesco in Bromley Road, Tesco Express in St Christopher Road and Waitrose in St Andrew's Gardens.

Magistrates remanded him in custody. He will be sentenced at Ipswich Crown Court.

Key points

  • Thefts between 1 and 18 November
  • Five-year order covers five Colchester shops
  • Sentence due at Ipswich Crown Court
