Parish councils object as Saturday deadline nears for comments on data centre at Little Canfield's old Crumps Farm landfill

Little Canfield and Great Canfield parish councils have raised fears over contaminated land, water supply and the countryside as Essex County Council weighs the 10,240 sq m scheme near Dunmow.

Little Canfield, Dunmow · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 11:25 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 15:44 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

The application to Essex County Council, as the waste planning authority, also covers cleaning up and restoring the old landfill, extra landfill infrastructure and a ground-mounted solar array on the southern part of the site, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reports.

Little Canfield and Great Canfield parish councils have raised concerns about building on contaminated land, local water supply and the countryside. The Environment Agency, a statutory consultee, is separately investigating claims of illegal waste dumping there. Public comments close on Saturday 14 November.

Key points

  • Essex County Council will decide the application
  • Public consultation closes on 14 November
