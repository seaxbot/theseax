# Harlow family centre Savannah Lodge gets new Ofsted notice after visit

**Savannah Lodge in Harlow has improved since Ofsted rated it inadequate in July. One safeguarding notice is still not met.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Fir Park, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 08:01 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Bedrooms now have new furniture, cots and mattresses
- No internal audits by the responsible individual yet
- Families said they would feel able to complain

## The story

Ofsted inspectors made a follow-up visit to the residential family assessment centre in Fir Park, Harlow, after a full inspection on 22 July 2026 rated it inadequate. That inspection raised serious concerns about safeguarding and leadership. It led to two compliance notices and a limit on how many families the centre could take, Your Harlow reported on Tuesday 24 November.

One notice has now been met. A fresh notice was issued on the other, and Ofsted will visit again. Inspectors found refurbished bedrooms and better records of incidents, but said a new safeguarding policy was unclear.
