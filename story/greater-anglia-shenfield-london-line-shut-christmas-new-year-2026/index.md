# Greater Anglia shuts Shenfield to London line for 8 days after Christmas

**Essex rail passengers face buses to the Tube for a week over New Year. Trains also finish early on Christmas Eve.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Shenfield, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 13:40 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Christmas Day: no Greater Anglia trains at all
- Buses run from Shenfield and Ingatestone to Newbury Park
- Last Stansted Express from Liverpool Street on Christmas Eve: 11.21pm

## The story

Greater Anglia will run no trains between Shenfield and London Liverpool Street from 27 December to 3 January while essential engineering work takes place, according to the travel advice published on National Rail. Trains from Southend Victoria, Clacton-on-Sea, Ipswich and Norwich will still run as far as Shenfield.

Passengers heading into London will need a rail replacement bus from Shenfield or Ingatestone to Newbury Park. From there they can take the Central line to Stratford or Liverpool Street at no extra cost.

## Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and Boxing Day

On Christmas Eve, services start thinning out from 7pm and stop by 10pm. The Stansted Express runs later: the last train to the airport leaves Liverpool Street at 11.21pm, and the last from the airport leaves at 11.58pm.

Rob Mullen, GBR Anglia's chief customer and commercial officer, said: "Our trains will finish earlier than usual on Christmas Eve." There are no trains on Christmas Day. On Boxing Day the only service is a limited Stansted Express between the airport and Tottenham Hale, calling at Harlow Town and Bishop's Stortford. Tickets can be used on the Underground between Tottenham Hale and Liverpool Street at no extra cost.

## More work in the new year

Late on Monday 4 January, work near Clacton changes two trains. The 10.56pm from Colchester to Walton-on-the-Naze will end at Clacton-on-Sea, with a bus on to Walton via Kirby Cross and Frinton-on-Sea. The 11.18pm from Liverpool Street to Clacton will be replaced by a bus between Colchester and Clacton.

The festive closure comes before the weekend cancellations planned on lines into London early next year, [as The Seax reported](https://theseax.com/story/essex-greater-anglia-elizabeth-line-weekend-cancellations-early-2027/). Greater Anglia is asking passengers to check before they travel, both on Christmas Eve and across the holiday.
