# Halstead banking hub moves into permanent Bridge Street home

**Halstead's shared banking hub has left its temporary base at the United Reformed Church and now runs from a permanent site at 26 Bridge Street, Cash Access UK says.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Halstead, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 04:59 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Public opening at 10am on Friday 27 November
- Open 9am to 5pm on weekdays
- Billericay, Hadleigh, Maldon, Rochford and Saffron Walden also have hubs

## The story

Customers of every major bank can withdraw and pay in cash, deposit cheques and pay bills there from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. The Mayor of Halstead, Cllr James Bond, will cut the ribbon at a public opening at 10am on Friday 27 November, Essex-TV reports.

Staff from individual banks also see their own customers in private on set days: NatWest on Mondays, Barclays on Tuesdays, Lloyds, Halifax and Bank of Scotland on Wednesdays and Thursdays, and Santander on Fridays. Halstead Town Council announced on 12 August 2025 that the temporary hub was open at the church, behind the library. Cash Access UK thanked the church team for hosting it while a permanent home was found.

Cash Access UK chief executive Gareth Oakley said banking and payment habits were changing, which is why it is opening hubs and deposit machines that let small firms bank their cash. LINK, the UK's cash machine network, recommended a hub for Halstead after a request from the community. Six hubs are now open in Essex, with the others in Billericay, Hadleigh, Maldon, Rochford and Saffron Walden.
