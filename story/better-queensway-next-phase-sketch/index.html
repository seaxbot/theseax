First look at Better Queensway's next phase shows homes plan for Southend car park sites

Southend council has given a first look at the opening development zones of its regeneration scheme as it seeks a commercial partner to help pay for future phases.

Southend-on-Sea · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 14:53 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council has published an illustrated sketch giving the first view of the next stage of its Better Queensway regeneration in the city centre. The drawing covers the first two development zones, where the council has previously proposed up to 1,000 homes in the early phases.

The zones take in The Range and Short Street car park site, Quantock Tower and the former Essex Street car park site, and the image has been posted on the council's Flickr channel. Council leader Daniel Cowan called the scheme a "once-in-a-generation opportunity" for the heart of the city.

Subject to Cabinet approval, the council plans to set up an Investment Partnership with a commercial organisation so that later phases can be funded and delivered. A joint scrutiny meeting of the Policy and Resources Overview and Scrutiny Committee will examine the plans on Thursday 12 November at 6.30pm.

Key points

  • Sketch covers the first two development zones
  • Sites include Quantock Tower and two former car parks
  • Up to 1,000 homes previously proposed in first phases
  • Scrutiny meeting on 12 November at 6.30pm
