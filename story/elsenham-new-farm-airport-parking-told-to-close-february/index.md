# Elsenham airport car park told to close by February after appeal fails

**The unauthorised Stansted parking site at New Farm has lost its fight with the council. The land must now be cleared.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Elsenham, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 06:33 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Property.

## Key points

- Inspector refused a six-month wind-down
- Enforcement notice first served in August 2025
- Site can hold about 1,000 vehicles, the BBC estimated

## The story

A planning inspector has dismissed most of an appeal against Uttlesford District Council's order to close the car park at New Farm, off Stansted Road in Elsenham. The ruling, published on Monday 23 November, gives the operator until 23 February 2027 to stop parking and storing vehicles on the land. The cars, shipping containers and hard standing must go, and the land must be put back as it was.

BBC News reported that the inspector, Graeme Robbie, upheld the enforcement notice but turned down a request for six months to wind the business down. He extended the time allowed to comply from two months to three. The appeal said the business had future bookings to honour, and that cancelling them would hurt the company and its customers.

## How we got here

The site, on former farmland beside the M11, began offering cheaper parking for Stansted passengers in early 2025. The council served its enforcement notice on 14 August 2025, after talks failed to persuade the operator to stop voluntarily. SC Parking Ltd appealed on 1 September 2025, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reported, and the notice was suspended while the Planning Inspectorate considered the case.

The car park kept growing in the meantime. A BBC investigation, reported in July, found it had expanded by almost two-thirds since the summer of 2025, with room for about 1,000 vehicles. Residents complained of diggers excavating more land. Customers reported long waits, crowded shuttle vans and cars returned dirty or damaged.

## What happens next

Uttlesford District Council said it would keep monitoring the site and work to make sure the operator complies within the inspector's timescale. In September the Bishop's Stortford Independent reported that if the notice was upheld and then ignored, the council could consider further enforcement, including prosecution. Stansted Airport has advised passengers to check that a parking firm is reputable before they book. The operators did not respond to the BBC's request for comment.
