A12 reopens near Ingatestone as Anglian Water blames blockage for collapse

The southbound A12 between Margaretting and Ingatestone is open again after a day and a night of diversions through the villages, with repairs finished on the broken carriageway.

Ingatestone, Margaretting · Updated Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:17 · First published Tuesday 17 November 2026, 09:50 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

National Highways said the southbound A12 between junction 15 near Margaretting and junction 13 at Ingatestone reopened early on Wednesday 18 November, the BBC reported. The carriageway had been shut since shortly after 9.20am on Tuesday 17 November, when the road surface broke up.

Anglian Water said the damage was caused by a blockage in one of its assets, which it says has now been removed. Earlier reports from the Daily Gazette and the Echo had pointed to significant water damage beneath the road, and drivers spent Tuesday on a diversion along the B1002 through Margaretting and Ingatestone, with delays of up to 48 minutes.

The closure ran far beyond National Highways' first estimate of a 2.15pm reopening on Tuesday, after contractors found the repair would take longer. Neither National Highways nor Anglian Water has said whether further work will be needed at the site.

Key points

  • Closed from shortly after 9.20am on 17 November
  • Diversion ran via the B1002 for almost a day
  • Anglian Water says the blockage has been removed
