A12 southbound near Ingatestone still closed on Tuesday afternoon as crater repairs continue

Drivers heading south through Essex towards Brentwood faced long delays after the tarmac between Margaretting and Ingatestone broke apart, with a water leak reported as the cause.

Ingatestone, Margaretting · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 09:50 · Updated Tuesday 17 November 2026, 16:44 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

The southbound A12 between junction 14 at Margaretting and junction 13 at Ingatestone stayed closed through the afternoon of Tuesday 17 November, well past the 2.15pm reopening National Highways had estimated earlier, the Echo and the Daily Gazette reported. No source read by The Seax had confirmed the road reopened by 4.45pm.

The road surface broke up shortly after 9.20am, and Essex Police warned it would stay shut for some time, the BBC reported. The Daily Gazette and the Echo report that a water leak caused the crater. National Highways sent contractors to assess and repair the damage, with delays of up to 48 minutes on the B1002 diversion along the old road through Margaretting and Ingatestone.

Key points

  • Closure began shortly after 9.20am
  • Diversion signed by National Highways via the B1002
  • Check National Highways before travelling
