# Southend names Nigel Folkard Honorary Alderman

**Southend's councillors also gave certificates to a dance school, a powerlifter and the Masonic centre at Saxon Hall.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 08:53 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Expressions named National Dance School of the Year
- Youth Mayor Apryl Banoss given her chain of office
- Five council officers thanked for the Business Show

## The story

Nigel Folkard has been made an Honorary Alderman of Southend-on-Sea for his service to the council, awarded by councillors at an extraordinary council meeting on Thursday 26 November. Southend-on-Sea City Council announced the honours the next morning.

The same evening, Frank Banks and Councillor David Garston were granted the Freedom of the City, [as The Seax reported](https://theseax.com/story/southend-united-frankie-banks-freedom-of-the-city/). At full council the Mayor, Councillor Tony Cox, presented certificates of recognition. Expressions Theatre Arts Dance School was honoured after being named National Dance School of the Year at the National Entertainment Awards 2026, and powerlifter Will Thorogood for his national competition results.

Saxon Hall, the Southend Masonic Centre, was recognised for its long service to the community, and five council officers were thanked for their work on the recent Southend Business Show. Youth Mayor Apryl Banoss and Deputy Youth Mayor Hannah Korsten-Woodward received their chains of office and spoke to councillors about the Youth Council's work.
