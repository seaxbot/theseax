Healthwatch Southend welcomes Government decision to keep patient watchdogs

Ministers have dropped plans to abolish Healthwatch England and local Healthwatch bodies, and say they will strengthen them instead.

Southend-on-Sea · Tuesday 3 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Healthwatch Southend
WhatsApp Facebook X

Healthwatch Southend has welcomed the Government's decision to drop its proposal to abolish Healthwatch England and the network of local Healthwatch organisations. The independent body gathers residents' views on health and care services in the city.

The Government has also committed to strengthening Healthwatch so patients have a stronger voice. Healthwatch Southend says no detail of those plans has yet been published and it is waiting for more information.

In the meantime it continues to operate as normal, alongside more than 150 other local Healthwatch organisations across England.

Key points

  • Plan to abolish Healthwatch dropped
  • Government promises to strengthen patients' voice
  • No details of the plans published yet
