Stansted's Meet the Buyers event turns 25 as local firms chase airport contracts

London Stansted's networking day for small Essex and East of England suppliers marks a quarter of a century this week.

Stansted · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 17:37 · Reported by London Stansted Airport
WhatsApp Facebook X

Meet the Buyers takes place on Thursday 19 November at the Radisson Blu Hotel at London Stansted, 25 years after the airport launched it in 2001. Firms get short face-to-face meetings with buyers from big employers in construction, engineering, logistics and other sectors.

The airport said the event has generated more than £37m of business for companies in London and the East of England over the past 15 years, and has since been copied at Manchester and East Midlands airports.

Key points

  • Launched in 2001 to help small suppliers win work
  • Works like business speed dating, the airport says
  • Rolled out nationally, with more than £78m in opportunities
