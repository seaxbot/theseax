Disqualified drink-driver jailed after three-vehicle crash on London Road, Harlow

Nison Rajeswaren, 23, admitted drink-driving, driving while banned and having no insurance, and will spend a total of 31 weeks behind bars.

Harlow · Friday 6 November 2026, 10:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 23-year-old man who was already banned from driving has been jailed after a three-vehicle collision on London Road in Harlow. Police were called shortly before 6.30am on Friday 23 October.

Nison Rajeswaren admitted driving over the alcohol limit, driving without insurance and driving while disqualified at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court. He was given 12 weeks in prison and recalled to serve a suspended sentence he was already under, making 31 weeks in total.

He was also banned from driving for five years, with 73 days added to his existing disqualification, which runs until October 2028.

Key points

  • Crash on London Road, 23 October
  • 12 weeks plus recall: 31 weeks in total
  • Five-year driving ban
