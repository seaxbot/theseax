# Three deny attempted murder over Braintree Market Place shooting

**Two men and a boy of 17 have pleaded not guilty. They are accused of trying to murder two teenagers in Braintree.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Braintree, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 15:03 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Two teenagers suffered serious injuries
- Trial due at Chelmsford Crown Court

## The story

Destiny Ema, 23, of Meadgate Avenue, Chelmsford, and Kayden Brown, 18, of Noakes Avenue, Chelmsford, denied two counts of attempted murder at Chelmsford Crown Court on Monday 23 November, BBC News reported. A 17-year-old boy from Braintree, who cannot be named because of his age, also pleaded not guilty.

All three denied two counts of wounding with intent and one of unlawful wounding. Ema also denied possessing an altered firearm without a certificate and having a bladed article. The charges relate to a shooting in Market Place, Braintree, on 31 August. A trial is due on 4 May.
