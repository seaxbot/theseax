# Colchester leaders rule out Leisure World rebuild ahead of cabinet vote

**Upgrading the ageing Cowdray Avenue centre is the only practical way to protect public swimming in the city, Colchester City Council says.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Leisure World, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 18:40 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Scrutiny councillors looked at the plans on 11 November
- This year's budget set aside £8m for the centre
- The Leisure Pool is due to reopen in early 2027

## The story

Colchester City Council looked at knocking down Leisure World and building a new centre, but judged that the cost and the years it would take ruled that out for now, it said on 16 November. Instead, council leaders want a programme of work on the existing site covering essential building infrastructure, customer facilities and the systems that keep the centre running.

Councillors on the Scrutiny Committee examined the plans on 11 November, and they go to the Cabinet for a final decision on Wednesday 2 December. Fuller details and a timetable for the work will follow if they are approved, the council says.

Council leader Paul Dundas, Conservative councillor for Tiptree, said that like many ageing public buildings, the site "now requires major investment". A brand-new centre would have been the ideal answer, he said, but it was not viable at this stage. He said it would not be "remotely acceptable" for a city of more than 200,000 people to have no public swimming, and called the plan the biggest investment in Leisure World for a generation.

No cost for the programme has been published yet. Colchester City Council's 2026/27 budget, agreed in February, set aside £8 million to be invested in Leisure World. After the scrutiny meeting, Mr Dundas told the Daily Gazette the centre would have faced permanent closure within months without the investment.

The work would follow the £1.4 million refurbishment of the Leisure Pool, which closed at the end of September and is due to reopen in early 2027. That project covers relining the pool, new changing areas, work on the outdoor flumes, better ventilation, a new splash pad and upgraded water features. The rest of Leisure World has stayed open, though its main pool [shut for cleaning after an incident](https://theseax.com/story/colchester-leisure-world-swimming-pool-closed-incident/) on Friday 20 November.

All this falls during the reorganisation of Essex councils. Colchester has said that during 2026/27 it expects to share any substantial financial decisions with the other councils that will form its new unitary authority.
