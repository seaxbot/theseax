# Braintree Recycling Centre opens free 'Santa's Grotto' for Christmas reuse

**Unwanted Christmas trees and decorations left at the Braintree tip are now free to take home.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Braintree, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 11:28 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Free to take: trees and non-electrical decorations
- Follows paint and furniture reuse at the site
- Visits must be booked in advance

## The story

Staff at Braintree Recycling Centre have set up a Christmas reuse container, nicknamed Santa's Grotto, where visitors can pick up festive items free of charge. Love Essex, the Essex Waste Partnership's recycling service, announced it on Friday 27 November.

The container takes things such as Christmas trees and decorations that are not electrical. It follows the site's paint and furniture reuse schemes, and Love Essex said items have been going out as quickly as they come in.
