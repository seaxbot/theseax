Zara owner's budget chain Lefties opens its second UK store at Lakeside

The 36,000 sq ft shop in West Thurrock follows the brand's UK debut in Liverpool in August.

West Thurrock · Tuesday 3 November 2026, 16:20 · Reported by Retail Gazette
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lefties, the lower-priced fashion chain owned by Zara parent company Inditex, has opened its second UK shop at Lakeside shopping centre in West Thurrock. The arrival gives south Essex shoppers one of only two UK branches of the brand, which is being positioned as a rival to Primark.

The Lakeside store covers 36,000 sq ft, larger than the 27,000 sq ft flagship on South John Street at Liverpool One where Lefties made its UK debut in August, Retail Gazette reports. Robert Jewell, managing director of asset management at centre owner Pradera, called the opening a significant moment for Lakeside.

Pradera says the signing marks the next stage of Lakeside's growth as a super-regional shopping centre and puts it firmly on the map as a lifestyle destination. The opening comes as the centre heads into the Christmas trading season.

Key points

  • Second UK Lefties store after Liverpool One
  • 36,000 sq ft unit at Lakeside
  • Brand is owned by Zara parent Inditex
