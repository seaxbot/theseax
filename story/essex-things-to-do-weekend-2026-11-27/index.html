Essex this weekend: light trails, Christmas markets and switch-ons

Essex · Thursday 26 November 2026, 12:41 · Reported by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Most towns have something festive on from Friday to Sunday. Many of the events are free.

Christmas takes over the Essex diary from Friday 27 November to Sunday 29 November. Light trails open, markets set up and several towns switch on their lights, with plenty of it free. Here is what is on, area by area, from The Seax's What's On listings.

Chelmsford and mid Essex

Enlightened, the light trail at the Hylands Estate, opens on Friday, with entry from 4.30pm at the Writtle Gate. RHS Glow lights up RHS Garden Hyde Hall from 4.30pm to 9pm, and Lumina at South Woodham Ferrers runs from 4.30pm to 7pm, with tickets from £12.95. Cinderella opens at Chelmsford Theatre on Friday, with lines written by local children.

On Saturday, Christmas at Stow at Stow Maries Great War Aerodrome runs from 10am to 4pm, costing £5 for adults with under-16s free. That evening Chelmsford Cathedral marks 40 years of The Glory with a textile evening from 6.15pm, at £7.50.

Southend and south Essex

Light Up Leigh fills Leigh Community Centre and the library gardens on Friday from 3pm to 8pm, with carols at 6pm and free entry. Adventure Island's Wonderland opens on Saturday from 10am, from £20, or £10 for annual pass holders. Canvey Island's Christmas event runs from noon to 5pm on Saturday. Barleylands near Billericay has its Christmas markets, and Scott's Light Up the Night in Rochford costs £7.

Colchester, Braintree and the coast

Halstead switches on its lights in the Public Gardens at 4pm on Saturday, with Santa's grotto from 2pm and jazz from 4.30pm. Dedham's Christmas market fills Royal Square from 12.30pm to 4.30pm that day, with free entry. Clacton Pier opens Santa's Enchanted Grotto on Saturday, while Colchester Zoo's Enchanted Trail of Light runs in the evenings. Colchester United holds a Christmas fair at the JobServe Community Stadium on Sunday.

West Essex and Uttlesford

Saffron Walden's late-night shopping evening is on Friday, with free town council buses from 4pm to 8pm. On Sunday, Ingatestone's festive fair runs along the High Street from noon to 5pm. Ongar's Christmas festival starts at 2pm, with its lights switched on at 7pm, and both are free. In Harlow, the Merry Minds festive fun run for Mind in West Essex starts at The Water Gardens at 10am on Sunday.

Key points

  • Light Up Leigh on Friday is free, with carols at 6pm
  • Halstead's lights go on at 4pm on Saturday
  • Ongar switches on its lights at 7pm on Sunday
