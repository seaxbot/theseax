# Stanford-le-Hope charity night to fund South Hill defibrillator

**Katie's Heart Appeal is holding a music night and raffle at the Railway Tavern in Stanford-le-Hope on Saturday 28 November to pay for a public defibrillator and bleed control kit at South Hill.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stanford-le-Hope, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Tickets cost £7 from the pub or on 07999 455935
- The charity has raised money for more than 12 years
- Music from the 1970s to the 2000s
