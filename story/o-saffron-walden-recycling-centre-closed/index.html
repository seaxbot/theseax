Saffron Walden recycling centre shut all week after serious incident, reopening Monday at the earliest

The Thaxted Road tip will stay closed until at least 9 November, and anyone already booked in will be contacted to rearrange.

Saffron Walden · Monday 2 November 2026, 09:00 · Updated Wednesday 4 November 2026, 06:05 · Reported by Love Essex (Essex County Council)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saffron Walden's household recycling centre on Thaxted Road is closed for the whole of this week following what Essex's Love Essex service describes as a serious incident. The closure leaves residents in the north-west of the county without their nearest tip just as autumn garden clear-outs are under way.

According to the site's notice, the closure follows a medical incident involving a member of staff over the weekend. The centre is not expected to reopen until Monday 9 November at the earliest, and people with bookings before then will be contacted and invited to rebook.

Residents are being asked to book a slot at another recycling centre in the meantime, as Essex households can use any of the county's 21 sites. Love Essex says it will give a further update when more information is available.

Key points

  • Thaxted Road site closed all week
  • Reopening Monday 9 November at the earliest
  • Existing bookings will be contacted to rebook
  • Any of Essex's 21 recycling centres can be used instead
