# Christmas in Essex: The Seax opens free guide to more than 80 festive events

**Lights switch-ons, light trails, Santa's grottos, markets, pantomimes and carols across the county are now on one page, and organisers can add their own.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 08:49 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: What's On.

## Key points

- Seven sections, from lights switch-ons to pantomimes
- Every listing links to the organiser or venue
- Send events to events@theseax.com

## The story

The Seax has opened Christmas in Essex, a single page listing festive events across the county, at theseax.com/whats-on/christmas. On Sunday 22 November it held 84 listings, each with the date, time, place and price where the organiser gives one, and a link through to the organiser or venue.

The page is split into seven sections with the soonest events first: six Christmas lights switch-ons, seven light trails and illuminations, 15 Santa, grotto and family days, 16 Christmas markets and fairs, eight pantomimes, 10 carol services, concerts and shows, and 22 other festive events such as wreath-making workshops and Christmas trains.
