Basildon Council heads for £3.8m overspend as homelessness costs rise

Basildon · Thursday 19 November 2026, 06:19 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Borough Council's Cabinet will be asked on Thursday 26 November 2026 to note that its General Fund, which pays for most day-to-day services, is forecast to end the year £3.8 million over budget. The forecast, based on spending to the end of September, has grown by £400,000 since the first quarter of the year.

The report blames the cost of the temporary accommodation the council must provide for homeless households, and in particular a shortage of alternatives to expensive nightly paid accommodation. Capital financing, staffing and the risk that planned savings are not delivered add to the pressure, partly offset by extra planning income. On the Housing Revenue Account, which covers council homes, the position has improved by £1.3 million since the first quarter, with the report pointing to rising repair costs, damp and mould cases and the cost of meeting a Regulatory Notice from the Regulator of Social Housing.

The report says any pressures that cannot be managed by the end of the financial year will have to be met from the council's revenue reserves. Services must now set out the actions they are taking each month under a formal recovery plan process, which officers say is showing early signs of working. The revenue figures went to the Finance, Procurement and Efficiency Scrutiny Committee on 16 November 2026 and will feed into setting future budgets.

Key points

  • General Fund forecast overspend £3.8m for 2026/27, up £400,000 since quarter one
  • Housing Revenue Account forecast overspend £3.2m, £1.3m better than quarter one
  • Nightly paid temporary accommodation is a key cost pressure
  • Cabinet meets Thursday 26 November 2026
