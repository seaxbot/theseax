Police seek doorbell and dash cam footage after young driver dies in Harlow crash at Kingsland

The man in his 20s was alone in a Ford Fiesta that hit a parked car shortly after 5.30am on Sunday, police said, and the road has since reopened.

Harlow · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:56 · Updated Monday 9 November 2026, 08:47 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man in his 20s has died after a crash at Kingsland, at its junction with Tendring Road in Harlow, early on Sunday 8 November. Essex Police said his car hit a parked car and that several other vehicles nearby were damaged.

Emergency services were called shortly after 5.30am. The driver, who was alone in a Ford Fiesta, was pronounced dead at the scene, police said. The road was closed for several hours while officers carried out enquiries, and drivers had been asked to stay away.

Essex Police are appealing for anyone who saw the collision, or who has dash cam or doorbell camera footage from the area, to come forward to help establish how it happened. No other injuries have been reported.

Key points

  • Crash happened shortly after 5.30am on Sunday at Kingsland and Tendring Road
  • The driver, alone in a Ford Fiesta, died at the scene
  • Several parked vehicles were damaged and the road was shut for hours
  • Police want dash cam and doorbell footage
