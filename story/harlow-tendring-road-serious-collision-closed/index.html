Several vehicles involved in serious crash that shut Tendring Road at Kingsland in Harlow

Essex Police said the Sunday morning collision appeared to involve more than one vehicle, and asked people to keep away while emergency crews worked at the scene.

Harlow · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:56 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring Road in Harlow was closed on Sunday morning, 8 November, after a serious collision at Kingsland. Essex Police said shortly after 8.30am that emergency services were at the scene and that the crash appeared to involve several vehicles.

Drivers were asked to avoid the area and use other routes. Police said they would give more details when they could; no reopening time or information about injuries had been reported.

Key points

  • Serious collision in Tendring Road at Kingsland, Harlow, on Sunday 8 November
  • Police said several vehicles appeared to be involved
  • No details of injuries or a reopening time have been reported
