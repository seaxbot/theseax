Police ask Harlow drivers to look out for missing William Knight and his white Seat Ateca

Essex Police are concerned for the 30-year-old and want anyone who sees him or the car to call 999.

Harlow · Thursday 12 November 2026, 17:11 · Updated Friday 13 November 2026, 09:50 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

William Knight was last seen at about 7am on Thursday 12 November 2026 near Latton Common in Harlow, Your Harlow reports, and Essex Police are carrying out local enquiries to find him.

Callers should quote incident 439 of 12 November.
