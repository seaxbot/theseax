# Maldon Local Plan consultation now open: have your say

**People across Maldon district can say what its next plan for homes and roads should cover. Views close on 22 December.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 21:38 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- The scoping stage asks what the plan should cover
- First Essex plan under the government's 30-month system
- It will replace the 2014 to 2029 Local Development Plan
- Later stages and the examination have no dates yet

## The story

Maldon District Council's scoping consultation on its new Local Plan opened on Wednesday 25 November. It asks a simple first question: what should the plan contain, and how do people want to be involved in shaping it?

A Local Plan is the council's rulebook for development. It sets out where new homes, jobs and roads can go, and planning applications are judged against it. Once adopted, the new plan will replace the Maldon District Local Development Plan, which covers 2014 to 2029.

## Why now

Councillors agreed the project's framework, timetable and resources at a meeting on 10 September. According to the council, Maldon is the first in Essex to prepare a Local Plan under the government's new 30-month system. That system is designed to get a plan adopted within two and a half years of work formally starting.

Council leader Councillor Simon Morgan said a new plan was "essential to make sure future development is plan-led". The council says the work remains important whatever the outcome of local government reorganisation, and that it must fit the wider Greater Essex spatial development strategy.

## What happens next

This is only the first stage. Further rounds will follow on the plan's vision, strategy, evidence and policies, then independent checks and a public examination before it can be adopted. The council has not yet given dates for those later stages. Details and the timetable are on its new Local Plan page.
