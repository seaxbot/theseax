Harlow's Avant Garde salon on The Rows shuts without warning

Harlow · Friday 20 November 2026, 10:11 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Clients of the long-running Harlow town centre hair and beauty salon found its doors shut this week.

Staff at Avant Garde Hair and Beauty, on The Rows above the market square, were told by text that the business had closed and to contact the management company about equipment left there, Your Harlow reported on Friday 20 November.

Some clients had regular bookings and reacted with anger and confusion. Your Harlow said the business had not replied to its questions, and that some people are in early talks about reopening the salon.

Key points

  • Salon had traded since the mid-1970s
  • Business did not answer Your Harlow's questions
