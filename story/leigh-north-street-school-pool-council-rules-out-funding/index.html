Southend council says it cannot step in to reopen Leigh North Street school's shut swimming pool

A report for Monday's cabinet says the pool's future is a matter for the school, despite a petition signed by more than 2,500 people.

Leigh-on-Sea · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 12:53 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Leigh North Street Primary School closed its pool in July after the head teacher said it had become too costly to run. A report going to Southend-on-Sea City Council's cabinet on Monday 16 November concludes the council has no power to intervene, the BBC's local democracy reporter says.

Campaigners have raised more than £17,000 towards a £20,000 target and are looking at forming a charity or taking the pool into community ownership. Governors say they would consider a workable community-led plan, but none has yet been submitted.

Key points

  • Petition to the council gathered 2,547 signatures
  • Cabinet member Chris Webster called the school's decision disappointing
  • Save North Street Pool fundraising has passed £17,000
