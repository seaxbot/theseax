Wickford station's new ticket office opens as Greater Anglia rebuild nears the finish

Rayleigh and Wickford MP Mark Francois opened the ticket office on Monday, with the rest of the station upgrade due by the end of 2026.

Wickford · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 10:59 · Reported by RailAdvent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Greater Anglia's new ticket office at Wickford opened on Monday 9 November, a milestone in the multi-million-pound rebuild of the station, RailAdvent reports. New waiting areas, a platform canopy, ticket machines and toilets are still to come, with the whole scheme due to be finished by the end of the year and an official reopening planned before Christmas.

Key points

  • Opened by MP Mark Francois with Greater Anglia's Andrew Goodrum
  • Waiting areas, canopy, ticket machines and toilets still to come
  • Official reopening planned before Christmas
