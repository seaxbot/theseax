# University of Essex students protest against MoD housing plan

**Some students on the Colchester campus say they feel uneasy about living alongside soldiers. The university says nothing has been decided.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Wivenhoe Park, Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:00 (UK time). Updated: Thursday 26 November 2026, 17:00. Section: News. Topic: Education.

## Key points

- The stay would be temporary, for up to two years
- Prof Greer said a decision is likely within 12 months
- The Army says it wants value for money and less pressure on local housing

## The story

Students at the University of Essex's Colchester campus have protested against a plan to house 150 Ministry of Defence personnel there, the Daily Gazette reported on 26 November. Talks are still at an early stage.

Some students told the BBC they feared for their safety, as soldiers would not wear uniform on campus. Prof Chris Greer, the deputy vice-chancellor, said the university was still checking whether the idea is workable, [as The Seax reported when the talks emerged](https://theseax.com/story/colchester-university-of-essex-army-personnel-student-accommodation/).
