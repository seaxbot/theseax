# Writtle Green benches moved away from moth-hit oak

**Writtle Parish Council has moved two benches from under a large oak on the Green that has been badly affected by oak processionary moth.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Writtle, Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 13:35 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- The Brewster bench now sits further along the row towards Wear Pond
- The 1897 Jubilee bench has moved to the far side of the Green, by Wear Pond
- The parish council calls it a precaution
