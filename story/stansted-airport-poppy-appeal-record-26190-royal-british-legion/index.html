Stansted Airport Poppy Appeal raises record £26,190

Stansted · Friday 27 November 2026, 07:56 · Reported by London Stansted Airport
WhatsApp Facebook X

Passengers and staff at Stansted gave more than ever to this year's Poppy Appeal. The total beat last year's record by over £2,000.

London Stansted said on Friday 27 November that passengers, airport staff and visitors gave £26,190 to the Royal British Legion in the run-up to Remembrance Sunday. It is the most the airport's annual appeal has ever raised.

Last year's total was itself a record, and this year's beat it by more than £2,000. The money came in through a team of volunteers who stood with collecting tins and poppies across the terminal.

Volunteers covered 148 shifts of three hours each. Soldiers from the 33 and 35 Royal Engineer Regiments added another 64 shifts.

Simon Bambridge, the Royal British Legion's Poppy Appeal organiser, thanked the volunteers, staff and passengers. He said the appeal works only because so many people give their time, and that every donation helps veterans and their families.

Next year's appeal

The Legion wants more volunteers for the 2027 appeal at the airport. Dan Turpin, the airport's community engagement manager, said the appeal is an important part of life at Stansted and that colleagues and passengers back it every year.
