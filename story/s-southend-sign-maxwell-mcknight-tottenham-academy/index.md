# Southend United sign former Tottenham academy defender Maxwell McKnight

**The 22-year-old, who played for Brentford B last season, went straight into Kieron Dyer's starting line-up against Aldershot Town on Saturday 14 November.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 14 November 2026, 14:17 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Club holds a 12-month extension option
- No first-team games at Tottenham

## The story

Southend United also hold an option to keep McKnight for a further 12 months, BBC Sport reports. He came through the academy at Tottenham Hotspur but never made a first-team appearance there.
