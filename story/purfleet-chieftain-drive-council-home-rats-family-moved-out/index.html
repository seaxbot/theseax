Purfleet grandmother says rats are still keeping her family out of their Chieftain Drive council home

A Thurrock Council tenant in Purfleet says her family cannot move back in, while a ward councillor has asked the council for an action plan with timescales.

Purfleet, Thurrock · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 23:21 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lisa Clark, a Thurrock Council tenant on Chieftain Drive in Purfleet, told Thurrock Nub News in a report published on Wednesday 11 November 2026 that her family still cannot live in their home because of a rat infestation, after rats were found on her four-year-old granddaughter's bed. She says neighbours can hear rats in their walls.

Labour ward councillor Aaron Green says there has been little meaningful progress and the bin sheds are likely to be contributing; he has asked for a plan covering pest control, bin areas, support for the family and compensation.

Key points

  • Cabinet member Robert Willoughby has already apologised to the family
  • Reform ward councillor Michelle Heal says she is confident the council will resolve it
  • Ms Clark says belongings lost to the infestation have not been compensated
