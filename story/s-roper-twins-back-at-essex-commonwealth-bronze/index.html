Roper twins back at the University of Essex after double Commonwealth Games bronze for Wales

The Ipswich-born gymnasts won their medals within an hour of each other in Glasgow and are now back studying at the Colchester campus.

Colchester · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 19:18 · Reported by University of Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Gymnasts Emily and Abigail Roper have returned to the University of Essex, where they study Sports and Exercise Science at the Colchester campus, after making Commonwealth Games history for Wales, the university reports. The 21-year-old twins were back on campus in their Essex Blades kit, holding up their Team Wales shirts from Glasgow 2026.

Abigail won bronze on vault with a score of 13.433, Wales' first Commonwealth Games vault medal since 1994. Emily, who had just missed out on the vault podium in fourth, took bronze on the uneven bars less than an hour later.

The sisters joined Essex on a sports scholarship in autumn 2025 with Glasgow as their goal. They train at Pipers Vale Gymnastics Club in Ipswich and also compete for the Essex Blades in student competitions, where Emily won gold and Abigail bronze at the BUCS championships in February.

Key points

  • Both medals came on 27 July 2026
  • Abigail: vault bronze, 13.433
  • Emily: uneven bars bronze
