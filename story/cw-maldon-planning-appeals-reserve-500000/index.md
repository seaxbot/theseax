# Maldon council expects 5 planning appeals this year costing about £500,000

**Maldon District Council's day-to-day spending is close to budget overall, with a forecast overspend of £126,000, or 0.9%, for 2026/27, its Director of Finance reports.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 06:19 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- 5 planning appeals expected in 2026/27, most costing around £100,000
- Previous appeals budget £184,000
- Forecast revenue overspend £126,000 (0.9%)
- Forecast capital spend £7,578,000 against a revised budget of £7,526,000

## The story

Maldon District Council's Strategy and Resources Committee will be asked on Thursday 26 November 2026 to confirm a change in how the council pays for planning appeals, where applicants challenge decisions made by its planning committee. The Director of Finance's report for the six months to 30 September says most appeals cost around £100,000 each and five are expected to be heard in 2026/27.

The existing provision of £184,000 is described in the report as clearly insufficient, and because it sits in the base budget repeated every year it does not reflect how much the number of appeals varies. The council's section 151 officer, in consultation with the committee chair and chief officers, has decided to remove that provision and pay for appeals from a new earmarked reserve instead.

The report calls the overall picture positive, saying capital spending is close to budget, debt is being reduced and reserves remain at a good level. Councillors are also asked to agree adjustments for one-off spending already approved in principle, now that the actual costs are known, and a further update is promised in the next monitoring report.
