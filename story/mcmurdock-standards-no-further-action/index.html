MPs' standards watchdog finds South Basildon MP James McMurdock started racial slur chain but takes no action

A Commons committee rejected the independent MP's explanations for a one-letter post on X but ruled the damage was to his own reputation, not Parliament's.

Basildon & Thurrock · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 08:03 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

James McMurdock, the independent MP for South Basildon and East Thurrock, will face no further action after a Commons standards investigation into a social media post, Thurrock Nub News reports. The Standards Committee's report, published on Tuesday, found he posted the letter "N" on X in August 2025 beneath a post criticising Sky News political reporter Mhari Aurora, starting a chain of posts that spelled out a racial slur against her.

The committee rejected his claims that a child had posted the letter or that it was posted by accident, and found on the balance of probabilities that he posted it himself. Standards commissioner Daniel Greenberg concluded he had breached the MPs' code of conduct and had taken no steps to distance himself from the slur. But the committee ruled he had not broken the rule on causing significant damage to the reputation of the House, saying any harm was to his own reputation, and recommended no further action.

Mr McMurdock, who was elected for Reform UK in 2024 before leaving the party, called the investigation a "ridiculous waste of taxpayers' money" and said he was glad a rational conclusion had been reached. He said he was mortified when he saw what followed his post and looked forward to moving on.

Key points

  • Standards Committee report published on 3 November
  • Committee found he posted the letter himself
  • No breach of the rule on damaging the reputation of the House
  • No further action recommended
