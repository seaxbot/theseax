Aveley cruise past Hutton 5-0 in the BBC Essex Senior Cup fourth round

Aveley's Isthmian Premier side were far too strong for their Essex Senior League hosts, with two substitutes scoring late on.

Aveley · Friday 13 November 2026, 14:34 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Aveley won 5-0 away to Hutton in the BBC Essex Senior Cup on Wednesday 11 November 2026, at Billericay's New Lodge stadium. Harry Critchley headed the first goal on 29 minutes and Francis Mampolo added a second early in the second half.

Shad Ngandu made it three before Said Mariche pounced on a goalkeeping error on 88 minutes and Alejandro Machado, also on from the bench, scored in the final minute.

Key points

  • Five different Aveley players found the net
  • Critchley's opener came from a corner
  • Mampolo struck within five minutes of the restart
