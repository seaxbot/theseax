# Weeley and Little Clacton bypasses to shut for four nights from 7 December

**Drivers heading to and from Clacton face overnight closures on two bypasses. The road is being resurfaced.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Weeley, Little Clacton, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 17:37 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Closures run 8pm to 5am
- Work is surface dressing, a road resurfacing treatment
- The order was made under the Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984

## The story

Essex County Council will close the Little Clacton Weeley Bypass and the Little Clacton Bypass for four nights from Monday 7 December. The roads will shut from 8pm to 5am each night, according to the temporary traffic order published by Weeley Parish Council.

The council says the closure is needed to keep the public and its workforce safe during surface dressing work. The Echo reports a signed diversion of about 19.5 miles. Full details are on the one.network roadworks map.
