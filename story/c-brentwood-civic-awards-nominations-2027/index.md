# Brentwood Civic Awards open for nominations

**Brentwood Borough Council wants names of people and groups who make a difference locally. Nominations close in February.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 11:37 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Two awards: Service to the Community and Lifetime Achievement
- Winners named at the Civic Dinner in April

## The story

Brentwood Borough Council opened nominations for its annual Civic Awards on Thursday 26 November. There are two awards: Service to the Community, for individuals or groups of any age, and Lifetime Achievement, for someone who has helped the borough over many years. Anyone who lives or works in Brentwood can be nominated, using the council's online form or a paper form from the Mayor's secretary. Nominations close on Friday 5 February 2027, and the awards will be presented at the Civic Dinner on Friday 16 April 2027.
