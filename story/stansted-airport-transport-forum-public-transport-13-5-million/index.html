Almost half of Stansted's passengers now reach the airport by train, bus or coach

More than 13.5 million passengers travelled to and from London Stansted Airport by rail, bus and coach over the past year, the airport says, the highest public transport share of any major UK airport.

Stansted · Friday 13 November 2026, 14:53 · Reported by London Stansted Airport
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rail alone now carries more than 35% of London Stansted Airport's passengers, the airport said on Friday 13 November, after the Stansted Airport Transport Forum marked its 25th anniversary at the Radisson Blu Hotel on 11 November. When the forum began, Stansted handled about 9 million passengers a year and about a third of them used public transport.

The forum brings together transport operators, councils, businesses and community groups.
