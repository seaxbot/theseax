Basildon's 492-flat Mercantile reaches full height over Market Square

The build-to-rent towers given permission on appeal in 2021 have topped out, though rents and a move-in date have still not been announced.

Basildon · Friday 6 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Mercantile, a development of 492 rental flats rising over Basildon's Market Square, has reached its full height, a milestone known as topping out. The scheme is one of the biggest changes to the town centre in years, although the flats are not yet finished and hoardings and cranes remain around the square.

The scheme is backed by Canadian investor Starlight Investments with Orwell Real Estate, and is being built by Ernest Park. It will have 492 one and two-bedroom flats in three buildings, all for rent from a single landlord, plus about 16,000 sq ft of shops and commercial space and residents' facilities including a gym, cinema and rooftop garden. Basildon Council did not approve the plans itself: a government planning inspector granted permission on appeal in December 2021 for blocks of up to 17 storeys after the council failed to decide in time.

Orwell's Neil Strelitz called it a real milestone for Basildon, and the council's property lead Jonathan Tizzard said it showed investor confidence in the borough. Basildon Today reports that rents, whether any flats will be below market rent, and an opening date have yet to be announced, with interior fit-out still to come.

Key points

  • 492 one and two-bedroom rental flats
  • About 16,000 sq ft of shops and commercial space
  • Permission granted on appeal in 2021
  • Rents and opening date not yet announced
