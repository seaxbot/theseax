Basildon's 492-flat Mercantile scheme reaches full height in Market Square

The build-to-rent development that replaced the old Market Square shopping block has topped out, though rents and a move-in date are still to be announced.

Basildon · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Mercantile, a 492-flat build-to-rent development in Market Square in Basildon town centre, has reached its full height. Owner Starlight Investments announced the topping out on 4 November, a milestone for one of the largest new housing schemes in the town centre, close to Basildon station.

The scheme, built by Ernest Park and co-developed by Orwell Real Estate, has 492 one- and two-bedroom flats in three buildings, all held by a single landlord, with about 16,000 sq ft of shops and commercial space at street level. Residents are promised a gym, cinema, co-working space and a rooftop garden.

Key points

  • 492 one- and two-bedroom build-to-rent flats in three buildings
  • About 16,000 sq ft of shops and commercial space at street level
  • Approved on appeal in 2021 after the council missed its decision deadline
