# M25 delays ease after crash just past A127 junction near Upminster

**A collision at about 6.45pm held traffic heading for Thurrock. Queues had cleared by about 7.25pm.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: M25, Upminster, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 18:58 (UK time). Updated: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 19:27. Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- National Highways data put the first delays at 10 to 20 minutes
- The same stretch is due to shut overnight from 10pm for planned work

## The story

Delays on the M25 clockwise near Upminster had eased by about 7.25pm. National Highways' live traffic data no longer showed queues between junctions 28 and 30, about 40 minutes after a collision brought traffic to a stop.

All traffic was held on the clockwise side just after junction 29, where the M25 meets the A127, after the collision. Essex County Council's Essex Travel News reported it at 6.45pm and warned that delays were building.

National Highways' live traffic data showed queues on the clockwise side between junction 28, for the A12 at Brook Street, and junction 30 for the A13 at Thurrock, from 6.43pm. At first it put the delays at 10 to 20 minutes.

There is no word on whether anyone was hurt. Essex Police had not posted about the crash by 7.25pm.

Separately, National Highways had already planned to shut the M25 clockwise between junctions 29 and 30 overnight, from 10pm until 5.30am, for roadworks.
