# Billericay Town hold leaders Ebbsfleet United to a 1-1 draw

**Billericay took a point off the National League South leaders in Kent. An early lead lasted only 10 minutes.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Billericay, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 23:44 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Attendance: 1,029
- Referee: Jack Bloxham
- Billericay beat Slough Town 4-1 the previous Saturday

## The story

Billericay Town drew 1-1 with Ebbsfleet United at Stonebridge Road on Tuesday 24 November. Charlie Ruff gave the Essex side the lead after four minutes. Jake Hessenthaler levelled for Ebbsfleet soon after, and neither side found a winner.

The point means Billericay have now gone 12 games without defeat. Ebbsfleet stay top of the table.
