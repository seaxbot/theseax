# Brentwood Town lose 3-0 at home to Ramsgate in Isthmian Premier

**All three goals came before half-time at the Brentwood club's ground, and a second-half penalty save stopped the margin growing.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 23:08 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Prall saved a second-half spot kick from Paxman
- Ramsgate have now won five of their last seven league games
- Brentwood are away at Wingate & Finchley on Saturday 21 November

## The story

Brentwood Town lost 3-0 at home to Ramsgate at the Brentwood Arena on Saturday 14 November 2026 in the Isthmian League Premier Division, while Stanway Rovers drew 0-0 at Lewes. The defeat leaves Brentwood nineteenth on 16 points, and Stanway sit twenty-first on nine.

Ramsgate led inside two minutes when Raphe Brown headed in after Brentwood goalkeeper Aiden Prall had saved a Billy Munday header. Alfie Paxman struck from the edge of the area for the second, then set up Callum Jones for the third before the break. Prall kept the margin down by stopping a Paxman penalty shortly before the hour.

At The Dripping Pan, Stanway followed a goalless home draw with Whitehawk a week earlier with another shut-out, a result that lifted them off the bottom of the division. Brentwood travel to Burgess Hill Town on Tuesday 17 November, with a 19:45 kick-off, and Stanway host Burgess Hill on Saturday 21 November.
