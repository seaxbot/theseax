# Shrimpers lose five-goal table-top thriller

**Tyreece Simpson and debutant Rico Richards struck two minutes apart after the break, but the Shrimpers could not complete the comeback against the runaway leaders.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 22:15 (UK time). Updated: Monday 23 November 2026, 23:11. Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- A crowd of 9,985 was at Roots Hall
- Former Shrimper Zak Brunt scored Boreham Wood's third
- Six Southend players were booked

## The story

Southend United lost 3-2 to Boreham Wood, the runaway National League leaders, at Roots Hall on Friday 20 November. The Shrimpers fought back from three goals down but fell one short.

Boreham Wood were 3-0 up by the 33rd minute, with a former Southend player among the scorers. Oliver Bainbridge finished inside the near post on 19 minutes. Ben Whitfield converted at the far post 10 minutes later, and Regan Booty set up both. Ex-Shrimper Zak Brunt then slammed in from close range from Whitfield's pass.

Southend made three changes at half-time, sending on Maxwell McKnight, Tyreece Simpson and Anthony Hartigan for Leon Chambers-Parillon, Omari Patrick and Zavier Massiah-Edwards. Simpson headed in McKnight's cross on 65 minutes. Two minutes later Rico Richards fired in off the far post from Promise Omochere's pass. He was making his debut, having come on for Oliver Coker just before the hour.

Roots Hall roared for an equaliser through the remaining 23 minutes and four minutes of added time, but it never came. Southend were shown six yellow cards, to Omochere, Daniel Cox, Ben Goodliffe, Simpson, Hartigan and Darren Oldaker.

Head coach Kieron Dyer was back in the dugout after a one-match touchline ban for a game shown live on DAZN.

Boreham Wood's 15th league win of the season stretched their unbeaten start to 18 matches and moved them 13 points clear at the top of the National League.
