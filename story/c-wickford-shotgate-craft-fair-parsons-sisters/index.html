Wickford sisters' Shotgate craft fair raises money for cancer and pet charities

Cancer Research and the PDSA are the beneficiaries of the latest Shotgate Gift & Crafts Fair in Wickford.

Wickford, Shotgate · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:52 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rayleigh and Wickford MP Mark Francois went along to support the fair, which two Wickford sisters have run at the Shotgate Community Association hall for several years, Basildon Today reported on Wednesday 18 November. Stalls offered art, jewellery, cakes and homemade sweets.

Key points

  • Organised by sisters Mags and Kath Parsons
