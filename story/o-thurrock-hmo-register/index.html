Grays has 54 of Thurrock's 172 licensed shared houses, council register shows

The council's HMO register for 2021 to 2026 shows licensed houses in multiple occupation clustered in Grays, Tilbury and Stanford-le-Hope.

Grays, Tilbury & Stanford-le-Hope · Saturday 31 October 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock (Local Democracy Reporting)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock Council's register of licensed houses in multiple occupation, where tenants from different households share kitchens or bathrooms, lists 172 entries from 2021 to 2026. Grays accounts for 54 of them, far more than anywhere else in the borough.

Tilbury has 25 entries, Stanford-le-Hope 18, Corringham 13 and Purfleet-on-Thames 11, while Bulphan, Horndon-on-the-Hill, Linford and Stifford Clays have one each. Thirteen entries carry a re-licensing date that has already passed, though licences may have been renewed under new entries.

A council spokesperson said HMOs tend to cluster near transport links, amenities and jobs where there is steady demand for shared housing. The figures do not show how many people live in each property.

Key points

  • 172 licensed HMOs on the register
  • Grays 54, Tilbury 25, Stanford-le-Hope 18
  • 13 entries past their stated re-licensing date
  • Council links clusters to transport and jobs
