c2c trains halted between Leigh and Shoeburyness after second disruption of the day

Rail passengers in Southend faced a second round of disruption as c2c stopped trains between Leigh-on-Sea and Shoeburyness while emergency services dealt with an incident near Chalkwell.

Leigh-on-Sea, Chalkwell, Southend-on-Sea, Shoeburyness · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 10:34 · Updated Tuesday 17 November 2026, 13:38 · Reported by c2c
WhatsApp Facebook X

Disruption was expected to last until about 2.15pm on Tuesday 17 November, with c2c accepting tickets on other routes after stopping services from about midday, according to live updates from London Now.

Earlier that morning a train came to a halt in a neutral section of the line between Westcliff and Southend Central, holding Shoeburyness services at Chalkwell, Leigh-on-Sea and Southend Central, with knock-on delays across the network until about 11.30am.

Key points

  • Morning trains were slowed by speed restrictions near Pitsea
  • Delays of more than 15 minutes may qualify for compensation
  • Evening works divert Basildon line trains to Liverpool Street until Wednesday night
