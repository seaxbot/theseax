# ATM thief found hiding in Dagenham loft jailed after targeting cash machines in Essex and across Britain

**Bogdan Gasparov, 40, was sentenced at Chelmsford Crown Court on 9 November to three years and six months after admitting theft from cash machines.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Friday 13 November 2026, 20:45 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Targets included Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk and Cambridgeshire
- He pleaded guilty within 24 hours of his arrest

## The story

Gasparov fitted cash-trapping devices to at least 180 machines between May and October 2026, taking more than £16,000, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reports. Officers from the Eastern Region Special Operations Unit found him in a loft crawl space at a Dagenham address on 8 October, and he also admitted re-entering the UK after deportation.
