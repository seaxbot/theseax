# Chelmsford City knocked out of the FA Trophy on penalties at AFC Telford United

**The Clarets were beaten 6-5 in a shootout in Shropshire despite two saves from goalkeeper Marriott.**

## The story

Chelmsford City went out of the FA Trophy second round at AFC Telford United on Saturday 14 November 2026 after a 1-1 draw. Green put City ahead on 20 minutes with a 40-yard strike that went in off the post, before Cawthorne levelled for Telford on 35 minutes. A late Tompkins header was ruled out for a foul, and Crowther sent the decisive kick over the bar as Telford won 6-5 on penalties.
