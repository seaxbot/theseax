# Essex turns mild on Monday before frost returns on Thursday

**Temperatures across Essex climb to the low teens at the start of the week. A sharp drop follows, with frost possible by Thursday morning.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 09:44 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Environment.

## Key points

- No Met Office weather warnings for Essex
- Gusts up to 34mph in Colchester on Saturday
- Fog or mist in Chelmsford and Harlow on Sunday

## The story

Essex turns much milder from Monday 30 November. The Met Office forecasts daytime highs of 12C to 13C on Monday and Tuesday across the county. Night-time lows rise from 4C or 5C on Sunday to about 10C on Monday, a jump of five degrees or more.

## The weekend

Saturday brings light rain and a blustery wind. Gusts could reach 34mph in Colchester and 29mph in Chelmsford and Harlow, and highs get no further than 8C or 9C.

Sunday looks calmer and mostly dry. Fog or mist is forecast in Chelmsford and Harlow. Colchester should get some sunshine, and highs are 9C to 10C.

## Mild, then cold

Monday and Tuesday are mild but often grey. Light rain is forecast on Tuesday, when gusts of up to 33mph are possible. Wednesday is wet in places too, and colder air follows behind the rain. Wednesday's lows fall to between 2C and 4C.

Thursday 3 December is forecast to be sunny but cold. The Met Office expects a low of -1C in Chelmsford and 0C in Colchester, cold enough for frost in places. Harlow should stay a little warmer at 2C, and highs reach only about 8C. Chelmsford's forecast for Friday 4 December is similar, with a high of 7C and a low of 1C.

No Met Office weather warnings were in force for Essex when the forecasts were checked on Saturday morning.
