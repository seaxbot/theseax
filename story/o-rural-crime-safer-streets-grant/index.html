£57,721 grant gives Essex Police's rural team an all-terrain vehicle and mobile ANPR

The commissioner's Safer Streets money will pay for kit to tackle hare coursing, machinery theft, poaching and wildlife crime wherever rural hotspots emerge.

Burnham-on-Crouch, Dengie · Thursday 5 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Burnham & Dengie Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police's Rural Engagement Team has been awarded £57,721 from the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner's Safer Streets fund to step up its fight against crime in the countryside. The money will be used across rural Essex, including the Dengie, where police have recently been cracking down on hare coursing.

The grant will buy a John Deere Gator all-terrain vehicle for remote locations, mobile number plate recognition cameras for three vehicles, trail cameras and signs for hotspots, and training for officers to drive the vehicle.

Key points

  • £57,721 from the commissioner's Safer Streets fund
  • Pays for a John Deere Gator, mobile ANPR for three vehicles and trail cameras
  • Targets hare coursing, machinery theft, poaching and wildlife crime
