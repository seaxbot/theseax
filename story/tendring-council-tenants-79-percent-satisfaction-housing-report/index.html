Tendring council tenants rate housing service above national average in first annual report

Tendring District Council says nearly four in five of its tenants are happy with the service, and better repairs top its housing priorities for 2026/27.

Clacton-on-Sea · Friday 13 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The national average is 73%, so Tendring's 79% puts it six points ahead, according to the council's first Annual Housing Improvement, Performance and Assurance Report, presented to cabinet in Clacton-on-Sea on Friday 13 November 2026. The report covers the year 2025/26.

Staff carried out 724 tenancy checks and 24 estate walkabouts during the year, giving tenants the chance to raise problems face to face. Surveys of the condition of council homes now cover most of the housing stock, which the council says gives it a firmer basis for planning maintenance. Andy Baker, cabinet member for housing and planning, said: "We want tenants to see that their feedback makes a difference."

A three-year Housing Improvement and Delivery Plan now guides the work. Alongside repairs, the 2026/27 list includes faster action when problems arise, safe and well-kept homes, more help for tenants who need it and a bigger say for the Tenant Panel, whose chairwoman Carol McDougall said it lets tenants raise concerns and suggest improvements.

Key points

  • 724 tenancy checks and 24 estate walkabouts in 2025/26
  • Stock condition surveys now cover most council homes
  • Tenant Panel chaired by Carol McDougall helped review policies
