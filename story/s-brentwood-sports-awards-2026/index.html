Elliott Smith named Brentwood Sports Personality of the Year

Brentwood Central Boxing Club was named Community Club of the Year at a joint awards night with Rochford, with Graham Gooch helping to present.

Brentwood · Saturday 7 November 2026, 07:00 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

Elliott Smith has been named Sports Personality of the Year at the 2026 Brentwood Sports Awards, which celebrated athletes, coaches and volunteers from across the borough. The winners were announced on Tuesday 3 November at a joint ceremony with Rochford at the Mill Arts and Events Centre in Rayleigh.

Brentwood Central Boxing Club took Community Club of the Year, Isabelle Curtis-Rich was Young Sports Personality and Alan Packman was named Coach of the Year.

Key points

  • Sports Personality: Elliott Smith
  • Community Club: Brentwood Central Boxing Club
  • Coach of the Year: Alan Packman
