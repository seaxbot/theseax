Braintree cat Zeus takes 37-mile solo train trip to Liverpool Street

Braintree · Monday 23 November 2026, 07:18 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Zeus, a half-British shorthair, boarded the 8.08pm service from Braintree on Tuesday 10 November, the BBC reports. Staff at Liverpool Street took him to their office, and Greater Anglia staff then travelled back to Braintree with him. His owner, Lily Burroughs, said her first thought was 'oh my God, he's done it again'. Zeus wears two trackers and used to wander from the family's old home in Stansted Mountfitchet to London Stansted Airport.
