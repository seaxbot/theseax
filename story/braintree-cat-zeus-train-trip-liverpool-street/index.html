Braintree cat Zeus takes 37-mile solo train trip to Liverpool Street

Braintree · Monday 23 November 2026, 07:18 · First reported by BBC News · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Braintree cat got on an evening train by himself. Station staff in London spotted him and sent him home.

Zeus, a half-British shorthair, got on the 8.08pm Greater Anglia service from Braintree on Tuesday 10 November, BBC News reported. He made the whole journey to London Liverpool Street alone.

Staff at Liverpool Street spotted him and took him to their office. Greater Anglia staff then travelled back to Braintree with him. His owner, Lily Burroughs, told the BBC her first thought was 'oh my God, he's done it again'.

A cat with a travel habit

It is not his first trip. Zeus first made headlines in summer 2025, when he boarded a Stansted Express train to the airport, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reported in June. The family then lived in Water Lane, Stansted Mountfitchet. He was a familiar face on the village station platform and in nearby shops and pubs.

In April the family moved to a home about five minutes from Braintree station. Zeus soon made himself at home there too, lounging on the platform steps and in the waiting room and getting fuss from commuters. He is microchipped, wears trackers and has his own Instagram account.

Key points

  • Boarded the 8.08pm from Braintree on 10 November
  • Greater Anglia staff took him home
  • First rode a Stansted Express to the airport in 2025
