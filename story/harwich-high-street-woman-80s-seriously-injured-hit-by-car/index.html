Woman in her 80s seriously hurt after being hit by a car on Harwich High Street

Essex Police are asking for dashcam and CCTV footage after a pedestrian was struck near the Park Road junction in Harwich on Saturday 14 November.

Harwich · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 10:30 · Reported by Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

The woman suffered serious injuries to her ankles when a white Ford Fiesta hit her shortly after 3pm on Saturday 14 November, and she was taken to hospital, Hits Radio Essex reports. No arrests have been made. Police want to hear from anyone with information or footage, quoting incident 775 of 14 November.

Key points

  • No arrests have been made
  • Reports can go to Essex Police online or Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111
