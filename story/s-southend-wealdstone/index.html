Late Wealdstone winner sinks Southend

Southend United led twice in Ruislip through Leon Chambers-Parillon, only for Wealdstone to fight back and snatch a stoppage-time winner.

Ruislip · Saturday 31 October 2026, 17:00 · Reported by ESPN
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend United were beaten 3-2 by Wealdstone in the National League on Saturday after Olufela Olomola scored deep into stoppage time. It was a painful afternoon for the Shrimpers, who twice held the lead in Ruislip but left with nothing.

Leon Chambers-Parillon put Southend ahead inside five minutes when the ball broke loose in the box, but Fejiri Okenabirhie levelled from the spot on 24 minutes after Dylan Kadji was fouled. Chambers-Parillon restored the advantage with a strike from outside the area on 39 minutes. Luke Norris headed Wealdstone level just after the hour before Olomola settled it in the 94th minute.

Kieron Dyer's side went into the game third in the table on 29 points from 16 matches, with Boreham Wood top on 44. Wealdstone started the day 18th on 18 points, so the defeat was a setback for Southend's push near the top of the division.

Key points

  • Chambers-Parillon struck on 5 and 39 minutes for Southend
  • Wealdstone levelled through an Okenabirhie penalty and a Norris header
  • Olomola won it four minutes into added time
  • Southend began the day third in the National League
