# Essex fixtures: Colchester host Gillingham on Saturday 14 November

**Southend United face Aldershot Town, Billericay Town and Waltham Abbey meet in an all-Essex FA Trophy tie, and there are two derbies in the Isthmian North.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Essex, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:32 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Chelmsford City face a long trip to Shropshire in the FA Trophy
- Westcliff's rugby game against Hertford starts at 2pm
- Colchester and Southend have both posted match previews on their club websites

## The story

Colchester United and Southend United are both at home on Saturday 14 November 2026, with 3pm kick-offs. Colchester play Gillingham in League Two at the JobServe Community Stadium, and Southend take on Aldershot Town in the National League at Roots Hall.

Fans going to Roots Hall should know that Essex Police brought in a dispersal order across central Southend from 9am. The city's Christmas lights switch-on is also today, so the centre is expected to be busy.

Five Essex clubs are in the FA Trophy second round, with kick-off times still to be confirmed. Chelmsford City travel to AFC Telford United and Braintree Town host Kettering Town. Aveley go to Walton & Hersham. Billericay Town's home tie with Waltham Abbey guarantees one Essex side a place in the next round.

In the Isthmian Premier Division, Brentwood Town host Ramsgate at the Brentwood Centre Arena and Stanway Rovers make the trip to Lewes.

The Isthmian North Division derbies are Concord Rangers against Bowers & Pitsea at Thames Road on Canvey Island, and Witham Town against Canvey Island at Spa Road. Grays Athletic host Gorleston and Buckhurst Hill welcome AFC Sudbury, while Takeley, Tilbury and Brightlingsea Regent are away at Fakenham Town, Felixstowe & Walton United and Wroxham. All kick off at 3pm.

In rugby union, Westcliff host Hertford in Regional 1 South East and Harlow are away at Woodford. On Sunday evening, Chelmsford Chieftains play Slough Jets in the ice hockey Challenge Cup at Riverside Ice and Leisure in Chelmsford, starting at 6pm.
