Chelmsford City captain Cav Miley falls down badger hole in training

Chelmsford · Monday 23 November 2026, 13:24 · First reported by Chelmsford City FC · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Manager Mark Cooper said the fall affected Miley's game as the Clarets let a two-goal lead slip at Walton & Hersham.

Chelmsford City captain Cav Miley played through Saturday's 2-2 draw at Walton & Hersham after falling down a badger hole in training, manager Mark Cooper has revealed.

Cooper said in his post-match interview that the mishap had affected the midfielder's performance. Miley still played the full game in Surrey on Saturday 21 November.

It was an afternoon of two halves for the Clarets. Jack Barham put them ahead on 27 minutes, slotting under goalkeeper Dillon Addai after Ash Clarke and Cam Green combined, and doubled the lead three minutes before the break from Clarke's outside-of-the-boot pass. They were his sixth and seventh league goals of the season.

Walton & Hersham hit back late. Makise Evans pulled one back on 80 minutes, and substitute Kleyton Dowglas De Biazze Junior equalised three minutes later. A crowd of 576 watched the National League South game, which came a week after City's FA Trophy exit at AFC Telford United.

Miley, 31, joined Chelmsford from Southend United in the summer on a two-year deal. He was Southend's player of the year in his first season, when he did not miss a game, and won the FA Trophy at Wembley in his last game for the Shrimpers. He began his career at Jersey side St Pauls while working as an electrician, before turning professional with Eastleigh at 22.

Key points

  • Jack Barham scored twice before half-time on Saturday
  • The hosts hit back with two goals in three minutes
  • Miley won the FA Trophy at Wembley in his last game for Southend
