Parking at Princess Alexandra Hospital in Harlow to cost more from 17 November

The trust says the rise is a small inflation-linked increase, with the first 20 minutes staying free and existing exemptions kept.

Harlow · Saturday 7 November 2026, 07:49 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Patients and visitors parking at Princess Alexandra Hospital in Harlow will pay slightly more from Tuesday 17 November, the hospital trust has announced. The trust describes the change as a small inflationary increase designed to keep charges in step with the cost of running and maintaining its car parks.

The rise is based on the April 2026 Consumer Prices Index rate of 2.6%, rounded to the nearest 10p. A stay of up to one hour goes from £3.50 to £3.60, up to two hours from £5 to £5.20, and 12 to 24 hours from £15 to £15.40. Chief People Officer Catrin Asbrey said the trust had kept the increase as small as possible.

The first 20 minutes remain free, and free parking continues for Blue Badge holders, parents and guardians staying overnight with young patients, frequent outpatient attendees and others with particular needs. The trust says regular small rises avoid a bigger jump later, following a 2025 review that brought in shorter tariff bands.

Key points

  • New charges start on Tuesday 17 November
  • Up to one hour rises from £3.50 to £3.60
  • First 20 minutes stay free
  • Blue Badge and other exemptions continue
