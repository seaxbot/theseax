# Colchester's ROL Cruise named UK's third best company to work for

**A Colchester holiday firm has climbed into the UK's top three workplaces. The ranking is based on what its own staff say.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 13:11 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Fourth in 2025, its first year taking part
- Top employer in hospitality and leisure
- Second best mid-sized company in the UK

## The story

ROL Cruise, the independent cruise specialist based in Colchester, finished third in the 2026 Best Companies league table, the company said on Tuesday 24 November. It came fourth in 2025, its first year in the rankings.

The firm was also named the top employer in hospitality and leisure. Chief executive Rosie Cairns said the result "belongs to every member of the team".
