NHS Essex looks for new online consultation tools for 204 GP surgeries

Suppliers can show their systems at an online event on 9 December, and a two-year contract is due to start in spring 2027.

Essex · Friday 20 November 2026, 13:42 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 16:04 · Reported by PublicTechnology
WhatsApp Facebook X

NHS Essex is looking for new software to handle online consultations at the 204 GP surgeries it covers, and has asked suppliers to show what their systems can do before it decides what to buy.

The integrated care board, which plans and pays for NHS services for about 1.9 million people in Essex, wants a contract lasting two years, PublicTechnology reported on Friday 20 November. The notice does not say how much it is worth.

Online consultations let patients contact their surgery through its website, to describe a problem or ask for advice, and the request goes straight to the practice. At the moment, Essex surgeries use a range of different systems, chosen to suit local needs, the notice says.

The board says practices with similar needs could be grouped together when it buys the new service, so not every surgery would necessarily end up with the same system. Nothing has been decided yet: not what it needs, how it will buy it or which company will win.

This first step is what the Procurement Act 2023 calls preliminary market engagement. Suppliers can demonstrate their systems at an online event on 9 December, where GP practices and the board can ask questions, and must register by 3 December. Taking part gives a company no advantage when the contract is later put out to tender, the notice says.

The plans come weeks after a problem with a website provider left some GP practice websites and online consultation services in north east Essex unavailable. NHS Essex said on 28 October that practices remained open, and pointed patients to the NHS App, Pharmacy First, phoning or emailing their practice, NHS 111, and 999 in a life-threatening emergency.

Any change to the systems patients use would not come before spring 2027, when the new contract is due to begin.

Key points

  • Supplier registration closes on 3 December
  • NHS Essex serves about 1.9 million people
  • No decision yet on the system or supplier

What happens next

  • Wednesday 9 December: NHS Essex holds an online event for suppliers of GP online consultation tools; registration closes on 3 December
