# Basildon neighbour rescues sleeping woman after hearing smoke alarm

**A woman who fell asleep while food was in the oven was helped out of her Basildon maisonette by a neighbour on 19 November.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 07:42 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Crews checked with a thermal imaging camera
- Free home safety visits: 0300 303 0088

## The story

The neighbour heard the alarm at about 11.05pm on Thursday 19 November, went to look and saw smoke, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. They woke the woman, got her to safety and switched off the oven.

Firefighters cleared the smoke from the maisonette and used a thermal imaging camera to check that the fire had not spread beyond the oven. They left at 11.40pm.

Essex County Fire and Rescue Service offers free home safety visits and fits free smoke alarms. Visits can be booked on 0300 303 0088.
