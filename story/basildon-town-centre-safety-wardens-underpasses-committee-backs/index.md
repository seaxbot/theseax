# Basildon town centre safety wardens and underpass clean-up get go-ahead

**Wardens would patrol five days a week from early 2027, alongside brighter lighting and deep-cleaned underpasses.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Basildon town centre, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 17:31 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Backed by councillors on 24 November
- Contractor due by February 2027
- Funded for two years

## The story

Plans to make Basildon town centre feel safer, with patrolling wardens, brighter lighting and cleaner underpasses, have won the backing of councillors.

Basildon Council's Regeneration Scrutiny Committee supported the package on Tuesday 24 November, the council said. The Seax reported the plans on 17 November, ahead of the meeting.

A Community Safety Warden Scheme is being brought back, with patrols five days a week. The wardens will work alongside police, businesses and community groups to tackle anti-social behaviour and help enforce the town centre's Public Spaces Protection Order.

Funding is secured for the first two years. The council aims to have a contractor in place by February 2027, subject to procurement.

The rest of the work includes clearing overgrown plants, deep cleaning the underpasses, improving lighting and removing barriers that are no longer needed. The council says better routes should encourage people to visit, stay longer and spend money in local businesses.

The wardens will add to the 63 CCTV cameras that already cover the town centre and bus station.

The money comes from Section 106 payments made by developers, as The Seax reported earlier. Keeping the wardens after the two funded years would need new money.
