# St Clare Hospice holds seven Light Up a Life services across west Essex

**Anyone can dedicate a star to a loved one, whether or not the hospice cared for them.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Loughton, Dunmow, Stansted Mountfitchet, Saffron Walden, Waltham Abbey, Hatfield Heath, Ongar, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 08:06 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Services run from 28 November to 15 December
- Stars can be dedicated at stclarehospice.org.uk/light-up-a-life
