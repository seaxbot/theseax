Halstead father paralysed in hospital now helping Colchester Hospital improve spinal injury care

David Bateman says he was left housebound after waking paralysed from the chest down, and is now working with the hospital's trust so other patients are pointed to specialist support.

Halstead & Colchester · Thursday 5 November 2026, 06:23 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News UK)
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Halstead man who woke up paralysed from the chest down while in hospital for cancer treatment says he was left feeling trapped in his own home, the BBC reports. David Bateman, 53, is now working with East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust, which runs Colchester Hospital, to improve the help given to patients with spinal cord injuries.

The father-of-two was diagnosed in 2017 with a tumour growing inside his spinal cord and lost the use of his lower body in March 2023.

Key points

  • David Bateman, 53, woke paralysed from the chest down in March 2023
  • He was not referred to a specialist spinal unit
  • Colchester Hospital teams are now made aware of the Spinal Injuries Association
