Nearly 55,000 sign petition telling Google to 'search elsewhere' for Arena Essex data centre

Wildlife groups took the petition to Google's London headquarters, saying plans for the former Purfleet raceway would wipe out most of a protected habitat.

Purfleet, Thurrock · Thursday 5 November 2026, 14:06 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Campaigners led by Buglife have taken a petition of almost 55,000 names to Google's London headquarters, urging the tech giant to drop plans for a data centre on the former Arena Essex Raceway near Purfleet. The 52-hectare site has been a designated Local Wildlife Site since 2006, and the campaign has become one of Thurrock's most widely backed environmental fights.

The plans include up to four data centre buildings totalling 130,500 square metres, plus a separate office building. Campaigners say the site supports hundreds of species, including rare bees, butterflies, nightingales and the endangered broad-leaved cudweed, and claim more than 80 per cent of its protected habitats would be lost. Buglife programmes manager Jamie Robins called it the wrong place for a data centre, while Essex Wildlife Trust's Emily Buttress warned the loss would be devastating for the area. Campaigners also say Google refused to accept the petition, which the company did not confirm or deny.

Google said data centres are vital infrastructure and that it shares the view that any development must be sustainable. It says it wants to regenerate the site, bring investment and jobs to Thurrock, and is working with Natural England to guarantee a 10 per cent biodiversity net gain, with public open space and retained habitat. Campaigners want the land opened as green space and the return of Thurrock Hammers speedway.

Key points

  • Petition stood at 54,970 signatures when handed over
  • Site is a 52-hectare Local Wildlife Site near Purfleet
  • Plans: up to four buildings totalling 130,500 sq m
  • Google promises 10% biodiversity net gain
