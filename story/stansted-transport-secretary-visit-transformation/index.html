Heidi Alexander tours Stansted's bigger security hall and new solar farm on Transport Secretary visit

The Transport Secretary was shown the airport's five-year transformation, including 17 new security scanners and skylink walkways under construction.

Stansted · Sunday 8 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander visited London Stansted last week to see progress on the airport's five-year transformation programme, Your Harlow reports. She toured the enlarged security hall, where 17 new scanners let passengers keep liquids and large electricals in their bags, and finished at the on-site solar farm that began operating in September. The centrepiece of the programme is a three-bay terminal extension, and the airport says it can grow to 51 million passengers a year without a new runway.

Key points

  • Security hall now has 17 new scanners
  • Solar farm on site began operating in September
  • Airport says it can reach 51m passengers without a new runway
