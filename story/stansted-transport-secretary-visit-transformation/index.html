Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander tours Stansted's terminal extension work and new solar farm

The minister saw the enlarged security hall, the Skylink walkways under construction and the airport's on-site solar farm during a visit to the Essex airport.

Stansted · Friday 6 November 2026, 11:38 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander has visited London Stansted to see progress on its five-year transformation programme, which includes a three-bay terminal extension adding 16,500 square metres of space for shops, bars and restaurants. The airport, which handled more than 30 million passengers in 2025, says it has planning permission to grow to 51 million passengers a year on its existing runway.

She was briefed on the Skylink walkways that will link the terminal to the satellite buildings and allow the track transit system to be taken out of use, toured the enlarged security hall with its 17 new scanners, and finished at the on-site solar farm that began operating in September. Ms Alexander said the government supports airport expansion where it balances growth with climate, noise and air quality duties.

Managing director Gareth Powell said the visit showed the momentum behind the airport's plans, in a year of record passenger numbers and new airlines. The airport says it is aiming for net zero carbon emissions from its direct operations by 2038 at the latest.

Key points

  • Three-bay terminal extension adds 16,500 sq m
  • Skylink walkways will replace the track transit system
  • Solar farm operating since September
  • Permission for up to 51 million passengers a year
