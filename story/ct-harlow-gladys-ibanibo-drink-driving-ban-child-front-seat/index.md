# Harlow driver banned for 40 months after drink-driving with child in front

**A Harlow woman has lost her licence for drink-driving. A toddler was sitting unrestrained in her front passenger seat.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 18:35 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Offences at Bush Fair on 14 July
- Breath reading of 45 micrograms
- Also fined £120

## The story

Gladys Ibanibo, 44, of Rundells, Harlow, was banned from driving for three years and four months and fined £120 at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court, Your Harlow reported on Friday 27 November.

She admitted driving a Nissan Qashqai at Bush Fair on 14 July with 45 micrograms of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath. She also admitted carrying a child under three in the front passenger seat with no child seat, the belt simply across the child's body.
