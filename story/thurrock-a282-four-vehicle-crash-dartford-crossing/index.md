# Two taken to hospital after A282 crash shuts QEII Bridge for hours

**Both were taken by road to the Royal London Hospital, and more closures are planned at the Dartford Crossing tonight and on Saturday.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Thurrock, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 13:25 (UK time). Updated: Friday 20 November 2026, 16:23. Section: News. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- AA reported delays of about 20 minutes on the bridge this afternoon
- West tunnel closed 9pm to 5.30am tonight
- QEII Bridge shut 10.30pm Saturday to 5.30am Sunday

## The story

A crash on the clockwise A282 in Thurrock shut the QEII Bridge at the Dartford Crossing for around three hours on Friday 20 November, and two people were taken to hospital, Brentwood Live reported.

The East of England Ambulance Service said it was called at 10.30am to a collision on the M25 near junction 31, on the approach to the bridge. It sent two ambulances, an ambulance officer, a rapid response vehicle and Essex & Herts Air Ambulance, and two patients were taken by road to the Royal London Hospital.

Firefighters freed the two people, who were trapped in their vehicles, and handed them to paramedics, the Echo reported. Essex Police said four vehicles were involved; the Echo reported that they included two lorries and a car, and that the air ambulance landed on a grass verge near junction 30.

Queues built up across south Essex. At about 12.15pm, National Highways' overhead signs showed the M25 closed at junction 30 and delays of more than an hour between junctions 29 and 1A. By the afternoon, the AA was reporting delays of about 20 minutes on the bridge and about 29 minutes for traffic heading to junction 30 from junction 27, according to Brentwood Live.

More closures are planned at the crossing. The west tunnel, which carries northbound traffic, is due to close from 9pm tonight until 5.30am on Saturday, according to National Highways' roadworks data. National Highways has also confirmed the QEII Bridge will close from 10.30pm on Saturday until 5.30am on Sunday, London Now reported.

The condition of the two people taken to hospital has not been released. This story will be updated.
