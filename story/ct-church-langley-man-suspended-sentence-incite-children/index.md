# Church Langley man, 51, gets suspended term over attempts to incite children

**A Harlow man was given a 20-month prison sentence, suspended for two years, at Chelmsford Crown Court for offences against children under 16 in Harlow in 2025.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Church Langley, Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 17:54 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Offences committed in Harlow between 29 September and 1 October 2025
- 150 hours of unpaid work
- Sexual Harm Prevention Order for 10 years

## The story

Christopher Sayer, 51, of Westbury Rise, Church Langley, was sentenced at Chelmsford Crown Court on Monday 16 November for attempting to incite a child under 16 into sexual activity and two counts of attempting sexual communication with a child, Your Harlow reports. The offences took place in Harlow between 29 September and 1 October 2025.

As well as the suspended sentence, he must do 150 hours of unpaid work and will be subject to sex offender notification requirements and a Sexual Harm Prevention Order, each for 10 years.
