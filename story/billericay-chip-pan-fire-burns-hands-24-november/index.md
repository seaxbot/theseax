# Billericay chip pan fire leaves resident with burned hands

**A Billericay resident was hurt in a kitchen fire. Firefighters say never move a burning pan.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Billericay, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 21:29 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Called out at 7.01pm
- Smoke alarm alerted the household
- Crews checked neighbours' alarms

## The story

Firefighters were called to a kitchen fire in Billericay at 7.01pm on Tuesday 24 November, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. Crews arrived to find the kitchen full of smoke and damaged by a chip pan fire.

The household had cooked chips in a saucepan of oil and left the hob switched on afterwards. Their smoke alarm went off when the pan began to smoke. One of them then picked up the pan, put it in the sink and turned on the tap. The burning oil flared up and burned their hands. The fire service has not said whether they needed hospital treatment.

Once the scene and the people inside were safe, crews knocked on neighbouring doors to reassure residents and to check that their smoke alarms worked. The service has logged it as incident 349633 and says the fire is now out.

Its advice is simple. Never leave a pan of cooking oil unattended, and turn off the hob as soon as you finish. If oil does catch fire, never move the pan and never pour water on it. Turn off the heat only if it is safe to do so, then leave the room, close the door and call 999.
