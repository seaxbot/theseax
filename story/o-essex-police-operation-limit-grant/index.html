Essex Police wins £30,000 to test how its drink and drug-drive crackdown deters offenders

The Road Safety Trust is funding a 12-month study of Operation Limit, the force's drink and drug-driving enforcement campaign.

Chelmsford · Monday 2 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police is one of four forces sharing £115,000 from The Road Safety Trust for policing research. Its £30,000 grant will pay for a year-long evaluation of Operation Limit, the force's drink and drug-driving enforcement operation.

The study is meant to look beyond roadside tests, arrests and charges to how enforcement actually deters people from offending and cuts harm on the roads. Staffordshire, Hampshire and Isle of Wight, and Lincolnshire forces were also funded, taking the trust's total awards to police to nearly £700,000.

The trust's chief executive, Ruth Purdie, said policing was fundamental to road safety. A second funding round has opened, with forces able to bid for projects worth up to £30,000.

Key points

  • £30,000 for Essex Police out of £115,000 shared by four forces
  • 12-month evaluation of Operation Limit
  • Focus on deterrence, not just arrests and charges
  • New funding round now open
