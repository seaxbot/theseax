# Harlow vigil reads out names of women killed this year

**People gathered in Harlow town centre to remember women killed across the UK in 2026.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 07:49 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Held on the UN day against violence towards women

## The story

A vigil in Harlow town centre on the evening of Wednesday 25 November marked the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. Your Harlow reported that the names of women killed this year were read aloud.
