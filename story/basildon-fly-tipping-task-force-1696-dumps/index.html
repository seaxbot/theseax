Basildon Council forms fly-tipping task force after clearing 1,696 dumps in six months

Hotspots across Basildon borough will be targeted first, with surveillance, intelligence gathering and faster clean-ups, Basildon Today reports.

Basildon · Friday 13 November 2026, 09:54 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

The council announced the task force on Wednesday 11 November as part of its "We're On Your Side" campaign. Over six months it prosecuted 23 people and businesses and issued 14 fixed penalty notices, and it will review whether its fines deter offenders.

Cabinet member for law and order Cllr Andrew Baggott said the council would act against illegal dumping wherever it could. Monthly enforcement updates will go on the council's website.

Key points

  • 70% of reports are cleared within three working days
  • 953 abandoned vehicles inspected over the same period
