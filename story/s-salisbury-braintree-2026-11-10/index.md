# Braintree Town beaten 2-0 at Salisbury despite facing ten men after half-time

**The Braintree side had an extra man for the whole second half in Wiltshire but could not find a goal in the National League South.**

## Key points

- Salisbury scorers: Dabre 20, Odokonyero 63
- Braintree's Alfie Matthews and Alexandru-Leonard Bolovan were booked
- The Iron used five substitutes, including Amine Sassi and Afi Adebayo

## The story

Braintree Town lost 2-0 away to Salisbury in the National League South on Tuesday 10 November 2026 at the Raymond McEnhill Stadium.

Mo Dabre put the hosts ahead on 20 minutes before Malachi Ogunleye was sent off in first-half stoppage time. Nathan Odokonyero settled it on 63 minutes, three minutes after coming off the bench.
