Walsall end Colchester's unbeaten home league run

Colchester United lost 3-1 to Walsall in League Two, their first home league defeat of the campaign.

Colchester · Saturday 31 October 2026, 17:30 · Reported by Gazette
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United suffered their first home league defeat of the League Two season on Saturday, beaten 3-1 by Walsall at the JobServe Community Stadium. The visitors were ahead inside three minutes and pulled away late on.

Declan Skura scored from close range after a corner in the third minute. Colchester thought Sean Raggett had headed an equaliser, but it was ruled out for a foul in the build-up, before Jack Payne levelled from the penalty spot in the 35th minute after Moses Sesay was fouled. Walsall keeper Jed Ward made several saves, Pressley headed the visitors back in front from Mason Hancock's cross in the 79th minute, and Clarke added a third in stoppage time.

The result ended Colchester's unbeaten home league record for the campaign. Walsall's supporters' site Bescot Banter described a much-improved second half from the visitors, who were more clinical in front of goal.

Key points

  • Skura put Walsall ahead after three minutes
  • Raggett header ruled out for a foul
  • Payne penalty levelled it on 35 minutes
  • Pressley and Clarke scored late for Walsall
