National Highways asks Thurrock to hold off on 200-home Grey Goose Farm scheme in Grays until February as residents object

Persimmon wants outline permission for homes north of Long Lane, but the roads agency says more modelling is needed on the busy North Stifford junction first.

Grays · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 13:13 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Persimmon Homes Essex has asked Thurrock Council for outline permission to build up to 200 homes at Grey Goose Farm, north of Long Lane in Grays, half of them shared ownership or below-market rent. Sixteen of the 17 public comments lodged so far object, citing traffic, stretched GP and dental services and lost countryside.

National Highways wants no approval before 3 February 2027 while it models the A13/A1012 North Stifford junction. Persimmon says the site is a logical extension of the town.

Key points

  • Up to 200 homes, half shared ownership or below-market rent
  • 16 of 17 public comments are objections
  • National Highways wants no approval before 3 February 2027
