# Clacton woman admits 14 shop thefts including Christmas selection boxes

**Tayla Sabaroche, 27, took more than £2,100 of goods from shops in Clacton and Walton. She will be sentenced in February.**

## The story

A Tendring woman has admitted stealing more than £2,100 of goods from shops in Clacton and Walton, Essex Police said.

Tayla Sabaroche, 27, of no fixed address, admitted 14 counts of shop theft before Basildon magistrates on Monday 23 November. She also admitted making off without paying for petrol at a garage in Manningtree.

The court heard she stole from Asda, Boots, Iceland, Morrisons and Tesco in Clacton, and the Co-op in Walton, between 5 September and 18 November. The goods included sweets, chocolates, toys, clothes and alcohol.

An officer from Clacton Local Policing Team arrested her in Kings Parade, Holland-on-Sea, on Sunday 22 November. She is due to be sentenced at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court on 15 February 2027.
