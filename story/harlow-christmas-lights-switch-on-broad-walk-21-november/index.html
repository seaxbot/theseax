Harlow to switch on bigger Christmas lights display on Broad Walk

Harlow · Friday 20 November 2026, 09:39 · Reported by Harlow Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The switch-on is on Broad Walk at 4pm on Saturday 21 November, with live entertainment, festive music and food and drink stalls. It opens Harlow Council's Christmas Extravaganza programme for the town.

Harlow on Ice, the town's seasonal skating rink, opens on the same day. A Christmas market follows from Saturday 12 December to Sunday 20 December, open 10am to 6pm daily, with stalls along Broad Walk and in Market Square.

The council has not said who will switch on the lights or whether any roads will close. Details of the wider programme are on its Christmas in Harlow website.

Key points

  • Event starts at 4pm
  • Christmas market open 10am to 6pm daily
  • Run by Harlow Council
