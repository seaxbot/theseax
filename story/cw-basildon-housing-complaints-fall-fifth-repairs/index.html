Complaints from Basildon council tenants fell by nearly a fifth last year, but most are still about repairs

Basildon Council's Housing Services received 759 first-stage complaints in 2025/26, 19.5% fewer than the year before, according to a report for the Housing Scrutiny Committee on Wednesday 25 November 2026.

Basildon · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 06:20 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Council's Housing Scrutiny Committee will review the council's annual housing complaints report for 2025/26 at its meeting on Wednesday 25 November 2026. The report, from the Portfolio Holder for Housing, says Housing Services received 759 Stage 1 complaints during the year, a fall of 19.5% on the previous year.

The report says 95.7% of Stage 1 complaints and 94.0% of Stage 2 complaints were answered within the Housing Ombudsman's published timescales. Most complaints still relate to housing repairs and property services, and the council lists repairs and maintenance, complaint handling quality, tenant engagement, staff training and new systems among its areas of focus. Social landlords must report annually against the Housing Ombudsman's Complaint Handling Code, and the report notes that failing to comply can lead to a Complaint Handling Failure Order.

The council submitted its annual return to the Housing Ombudsman Service on 29 September 2026, and the report says the ombudsman confirmed the documents had been received and published. Councillors will also see the council's self-assessment against the code and the governing body's response. The same meeting is due to consider a stock condition report and the housing performance report for July to September 2026.

Key points

  • 759 Stage 1 housing complaints in 2025/26, down 19.5%
  • 95.7% of Stage 1 and 94.0% of Stage 2 complaints answered within ombudsman timescales
  • Most complaints concern repairs and property services
  • Annual return sent to the Housing Ombudsman on 29 September 2026
