# Southend signs 12-month contract for enviro-crime crackdown officers

**Extra enforcement officers will target dog fouling and littering in Southend town centre.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 13:49 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- It is the first phase of the council's Enviro-Crime Crackdown Taskforce
- The council set up its EnviroCrime unit in March 2025
