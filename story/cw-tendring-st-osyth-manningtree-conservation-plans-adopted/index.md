# Tendring adopts St Osyth and Manningtree conservation area plans

**Tendring District Council's Cabinet has adopted new appraisals and management plans for the St Osyth and Manningtree & Mistley conservation areas, including revised boundaries where applicable. Planning decisions in both areas must now take them into account.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Manningtree, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- Cabinet adopted the St Osyth and Manningtree & Mistley plans on 13 November 2026
- Revised conservation area boundaries are included where applicable
- Public consultations on the plans ran between 2022 and 2026
- All 20 conservation areas in Tendring now have updated appraisals

## The story

Tendring District Council's Cabinet adopted new character appraisals and management plans for the St Osyth and Manningtree & Mistley conservation areas on Friday 13 November 2026. The decision includes revised conservation area boundaries where applicable. Cabinet also noted the results of the public consultations on the two documents.

The appraisals assess what makes each area special and set out ways to protect and improve it, councillors were told. Once adopted, they become material planning considerations, so they will help guide planning decisions in both areas. The documents were shaped by a series of public consultations held between 2022 and 2026, and feedback led to changes to the documents and, where appropriate, to the boundaries.

These are the last two of the district's 20 conservation areas to get updated plans, which the council's Tendring Heritage Strategy (2020) made a key priority. The Portfolio Holder for Housing & Planning, Councillor Baker, said all twenty had been approved and adopted since July 2024. Five of the district's conservation areas are on Historic England's Heritage at Risk Register, the minutes say.
