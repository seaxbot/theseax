# Essex football fixtures: Colchester go to Tranmere on Saturday

**Three Isthmian North Division games are all-Essex derbies, while Billericay Town and Chelmsford City face the two bottom clubs in the National League South.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Essex, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 07:11 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Braintree beat Torquay 1-0 when the clubs last met at Cressing Road in February 2024
- Chelmsford City are back at home to Maidstone United on Monday night
- Brentwood Town and Bowers & Pitsea meet in the League Cup on Tuesday

## The story

Colchester United lead the Essex fixtures on Saturday 21 November 2026 with a League Two trip to Tranmere Rovers at Prenton Park, kick-off 3pm. Southend United are not in action after losing 3-2 at home to National League leaders Boreham Wood on Friday 20 November.

Colchester defender Ellis Iandolo had stitches in an ear injury and could wear headgear, and Sean Raggett has been kept from heading the ball in training. Danny Cowley hoped both would be available. Striker Kylian Kouassi is still out with a hamstring injury.

In the National League South, sixth-placed Billericay Town host bottom club Slough Town, and Chelmsford City travel to Walton & Hersham, who are 23rd. Braintree Town welcome Torquay United to Cressing Road. Torquay's preview says Braintree's Ben Isaacson is an injury concern after being forced off with a collision, while Torquay are without Tegan Finn. All three games kick off at 3pm.

In the Isthmian Premier Division, Aveley go to Carshalton Athletic, Maldon & Tiptree visit Chatham Town and Brentwood Town are away at Wingate & Finchley. Stanway Rovers host Burgess Hill Town.

The Isthmian North Division has three Essex derbies: Brightlingsea Regent against Grays Athletic, Canvey Island against Buckhurst Hill, and Takeley against Concord Rangers. Bowers & Pitsea host Felixstowe & Walton United, Little Oakley play Hashtag United, Tilbury take on Wroxham and Witham Town travel to AFC Sudbury. Every Isthmian game kicks off at 3pm.
