# Colchester United lose 2-1 at Tranmere to 90th-minute winner

**Dylan Jones struck at the death at Prenton Park after Sean Raggett had cancelled out Tom Ince's opener for the U's.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 18:41 (UK time). Updated: Monday 23 November 2026, 23:11. Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Tranmere had lost 1-0 at Fleetwood a week earlier
- Ince came in for Lee O'Connor in the only Tranmere change
- York City visit Colchester next

## The story

Colchester United were beaten by Tranmere Rovers in League Two at Prenton Park, Birkenhead, on Saturday 21 November, with the home side's winner coming in the 90th minute. It ends a short lift for Danny Cowley's side, who had beaten Gillingham 2-0 a week earlier for their first league win since the end of August.

Tranmere had the better of the opening stages and went ahead shortly before the half-hour mark, when Ince, back in Darrell Clarke's starting line-up, picked up a loose ball, drove forward and finished. Colchester were level just before the break as Raggett scored from a Jack Payne cross, and the sides went in at 1-1. Jones then won it for Tranmere in the final minute of normal time.

Colchester went into the game 21st in League Two, three points above the relegation zone, and stay on 15 points. York City are next, at the JobServe Community Stadium on Saturday 28 November at 3pm. The U's then travel to Grimsby Town the following Tuesday. Mansfield Town visit in the FA Cup second round on Saturday 5 December.
