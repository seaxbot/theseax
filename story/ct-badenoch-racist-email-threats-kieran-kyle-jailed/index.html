Former teacher jailed for racist emails threatening Kemi Badenoch, MP for North West Essex

Kieran Kyle has been jailed after sending the Conservative leader emails that contained racist references and a threat to shoot her, according to reports of the court case.

Saffron Walden · Monday 9 November 2026, 15:00 · Reported by BBC Essex News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Kieran Kyle, described as a former teacher, has been jailed after sending emails to Kemi Badenoch, the Conservative Party leader and MP for North West Essex. A court heard the emails contained racist references, and reports say one told her he would have shot her dead.

Key points

  • Emails were sent to the Tory leader, MP for North West Essex
