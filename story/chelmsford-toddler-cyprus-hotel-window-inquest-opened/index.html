Inquest opens into death of Chelmsford boy, 2, who fell from Cyprus hotel window

Jaxon Foster died from multiple severe injuries on the first day of a family holiday in July, Essex Coroner's Court has heard.

Chelmsford · Friday 13 November 2026, 15:48 · Reported by Essex Coroner
WhatsApp Facebook X

Jaxon fell around 12 metres (nearly 40ft) from a fourth-floor window at the five-star King Evelthon Beach Hotel and Resort in Chlorakas, near Paphos, on 12 July. The inquest, opened and adjourned on Friday, heard that his provisional cause of death was severe head injuries and multiple injuries to his body and vital organs. He was confirmed dead in hospital later that day.

Lincoln Brookes, Essex's senior coroner, called it a "particularly tragic" case and offered his condolences to the family.

The family had landed in Cyprus only hours earlier and spent the afternoon by the pool. At around 6pm, Jaxon, his father and his grandfather were heading to a lift to go to dinner when the boy spotted a waterpark outside. His father, Joshua Foster, 37, an auditor, lifted him onto a windowsill so he could see the view, not realising that part of the window was open, and Jaxon fell before he could react.

In October, a court in Paphos fined Mr Foster €3,000 (about £2,500) after he admitted causing his son's death through a reckless, rash or dangerous act. The judge found the window did not appear to carry markings that would have shown one section was open, said there was no evidence he had deliberately put Jaxon at risk, and said no sentence could match the family's loss. His lawyer described what happened as a momentary error with fatal consequences.

In an earlier statement, the family said Jaxon was deeply loved and would be missed forever.

Key points

  • A full inquest is listed for 6 January
  • His father was fined €3,000 by a court in Paphos in October
