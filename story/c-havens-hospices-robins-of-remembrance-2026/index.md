# Havens Hospices opens Robins of Remembrance appeal for Christmas

**Families can dedicate a robin to a loved one for £18. Matching robins will hang on trees at Fair Havens and Little Havens.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Prittlewell, Southend-on-Sea; Thundersley, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 12:39 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Trees on show from 1 December to 3 January
- More than 1,000 robins were dedicated last year

## The story

Havens Hospices has opened its third Robins of Remembrance appeal, inviting people across south Essex to remember someone they have lost this Christmas. For £18, supporters get a limited-edition robin decoration for their own tree. A matching robin hangs on the Christmas trees outside Fair Havens in Prittlewell or Little Havens in Thundersley, on show from 1 December 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Irene Paskins is dedicating a robin to her husband Trevor, who was cared for by Fair Havens for nearly three years and died there on Christmas morning last year.
