# South Woodham Ferrers shop thefts: two sentenced after targeted patrols

**Neighbourhood officers from the Chelmsford and Maldon districts focused on a South Woodham Ferrers business that had been hit repeatedly by bulk shoplifting.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: South Woodham Ferrers, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Patrols ran in both uniform and plain clothes
- The man was ordered to pay the business compensation

## The story

A 32-year-old woman from the Benfleet area was given an eight-week custodial sentence, suspended for 12 months, after being arrested at the same South Woodham Ferrers business, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reported on Monday 16 November. A 37-year-old man from the Stansted area was charged over the incidents and other outstanding shop thefts, fined in court and ordered to pay the business compensation. Sergeant Matt Dalby, of the Maldon Neighbourhood Policing Team, said officers patrol shopping areas in uniform and plain clothes.
