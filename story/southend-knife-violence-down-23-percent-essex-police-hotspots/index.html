Southend knife violence falls 23% but city still tops Essex list

Across Essex, serious violence involving knives has fallen 17% over two years, Essex Police says, while arrests for carrying knives rose 19% in the past 12 months.

Southend · Thursday 19 November 2026, 11:54 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend recorded 52 fewer reports of serious knife violence over the past 12 months, figures released by Essex Police on Thursday 19 November 2026 show, although the city still has more knife incidents than anywhere else in the county.

Extra patrols in six hotspot zones, paid for by the Home Office through the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner for Essex, have led to 60 arrests since May in Chelmsford, Southend, Clacton, Colchester, Grays and Basildon. Over the past year a quarter of suspects and victims in Essex knife crime cases were aged 19 or under. Superintendent Darren Deex, the force's lead for knife crime, said officers "can't just arrest our way out of knife crime".

Officers are visiting schools and colleges this week as part of Operation Sceptre. Young people arrested for carrying a weapon can be referred to the Essex Violence and Vulnerability Unit, and 15 knife bins at police stations let people hand in blades safely. Roger Hirst, the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner, chairs the Violence and Vulnerability Partnership.

Key points

  • 60 arrests since May from hotspot patrols in six towns
  • A quarter of knife crime suspects and victims were 19 or younger
  • 15 knife bins at Essex police stations
