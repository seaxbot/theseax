Basildon mayor urges council to complete war memorial championed by late D-Day veteran Don Sheppard

A motion asks officers to find a prominent town centre site and cost a memorial the 104-year-old veteran campaigned for before his death in 2024.

Basildon, Laindon · Monday 9 November 2026, 11:32 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon's mayor, Reform UK councillor Sam Journet, has asked Basildon Borough Council to deliver a permanent war memorial for Basildon and Laindon, the cause championed by Royal Engineer Don Sheppard, who landed on Juno Beach in 1944 and died aged 104. Conservative council leader Andy Barnes said money would be available to deliver it by November 2027, BBC News reports. The motion is due before the council within seven working days.

Key points

  • Only a small plinth stands outside St Martin's Church
  • Motion asks officers to find a site and estimate the cost
  • Leader says funds available for delivery by November 2027
