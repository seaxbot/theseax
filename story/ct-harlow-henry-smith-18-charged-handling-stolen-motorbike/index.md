# Old Harlow man, 18, charged with handling stolen motorbike

**An 18-year-old from Old Harlow is accused of handling a stolen Yamaha motorbike worth about £6,000. He is on bail until January.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Old Harlow, Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 09:17 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Alleged offences: 9 July in Harlow
- Next court date: 28 January 2027

## The story

Henry Smith, 18, of Chalk Lane, Old Harlow, has appeared at Colchester Magistrates' Court charged with handling stolen goods, Your Harlow reported on Saturday 28 November. The charge relates to a Yamaha Tracer 700 motorbike, valued at about £6,000, in Harlow on 9 July.

He is also charged with criminal damage to two motorbikes and two chains. He was released on unconditional bail and is next due in court on 28 January 2027.
