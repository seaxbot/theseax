Southend sell 9,700+ tickets for clash with leaders Boreham Wood

Kieron Dyer is back on the touchline as third-placed Southend United try to become the first side to beat the National League leaders this season.

Southend-on-Sea · Friday 20 November 2026, 08:24 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 11:42 · Reported by Boreham Wood FC
WhatsApp Facebook X

Roots Hall stages the biggest game of the National League season so far on Friday 20 November, when Southend United, third in the table, meet Boreham Wood, who are top and have not lost any of their 17 league games. Kick-off is 7.45pm and the match is live on DAZN. A big crowd is expected: Southend United said shortly after 11am on Friday that more than 9,700 tickets had already been sold, and the club has published a pre-match guide for fans travelling to the ground. Boreham Wood have 45 points from 14 wins and three draws. Southend have 32, three behind second-placed Harrogate Town.

Team news: head coach Kieron Dyer returns to the dugout after serving a one-match touchline ban during last Saturday's 2-1 win over Aldershot Town. Goalkeeping coach Anssi Jaakkola is still serving a six-match ban. Defender Maxwell McKnight, the former Tottenham academy player signed last week, went straight into the side against Aldershot. Tyreece Simpson is pushing for a start after coming off the bench at half-time to score the winner. Kyle McAdam got the equaliser. Jack Stone is unavailable, having rejoined Grays Athletic on a month's loan. Neither club has published injury news in writing ahead of the game.

Southend midfielder Leon Chambers-Parillon, who had six goals by mid-October, is the player Boreham Wood's own preview picks out as the one to watch. That win over Aldershot ended a run of four games without one in all competitions for a Southend side rebuilt with 15 summer signings in Dyer's first job as a manager. Boreham Wood, managed by Luke Garrard, drew 2-2 at Yeovil Town last weekend through a Lewis Richardson equaliser.

Recent history favours the visitors. Boreham Wood won 2-0 at Roots Hall in February, with goals from Abdul Abdulmalik and Matt Rush, after a 1-1 draw between the sides in September 2025. Southend have scored 40 league goals this season and Boreham Wood 46, the two highest totals in the division. A home win would cut the gap between them to 10 points.

Key points

  • Kick-off 7.45pm at Roots Hall, live on DAZN
  • Dyer back after a one-match touchline ban
  • Boreham Wood won 2-0 at Roots Hall in February
