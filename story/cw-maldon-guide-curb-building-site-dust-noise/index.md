# Maldon drafts guide to curb dust and noise from building sites

**Councillors want builders to plan better for dust, noise and debris. A new guide goes to a committee on 3 December.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- Decision due: Overview and Scrutiny Committee, Thursday 3 December
- Covers dust, noise, debris, wheel washing, construction traffic and road sweeping
- Would be published on the council's planning web pages
- The report lists no extra cost to the council

## The story

Maldon District Council's Overview and Scrutiny Committee will be asked on Thursday 3 December to agree new guidance for developers. It covers construction environmental management plans, which set out how a building site will control dust, noise and other effects on the people around it. If agreed, the advice note would go on the council's planning web pages. Applicants, agents and developers would be told about it so they can check what to include before they submit a plan.

Councillors on a working group asked for the guide. On 12 October they heard concerns about dust, noise and debris from building work, including whether sites have enough water and power to run proper controls in extreme weather. They wanted a short checklist for planning officers that points to industry standards and official guidance. At a second meeting on 3 November, members asked for more on wheel and boot washing, loose materials near roads, construction traffic, road sweeping and parking.

A report to the committee says the guidance should shape planning conditions so they can be enforced. It is not meant to cover supervising work inside a site, or matters for the Health and Safety Executive and other national bodies. Officers have changed the draft to reflect the councillors' comments. According to the report, the note would bring a clearer and more consistent approach, and it lists no extra cost to the council.
