Maldon's share of the halted Mid Essex unitary council set-up costs put at about £26,000

Maldon District Council councillors have asked for a full breakdown, including officer time, of spending on the Mid Essex council plan before the government paused the Essex shake-up.

Maldon · Thursday 12 November 2026, 06:24 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Maldon District Council received a report from its Chief Executive on Thursday 22 October 2026 setting out what it spent preparing for a Mid Essex unitary council before the government paused local government reorganisation. Councillors agreed to ask for a full breakdown of the costs, including officer time, and details of how the Mid Essex Director was appointed.

The report says the Secretary of State for Housing, Communities and Local Government withdrew the five-unitary model for Essex on 7 September 2026 and paused reorganisation for review. Costs between the choice of that model and the pause are estimated at £78,000, with Maldon expected to pay a third, about £26,000, for IT, a councillor engagement event, staff support and specialist project management. No posts were recruited specifically for the work, and earlier Freedom of Information requests about the costs were refused because the figures were due to be published.

The final figure is due to be confirmed by the Mid Essex Programme Board in November, and the council is still waiting to hear whether there are Essex-wide costs to share. Councillors were also told that Essex County Council has consented to a Greater Essex mayoral strategic authority with Southend-on-Sea City Council and Thurrock Council, though all three must agree, and that the first mayoral election is now planned for May 2028.

Key points

  • Mid Essex set-up costs estimated at £78,000
  • Maldon's expected one-third share: about £26,000
  • Five-unitary model withdrawn on 7 September 2026
  • Greater Essex mayoral election planned for May 2028
