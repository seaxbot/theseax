Police still seeking witnesses to Sunday's early-hours crash that killed two people on Chelmsford's Copperfield Road

Essex Police wants doorbell, dashcam and eyewitness accounts after a driver and passenger died when their Ford Fiesta struck a property wall near Havisham Way.

Chelmsford · Monday 9 November 2026, 13:59 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 00:49 · Reported by BBC News (via Gateway 97.8)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people, the driver and a passenger, died when the car they were in struck a wall in Chelmsford in the early hours of Sunday 8 November. No other vehicle was involved.

Essex Police said it was called shortly after 1.45am to Copperfield Road, close to where it meets Havisham Way. The Ford Fiesta hit and damaged the wall of a property, and the road was closed for a time afterwards. The names of the two people who died have not been released.

Essex Police is asking anyone who saw the crash, or who has doorbell or dashcam footage from the area, to get in touch through its website or its 24/7 online Live Chat service. Greatest Hits Radio Essex and BBC News have both reported the appeal.

Key points

  • Called shortly after 1.45am on Sunday 8 November
  • Copperfield Road, near the junction with Havisham Way
  • Driver and passenger died at the scene; no other vehicle involved
  • Police want doorbell and dashcam footage
