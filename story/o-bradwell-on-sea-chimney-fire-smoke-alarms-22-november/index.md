# Smoke alarms wake Bradwell-on-Sea residents to log burner fire

**Firefighters had to take part of a wall apart to put out the fire, which had spread into the chimney of the house in the early hours of Sunday 22 November.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Bradwell-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 23:33 (UK time). Updated: Monday 23 November 2026, 10:01. Section: Public Services. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Crews called at 4.27am
- Fire out by 5.51am
- Test smoke alarms once a month

## The story

It was 4.27am when crews were sent to the house, after the residents' smoke alarms went off and woke them, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. The fire had started in a log burner. Firefighters had it out by 5.51am.

Watch Manager Kris Mortimer, from Tollesbury Fire Station, said: "Incidents like this show why it's so important to have working smoke alarms at home. Thankfully, the smoke alarms went off and woke the residents up. If they didn't have them, it could have been a very different incident."

The fire service advises having at least one working smoke alarm on every floor of a home and testing it once a month. Anyone without working alarms can contact the service at essex-fire.gov.uk/book or on 0300 303 0088.
