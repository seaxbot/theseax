Thurrock Council looks for £27.3m of cuts, with 72 jobs in line to go

Thurrock Council's Reform UK leadership wants savings over two years to balance a budget in which a quarter of spending goes on debt interest.

Grays, Thurrock · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 06:07 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock Council proposes to close 72.5 full-time posts, 29 of them long-empty vacancies, the BBC reported on Wednesday 18 November. Children's services would save £2.45m and adult social care £1.67m over two years, with £2.9m from homelessness services.

About a quarter of the council's £243.6m yearly spending goes on interest. Finance cabinet member Sara Poyntz said the council would try to shield residents; Labour councillor Aaron Green said he would challenge the plans. Corporate Scrutiny Committee discusses them on Tuesday 24 November.

Key points

  • Council says it needs more than £60m from government for 2027-28
  • £450,000 to come from higher fees and charges
  • Government gave £200m of debt relief this year
