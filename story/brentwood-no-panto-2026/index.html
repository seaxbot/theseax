No pantomime in Brentwood this Christmas as town's two venues leave panto off the bill

Brentwood Theatre has a festive line-up of shows and films instead, while families wanting a traditional panto are being pointed to Basildon and Chelmsford.

Brentwood · Thursday 5 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Phoenix FM
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brentwood will go without a traditional pantomime this Christmas, Phoenix FM reports, with neither Brentwood Theatre nor the Brentwood Centre listing one for 2026. Last year the Brentwood Centre staged Beauty and the Beast, so the gap will be noticed by families who make a festive show part of their December.

Brentwood Theatre is instead running a season that includes the youth production Elf the Musical Jr this weekend, The Hutton Players in Great Expectations from 18 to 21 November and A Christmas Carol from 1:4:9 Productions on 3 to 5 December. From 10 to 24 December it stages The Elves and the Shoemaker, an interactive musical for children aged three to seven, with £5 screenings of The Muppets Christmas Carol and It's A Wonderful Life in mid-December.

The article gives no reason for the absence of a panto. Brentwood Online's guide names Basildon and Chelmsford as the nearest places to see a traditional pantomime this year; Chelmsford Theatre's Cinderella opens on 27 November.

Key points

  • Neither Brentwood Theatre nor the Brentwood Centre lists a 2026 panto
  • The Elves and the Shoemaker runs 10 to 24 December, £15
  • £5 festive film screenings on 16 and 17 December
  • Nearest traditional pantos are in Basildon and Chelmsford
