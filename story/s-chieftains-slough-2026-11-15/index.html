Chelmsford Chieftains beat Slough Jets 6-3 with a strong final period

Lane Paddison broke a 3-3 deadlock early in the third period as the Chelmsford club won game five of their Challenge Cup campaign at Riverside Ice and Leisure.

Chelmsford · Sunday 15 November 2026, 22:17 · Reported by Chelmsford Chieftains
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford Chieftains beat Slough Jets 6-3 in the Challenge Cup on Sunday 15 November 2026, outshooting the visitors 39 to 31 at Riverside Ice and Leisure in Chelmsford.

Grant Bartlett, Tom Soar and Hallden Barnes-Garner scored in the first two periods. After Paddison's goal, Ollie Baldock converted on the power play and Josh Batch, named man of the match, found an empty net with 14 seconds left.

Key points

  • Netminder Brandon Stones played all 60 minutes
  • Juha Lindgren, Brandon Miles and Oli Hemmings-Maher scored for Slough
  • Next: away at Streatham Redhawks on Sunday 22 November
