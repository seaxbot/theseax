Only 49% of Thurrock residents feel safe after dark, figure in new borough health plan shows

Thurrock's draft Health and Wellbeing Strategy for 2027 to 2030 quotes the finding from last year's residents survey and makes community safety one of its four priorities.

Grays, Thurrock · Monday 9 November 2026, 13:35 · Reported by Your Thurrock (Local Democracy Reporting Service)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fewer than half of people who answered Thurrock's 2025 residents survey said they felt safe after dark, a figure set out in the council's draft Health and Wellbeing Strategy for 2027 to 2030, Your Thurrock reports from Local Democracy Reporting.

The plan's priorities are childhood, healthy lifestyles, ageing and community safety. The council said detailed actions on feeling safe will sit in a delivery plan drawn up with Essex Police, Essex Fire and the police, fire and crime commissioner, and councillors are due to consider the strategy next week.

Key points

  • 49% said they felt safe after dark in the 2025 survey
  • Strategy covers 2027 to 2030
  • Progress to be reported to the Health and Wellbeing Board
