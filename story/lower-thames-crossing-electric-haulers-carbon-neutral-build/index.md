# Lower Thames Crossing puts world-first electric haulers to work

**Battery-powered trucks are now moving earth on the route of the new road tunnel between Essex and Kent.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Thurrock, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:44 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Major tunnelling is due to start in 2028
- Early works began in Essex and Kent in March 2026
- National Highways expects it to open in the early to mid-2030s

## The story

Four battery-powered Volvo haulers, each able to carry up to 39 tonnes, have started work on the £10bn Lower Thames Crossing, the BBC reported on 24 November. National Highways says they are the first machines of their size to work on any site in the world.

National Highways says diesel has not been used on its worksites since 2025, and it wants the crossing to be the UK's first major project built carbon neutral. Andrea Griffiths of CPRE Kent said any new road is still bad for the environment.
