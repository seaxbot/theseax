Southend's Cliffs Pavilion unveils new foyer, bars and restaurant

Westcliff-on-Sea, Southend · Thursday 26 November 2026, 11:06 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Westcliff theatre's first big makeover since the 1990s is finished. Government levelling up money helped pay for it.

Southend-on-Sea City Council unveiled the refurbished Cliffs Pavilion in Westcliff-on-Sea on Thursday 26 November. Its new foyer, bars and restaurant opened to audiences for the first time that day, according to Trafalgar Entertainment, which runs the theatre for the council.

What has changed

Visitors now walk into a redesigned foyer with a café by day that turns into a bar on show nights. The box office is bigger, easier to use and back beside the main entrance. There is a new merchandise counter, a sweet kiosk open to all and a new suite of women's toilets on the ground floor.

Upstairs, the Pavilion restaurant has been extended and modernised, with more seats and its own bar. A new café bar in the lobby serves light lunches, coffee and cake during the day, with USB points for people who want to work there. Trafalgar said room for pre-show and interval drinks has almost doubled.

Who paid

Work began in 2024 and shows carried on throughout. It is the first major work on the venue, which is more than 60 years old, since the 1990s. The project was backed by £5.5m from the government's Levelling Up Fund, with more money from the council, which owns the building, and from Trafalgar. Neither has said what the total bill came to.

Councillor Matt Dent, the council's cabinet member for business, culture, music and tourism, called the Cliffs Pavilion "one of Southend's most iconic landmarks".

What happens next

Pre-show dining returns for the Christmas pantomime. Oliver Stacey, venue director at Southend Theatres, said a new pre-show menu launches with the restaurant, and a pantomime brunch is being added before selected afternoon performances.

Key points

  • Drinks space before shows and at the interval almost doubles
  • Box office moves back beside the main entrance
  • Pantomime brunch added before selected afternoon shows
