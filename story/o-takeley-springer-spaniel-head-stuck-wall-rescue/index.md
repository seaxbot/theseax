# Takeley firefighter chisels trapped spaniel's head free from wall

**A two-year-old Springer Spaniel got her head stuck in a decorative cut-out in a Takeley wall. A fire officer freed her with small tools.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Takeley, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:58 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Call came in at 3.42pm
- Freed using small tools
- Dog unhurt and back with her owners

## The story

Station Manager Antony Clement was called to Takeley at 3.42pm on Tuesday 24 November, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. He worked with the owners to chisel away at the wall feature until the dog could squeeze her head back out.

He said she had become "quite distressed" while she was trapped. "She was quite happy to be reunited with her owners and hopefully she won't be getting into any more mischief," he added.
