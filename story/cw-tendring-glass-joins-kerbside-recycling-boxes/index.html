Tendring households can now recycle glass at the kerbside after 70,000 extra boxes go out

The council's environment lead told cabinet the expanded collections began on 5 October, and arts grants to the district have risen six-fold.

Clacton-on-Sea · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 06:24 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council's Portfolio Holder for Environment and ICT told the cabinet on 23 October 2026 that around 70,000 additional green recycling boxes had been delivered across the district. The expanded kerbside recycling service started on 5 October. Residents can now put out glass bottles and jars, along with a wider range of other materials, for collection from their homes.

The portfolio holder described the move from delivering the boxes to collecting the extra recycling from households as an important milestone, and said the change made recycling easier and more convenient for residents. The statement was made as one of the cabinet members' announcements, which the cabinet noted.

At the same meeting the Portfolio Holder for Arts, Culture and Heritage said Arts Council England had highlighted that National Lottery Project Grants investment in Tendring had grown from £85,000 in 2020/21 to more than £505,000 in 2025/26. The portfolio holder said the council's Connect Tendring cultural newsletter, launched this year, already has around 450 subscribers.

Key points

  • Around 70,000 additional green recycling boxes delivered
  • Expanded kerbside service, including glass bottles and jars, began on 5 October
  • Arts Council project grants to Tendring up from £85,000 (2020/21) to more than £505,000 (2025/26)
  • Connect Tendring newsletter has around 450 subscribers
