Gibberd Garden creative space and Parndon Mill festival share in Harlow's £75,000 arts pot

Harlow's Cultural Enabler Grant Scheme, backed by Arts Council England, is funding young writers and SEND acting groups as well as new creative spaces in the town.

Harlow · Thursday 12 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Harlow Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

A new creative space at the Gibberd Garden and a community festival at Parndon Mill are among the projects funded by Harlow Council's £75,000 cultural grants round, announced on Thursday 12 November.

Harlow Council Leader Councillor Dan Swords said the town had long had cultural talent and needed "a bigger stage". Councillor Danielle Brown, cabinet member for culture and community, said the money would help more people find and join in with the town's creative life. The council has not published how many groups were funded or how much each received.

The grants run alongside building work on an Arts and Cultural Quarter in front of Harlow Playhouse, part of the Building Harlow's Future programme. Plans there include a live performance venue, a new home for the Gibberd Gallery, a music school, recording studios and an outdoor amphitheatre; no completion date has been given.

Key points

  • Money comes from Harlow Council and Arts Council England
  • Young writers and SEND acting groups also supported
  • No completion date yet for the Playhouse cultural quarter
