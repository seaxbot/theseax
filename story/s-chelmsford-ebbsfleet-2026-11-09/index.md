# Chelmsford City hold leaders Ebbsfleet United to a 2-2 draw

**Barnes scored twice for the National League South leaders, but the Clarets twice hit back in front of 1,390 fans.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Monday 9 November 2026, 21:48 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Barnes scored both Ebbsfleet goals, on 33 and 68 minutes
- Clarke (35) and Barham (52) scored for City
- Manny Egbo started in place of Kian Ronan, away with Gibraltar

## The story

Chelmsford City drew 2-2 with National League South leaders Ebbsfleet United at the Dunmow Group Community Stadium on Monday night. Barnes put Ebbsfleet ahead on 33 minutes, Ash Clarke levelled two minutes later and Jack Barham headed City in front from a Manny Egbo cross on 52 minutes.

Barnes equalised on 68 minutes, and both sides pushed for a winner, with keeper Marriott saving late on. The crowd was 1,390.
