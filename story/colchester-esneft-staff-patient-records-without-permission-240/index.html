Colchester hospital trust logs 240+ cases of staff snooping on records

East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust, which runs Colchester and Clacton hospitals, is among NHS bodies counting staff who viewed confidential files with no good reason, as national figures show thousands of cases across England.

Colchester, Clacton-on-Sea · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 00:01 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 11:44 · Reported by PA Media (via Yahoo News UK)
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Daily Gazette reported the trust's total on Wednesday 18 November from Freedom of Information figures. Separately, the Press Association asked 143 acute hospital and ambulance trusts in England how often staff had looked at records inappropriately, and the 79 that gave precise figures for 2021/22 to 2025/26 logged 3,029 incidents between them.

University Hospitals of Morecambe Bay had the highest total with 242, followed by West Suffolk with 213 and Northumbria Healthcare with 195, PA found. The real national number is likely to be higher because some trusts gave only "fewer than five" for a year to avoid identifying staff. The Information Commissioner's Office has received more than 300 reports this year of staff accessing records without good reason.

East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust suspended or removed 10 staff from duty after a possible breach of the records of three-year-old Noah Woods, PA reports. The trust has apologised to his family, reported the matter to the Information Commissioner's Office and said it will take disciplinary action where its findings require it. NHS England began a crackdown in September, and its chief executive, Sir Jim Mackey, said suspected staff should be suspended at once and lose access to online systems.

Key points

  • 79 trusts in England logged 3,029 incidents over five years
  • The national count covers 2021/22 to 2025/26
  • NHS England began a crackdown in September
