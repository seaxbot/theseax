Police helicopter sent to Dengie marshes after reports of hare coursing

Essex Police's Rural Engagement Team, backed by the force helicopter, seized four dogs and four vehicles on the Dengie marshes, and five men were reported for hare coursing.

Dengie marshes, Burnham-on-Crouch · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 10:00 · Reported by Farmers Guardian
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Operation followed reports at about 8am on Wednesday 28 October
  • Four dogs and four vehicles seized
  • Five men in their 20s and 30s reported under the Hunting Act
  • All five given three-year community protection warnings; three more to be interviewed
