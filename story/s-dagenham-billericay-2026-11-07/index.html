Elliot Long double earns Billericay Town a 2-2 draw at Dagenham & Redbridge

Danny Scopes' side twice found the net through their striker and held on for a point against the Daggers in the National League South.

Billericay · Sunday 8 November 2026, 00:00 · Reported by The Non-League Football Paper
WhatsApp Facebook X

Billericay Town came away from Dagenham & Redbridge with a 2-2 draw in the National League South on Saturday. Elliot Long scored both goals for Billericay, who had to dig in after the break to protect a share of the points.

Long put Billericay ahead on 27 minutes before Henry Hawkins levelled for the hosts three minutes before half-time. Beautyman then gave Dagenham the lead from the penalty spot on 55 minutes, but Long struck again on 68 minutes to make it 2-2.

Billericay manager Danny Scopes was pleased with what he saw from his players. "I'm delighted with the team performance," he said after the game.

Key points

  • Dagenham & Redbridge 2-2 Billericay Town, National League South
  • Billericay scorer: Elliot Long 27 and 68
  • Dagenham scorers: Henry Hawkins 42, Beautyman 55 pen
