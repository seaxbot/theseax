# Thundersley's Little Havens welcomes back patron Ferne McCann

**TV star Ferne McCann spent an evening with families at Essex's only children's hospice. She is backing its Christmas appeal.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Thundersley, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 11:57 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Little Havens cares for babies, children and young adults
- She will help raise awareness through December

## The story

Ferne McCann, patron of Little Havens, visited the hospice in Thundersley on Tuesday 17 November, Havens Hospices said. She met families cared for there, some for the first time.

The charity's Big Christmas "Yes!" Appeal asks people to fundraise so its nurses can support every family who needs them. She will also attend its Ladies Committee Christmas Lunch in December.
