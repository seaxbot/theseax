# Saffron Walden: two arrested after man seriously hurt in Market Row

**Essex Police are also appealing for anyone who saw an earlier altercation inside the Nexus Lounge on Market Hill on the Saturday evening.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Saffron Walden, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 17 November 2026, 09:06 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45. Section: Public Services. Topic: Appeal.

## Key points

- Man in his 30s taken to hospital with facial injuries
- Arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm
- Quote incident 81 of 15 November

## The story

Two men, aged 19 and 25, were arrested on suspicion of grievous bodily harm after a man in his 30s was seriously assaulted in Market Row, Saffron Walden, Essex Police said. Officers were called at about 1.40am on Sunday 15 November.

Police said the man was attacked by a number of people and suffered several facial injuries. He was taken to hospital, and his injuries are not thought to be life-threatening. The two men were arrested on the Sunday afternoon and taken into custody for questioning.

Officers want to hear from anyone who saw an altercation earlier on the Saturday evening inside the Nexus Lounge on Market Hill, which they believe may be linked. Anyone with information or footage can contact Essex Police online or on 101, quoting incident 81 of 15 November.
