No pantomime in Brentwood this Christmas, but the town's theatre has a festive line-up instead

Neither Brentwood Theatre nor the Brentwood Centre lists a panto for 2026, so families are being pointed to plays, a youth musical and a children's show.

Brentwood · Thursday 5 November 2026, 07:05 · Reported by Phoenix FM
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brentwood will go without a Christmas pantomime this year, according to Phoenix FM, which found no panto listed at Brentwood Theatre for 2026. Brentwood Online has reported that the Brentwood Centre has none either, leaving families in the borough to look elsewhere for the traditional festive outing.

The theatre's winter programme instead includes The Hutton Players in Great Expectations from 18 to 21 November at £17, and a youth production of Elf the Musical Jr this weekend at £15. In December, 1:4:9 Productions stage A Christmas Carol from 3 to 5 December, there are £5 screenings of The Muppet Christmas Carol on 16 December and It's A Wonderful Life on 17 December, and The Elves and the Shoemaker runs for children aged three to seven from 10 to 24 December.

No reason for the absence of a panto was given. Phoenix FM took the listings from the theatre's website on 5 November and says dates, times and prices can change, so audiences should check with Brentwood Theatre before booking.

Key points

  • No 2026 panto listed at Brentwood Theatre or the Brentwood Centre
  • Great Expectations runs 18 to 21 November, tickets £17
  • The Elves and the Shoemaker, for ages 3 to 7, runs 10 to 24 December
  • Cheap £5 festive film screenings on 16 and 17 December
