Southend aims to bring council home repairs up to industry norms by Christmas after regulator judgement

Electrical safety checks on council homes are now close to complete, but councillors heard that repairs remain the weak spot in Southend's response to the social housing regulator.

Southend-on-Sea · Monday 9 November 2026, 06:20 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council's Audit and Governance Committee received an update on 19 October 2026 on the council's progress in responding to a judgement by the Regulator of Social Housing. The report, from the Executive Director for Environment and Place, also covered the council's dealings with the Housing Ombudsman and the Building Safety Regulator.

The Head of Housing told the committee that monthly meetings with the regulator were continuing and that significant progress had been made on the council's Provider Improvement Plan. The council has reached 100% coverage of electrical safety checks in communal areas and 99% in homes, and the minutes say the regulator now regards electrical safety as business as usual. Repairs remain an area of focus, and a targeted improvement plan has been agreed with the council's repairs contractor to cut work in progress and bring performance up to industry norms by Christmas 2026.

Councillors were told the Housing Ombudsman's investigation has concluded, with the council due to submit a learning statement as the final stage. Officers said this was the first inspection under the new consumer regulation framework and that the council knew of areas needing improvement before the inspections took place. The committee asked for a further update on repairs performance.

Key points

  • Communal electrical safety check coverage: 100%; homes: 99%
  • Repairs contractor has a targeted improvement plan with a Christmas 2026 deadline
  • Housing Ombudsman investigation concluded; council to submit a learning statement
  • Committee asked for a further update on repairs
