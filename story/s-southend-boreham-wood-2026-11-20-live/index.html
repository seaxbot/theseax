Shrimpers fighting back in table-topping thriller at Roots Hall

Southend-on-Sea · Friday 20 November 2026, 20:35 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 21:18 · Reported by The Seax (half-time score)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend United have pulled two goals back against National League leaders Boreham Wood and trail 3-2 after 70 minutes.

Tyreece Simpson and Rico Richards scored two minutes apart midway through the second half. Simpson struck on 65 minutes from Maxwell McKnight's pass, and Richards made it 3-2 on 67 minutes, set up by Promise Omochere. Simpson was booked moments later.

Boreham Wood had led 3-0 at half-time through Oliver Bainbridge on 19 minutes, Ben Whitfield on 29 and Zak Brunt on 33. Whitfield went off on 70 minutes, replaced by Lewis Richardson.

More than 10,000 fans were expected at Roots Hall for the game, which is live on DAZN. The Seax will have the full-time result tonight.

Head coach Kieron Dyer was back in the dugout after a one-match touchline ban. Goalkeeping coach Anssi Jaakkola is still serving a six-match ban, and Jack Stone is on a month's loan at Grays Athletic.

Southend's 2-1 win over Aldershot Town last Saturday ended a run of four games without a win in all competitions, for a side rebuilt with 15 summer signings in Dyer's first job as a manager. Boreham Wood, managed by Luke Garrard, drew 2-2 at Yeovil Town last weekend.

Recent history favours the visitors. Boreham Wood won 2-0 at Roots Hall in February, after a 1-1 draw between the sides in September 2025. Before kick-off, Southend had scored 40 league goals this season and Boreham Wood 46, the two highest totals in the division.
