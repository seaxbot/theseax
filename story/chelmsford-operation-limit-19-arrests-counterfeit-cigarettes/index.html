Chelmsford roads operation ends in 19 arrests and suspected fake cigarettes seized

Essex Police's Operation Limit stopped drivers across the Chelmsford area and turned up crimes that had nothing to do with driving.

Chelmsford · Thursday 12 November 2026, 11:08 · Reported by Chelmsford City Hub
WhatsApp Facebook X

Fourteen of the people arrested during the operation on Saturday 7 November 2026 were suspected of drink or drug driving, Chelmsford City Hub reports, citing Essex Police. Officers also detected 137 speeding offences and seized six vehicles.

One disqualified, uninsured driver with a foreign licence was found with cigarettes suspected to be counterfeit, a case now being taken forward with Trading Standards. Essex Police says enforcement will step up before its Christmas drink and drug-driving campaign.

Key points

  • Three arrests were on suspicion of immigration offences
  • Two wanted suspects were found during vehicle checks
  • An earlier Operation Limit day on 26 September led to 17 arrests
