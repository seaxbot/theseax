Reform county councillor for Great Notley defends deleted immigration post as Braintree Conservatives demand he reflects

Carl Johnson, who sits on Essex County Council for Three Fields with Great Notley, says he stands by free speech after a Facebook post about who should hold elected office drew criticism.

Great Notley, Braintree · Monday 9 November 2026, 14:00 · Reported by Your Harlow (Local Democracy Reporting Service)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex County Council's Reform UK member for Three Fields with Great Notley, Carl Johnson, has defended a 4 November Facebook post, since taken down, which said no foreigner should hold elected office in the UK. Braintree Conservatives criticised the remarks and urged him to reflect on them, according to the Local Democracy Reporting Service.

Mr Johnson says he stands by free speech and that concerns about politicians' loyalties are legitimate. Reform UK said he is entitled to his own personal views.

Key points

  • Post published on 4 November and later removed
  • Braintree Conservatives urged him to reflect on his statements
  • Reform UK says he is entitled to his own views
