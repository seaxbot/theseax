Retired Wethersfield camp teacher says Reform councillor Carl Johnson likened her to a Nazi collaborator, days after his deleted immigration post

Mair Godley, 78, who taught English at the Wethersfield asylum camp, told ITV News Anglia she was targeted by the Reform UK councillor at a recent district council meeting.

Wethersfield, Great Notley, Braintree · Monday 9 November 2026, 14:00 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 18:49 · Reported by Your Harlow (Local Democracy Reporting Service)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mair Godley, a 78-year-old retired teacher who taught English at the Wethersfield asylum camp, says Reform UK councillor Carl Johnson compared her to a Second World War Nazi collaborator at a recent district council meeting, ITV News Anglia reported. Reform UK said he is entitled to his own views; Mr Johnson had not responded to ITV's request for comment.

Mr Johnson, Essex County Council's member for Three Fields with Great Notley, had already faced criticism from Braintree Conservatives over a deleted 4 November Facebook post saying no foreigner should hold elected office, which he defended on free speech grounds.
