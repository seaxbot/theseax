Fun Walk Trust shares record £123,107 among 62 Essex good causes

Charities, schools, churches and community groups from Billericay, Brentwood and beyond collected their cheques at the trust's 25th anniversary celebration.

Billericay · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 06:57 · Reported by Phoenix FM
WhatsApp Facebook X

The total, the highest in the walk's 25-year history, was announced at a celebration evening at Canon Roche Hall in Billericay on Friday 13 November, Phoenix FM reports. It includes a bonus pot of more than £35,000 from sponsors, which added 40% to what each of the 62 organisations raised.

Trust chairman John Baron presented the cheques. The walk began in 2002 with £6,000 for six causes.

Key points

  • Walkers have taken part wherever they choose each September since 2020
  • £1,879,000 raised since 2002
