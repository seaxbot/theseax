Trading Standards seize 460,000 illicit cigarettes

Essex Trading Standards has taken part in Operation Machinize, a national crackdown on illegal tobacco and vapes linked to organised crime.

Braintree & Chelmsford · Friday 30 October 2026, 10:00 · Updated Tuesday 3 November 2026, 08:40 · Reported by Essex County Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • About 238kg of hand-rolling tobacco and over 9,000 illegal vapes also seized
  • Braintree High Street: more than a dozen shops visited and hundreds of illegal cigarettes seized
  • Essex Police, HMRC and Immigration Enforcement joined Trading Standards
  • Report concerns via Citizens Advice on 0808 223 1133
