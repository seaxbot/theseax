Last chance for Southend residents to comment on Local Plan the Government ordered the council to publish

The Regulation 19 consultation on where homes, jobs and infrastructure could go across the city runs until Monday 21 December, before the plan goes to a planning inspector.

Southend-on-Sea · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council has opened the final round of public consultation on its draft Local Plan, the document that will guide where new homes, employment land, infrastructure and community facilities may go across the city. It runs from Friday 6 November until Monday 21 December 2026.

The council said central government formally directed it on 23 October to publish the draft for consultation by 6 November, and the cabinet agreed unanimously on 5 November to do so. Comments at this stage must address whether the plan is legally compliant and sound under national planning policy. Cllr Kevin Robinson, cabinet member for planning, property and governance, urged residents to read the draft and respond.

Documents and the response form are on the council's Local Plan website, with paper copies at Southend libraries and the Civic Centre reception. Full council is due to consider the final plan in early 2027, when it will be submitted for independent examination by a planning inspector.

Key points

  • Consultation closes Monday 21 December
  • Government directed the council to publish by 6 November
  • Paper copies in libraries and at the Civic Centre
  • Examination by a planning inspector expected in early 2027
