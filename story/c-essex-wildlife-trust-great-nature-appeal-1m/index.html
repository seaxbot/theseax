Essex Wildlife Trust aims to raise £1m for nature in three-year appeal

Essex · Thursday 19 November 2026, 06:14 · Reported by Business Wire Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

The county's wildlife charity will use its Christmas appeal to start raising money for habitats, species and waterways across Essex as it nears its 70th birthday.

Essex Wildlife Trust's Great Nature Appeal will run for three years, which the trust calls critical as it works towards a global goal of restoring 30% of land and sea by 2030, Business Wire Essex reports. Corporate partnerships manager Justine Addison said the money would help care for the trust's reserves, protect vulnerable species and improve the health of waterways.

Key points

  • Gifts made from 1 to 8 December 2026 will be doubled through The Big Give
