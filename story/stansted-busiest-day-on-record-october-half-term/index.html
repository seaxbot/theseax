Half-term getaway gives Stansted its busiest day ever with 109,881 passengers

October brought 2.79 million travellers through the Uttlesford airport, up 4.2% on the same month last year.

Stansted · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 14:55 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

London Stansted had its busiest day on record on Sunday 25 October, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reports, as school half-term getaways and overseas visitors pushed numbers up. Managing director Gareth Powell called October another landmark month for the airport, which handles nearly 31 million passengers a year and has permission to grow to 51 million.

Key points

  • More than 101,000 people flew to or from Istanbul in October
  • Dublin and Edinburgh were the next busiest destinations
  • Spain was the most popular country, ahead of Italy and Turkey
