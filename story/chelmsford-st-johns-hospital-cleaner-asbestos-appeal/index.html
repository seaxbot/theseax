Family of St John's Hospital cleaner who died of mesothelioma ask former staff for asbestos information

Lawyers for the family of Lillian Lamb, 89, are investigating whether asbestos was present at the Chelmsford hospital where she cleaned from 1973 until it closed in 2010.

Chelmsford · Monday 9 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Leigh Day
WhatsApp Facebook X

Lillian Lamb, from Chelmsford, died in February at the age of 89, months after being diagnosed with mesothelioma late last year, according to law firm Leigh Day, which represents her family.

The firm says it is investigating whether asbestos was present at the former St John's Hospital in Wood Street, demolished in 2012, and is asking anyone who worked there to share what they remember about the building and working conditions.

Key points

  • Services moved to Broomfield Hospital when St John's closed in 2010
  • Leigh Day partner Daniel Easton is leading the appeal
