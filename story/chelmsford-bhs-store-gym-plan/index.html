The Gym Group applies to move into Chelmsford's empty former BHS store in The Meadows

One of the city's largest vacant shops, empty in large part since 2016, could become a gym with retail kept on part of the ground floor.

Chelmsford · Thursday 5 November 2026, 23:00 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Gym Group has applied for planning permission to take over part of the former BHS store in The Meadows Shopping Centre, Chelmsford. The three-floor building is one of the largest empty shops in the city centre, and filling it would bring a new reason for people to visit The Meadows.

Under the application, part of the ground floor would become an entrance and the first floor would be used as a gym, with the rest of the ground floor kept for shops. BHS's Chelmsford branch closed in August 2016 after the chain collapsed; since then a ping-pong pop-up was approved but never opened, the furniture store Stacks came and went in 2018, and a temporary Job Centre used the ground floor until November 2023.

No decision has been made yet, and the application does not set out an opening date or job numbers. Chelmsford City Council will decide the plans in due course.

Key points

  • Application by The Gym Group for former BHS, The Meadows
  • Gym on first floor, retail kept on part of ground floor
  • Store has largely stood empty since 2016
  • No decision yet
