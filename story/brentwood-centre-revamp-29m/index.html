Brentwood Centre revamp cost rises to £29m as planned spa is dropped

Councillors will consider a final business case on 12 November, with every recommendation depending on Full Council finding an extra £3.82m.

Brentwood · Thursday 5 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

The cost of redeveloping the Brentwood Centre has risen to up to £29.02m, about £4m more than the £25m approved in September, and the planned spa has been cut from the scheme. The leisure centre is one of the borough's biggest public investments, so the extra money will be closely watched by residents.

Brentwood Borough Council's share would rise from £23m to £26.82m, with a £2.2m Football Foundation grant covering pitches and related facilities. Building tenders came in over budget, so the design was scaled back: the spa space becomes a health hub with dry changing rooms, the first-floor extension is now single-storey and the foyer has been redesigned to include football changing rooms. Officers describe the scheme as financially supportable but a significant commitment with material delivery risks.

The Assets and Strategy Committee will consider the final business case on Thursday 12 November, and every recommendation depends on Full Council agreeing the extra £3.82m. Start and completion dates are still to be confirmed; the council says the centre will stay open as far as possible, but tenants leasing space must move out before building begins.

Key points

  • Cost up to £29.02m, up from £25m
  • Council share rises to £26.82m
  • Spa dropped for a health hub
  • Committee decision on 12 November
