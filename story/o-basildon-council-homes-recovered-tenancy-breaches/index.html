Basildon Council takes back three homes in St Martin's, Billericay West and Lee Chapel North after serious tenancy breaches

The council's antisocial behaviour team and Essex Police worked together to recover the properties, which will be repaired and relet to households in need.

Basildon, Billericay · Monday 9 November 2026, 13:38 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Three council homes in Basildon and Billericay have been returned to the housing stock after serious breaches of tenancy, Your Thurrock reports. In each case the council served a notice seeking possession on mandatory grounds after information from Essex Police, and all three tenants gave up their tenancies, so no court hearing was needed. Cabinet member Andrew Baggott said the council would act firmly on serious breaches.

Key points

  • Homes are in St Martin's, Billericay West and Lee Chapel North
  • Tenants ended their tenancies after possession notices
  • Properties will be repaired and relet
