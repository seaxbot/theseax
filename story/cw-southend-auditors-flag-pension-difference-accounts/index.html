Auditors flag £1.7m pension difference in Southend's accounts but expect to finish on time

KPMG told councillors its audit of the 2025/26 accounts is on track, with work still under way on the value of council homes.

Southend-on-Sea · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 06:24 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council's external auditor, KPMG, reported on its audit of the council's accounts for the year ended 31 March 2026 at the Audit and Governance Committee on 19 October 2026. KPMG said the work was progressing well and remained on track to be completed ahead of the statutory deadline. The committee noted the report.

Councillors were told of a £1.7 million unadjusted audit difference relating to pension liabilities, along with several control findings on property valuations and declarations of related party interests. Some risks identified at the planning stage had been removed after testing, but work was continuing on the valuation of council dwellings and on journal testing. Members asked about a fall in the value of council homes, proposed changes to the audit fee and how related party declarations are handled.

KPMG confirmed that officers had continued to support the audit and said there were no concerns about meeting the January 2027 deadline. At the same meeting the committee heard that a national financial resilience index placed Southend 30th of 62 comparable unitary councils, which officers described as a broadly average position.

Key points

  • Unadjusted audit difference of £1.7 million relating to pension liabilities
  • Control findings on property valuations and related party declarations
  • Work ongoing on council dwelling valuations and journal testing
  • Audit expected to finish before the January 2027 deadline
