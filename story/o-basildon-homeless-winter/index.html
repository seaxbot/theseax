Basildon sets out winter homelessness help

The council has £312,582 from the national Rough Sleeping Programme for 2026/27, including money for a winter night shelter and severe weather beds.

Basildon · Saturday 31 October 2026, 10:00 · Updated Sunday 1 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • £312,582 from the government's Rough Sleeping Programme this year
  • Autumn 2025 snapshot found 27 people sleeping rough, the most since 2010
  • £85,000 to help 24 rough sleepers into shared housing
  • £12,000 for the BENS Winter Night Shelter
