Stansted flight to Sofia diverted to Plovdiv after bomb alert as hoax threats hit flights across Europe

A Ryanair service that left Stansted at 6am made an emergency landing in Bulgaria, one of at least a dozen European flights caught up in bomb alerts on Thursday.

Stansted · Thursday 5 November 2026, 11:06 · Reported by Yahoo News UK
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Ryanair flight from London Stansted to the Bulgarian capital Sofia was diverted on Thursday morning and made an emergency landing at Plovdiv after a bomb alert. Flight FR1730 had left the Essex airport at 6am and was due in Sofia at 9.25am, and it comes in a week when several flights linked to UK airports have been targeted.

Yahoo News UK reported that the Stansted service was one of at least 12 flights across Europe affected by bomb alerts on the same day, and the only one departing from or landing near London. Czech police said four flights in their country had received threats but rated them as of low relevance.

On Tuesday a LOT Polish Airlines flight from Heathrow made an emergency landing near Warsaw after a bomb alert that turned out to be a hoax, and the UK Government said UK flights that day had been the target of malicious hoax threats.

Key points

  • Ryanair FR1730 left Stansted at 6am for Sofia
  • Diverted to Plovdiv after a bomb alert
  • At least 12 European flights affected on Thursday
  • Government called Tuesday's threats malicious hoaxes
