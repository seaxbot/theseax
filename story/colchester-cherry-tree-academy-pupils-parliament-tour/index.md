# Colchester's Cherry Tree Academy pupils win tour of Parliament

**About two dozen Year 4 pupils from a Colchester primary school won a trip to Westminster through UK Parliament Week.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 06:29 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Education.

## Key points

- Parliament's education team says 2.9 million people took part in this year's UK Parliament Week

## The story

Cherry Tree Academy's eight- and nine-year-olds earned the visit after their constituency scored highly for taking part in UK Parliament Week activities, BBC News reported. On the tour they learned how the elected House of Commons differs from the appointed House of Lords.

Sitting on the green benches of St Stephen's Hall, the children debated smoking bans and littering fines, and a majority voted to make school holidays shorter. Deputy head Victoria Donnelly said news being so easy to find has more children asking why things happen.
