Colchester United edge Ipswich Town U21 on penalties after goalless EFL Trophy dead rubber

Thimothée Lo-Tutala saved twice in the shootout as the U's took the bonus point at the JobServe Community Stadium.

Colchester · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 22:12 · Reported by TWTD
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United and Ipswich Town U21 drew 0-0 on Tuesday night in a group game that mattered little, with both sides already out. Colchester won the shootout 4-3 to take an extra point, which lifted them above Town.

Paul Digby, Milton Oni, Jaden Williams and Adrian Akande scored their spot-kicks, while Lo-Tutala saved from Steven Turner and Josh Pitts.

Key points

  • Kane Vincent-Young had Colchester's only miss in the shootout
  • Ipswich U21 finish bottom of the group on one point
  • Jack Tucker captained the U's
