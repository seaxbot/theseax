# Aveley win 2-0 at Carshalton Athletic in Isthmian Premier

**The Thurrock club kept a clean sheet on the road, on a Saturday when none of the four Essex sides in the division lost.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Aveley, Essex, England. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 07:10 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Brentwood Town took all three points in north London
- Six goals were scored at Chatham Town
- Stanway Rovers picked up a home point against Burgess Hill Town

## The story

Aveley won 2-0 away to Carshalton Athletic in the Isthmian League Premier Division on Saturday 21 November 2026. Brentwood Town also won on their travels, by 2-1 at Wingate & Finchley.

Maldon & Tiptree came back from Chatham Town with a 3-3 draw. Stanway Rovers, at home, drew 2-2 with Burgess Hill Town.
