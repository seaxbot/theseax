# Colchester Institute students serve breakfast at Essex business festival

**Hospitality and professional cookery students from Colchester Institute fed delegates at the Essex Chambers of Commerce Festival of Business at Braxted Park.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Braxted Park, Great Braxted, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 14:08 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- The festival was held on Wednesday 11 November
- Patisserie students baked the pastries
- The college also sponsored and exhibited at the event
