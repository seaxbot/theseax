# Two Rushes Mead residents in Harlow charged with assault

**Rebecca Green, 37, and Adam Southall, 40, both of Rushes Mead, Harlow, have each been charged with assault by beating, Your Harlow reports from court records.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 13:03 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Adam Southall faces two counts of assault by beating
- Both were given conditional bail

## The story

The charges relate to 7 August 2026 in Harlow, according to official information from HM Courts and Tribunals Service published by Your Harlow. Rebecca Green is also charged with driving a Nissan Juke without due care and attention on Rushes Mead. Both appeared at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court and were given conditional bail. No pleas were reported.
