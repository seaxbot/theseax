Burnham-on-Crouch signs friendship pact with Carpathian town of Yaremche

Burnham-on-Crouch Town Council has become the first in Essex to twin with a Ukrainian community, linking the River Crouch town with Yaremche in the Ivano-Frankivsk region.

Burnham-on-Crouch · Thursday 12 November 2026, 13:03 · Reported by Lviv Herald
WhatsApp Facebook X

Burnham-on-Crouch Town Council held a twinning ceremony with Yaremche, a town on the River Prut in Ukraine's Carpathian Mountains, at 10am on Thursday 12 November 2026, the Lviv Herald reports. The event in the council chambers was set to include a memorandum of friendship and cooperation and an exchange of civic gifts.

The link was built by Councillor Stephen Stratton through talks with Yaremche's mayor, Andriy Myronyak. Burnham has been twinned with L'Aiguillon-sur-Mer in France since 1980.

Key points

  • Mayor Councillor Douglas Bown opened the ceremony
  • Yaremche relied on tourism before Russia's full-scale invasion and has housed displaced people
