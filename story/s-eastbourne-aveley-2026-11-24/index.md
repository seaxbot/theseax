# Aveley beaten 3-1 away at Isthmian Premier high-flyers Eastbourne

**Aveley hit the woodwork and briefly drew level in Sussex. Two goals either side of half-time settled it.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Aveley, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 14:54 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Eastbourne goalkeeper Lewis Carey denied Malachi Napa and Anthony Jeffery
- Eastbourne waved away penalty appeals early in the second half
- Next: AFC Whyteleafe away, 3pm kick-off

## The story

Aveley lost 3-1 away to Eastbourne Borough in the Isthmian Premier Division on Tuesday 24 November. The win lifted Eastbourne back up to third. Aveley stay 14th, with 23 points from 18 games.

Freddie Carter put the home side ahead in the 25th minute, shortly after Romel Aarons-Royal hit the woodwork for Aveley. Matt Johnson levelled for the visitors before the break. Josh Rees restored the lead on the stroke of half-time, and Kai Corbett made it three four minutes into the second half. Eastbourne also had a goal ruled out by the assistant referee's flag.

Your Thurrock noted that November has brought Aveley two league wins, two league defeats and exits from two cups. Their next game is away to AFC Whyteleafe on Saturday.
