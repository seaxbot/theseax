Chelmsford Chieftains lose 6-5 to Swindon Wildcats 2 after a shootout

Sasha Maltsev was named man of the match after scoring twice, including a shorthanded goal, at the Link Centre in West Swindon.

Chelmsford · Sunday 8 November 2026, 08:23 · Reported by Chelmsford Chieftains
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford Chieftains were beaten 6-5 by Swindon Wildcats 2 on Saturday night, with the NIHL National game settled by a penalty shootout. The two sides could not be separated through regulation and overtime, finishing level at 5-5.

Grant Bartlett, Lane Paddison twice and Sasha Maltsev twice scored for the Chieftains. Maltsev's second goal came while Chelmsford were shorthanded, and he was named the visitors' man of the match.

The sides were level at 1-1 after the first period, and Swindon held a 4-3 lead going into the third before Chelmsford drew level at 5-5.

Key points

  • Swindon Wildcats 2 6-5 Chelmsford Chieftains, decided on penalties
  • Level at 5-5 after overtime
  • Maltsev scored twice, one shorthanded; Paddison also scored twice
