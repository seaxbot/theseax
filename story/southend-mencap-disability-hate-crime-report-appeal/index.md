# Southend Mencap urges disabled people to report hate crime

**Southend Mencap's new director says disabled people in the city have been spat at and pressured into handing over cash.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 06:30 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Home Office figures record 10,140 disability hate crimes in England and Wales in the year to March 2026
- Paxman worked as a legal mediator and in banking before joining the charity

## The story

Sharon Paxman, director of Southend Mencap since September, told BBC Essex presenter Ian Puckey that disabled people in and around Southend-on-Sea have been intimidated, spat at and pushed to take money out of cash machines. In one case a woman was pushed out of her wheelchair, which she called "absolutely heartbreaking and completely unacceptable".

Her charity already takes hate crime referrals from Essex Police and wants to build on that link. Essex Police said it solved 560 hate crimes across the county last year, 25% more than five years earlier, and urged victims to come forward.
