# Tendring's first council housing report flags repairs and complaints

**Tendring District Council's first annual report on its council housing says tenant engagement has improved, but repairs satisfaction and complaint handling still need work. A public summary for tenants is planned.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Clacton, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- First report of its kind for Tendring District Council, covering 2025/26
- Areas to improve include repairs satisfaction, complaint handling and data quality
- The final report and a public summary for tenants will be published
- The report says it has no direct financial implications

## The story

Tendring District Council's Cabinet noted the council's first Annual Housing Improvement, Performance & Assurance Report 2025/26 on Friday 13 November 2026. The report covers how the council performs as a landlord to its tenants. Cabinet treated it as a draft and backed the improvement priorities it sets for 2026/27.

The report says good progress has been made in tenant engagement, governance, tenancy sustainment, staff development and understanding tenants' diverse needs. It also names areas that still need to improve: repairs satisfaction, complaint handling, data quality, new systems and settling in the revised Housing Service structure. Tenant Satisfaction Measures run through the report, which is meant to give evidence to tenants, Cabinet and the Regulator of Social Housing.

Cabinet gave the Corporate Director (Operations & Delivery) the power to publish the final report, in consultation with the Portfolio Holder for Housing & Planning. A shorter public summary for tenants and the wider public will show both good performance and areas of concern, the report says. It adds that the report has no direct financial cost and that extra capacity from the recently approved Housing Service restructure will support the work.
