Brentwood Town edge Burgess Hill Town 1-0 in Isthmian Premier

Brentwood · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 18:20 · Reported by Isthmian League
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brentwood Town, who had taken one point from their previous four matches, climb out of the Isthmian Premier Division's bottom four.

Alfie Cerulli scored eight minutes before half-time at Burgess Hill Town on Tuesday 17 November 2026, and Brentwood held on in front of 498 spectators. The Sussex side had wanted revenge for their opening-day defeat by Brentwood, the Isthmian League reported.

Burgess Hill are 13th. Brentwood go to Wingate & Finchley on Saturday 21 November.

Key points

  • Winning goal: Alfie Cerulli
  • Brentwood now 18th on 19 points
  • Attendance: 498
