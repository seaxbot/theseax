£12m recycling plant upgrade lets Braintree district households put glass out at the kerbside

Biffa's revamped sorting plant near Ipswich will handle the district's new two-bin recycling system, including glass bottles and jars.

Braintree, Witham · Thursday 5 November 2026, 16:37 · Reported by Braintree District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Braintree District Council says a £12 million upgrade of Biffa's Masons materials recovery facility near Ipswich has been completed, paving the way for the district's new kerbside recycling system. The redeveloped plant can now take extra materials, including glass bottles and jars, which residents have not previously been able to put out at the kerbside.

Biffa put in £6m, Suffolk County Council £5m and Braintree District Council £1m, using money from the government's packaging Extended Producer Responsibility scheme. Once fully running the plant will process about 65,000 tonnes a year, and new fire detection and suppression systems have been fitted to tackle fires caused by vapes and batteries thrown away in the wrong bin. Steve Cole, managing director of Biffa Municipal, said one of the biggest benefits was that residents could recycle glass at the kerbside.

Under the Simpler Recycling system, households use a red-lidded bin for paper and card and a blue-lidded bin for glass, plastics and cans. Councillor Peter Schwier, the council's cabinet member for operations and pride in place, welcomed the upgrade. The council has not given a start date for the new collections in its announcement.

Key points

  • £12m upgrade of Biffa's Masons plant near Ipswich
  • Funded by Biffa (£6m), Suffolk County Council (£5m) and Braintree District Council (£1m)
  • About 65,000 tonnes a year once fully operational
  • Red-lidded bin for paper and card, blue-lidded bin for glass, plastics and cans
