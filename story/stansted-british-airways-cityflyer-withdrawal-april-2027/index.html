Stansted to lose British Airways next spring after ten years, with Nice and Palma among routes cut

BA CityFlyer is leaving the Uttlesford airport after the winter ski season, and travellers to Florence will be left with no direct summer flight.

Stansted · Thursday 12 November 2026, 16:13 · Reported by Chelmsford City Hub
WhatsApp Facebook X

The last scheduled British Airways flights from Stansted leave on Sunday 11 April 2027, Chelmsford City Hub reports. The airline's summer timetable for next year drops all five of its warm-weather routes from the airport: Bergerac, Nice, Florence, Olbia in Sardinia and Palma de Mallorca.

Nice had been flown daily, Palma three times a day and Olbia twice a week, according to Simple Flying. Most of that demand will not be stranded: Ryanair plans to keep flying from Stansted to Bergerac, Nice, Olbia and Palma next summer, and Jet2 and TUI serve some of the same airports.

British Airways said it regularly reviews its network against demand and will use the aircraft elsewhere. It has not said whether the routes were losing money. CityFlyer first flew from Stansted in 2016, stopped during the Covid-19 pandemic and came back in 2024; its main base remains London City Airport.

Key points

  • Ryanair, Jet2 and TUI still fly to most of the same destinations
  • Geneva and Chambéry ski flights run until the winter season ends
  • BA CityFlyer's main base stays at London City Airport
