Grays Athletic bring back Darren Manning as director of football

Grays · Tuesday 24 November 2026, 14:40 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Grays Athletic have brought back a former manager in a new boardroom role. He left in February with the Thurrock club fighting relegation.

Grays Athletic announced on Monday 23 November that Darren Manning, the club's first-team manager for three years, is returning as director of football, Your Thurrock reported. He will work with chairman Lee Vehit and vice-chairman Gavin Holland on recruitment, player development and links with the club's Community Academy.

Lewis Clark has managed the Isthmian North side since October, when Mitch Hahn and Ash Miller left.

  • Club statement issued on Monday 23 November
  • Lewis Clark stays in charge of the first team
