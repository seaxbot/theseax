Police dispersal order covers central Southend on match day and Christmas lights night

Essex Police cited intelligence about possible trouble between groups of young supporters of Southend United and Aldershot Town.

Southend-on-Sea · Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:01 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police can order people to leave much of central Southend today, Saturday 14 November, under a dispersal order that came into force at 9am ahead of Southend United's 3pm home game against Aldershot Town.

Essex Police said the order follows intelligence about the potential for disorder involving groups of youth supporters linked to both clubs. The match falls on the same day as Southend's Christmas lights switch-on, and the force expects the city centre to be especially busy with families, children and visitors, as well as people using shops, pubs and public transport.

The zone, shown on a police map, runs from Prittlewell in the north to the seafront at Western Esplanade and takes in Southend Victoria and Southend Central stations. It gives officers powers to step in early where trouble looks likely, and the force said anyone who does not follow officers' directions will face arrest.

Essex Police said it wants everyone coming into Southend to enjoy their Saturday without their plans being disrupted by a small minority.

Key points

  • In force from 9am, six hours before kick-off
  • Zone stretches from Prittlewell to the seafront
  • Anyone ignoring officers' directions faces arrest
