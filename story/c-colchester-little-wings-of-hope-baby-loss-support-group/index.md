# Little Wings of Hope starts Colchester baby loss support group

**Bereaved parents in Colchester will soon have a monthly group to meet others who understand. It starts in January.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Meets on the third Thursday of each month
- Venue is St Anne's Community Centre
- For stillbirth, neonatal death and other baby loss

## The story

Little Wings of Hope, a baby loss charity, will hold the first meeting of a monthly peer support group in Colchester on Thursday 21 January 2027, as first reported by the Daily Gazette. The group is for families affected by stillbirth, neonatal death and other kinds of baby loss. It will meet at St Anne's Community Centre on the third Thursday of every month. Its founder, Leila Hobart, wants it to be a welcoming place for families to share what they have been through.

The charity is registered in England and Wales and supports bereaved parents across Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk and Kent. Its website says it has worked with Colchester Hospital and Broomfield Hospital in Chelmsford, giving bereaved parents memory journals and candle holders, and that it also runs online support groups and a befriending service. Families can contact it at support@littlewingsofhope.co.uk.
