Slim Chickens to open at Lakeside in early December with 50 jobs

The chicken restaurant chain is taking a Level 1 unit next to Wingstop, its fourth Essex site after Basildon, Chelmsford and Colchester.

West Thurrock & Grays · Friday 6 November 2026, 17:10 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Slim Chickens is set to open a new restaurant at Lakeside Shopping Centre in West Thurrock in early December, creating 50 jobs. The restaurant will be on Level 1, next to Wingstop.

The branch is being run by franchise operator JRK Restaurants and will be its ninth Slim Chickens site, joining Essex branches in Basildon, Chelmsford and Colchester. General manager Jitenda Thakur said the team was thrilled to be opening at Lakeside. The menu centres on buttermilk-marinated chicken tenders and wings, with dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

The chain, founded in Fayetteville, Arkansas, has been expanding across the UK. Your Thurrock reports that new app users get free food and rewards, and students get 20% off from Sunday to Thursday.

Key points

  • Opening early December 2026
  • 50 new jobs
  • Level 1, next to Wingstop
  • Ninth site for operator JRK Restaurants
