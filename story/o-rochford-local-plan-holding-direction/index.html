Rochford leader urges ministers to lift block on Local Plan and allow more time to consult

A Government holding direction in place since 2 October stops the council starting its final round of public consultation.

Rochford, Hawkwell · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Rochford District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rochford District Council has called on the Government to decide quickly on its Local Plan and lift the holding direction that has paused it since 2 October. The council says it cannot start the final public consultation while the direction stays in place, and is also asking for the submission deadline to be extended, which it believes could run to March.

Council leader Cllr Adi Malviya said the district needs an up-to-date plan to avoid speculative applications in the wrong places. The council says it sent the evidence ministers requested on 7 October, and that before the direction it was on track to submit by the 31 December deadline.

If the direction is lifted without an extension, the council says consultation would probably run over Christmas and close on the day the plan must be submitted, leaving councillors no time to consider responses. Separately, the public inquiry into the Mount Bovers appeal on land west of Main Road, Hawkwell, resumes on 8 December.

Key points

  • Council sent requested evidence on 7 October
  • It wants the submission deadline pushed back, possibly to March
  • Hawkwell appeal inquiry resumes on 8 December
