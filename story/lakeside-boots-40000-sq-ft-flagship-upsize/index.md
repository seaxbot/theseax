# Lakeside's Boots to grow into one of the chain's biggest stores

**Boots is expanding its Lakeside shop into a single-floor flagship. It will be one of the largest Boots stores in the UK.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: West Thurrock, Lakeside, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 10:15 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- The new store will be on a single level
- Boots has been at Lakeside for almost 35 years
- No opening date has been announced

## The story

Boots will turn its existing store at Lakeside Shopping Centre in West Thurrock into a 40,000 sq ft flagship on one level, Completely Retail News reported on Tuesday 24 November. That is a little over half a football pitch.

Boots has traded at the centre for almost 35 years. No opening date or jobs figure has been given. Lakeside's asset manager, Pradera, called the centre the dominant shopping destination for the region.
