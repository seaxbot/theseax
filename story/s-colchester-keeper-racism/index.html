Walsall pledge support for Colchester keeper after alleged racist remark

An investigation is under way into a report of discriminatory language aimed at a Colchester United player during Saturday's League Two game against Walsall.

Colchester · Sunday 1 November 2026, 10:00 · Reported by BBC Sport
WhatsApp Facebook X

Walsall have said they will support Colchester United goalkeeper Thimothee Lo-Tutala after a fan reportedly made a racist comment during Saturday's League Two match between the sides. A report of discriminatory language directed at a Colchester player is now being investigated by the relevant authorities.

Walsall won the game 3-1. In a joint statement, the two clubs said "discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated" and that no one, in football or wider society, should have to face it. Anyone found responsible could face arrest or a football banning order.

No details have been given of who made the report or what action has been taken so far, and the investigation is continuing. Walsall's offer of support for Lo-Tutala sits alongside the clubs' joint condemnation of the alleged abuse.

Key points

  • The incident is said to have happened during Saturday's League Two fixture
  • Walsall won the match 3-1
  • Both clubs issued a joint statement condemning discrimination
  • The investigation is ongoing
