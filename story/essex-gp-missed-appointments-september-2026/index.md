# Essex patients missed almost 37,000 GP surgery appointments in September

**It is the highest monthly figure this year. NHS Essex is asking patients to cancel appointments they cannot make.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 14:15 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Health.

## Key points

- About one in every 28 appointments offered went unused
- Missed GP appointments cost an estimated £465,150
- Cancel through the NHS App or by phoning your surgery

## The story

Patients missed 36,925 appointments at GP surgeries across Essex in September, the highest monthly total so far this year, according to NHS figures.

The figures come from the county's Local Medical Committees, which represent GPs, and were published by NHS Essex, the body that plans and pays for health services in the county. They count appointments booked at surgeries where the patient did not turn up and did not cancel.

Surgeries offered 1,022,806 appointments in September, so about one in every 28 went unused. Of those missed, 11,075 were with GPs and 25,430 with nurses and other practice staff, such as clinical pharmacists and physiotherapists. For another 420, the type of staff was not recorded.

That is a little below the national rate. Across England, about one GP surgery appointment in 23 (4.3%) was missed in 2025, according to NHS England. The figures are not measured in exactly the same way: Essex's count is set against appointments offered, England's against appointments recorded.

Missed video and online appointments with GPs have risen sharply. There were 788 in September, more than four times the 171 missed in January.

NHS Essex puts the cost of the missed GP appointments at £465,150 for September and about £3.5 million since January. It works this out at £42 an appointment, an estimate of the doctor time and resources involved, and says it is not a direct financial loss to surgeries.

Dr Ian Perry, a GP and primary care partner member at NHS Essex, said people were often frustrated at not being able to get an appointment. "These are appointments that could have been given to someone else," he said.

"If you no longer need your appointment or cannot make it, please cancel in good time by using the NHS app or phoning your practice. That way, we can offer it to someone else who needs it."

September was one of five months this year in which Essex surgeries offered more than a million appointments, along with January, March, June and July. More than 45% of appointments took place on the day they were booked, and about two in three were face to face.

NHS Essex is also adding appointments to cope with winter. [Surgeries are due to offer 32,000 extra appointments this winter](https://theseax.com/story/essex-nhs-32000-extra-gp-appointments-winter-2026/), as The Seax reported on Thursday.

Appointments can be cancelled through the NHS App or by phoning your surgery. Anyone who still needs to be seen can ask the practice to rearrange.
