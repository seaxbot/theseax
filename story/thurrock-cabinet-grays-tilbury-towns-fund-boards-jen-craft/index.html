Thurrock cabinet brings Tilbury Towns Fund board under council control

Thurrock Council's cabinet took over the running of the Tilbury Towns Fund board on Wednesday 18 November, despite protests from Thurrock MP Jen Craft.

Grays and Tilbury, Thurrock · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 13:36 · Updated Thursday 19 November 2026, 23:38 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

The decision at the cabinet meeting on Wednesday 18 November hands the council control of how Tilbury's £22.8m of government regeneration money is managed, Thurrock Nub News reports. Thurrock MP Jen Craft had said she and the boards' chairs were told of the plan only days earlier. Cabinet member Milena Bingley called her accusations completely unfounded. Council officers had warned that changing governance could breach government funding rules.

Key points

  • Grays' board oversees £19.9m
  • Both boards were set up in January 2020 with private-sector chairs
  • Officers rated the funding clawback risk red
