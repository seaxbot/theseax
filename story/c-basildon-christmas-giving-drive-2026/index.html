Basildon Christmas Giving Drive asks for £5 vouchers by 4 December

Basildon Council is collecting gift vouchers worth £5 or more, supermarket vouchers above all, to pass to families and vulnerable residents through local charities.

Basildon · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Runs until 4 December
  • Drop-offs include the Basildon Centre, Gateway 97.8 studio and Billericay and Wickford town councils
  • Drop-off hours Monday to Friday, 10am to noon
