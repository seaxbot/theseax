# Epping Forest council crushes fly-tipping truck in a first for the district

**A truck linked to a large fly-tip on Epping Forest heathland has been crushed. It is the council's first use of the power.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Epping Forest, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 09:20 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Environment.

## Key points

- Truck seized in August, months after the January dump
- City of London Corporation and Essex Police helped
- Report fly-tipping on 01992 564000

## The story

Epping Forest District Council crushed the Ford Transit truck on 11 November and announced it on Tuesday 24 November. It is the first vehicle the council has seized and destroyed over fly-tipping and illegal waste offences in the district.

According to the council, the truck was involved in a fly-tip in January, when more than a tonne of construction and demolition waste was dumped on heathland in the forest. It was seized in August after its driver was arrested on a separate matter.

The City of London Corporation, which owns and manages Epping Forest, spotted the truck's link to the January dump and contacted the district council. The council holds the legal power to seize and keep vehicles, and Essex Police helped with the operation.

## A tougher line on dumping

Councillor Darshan Sunger, the council's portfolio holder for public protection and environment, said crushing the truck sent a clear message. "We will not hesitate to do it again," he said. He added that the council would use CCTV, fines and vehicle seizures against offenders, and would prosecute where it had evidence.

Councils have been given renewed government guidance urging them to destroy vehicles used for illegal dumping, the council said. It warned that fly-tippers risk fines of up to £5,000 as well as losing their vehicles. Earlier in November, a motion to full council asked for the district's top fixed penalty to rise from £1,000, [as The Seax reported](https://theseax.com/story/cw-epping-fly-tipping-fine-5000-motion/).

## How to report it

Anyone who sees fly-tipping should not confront the people involved or touch the waste, which can hide broken glass, needles or asbestos. A crime happening now should be reported on 999. Dumped waste can be reported to the council online at any time, or on 01992 564000 during office hours.
