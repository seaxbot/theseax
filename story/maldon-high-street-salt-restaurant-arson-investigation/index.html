Maldon High Street arson: police appeal for information

Maldon · Updated Friday 27 November 2026, 12:48 · First published Friday 27 November 2026, 12:26 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Two people tried to break into a High Street business and start a fire at about 2.30am. Salt restaurant says it is open as usual.

Essex Police is investigating reports of an arson in Maldon High Street in the early hours of Friday.

Emergency services were called at about 2.30am to reports that two people had tried to force their way into a business and start a fire before leaving the area, the force said.

Officers and firefighters went to the scene to make it safe. Police said enquiries are continuing.

Salt Bar & Restaurant says it is open as usual. Its manager, Robert Muca, said a gas meter seen in pictures of the damage has nothing to do with the restaurant. "The gas meter serves the residential flats above and is entirely unrelated to Salt Bar & Restaurant," he told Maldon Community Hub.

"We remain open and are operating as usual without any disruption."

Sark Restaurant & Cocktail Bar, next door, posted on Facebook earlier, before Salt commented. It said it had been "a difficult evening for our neighbours at SALT following an unfortunate break-in and attempted fire at their restaurant".

Sark said its staff were there to lend a hand. "As a community, it's important that we stand together and support one another, especially during difficult times," it said.

Sark said it had not been affected and was open from 11am as usual.

Police have not said whether anyone was hurt or whether any arrests have been made.

Anyone with information, including dashcam, doorbell or CCTV footage from the High Street overnight, should contact Essex Police online or on 101, quoting incident 83 of Friday 27 November. Information can also be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Key points

  • Police log: incident 83 of Friday 27 November
  • Salt and next-door Sark are both open
