# Stansted Airport College welcomes Kemi Badenoch for student talks

**More than 15,000 Harlow residents are classed as economically inactive, as the Conservative leader met students learning airport trades.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stansted, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 11:10 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45. Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- College opened in 2018 at the end of Stansted's runway
- Partnership between the airport and Harlow College
- College trains 500+ young people a year

## The story

Your Harlow reported that Kemi Badenoch, the Conservative leader and MP for North West Essex, visited Stansted Airport College on the afternoon of Friday 20 November. She went to see how its students move from education and training into work. Harlow Council leader Dan Swords and Harlow College principal Karen Spencer joined her as she toured the workshops, before she sat down with students for a roundtable.

The visit came in a week when young people not in education, employment or training, and the number of people out of work and not looking for a job, were in the news. Your Harlow says more than 15,000 Harlow residents are classed as economically inactive. Mrs Badenoch gave the site an interview on jobs, apprenticeships and welfare.

The college, at the end of Stansted's runway, opened in September 2018 as a partnership between London Stansted Airport and Harlow College, the first of its kind at a UK airport. It trains more than 500 young people a year in aviation engineering and other airport skills, and the airport, in Mrs Badenoch's constituency, has said its expansion plans would let the college take more students.
