Young jobless claimants in Brentwood reach highest level since early 2022

In Brentwood, 225 people aged 18 to 24 were on the claimant count in October 2026, up 18.4% on a year earlier and roughly double the rise across England.

Brentwood · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 08:39 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

Across all ages the borough had 1,245 claimants in October, equal to 2.6% of residents aged 16 to 64, Office for National Statistics figures reported by Brentwood Online show. Every Essex district had more claimants than a year earlier. Uttlesford had the biggest overall rise, at 9.4%, and only Uttlesford, Tendring and Maldon saw young claimant numbers grow faster than Brentwood.

Key points

  • England's young claimant count rose 9.1%
  • The pandemic peak, in February 2021, was 450
  • Local figures are rounded to the nearest five
