Billericay switches on its Christmas lights on 26 November, with a 150-stall market to follow

Billericay's town centre business group is staging a free late-night shopping evening on the High Street, and a Rotary club is running a free-entry market in December.

Billericay · Thursday 12 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Phoenix FM
WhatsApp Facebook X

Billericay Town Council's chairman and Father Christmas will switch on the High Street lights at 5.30pm on Thursday 26 November, followed by a carol singalong, Phoenix FM reports. Your Billericay, the town's Business Improvement District, runs the evening from 4pm to 8pm with fairground rides and the Billericay Lions sleigh, and the top of the High Street will be closed to traffic.

The Rotary Club of Billericay Mayflower's market follows on Sunday 6 December, from 11am to 5pm, with a stage, Santa's parade and a free grotto.

Key points

  • Free parking for the market at Billericay Station, Radford Way, Lake Meadows and Sun Corner
  • The Rotary club says it has raised more than £500,000 in its first 13 years
