# Aveley out of Velocity Cup after 4-3 defeat at Cheshunt

**The Thurrock club were one of three Essex sides knocked out of the Isthmian League cup in the third round, with Stanway Rovers and Grays Athletic both beaten 3-0.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Aveley, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 13:44 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Kymani Thomas scored twice for Aveley
- Cheshunt led 3-2 at half-time
- Stanway changed goalkeeper after 19 minutes at Dulwich

## The story

Aveley went 2-0 down inside 20 minutes against Cheshunt at Theobalds Lane on Tuesday 17 November 2026, after goals from Tommy Wood and Raul Fernandes Da Silva. Kymani Thomas replied on 28 minutes and, after Kenny Coker's penalty, Anthony Jeffrey made it 3-2 in the last minute of the first half, Your Thurrock reports.

Joe Re restored Cheshunt's two-goal cushion on 51 minutes. Thomas struck again on 66 minutes, but the Millers could not find a leveller and lose their grip on the Alan Turvey Trophy, which they last won in 2023. Jamie Grossart was back in goal and William Turner-Smith made his debut.

Stanway Rovers lost at Dulwich Hamlet in front of 414 at Champion Hill, where Amadou Kassarate and Michael Chambers were among the scorers, and Grays Athletic were beaten at Newmarket Town. All three are back in league action on Saturday 21 November: Aveley at Carshalton Athletic, Stanway at home to Burgess Hill Town and Grays at Brightlingsea Regent.
