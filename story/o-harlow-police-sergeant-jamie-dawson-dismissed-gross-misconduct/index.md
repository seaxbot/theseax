# Harlow police sergeant sacked over comments about a detainee

**An Essex Police sergeant has been dismissed for gross misconduct. A hearing found he made derogatory remarks about someone held at Harlow Police Station.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 18:05 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- Outcome: dismissal without notice

## The story

Sergeant Jamie Dawson has been dismissed without notice after a misconduct hearing found gross misconduct proven, Your Harlow reported on Wednesday 25 November. The case concerned derogatory, unprofessional and inappropriate comments he made on duty about a detainee at Harlow Police Station.

He had been accused of breaching the police standards of professional behaviour covering authority, respect and courtesy.
