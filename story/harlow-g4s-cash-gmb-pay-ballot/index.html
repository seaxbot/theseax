GMB opens strike ballot at G4S Cash in Harlow, raising prospect of action before Christmas

Staff at the cash handling site are under a pay freeze, the union says, and voting runs until midday on 25 November.

Harlow · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 12:39 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

GMB members at G4S Cash in Harlow have begun voting on industrial action after a two-year pay claim lodged in September made no satisfactory progress, Your Harlow reports. The union wants an urgent pay meeting, a deal for 2026 and a firm offer for 2027, and says action could follow in the run-up to Christmas if the company does not return to talks.
