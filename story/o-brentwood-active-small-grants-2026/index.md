# Brentwood offers small grants for projects that get residents active

**Brentwood groups with an idea to get people moving can bid for council funding. Applications close in January.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 09:35 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Closing date: Thursday 7 January
- Open to residents as well as clubs and groups
- Aims to remove barriers to taking part

## The story

Brentwood Borough Council opened its Active Brentwood Small Grants Scheme on Wednesday 25 November. Residents and community groups can apply for money for sport sessions, walking and cycling groups, fitness classes and activities for older people. The council says it especially wants ideas that help people of all ages and abilities who find it hard to take part.

Councillor Dr Tim Barrett, who chairs the council's housing, health and community committee, said the grants were "a fantastic opportunity" for local groups. Details and the application form are on the council's Active Brentwood page.
