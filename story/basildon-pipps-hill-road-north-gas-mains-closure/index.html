Gas mains work shuts Pipps Hill Road North near Basildon for almost four weeks from today

Cadent is replacing gas mains, with two more nearby roads due to close from 16 November.

Basildon, Crays Hill · Sunday 8 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Pipps Hill Road North and Hardings Elm Road, between Oak Road and the A127, close from Monday 9 November for about 26 days under Essex County Council orders so Cadent can replace gas mains, Basildon Today reports. Signed diversions use the A127, A132, A129 and A176.

Wash Road and the Crays Hill end of Hardings Elms Road are due to follow from 16 November, and the notices warn dates may change.

Key points

  • About 780 metres closed from 9 November
  • Reopening estimated around 4 December
  • Further closures nearby from 16 November
