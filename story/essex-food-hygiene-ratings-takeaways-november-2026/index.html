One in three Essex takeaways misses top food hygiene rating

Essex · Friday 27 November 2026, 13:20 · Reported by Food Standards Agency
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Seax checked every food hygiene rating in Essex. Takeaways fare worst, and Tendring's lag the rest of the county.

Every restaurant, takeaway, pub and cafe in England is given a food hygiene rating from 0 to 5 by its local council. Inspectors look at how food is handled, the state of the kitchen and how well the business manages food safety. A 5 means very good. A 0 means urgent improvement is necessary.

The ratings are public, but businesses in England do not have to display them. With the Christmas party season starting, The Seax has gone through every rating held by Essex's 14 councils, covering 15,436 food businesses, as published by the Food Standards Agency on 27 November.

Most places to eat out in Essex do well. Of the 6,732 restaurants, cafes, takeaways, pubs, hotels and mobile caterers with a rating, 5,289 (79%) have the top score. Only 159 (2%) were rated 2 or below.

Takeaways are the exception. Of 1,254 rated takeaways and sandwich shops, 839 have a 5. That leaves 415, one in three, below the top rating. For restaurants, cafes and pubs the figure is about one in five.

Most of those 415 takeaways are not failing. 232 were rated 4, meaning good, and 152 were rated 3, generally satisfactory. Thirty-one were rated 2 or below.

Where you live makes a difference. In Tendring, 62 of 135 rated takeaways (46%) have a 5. It is the only Essex council area where fewer than half do. Thurrock is next lowest at 53%.

Castle Point does best: 66 of its 74 rated takeaways (89%) have a 5. Uttlesford (87%) and Maldon (82%) follow.

Tendring also has the lowest share of places to eat rated 5 overall, at 59.5%, and the most rated 2 or below, with 28. Rochford has the highest share of low ratings: 15 of its 258 rated places to eat (5.8%). Castle Point has none rated below 3.

Seven places to eat out were rated 0, urgent improvement necessary, at their most recent inspection. In Basildon, De-Source Africa Restaurant/ Miolad on Southernhay was inspected in February 2026.

In Colchester, Coffee Link at Colchester railway station was rated 0 in April 2025 and Bees Indian Restaurant & Bar in the High Street in October 2026. In Southend, Pallavas in London Road was rated 0 in September 2026.

In Clacton, Efes BBQ in Old Road was rated 0 in June 2026 and Welcome 168 Ltd in North Road in September 2026. The Bull And Willow Ltd, which runs The Bull in Great Totham, was rated 0 in September 2026 and has a new rating pending.

A rating shows what inspectors found on the day. Businesses can make improvements and ask the council to inspect again, and some may already have done so. The Food Standards Agency's website always shows the latest rating.

There is also a waiting list. 418 places to eat out in Essex are still waiting for their first inspection, usually because they have recently opened or changed hands. Colchester has the most, with 80, followed by Uttlesford with 69.

Some ratings are old, too. 184 places to eat were last inspected more than five years ago. Councils set how often they inspect each business according to the risk it poses.

How we did this: The Seax downloaded every food hygiene rating held by Essex's 14 councils from the Food Standards Agency on 27 November 2026. We checked the totals against the agency's own daily data files for each council. Places to eat out are the agency's restaurant, cafe and canteen, takeaway and sandwich shop, pub and bar, hotel and B&B, and mobile caterer categories. Businesses that are exempt, awaiting inspection or awaiting publication are left out of the percentages.

If you are worried about hygiene at a food business, you can report it to the local council through the Food Standards Agency's website.

Key points

  • Fewer than half of Tendring's takeaways are rated 5
  • Seven places to eat rated 0, urgent improvement necessary
  • 418 places to eat still await a first inspection
  • Castle Point has no place to eat rated below 3
