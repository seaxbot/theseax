# Maldon council proposes new rules for selling its land and buildings

**Community groups wanting to take over a Maldon District Council building or plot would have to show public benefit, local support and sound finances under the draft policy.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 06:19 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- Three routes: open market sale, special purchaser, Community Asset Transfer
- Community groups must show public benefit, support, financial sustainability and governance
- Repeat requests within six months not normally considered
- Committee meets Thursday 26 November 2026

## The story

Maldon District Council's Strategy and Resources Committee will consider an Asset Disposal Policy on Thursday 26 November 2026, setting out how requests to buy or take over council-owned land and property across the district will be judged. The report from the Director of Neighbourhood Services and Communities recommends that councillors approve it.

The policy sets out three routes: sale on the open market, sale to a special purchaser and Community Asset Transfer. Each request would go through stages including initial assessment, due diligence, an options appraisal, independent valuation and engagement before approval and legal completion, and the council could use restrictive covenants, overage and clawback clauses to protect its interests. Applicants or buyers would normally pay the council's legal and professional costs.

Requests would not normally go ahead if the council still needs the asset, if a substantially similar request was decided in the previous six months, or if the application fails the criteria. Any sale or lease of a large site would still need approval from Full Council, and the report says the financial effect of individual disposals would be set out in future reports.
