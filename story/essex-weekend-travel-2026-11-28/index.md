# Buses replace trains across south Essex this weekend

**Rail passengers from Shenfield, Southend and the Crouch Valley face replacement buses on Saturday and Sunday. Dartford Crossing works are paused for Black Friday.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 15:40 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- West tunnel works resume at 9pm on 3 December
- Underground tickets accepted from Newbury Park
- Melbourne Avenue in Chelmsford shut overnight from 30 November

## The story

Rail passengers across south Essex face a weekend of replacement buses on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November. Transport for London says no Elizabeth line trains will run between Paddington and Shenfield on either day. Buses run instead from Shenfield to Chadwell Heath and Newbury Park, and from Stratford to Romford.

## Greater Anglia

Network Rail is also maintaining the Southend Victoria and Southminster lines. Buses replace trains from Southend Victoria and from Ingatestone to Newbury Park, the Central line station in east London, and between Southminster and Wickford. Trains from Norwich, Ipswich and Clacton run only as far as Shenfield, where passengers change to buses via Ingatestone. Underground tickets are accepted between Newbury Park and Stratford or Liverpool Street, as [The Seax reported on Monday](https://theseax.com/story/southend-victoria-southminster-lines-rail-buses-28-29-november/).

## Roads

In Wickford, Station Approach is shut on both days, and First Essex's 13, 13A, 13B and X10 buses take a diversion. Drivers heading for Kent get a clear run at night. National Highways says it will not close the Dartford Crossing tunnels or the QEII Bridge overnight from Friday 27 November until 3 December. The pause is to help shoppers, hauliers and delivery firms through Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

## Next week

Overnight works in the Dartford west tunnel resume at 9pm on 3 December, and the east tunnel follows from 6 December. In Chelmsford, Melbourne Avenue shuts from 8pm to 5am between 30 November and 2 December for roadworks, with First Essex's C2 and C3 diverted. Looking further ahead, Greater Anglia trains will not run between Shenfield and London Liverpool Street from 27 December to 3 January.
