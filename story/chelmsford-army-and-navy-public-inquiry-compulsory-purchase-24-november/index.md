# Chelmsford's £81m Army and Navy scheme faces land inquiry from 24 November

**The £81m scheme would replace the roundabout with a "hamburger" design, and the Chelmer Valley Park and Ride expansion has already been dropped to save money.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Army and Navy roundabout, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 10:56 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45. Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Inquiry opens at County Hall at 10am on Tuesday
- The inspector reports to the Transport Secretary, who decides
- 60% of consultation replies backed the hamburger design

## The story

Chelmsford City Hub reported that a public inquiry into Essex County Council's plans to buy land for the £81m Army and Navy scheme was due to open at 10am on Tuesday 24 November. The venue was the fourth floor of County Hall. A government-appointed inspector will hear objections to the council's compulsory purchase order and side roads order, then report to the Transport Secretary, who decides whether to confirm them.

The scheme would replace the roundabout with a "hamburger" roundabout, with a road running through its middle, alongside walking, cycling, bus and Park and Ride improvements. It won planning permission in November 2024, and the government confirmed it would go ahead after its spending review. When the council consulted, 60% of more than 840 people who replied chose the hamburger design.

The council published compulsory purchase orders in case it could not agree terms with landowners, and warned early on that objections could lead to an inquiry. Objections now cover land acquisition, environmental mitigation, speed limits, a business car park and land for expanding Sandon Park and Ride. Plans to expand the Chelmer Valley Park and Ride were dropped from the scheme this year to keep it within budget after construction costs rose.
