Public inquiry into land for Chelmsford's £81m Army and Navy scheme opens next week

A government-appointed inspector will hear objections to the land deals and road changes Essex County Council needs to replace the Army and Navy roundabout in Chelmsford.

Chelmsford, Army and Navy roundabout · Monday 16 November 2026, 10:56 · Reported by Chelmsford City Hub
WhatsApp Facebook X

The inquiry opens at 10am on Tuesday 24 November on the fourth floor of County Hall, Chelmsford, and covers the council's compulsory purchase and side roads orders, Chelmsford City Hub reports. Planning permission came in November 2024; the inspector will report to the Transport Secretary, who decides whether to confirm the orders.

Objections cover land acquisition, environmental mitigation, speed limits, a business car park and land for expanding Sandon Park and Ride.

Key points

  • Construction planned from 2028 to 2031
  • Department for Transport has agreed £68.75m towards the cost
  • The junction carries up to 75,000 vehicles a day, the council says
