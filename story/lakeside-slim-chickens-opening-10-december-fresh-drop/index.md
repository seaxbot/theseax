# Lakeside's Slim Chickens restaurant opens on 10 December

**The US chicken chain is bringing 50 jobs to the Thurrock shopping centre. Its first 5,000 customers each get a prize card.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: West Thurrock, Lakeside Shopping Centre, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 12:41 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Doors open at noon on Level 1
- One prize card hides free food for life
- First 100 customers on opening day get free tenders
- Ninth Slim Chickens for franchise partner JRK Restaurants

## The story

Slim Chickens will open its restaurant on Level 1 of Lakeside Shopping Centre in West Thurrock at noon on Thursday 10 December. The East Anglia Gazette reported the confirmed date, three weeks after the chain first said it was coming before Christmas.

Your Thurrock reported on 6 November that the restaurant would create 50 jobs and serve dine-in, takeaway and delivery customers. It will be the ninth Slim Chickens run by franchise partner JRK Restaurants, part of the Boparan Restaurant Group. The group already has Essex restaurants in Basildon, Chelmsford and Colchester.

## What the opening offers

The first 5,000 people to order will get a card with a code to enter in the Slim Chickens app. Prizes include free chicken tenders, sandwiches, shakes and Texas toast. One card hides a pass worth free Slim Chickens for life. The first 100 customers through the doors on opening day also get free chicken tenders.

Jitenda Thakur, general manager of the Lakeside restaurant, said the firm was proud to be creating jobs in the area. Students will be able to get 20% off their bill from Sunday to Thursday, according to Your Thurrock.
