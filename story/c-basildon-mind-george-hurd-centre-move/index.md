# Basildon Mind moves counselling to the George Hurd Centre

**The mental health charity is leaving its long-time Whitmore Way base for the town centre. Supporters are asked to buy a £50 brick.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 17:16 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Move costs more than £25,000
- Four counselling rooms at the new base
- Helpline: 01268 284130

## The story

Basildon Mind's counselling service and helpline are moving to the first floor of the George Hurd Centre, Basildon Today reported on 25 November. The charity says its landlord, Basildon Council, wants businesses in the Whitmore Way building and community groups in community buildings.

Building work, moving and legal costs come to more than £25,000, all paid from reserves. The charity is selling bricks for a supporters' wall and offering sponsorship of counselling rooms.
