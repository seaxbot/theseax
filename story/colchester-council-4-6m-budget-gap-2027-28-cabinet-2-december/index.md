# Colchester City Council faces £4.6m budget gap

**Colchester City Council must find millions to balance next year's books. Homelessness, leisure and bin costs are driving the shortfall.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- Cabinet discusses the draft budget on 2 December
- Consultation closes at 9am on 14 December
- Full council sets the budget on 24 February 2027

## The story

Colchester City Council has an emerging gap of £4.6 million in its budget for 2027/28, according to a report going to its cabinet on Wednesday 2 December. The council said rising demand and costs in homelessness and temporary accommodation, sport and leisure, and waste services are behind it.

The council called this an early stage in the budget process, with a lot of work still to do. It has not said how much each pressure costs. The Daily Gazette reported that the gap could grow to £10.6 million within five years.

## How the council plans to close it

Officers and councillors are reviewing spending, looking for savings and new income, and checking whether planned building and investment projects are still affordable. Ideas include changing how services work, making better use of council buildings and land, using digital tools and AI, and spending more on prevention to cut costs later. No figures have been given for any of these.

Councillor William Sunnucks, the cabinet member for resources, said the outlook remains challenging. “Our priority remains protecting essential services for residents,” said Councillor Paul Dundas, the council leader.

## Why housing costs matter

Temporary accommodation is one of the council's biggest pressures. [As The Seax reported on 16 November](https://theseax.com/story/colchester-temporary-accommodation-588-households/), nearly 100 Colchester households were living in bed and breakfasts as demand for emergency housing climbed.

## What happens next and how to have your say

Cabinet discusses the draft budget and the council's medium-term financial forecast on 2 December. Final proposals go to cabinet on 27 January 2027, and full council sets the budget on 24 February 2027. Residents can comment through the council's online budget consultation until 9am on Monday 14 December.
