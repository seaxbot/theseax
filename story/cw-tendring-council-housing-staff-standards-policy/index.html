Tendring adopts new skills and conduct standards for council housing staff after tenant feedback

Cabinet adopted a policy setting out the skills, qualifications and conduct expected of housing staff, to meet a new regulator standard.

Clacton-on-Sea · Monday 9 November 2026, 06:20 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council's cabinet adopted a Housing Competence and Conduct Policy on 23 October 2026. The policy sets out the knowledge, skills, experience and behaviour expected of council housing staff and contractors, and includes a Housing Code of Conduct for staff.

The report says the policy is needed to comply with the Regulator of Social Housing's Competence and Conduct Standard, which requires landlords to have a written policy, and that failing to meet it could expose the council to regulatory scrutiny and enforcement. It covers recruitment, training, professional qualifications, appraisal and performance management. Tenants told the council their priorities were being treated with respect, being listened to, getting an empathetic response and being kept informed.

Tenant consultation on the code of conduct closed on 28 October 2026, and the cabinet authorised the Corporate Director (Operations and Delivery) to make changes in response, in consultation with the housing portfolio holder. The policy will be reviewed annually and no later than October 2027.

Key points

  • Adopted by cabinet on 23 October 2026
  • Meets the Regulator of Social Housing's Competence and Conduct Standard
  • Review due no later than October 2027
