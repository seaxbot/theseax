# Ex-England goalkeeper raises £5,642 for Mersea charity

**Peter Shilton has auctioned football keepsakes from his Mersea Island home. The money goes to the gambling charity he runs with his wife.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: West Mersea, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 07:30 (UK time). Updated: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 01:01. Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Shown on Celebrity Yorkshire Auction House on QUEST
- A mixed lot estimated at £80 to £100 sold for £1,500

## The story

PA reported on Tuesday 24 November that Shilton and his wife Steph let TV auctioneer Angus Ashworth pick items from their Mersea Island bungalow for a filmed sale in North Yorkshire.

A Royal Crown Derby piece marked for his Nottingham Forest days made £3,000. A gold disc for World In Motion fetched £260. Shilton, who beat a 45-year gambling addiction, set up the charity to help people with gambling disorders.
