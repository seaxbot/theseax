# Basildon Borough Volunteer Awards open for nominations

**Nominations close at 11.59pm on Friday 18 December. Winners will be named at the Towngate Theatre in March.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 17:31 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Ceremony: Tuesday 9 March 2027
- Open to groups and individuals

## The story

Basildon Council is asking residents to nominate volunteers and community groups for the 2027 Basildon Borough Volunteer Awards.

The awards thank volunteers, community groups, charities, sports clubs and voluntary organisations across the borough, from people who keep local activities running to those who support their neighbours.

Nominations are made online and only need to say what the person or group does and why it matters. They close at 11.59pm on Friday 18 December.

The awards ceremony takes place on Tuesday 9 March 2027 at the Towngate Theatre, St Martin's Square, Basildon.
