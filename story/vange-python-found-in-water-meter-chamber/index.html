Water workers find a carpet python hissing in a Vange meter chamber during leak repair

The cold and injured snake, probably someone's pet, is being cared for by South Essex Wildlife Hospital, which will rehome it if no owner comes forward.

Vange, Basildon · Saturday 7 November 2026, 06:21 · Reported by BBC News (via Yahoo News)
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Essex and Suffolk Water crew fixing a leak in Vange, Basildon, stopped work after hearing hissing from a meter chamber and found a carpet python inside. The non-venomous snake, native to Australia and New Guinea, measured three to four feet and is thought to have been a pet.

The crew called South Essex Wildlife Hospital, whose volunteers from Orsett collected the snake and confirmed the species. It was dehydrated, cold and injured, and founder Sue Schwar said it was not very happy when it arrived. The water company said its engineers had come across plenty in their work, but a python was a first.

How the python ended up underground is not known, and the hospital suggested it may have escaped down a toilet or been dumped. It is now being treated, and the hospital says it will look for a new home for the snake if its owner does not come forward.

Key points

  • Found by Essex and Suffolk Water crew in Vange
  • Carpet python, three to four feet long
  • Being treated at South Essex Wildlife Hospital
  • Will be rehomed if no owner comes forward
