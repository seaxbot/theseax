Unwanted crutches and walkers can now be dropped at 13 Essex tips for reuse

Love Essex has teamed up with MedEquip so residents can hand in community equipment such as crutches, trolley walkers and commodes at recycling centres to be refurbished and rehomed.

Braintree, Brentwood, Canvey, Chigwell, Clacton, Colchester, Harlow, Kirby le Soken, Maldon, Mountnessing, Saffron Walden, South Woodham Ferrers & Witham · Friday 30 October 2026, 10:00 · Reported by Love Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Items are cleaned, tested and passed on; parts of anything unusable are recycled
  • Book a recycling centre visit and speak to staff on arrival
  • Equipment loaned by MedEquip can be collected from home instead
