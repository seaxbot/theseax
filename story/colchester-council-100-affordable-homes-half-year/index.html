Colchester council says it delivered 100 affordable homes between April and September

Colchester City Council's half-year performance report also counts 616 households in temporary accommodation and progress on Trinity Square and St Botolph's Circus.

Colchester · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The figure covers April to September 2026 and comes from a half-year performance report that went to Colchester City Council's Scrutiny Panel on Wednesday 11 November. Council leader Paul Dundas called it a positive step at a time of extremely high demand for housing.

Of the 100 homes, 37 came through partnerships with registered providers, 13 through the council's own building and buying programme and 50 through Section 106 agreements with developers. The report says 3,311 households got cost-of-living help in the six months, and five play parks were improved.

The report goes to Colchester City Council's Cabinet on Wednesday 2 December 2026. It says work on Trinity Square is expected to finish in early 2027, building is under way at St Botolph's Circus and on the East Hill cycle route, and residents are being consulted on a new bus station interchange and the new Local Plan.

Key points

  • 37 homes came via housing associations, 13 from the council's own programme and 50 through developer agreements
  • Cabinet considers the report on Wednesday 2 December
  • Trinity Square works are due to finish in early 2027
