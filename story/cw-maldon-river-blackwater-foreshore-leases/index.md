# Maldon council to decide on 25-year River Blackwater foreshore leases

**A boatyard business near Downs Road in Maldon uses two council-owned stretches of foreshore for access. Officers say it has a legal right to renew.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- Two 25-year leases, both ending in 2051
- Same tenant has leased foreshore from the council since 1993
- Committee meets on Thursday 26 November 2026
- No impact on council finances, the report says

## The story

Maldon District Council's Strategy and Resources Committee will consider the leases on Thursday 26 November 2026. The report recommends a new 25-year lease for the foreshore next to Downs Recreation Ground and another for the foreshore next to Downs Road Boatyard. Both leases would then end in 2051.

The tenant has leased the foreshore by Downs Recreation Ground since 1993 and the boatyard foreshore since 2008. The boatyard lease has expired, but the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 lets the tenant stay on the old terms while a new one is agreed. The report says the council must grant a new lease on broadly similar terms unless it has legal grounds to refuse.

The report warns that if councillors say no, the tenant could go to court. A judge would then likely set the rent and terms. The report says the renewals will not affect the council's finances, and council staff will do the legal work.
