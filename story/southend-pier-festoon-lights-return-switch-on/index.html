Festoon lights back on Southend Pier for the first time in over 20 years, with switch-on set for Saturday

More than a mile of lights will run the length of the pier, launching an evening of festive events across the city on 14 November.

Southend-on-Sea · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend Pier's festoon lighting is returning for the first time in more than two decades, Southend-on-Sea City Council has announced. More than a mile of the lights will stretch along the world's longest pleasure pier, and new colourful neon lighting and light-up benches have been added at the On the Decks area at the Pier Head.

The official pier switch-on is at 5pm on Saturday 14 November, with music, food and drinks at the Royal Pavilion afterwards. Council leader Daniel Cowan said the council had promised to bring the lights back and had done so, while cabinet member Matt Dent called it a special occasion for the city.

The same evening the main city centre Christmas lights are switched on at Victoria Circus at 6pm, followed by the season's last seafront fireworks at 7.30pm. Southend City BID's Christmas in the City runs on the High Street during the day, with an artisan market, live music and family activities.

Key points

  • Pier open 10.15am to 8pm on the day
  • Walking admission £3.10 for adults, £2.10 for children and concessions
