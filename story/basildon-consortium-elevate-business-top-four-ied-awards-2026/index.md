# Basildon business support scheme ranked in UK's top four for impact

**A Basildon scheme for new and growing firms has won national recognition. It did so on a fraction of its rivals' budgets.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 12:56 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Commissioned by Basildon Borough Council
- Funded by the UK Shared Prosperity Fund
- Greater Manchester Growth Hub won the category

## The story

The Consortium (East) Ltd was placed among the top four in the Outstanding Impact category of the Institute of Economic Development's 2026 awards. It was shortlisted for Elevate Basildon Business, which Basildon Borough Council paid for in 2025/26 with UK Shared Prosperity Fund money.

The firm said the programme reached 342 people, helped start 55 businesses and supported 29 new jobs. Greater Manchester Growth Hub came first.
