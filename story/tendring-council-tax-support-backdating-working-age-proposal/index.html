Working-age Tendring households could backdate council tax help by three months

Tendring District Council wants people of working age who are eligible for its council tax support to be able to claim it for the months before they applied, as pensioners already can.

Clacton-on-Sea · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 12:17 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council's cabinet backed the change at a meeting on Friday 13 November, the council said on Tuesday 17 November. At present a working-age claimant only gets the discount, worth up to 80% of their council tax, from the day they apply.

Under the proposal they could go back three months, provided they can prove they would have been eligible. Councillor Mark Stephenson said people often delay applying because of their circumstances, other priorities, the hope that things will improve, or worry about stigma. The council tax premium on second homes stays, and he said the number of properties paying it has fallen since it was introduced.

The change now goes out to consultation, including with the other authorities whose charges appear on council tax bills, before going to full council for approval in the new year. Councillor Stephenson also said the council's finances remain stable, with no new major problems.

Key points

  • Pensioners already have the same right
  • Support for care leavers is unchanged
  • Full council vote due in the new year
