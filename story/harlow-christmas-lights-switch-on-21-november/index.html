Harlow sets 21 November for Christmas lights switch-on in revamped Market Square, with ice rink opening the same day

Harlow Council has published the timings for its Christmas launch, promising the town's biggest festive display yet.

Harlow · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 15:29 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow town centre will mark the start of Christmas on Saturday 21 November, with celebrations running from 1pm to 6.30pm and the lights switched on in the newly transformed Market Square at 6pm, Your Harlow reports.

Local performers will provide music through the afternoon, an interactive show leads into the switch-on, and the town's real ice rink also opens that day. Details are at christmasinharlow.com.

Key points

  • Celebrations run from 1pm to 6.30pm
  • An interactive show leads into the 6pm switch-on
  • Council leader Dan Swords promised the town's biggest Christmas yet
