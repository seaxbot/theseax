# Southend Airport gets easyJet flights to Corfu from May

**Holidaymakers in south Essex will be able to fly direct to the Greek island next summer. Tickets are already on sale.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend Airport, Rochford, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Flights run on Tuesdays and Saturdays
- Rhodes is easyJet's other Greek route from Southend
- Belfast City, Budapest and Lyon routes also start within a year

## The story

London Southend Airport announced on Thursday 26 November that easyJet will fly to Corfu twice a week from 11 May 2027. Flights will run on Tuesdays and Saturdays through the summer, and easyJet holiday packages are on sale too.

It is easyJet's second route between Southend and Greece, after the Rhodes service announced in September. The airport said new routes to Belfast City, Budapest, Edinburgh, Lyon, Rhodes and Sharm El Sheikh all start within the next 12 months, giving it a network of 31 destinations. Swiss carrier [Chair Airlines' daily Zurich flights](https://theseax.com/story/southend-airport-chair-airlines-zurich-daily-flights/) are also on sale for spring.

According to the airport, Southend was the UK's fastest-growing airport in 2025, with passenger numbers up 150% on 2024. It said its average delay that year was 10 minutes and 24 seconds, the second best in the country.

Nigel Mayes, the airport's business development director, called Corfu "a fantastic addition to our growing network". Kevin Doyle, easyJet's UK country manager, said the airline looked forward to welcoming customers on board from May.
