# Witham bus link to Broomfield Hospital starts year-long trial

**Witham patients and visitors get a direct bus to Broomfield Hospital from Monday 30 November. It runs on demand for a 12-month trial.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Witham, Broomfield Hospital, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 07:42 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Funded by £50,000 from Love Your Bus and £30,000 from Braintree District Council
- Book through the TravelEssex app or by phone
- Free for bus pass holders after 9am

## The story

Witham people will be able to book a direct bus to Broomfield Hospital from Monday 30 November, after years of campaigning, the Braintree and Witham Times reported. The service is run by DigiGo, Essex County Council's on-demand minibus scheme, and starts as a one-year trial.

Witham Town Council won £50,000 for the link from Essex County Council's Love Your Bus grant fund. Braintree District Council is adding £30,000 of Section 106 money, paid by developers to fund local services. The town council thanked the local groups, residents and councillors who gave evidence for the bid.

## How it will work

According to the town council's September newsletter, the bus runs Monday to Friday, with up to four return journeys a day. There is no fixed timetable. Passengers book a trip in advance or on demand through the TravelEssex app or a telephone booking line, and can pay and track the bus in the app. Virtual pick-up and drop-off points across Witham link straight to the hospital.

Holders of a concessionary bus pass travel free after 9am. The town council has not published other fares, and the full list of pick-up points was not available when The Seax checked.

## What happens next

The trial lasts 12 months. Witham Town Council says the service could carry on after that if it becomes self-funding, so how many people use it will decide its future. Details are on the town council's Broomfield bus link page and at digigo.uk.
