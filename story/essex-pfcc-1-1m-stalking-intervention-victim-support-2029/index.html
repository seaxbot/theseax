Essex wins £1.1m to tackle stalkers and help their victims to 2029

Essex · Friday 27 November 2026, 08:04 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Stalkers across Essex will be offered help to change their behaviour. Victims get more specialist support, including help with online tracking.

Roger Hirst, the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner for Essex, has secured £1,135,634 of Home Office money to run from October 2026 to March 2029, Your Harlow reported on Friday 27 November.

The largest share, £658,538, pays for a Stalking Intervention Service run by The Change Project, which works with people whose obsessive behaviour includes stalking linked to domestic abuse. A further £338,015 goes to victim support through the Ending Violence in Essex partnership, with £118,709 of that for a digital safety specialist.

Key points

  • Money runs from October 2026 to March 2029
  • The Change Project runs the perpetrator service
  • Ending Violence in Essex partnership leads victim support
