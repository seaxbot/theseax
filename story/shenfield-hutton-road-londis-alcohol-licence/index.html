Long-empty Shenfield shop on Hutton Road cleared to become a Londis

Brentwood Borough Council has licensed the empty unit at 144 Hutton Road, Shenfield, to sell alcohol from 8am to 11pm every day as a Londis convenience store.

Shenfield · Saturday 14 November 2026, 07:33 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

A licensing sub-committee granted Havengoods Ltd's application in full on Tuesday 10 November, Brentwood Online reports. Two residents objected over crime, antisocial behaviour and the number of licensed shops nearby, but the councillors found their concerns did not show the licence would undermine its objectives.

Essex Police did not object after agreeing conditions including CCTV and a Challenge 25 policy. No opening date has been given.

Key points

  • The applicant cut its proposed closing time from 11.30pm to 11pm at the hearing
  • Objectors can appeal to the magistrates' court within 21 days
