# Southend Pier trains: calls grow for a firm date to restart £3.5m railway

**The pier's two battery-electric trains have been out of service since a collision in October 2024, and the council has given no date for their return.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 10:16 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45. Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Electric trains entered service in 2022
- 1986 diesel trains still carrying passengers
- Pier voted Essex's top icon in 2025

## The story

Southend-on-Sea City Council is facing growing calls to set a firm date for the pier's £3.5m electric trains to run again, the Yellow Advertiser reports.

The council bought two battery-electric trains from the manufacturer Severn Lamb, and they entered service in 2022. They have been out of use since a collision on the pier railway in October 2024, which led to a series of investigations. In June 2026 the council confirmed there was still no timescale for their return, the National Piers Society reported, and in August the Echo reported there was still no date.

In the meantime the council has kept the pier railway running with its diesel trains, built in 1986, which were meant to have been retired when the new trains arrived. The railway runs for 1.25 miles of the 1.34-mile pier, the longest pleasure pier in the world, which was voted the top icon of Essex in Visit Essex's 2025 poll.
