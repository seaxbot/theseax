# Essex's Prodigy star Keith Flint honoured with fossil snail name

**Scientists have named a 99-million-year-old snail after the late Prodigy singer. Its hairy shell reminded them of his famous spiky hair.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 22:45 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- The fossil comes from Kachin amber in Myanmar
- Hungarian and Chinese scientists worked on the study
- Flint died in 2019

## The story

A snail preserved in amber for about 99 million years has been named after Keith Flint, the Prodigy frontman who died at his Essex home in 2019. The new species, Archaeospirostoma flinti, was described in the journal Ecologica Montenegrina on 19 November.

According to the research team's release on EurekAlert, the hairs covering the shell reminded the authors of Flint's distinctive hairstyle. The hairs are part of the shell's outer protein layer. In this species they grow in flat fringes of equal length, unlike the single spikes seen on other hairy snails trapped in Burmese amber.

Dr Barna Páll-Gergely, of the HUN-REN Centre for Agricultural Research and Széchenyi István University in Hungary, led the study. He worked with Márton Szabó of Eötvös Loránd University and the Hungarian Natural History Museum, alongside the University of Szeged and the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

## What makes it unusual

The best-preserved specimen still has its operculum, the trapdoor-like cover a snail uses to seal its shell. Dr Páll-Gergely said its cone shape is "entirely unique" among this family's snails found in Burmese amber. The team says the snail lived on land, and the purpose of the cone-shaped cover is not yet clear.

It is the ninth member of its snail family found in Burmese amber, Mr Szabó said, and seven of the nine have hairy shells.
