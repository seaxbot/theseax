Harlow's ECCO launches Green Lottery with first £150 draw this Friday

The environmental charity's new fortnightly lottery will raise money for local green, community and anti-poverty projects.

Harlow · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 11:55 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

ECCO Environmental Charity, which has worked in Harlow for more than 30 years, is launching a Green Lottery, with the first draw this Friday. Money raised will go to environmental, community and poverty-related projects in the town.

The opening draw carries a £150 cash prize, and draws will then be held every two weeks. ECCO says both the prize pot and the money for good causes can grow as more people sign up. The charity has already supported forest schools, allotments, Harlow Food Bank and cycling schemes.

Key points

  • First draw this Friday, prize £150
  • Draws every two weeks
  • Backs green, community and anti-poverty projects
