Henry Nowak's killer has minimum term raised to 25 years as appeal judges rule 21 years was unduly lenient

The Lady Chief Justice's panel found Vickrum Digwa's original sentence did not reflect the seriousness of the Chafford Hundred student's murder, and threw out his own bid to have it cut.

Chafford Hundred · Monday 2 November 2026, 13:30 · Updated Thursday 5 November 2026, 16:15 · Reported by Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Vickrum Digwa, who murdered Chafford Hundred teenager Henry Nowak, must now serve at least 25 years before he can be considered for release after the Court of Appeal increased his minimum term from 21 years. The ruling, delivered on Thursday afternoon, follows a referral by Attorney General Ellie Reeves, who argued the original term was unduly lenient.

Lady Chief Justice Baroness Carr, sitting with Mr Justice Nicklin and Mr Justice Wall, said the 21-year term fell outside the range reasonably open to the trial judge and did not adequately reflect the overall seriousness of the murder. The judges rejected as unarguable Digwa's own claim that his sentence was manifestly excessive. Jonathan Polnay KC, for the Attorney General, had told the court that Digwa repeatedly used a knife on an unarmed stranger, abused the privilege Sikhs have to carry blades and zoomed in on Henry's face while filming him as he lay dying.

Digwa, 23, who stabbed Henry, 18, in Southampton on 3 December 2025, watched by video link from HMP Frankland and showed no visible reaction; members of Henry's family were at the Royal Courts of Justice and one was seen wiping their eyes. Before the hearing the family told ITV News they were angered that Digwa's mother, jailed for hiding the blade, is due for early release in December. Their documentary Dear Henry: Our Fight For Justice airs on ITV at 9pm tonight.

Key points

  • Minimum term raised from 21 to 25 years
  • Judges call the original term unduly lenient
  • Digwa's bid to have his sentence cut rejected
  • Henry, 18, from Chafford Hundred, was stabbed in Southampton in December 2025
