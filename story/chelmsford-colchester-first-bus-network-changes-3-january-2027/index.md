# Chelmsford bus C3 to reach Boreham and Beaulieu Park station in January

**Chelmsford's C3 bus will link Boreham with the new Beaulieu Park station. A Sunday route and an extra hospital shift trip are also coming.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Boreham, Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 09:15 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- New C3A runs on Sundays from Beeleigh Link to North Melbourne
- One extra Saturday C2 trip for Broomfield Hospital shifts
- Colchester's 520 becomes the 320

## The story

First Bus will change its Essex network on Sunday 3 January 2027, with most of the changes in and around Chelmsford. Its C3 service is being extended to Boreham. That gives the village a bus to Beaulieu Park station in the morning and evening peaks from Monday to Saturday, and all day on Sundays.

Beaulieu Park is Chelmsford's new station on the Great Eastern main line. First Bus says the longer route will make it easier for commuters to catch trains towards Chelmsford, Colchester and London.

## Sunday service and hospital shifts

A new Sunday route, the C3A, will also start. It will run from Beeleigh Link, in the Chelmer Village development east of Chelmsford, through Boreham and the city centre to North Melbourne in the west.

On Saturdays the C2 gets one extra journey. It is timed to fit shift changeovers at Broomfield Hospital, so more staff can get to and from work by bus.

In Colchester, route 520 will be renumbered 320. First Bus says the new number is meant to make travel simpler for passengers.

Julian Elliott, head of network for First Bus in the East and Midlands, said the company had listened to customer feedback before planning the changes. He said they should help people "travel to work more easily around Chelmsford and Colchester".

## What happens next

Full timetables for each route are on the First Bus Essex website. Passengers who use the C2, C3 or 520 can check their times there before the changes start.
