Essex road deaths rose to 58 in 2025 as Road Safety Week approaches

The Safer Essex Roads Partnership is sharing a film of its Chelmsford city centre display, which used a pair of shoes for each life lost on the county's roads.

Chelmsford · Saturday 14 November 2026, 07:14 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brake's Road Safety Week begins on Sunday 15 November 2026, when Chelmsford Cathedral and the partnership hold a 2.30pm memorial service for road traffic victims, Your Harlow reports. The display in November 2025 marked 51 deaths from 2024, and bereaved families brought shoes of their own, including the trainers of a 19-year-old who died in a collision in Waltham Abbey.

Roger Hirst, the Essex Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner who chairs the partnership, said drug driving had reached epidemic levels in the county.

Key points

  • Memorial service at Chelmsford Cathedral, 2.30pm on 15 November
  • Last year's display reflected 51 deaths in 2024
  • Vision Zero aims to end deaths and serious injuries on Essex roads by 2040
