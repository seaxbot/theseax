A130 southbound shut all weekend between Howe Green and the Rayleigh Spur for maintenance

Drivers heading south from Chelmsford towards Basildon and Southend face a signed diversion via the A12 and A127 until early on Monday.

Howe Green & Rayleigh · Friday 6 November 2026, 19:30 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

The southbound A130 between the Howe Green Interchange near Chelmsford and the Rayleigh Spur Roundabout closed at 8pm on Friday for a full weekend of maintenance. The road is one of the main links between mid and south Essex, so drivers travelling towards Basildon, Rayleigh, Canvey and Southend will need to plan ahead.

The works are described as essential maintenance and are expected to finish by 6am on Monday 9 November. The northbound carriageway stays open throughout, and a signed diversion takes southbound traffic along the A12 and the A127 via Gallows Corner.

Drivers should not expect a quick return to normal: a further weekend of works is planned for the northbound carriageway the following weekend. Anyone using the route on Saturday or Sunday should allow extra time for the longer diversion.

Key points

  • Southbound only: Howe Green Interchange to Rayleigh Spur
  • 8pm Friday 6 Nov to about 6am Monday 9 Nov
  • Diversion via A12 and A127 at Gallows Corner
  • Northbound works planned for the following weekend
