Christmas breakfast, market and Santa's grotto planned for Jaywick Sands at the Sunspot

Tendring District Council is inviting families in Jaywick Sands to a day of festive events at the Sunspot business centre in Brooklands.

Jaywick Sands · Thursday 12 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The day opens with a community Christmas breakfast from 9am to 11am on Friday 4 December, with festive stalls and family activities running into the evening, Tendring District Council announced on Thursday 12 November.

A Christmas market with local businesses, crafts and gifts opens from 2pm, and from 4pm there will be Santa's grotto, a family reindeer hunt, face painting and carols with the Jaywick Sands Singers. A children's party is also planned. Sunspot tenants and partners are helping to pay for the day, which is run through the council's Tendring4Growth programme.

Councillor Ivan Henderson, Tendring's cabinet member for economic growth and regeneration, said he hoped local families would come along for any part of the day. Details are on the Sunspot website.

Key points

  • Breakfast runs from 9am to 11am
  • Santa's grotto and carols from 4pm
  • Organised through Tendring4Growth
