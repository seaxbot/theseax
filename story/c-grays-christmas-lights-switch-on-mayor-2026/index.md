# Grays Christmas tree lights switched on by Mayor of Thurrock

**Families packed Grays High Street for the town's annual switch-on and Christmas fair.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 14:39 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Panto cast from Thameside Theatre took part
- White's Sound & Lighting ran the stage

## The story

Cllr Bill Cook, the Mayor of Thurrock, switched on the Christmas tree lights in Grays High Street on Thursday 26 November, Your Thurrock reported. School children and a community choir performed, and cast members from Thameside Theatre's panto, Rapunzel: A Tangled Tale, joined in.

DP World paid for this year's tree and Impulse Leisure sponsored the evening.
