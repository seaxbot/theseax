Essex to open 2027 T20 season at Hove, with six games in Chelmsford

Chelmsford · Wednesday 25 November 2026, 09:59 · Reported by Essex Cricket
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Cricket has published next summer's T20 fixtures for its men and women. Members can book first, from 2 December.

Essex men's side will start the 2027 Vitality Blast away to Sussex Sharks at Hove on Friday 21 May, Essex Cricket announced on Wednesday 25 November. Their first home game is the Kent Spitfires derby at the Ambassador Cruise Line Ground in Chelmsford a week later. The club bills that match as the first part of its 'Battle of the Bridge'.

Sky Sports reported that the competition keeps three groups of six counties. Essex are in Group C with Kent, Hampshire, Middlesex, Surrey and Sussex. Each county plays 12 group games, six of them at home. Essex's other Chelmsford dates are Middlesex on 30 May, Surrey on 4 June, Sussex on 27 June, Hampshire on 2 July and Warwickshire Bears on 4 July.

Group winners and runners-up go through to the quarter-finals, along with the two best third-placed sides. Men's Finals Day is at Edgbaston on Saturday 17 July, and the women's Finals Day is at Trent Bridge the next day. Essex Women begin away to Surrey at The Kia Oval on 23 May. Their first home game is against Glamorgan on 30 May, as a double-header with the men's Middlesex match.

Tickets

Members get the first chance to book, in a priority window from 2 December. Passes covering every Blast fixture go on sale on 9 December, and general sale opens on 17 February. Essex Cricket said single match tickets will be released in due course.

Key points

  • Group C: Essex, Kent, Hampshire, Middlesex, Surrey, Sussex
  • Last group game: Middlesex at Lord's on 11 July
  • General sale opens on 17 February
