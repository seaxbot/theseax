# South Essex father jailed for abusing his two vulnerable children

**Detectives showed him video of what he had done, and a judge called it "gratuitous degradation". The children's mother, who delayed telling police, was given a community order.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 18:12 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Father admitted child cruelty and intentional suffocation
- He faces an extended licence of three years on release
- Police chose not to name the parents to protect the children
- Both children now live in a safe and loving home, police say

## The story

A father from south Essex has been jailed for four years and nine months for abusing his two young children, who have additional and complex care needs. He admitted two counts of child cruelty and one of intentional suffocation and was sentenced at Basildon Crown Court on Monday 23 November. He will be on an extended licence of three years when he is released, Essex Police said.

Police said evidence showed he repeatedly physically abused the children in the family home, including an incident in which one child was suffocated. The force has chosen not to name either parent. It said this was not to protect them but to protect the children's anonymity and wellbeing.

## The mother

The children's mother admitted two counts of child neglect, which related to her delay in telling police what she knew was happening. Police said she was aware of abusive behaviour towards the children but failed to protect them before eventually contacting officers in December 2025. She was given a two-year community order with 150 hours of unpaid work.

## The investigation

Both parents were arrested and the case went to Essex Police's Child Abuse Investigation Team. In interview the father at first played down his behaviour and denied that it amounted to abuse, until detectives showed him video evidence recovered during the investigation. Detective Constable Scott Cameron told him the recording had "shocked me to my core", and that the only thing more shocking was his lack of reaction while watching it. Faced with the evidence, the father admitted the offences.

DC Courtney White, who led the investigation, said the children "should have been able to depend upon the adults around them to keep them safe". She said detectives examine distressing evidence in painstaking detail because abused children "may not always be able to tell somebody what is happening to them".

## The judge

Sentencing, His Honour Judge Collery KC told the father he appeared to be "seeking to torment the children" and described what happened as "gratuitous degradation, which could almost be said to be sadistic". He told him he was dangerous and presented a substantial risk of causing serious harm to children in the future.

## Where to get help

Anyone worried that a child is being abused can call Essex Police on 101, or 999 in an emergency, or the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000. Children and young people can call Childline free, any time, on 0800 1111.
