# Essex Senior Cup quarter-final draw sets holders Billericay Town's path

**Witham Town could meet Colchester United, and Aveley host Hullbridge Sports, in the last eight of the BBC Essex Senior Cup.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Aveley, Witham, Billericay, Dagenham, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 14 November 2026, 13:57 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Dagenham & Redbridge v Stanway Rovers
- Hullbridge Sports back in the last eight after 17 years
- Ties due in the week beginning 7 December

## The story

Billericay Town, who beat Little Oakley 7-0 in the last round, will play Redbridge or Grays Athletic in the quarter-finals; those two clubs meet on Monday 23 November. Witham Town face Colchester United or Hashtag United, who play their tie on Tuesday 17 November, Your Thurrock reports.

Stanway are in the quarter-finals for the first time, and Hullbridge Sports for the first time in 17 years. BBC Essex's Victoria Polley drew the home clubs on Friday 13 November.
