# Brentwood and Rochford invite businesses to become Safe Spaces for women

**Both councils are holding drop-in events through the 16 Days of Activism against gender-based violence to recruit businesses and community groups to the Women's Safety Charter.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brentwood, Rayleigh, Hawkwell, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 13:08 (UK time). Updated: Monday 23 November 2026, 21:13. Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Accredited Safe Spaces get staff training on how to help
- Events at the Baytree Centre, Rayleigh and Hawkwell
- Essex Police and Victim Support among those taking part

## The story

Businesses and community groups in Brentwood and Rochford district are being asked to become places where women and girls can go for help. Both councils launched drives on Monday 23 November to sign up more organisations to the Women's Safety Charter. Rochford District Council calls it a county-wide scheme.

## What a Safe Space is

Organisations that join can be accredited as Safe Spaces: visible places where a woman or girl who feels unsafe can ask for help. Their staff get guidance and training on how to respond, Brentwood Borough Council says. The charter also backs White Ribbon, the campaign that asks men and boys to challenge harmful attitudes.

## Drop-ins in Brentwood

The events run through the 16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence, an international campaign that starts on Wednesday 25 November and ends on 10 December. Brentwood's first drop-in is at Unit 10 in the Baytree Centre that Wednesday, from 10am to noon. The second is outside Marks & Spencer on the High Street on 3 December, also in the morning. The last is back at the Baytree Centre on 10 December, from 2pm to 4pm.

Essex Police, Victim Support, Changing Pathways, Renew Counselling, Phoenix Futures, Active Essex and Essex County Fire and Rescue Service will be there, with the council's housing team. Councillor Dr Tim Barrett chairs the council's housing, health and community committee. He said the council was working with local organisations and businesses "to create safer spaces".

## Drop-ins in Rochford district

Rochford's events start at The Mill Arts and Events Centre in Rayleigh on 25 November, then move to Clements Hall Leisure Centre in Hawkwell on 3 December. Both run from 2pm to 4pm. The last is outside the Star Café pie and mash shop on Rayleigh High Street on 10 December, from 10am to noon.

Essex Police, Active Essex, Phoenix Futures, Bar 'n' Bus and Sanctuary Housing are joining the council's domestic abuse and community safety teams. Councillor Jo McPherson, who chairs Rochford's community and housing committee, urged residents to "come along to our events" and meet the organisations involved.

## How it started

Brentwood has been working on this for at least two years. In November 2024 it ran a survey with the charter asking women how safe they feel across the borough, with a focus on specific male behaviours. Tendring District Council unveiled its own Women's Safety Charter to businesses in February 2026, paid for by the Essex Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner's Safer Streets programme.

## How to join

Organisations can find out more at brentwood.gov.uk/womens-safety-charter and rochford.gov.uk/womens-safety-charter. Anyone in immediate danger should call 999.
