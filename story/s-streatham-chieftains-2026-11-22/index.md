# Chelmsford Chieftains lose 5-3 at Streatham in the Challenge Cup

**Chelmsford's ice hockey side, who won 3-2 at Streatham in the league in October, trailed 3-1 after two periods and could not recover.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 18:13 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Ice Hockey.

## Key points

- Ollie Baldock was named Man of the Match
- Streatham served seven minor penalties to Chelmsford's five
- Next: away at Romford Buccaneers on Saturday 28 November

## The story

Chelmsford Chieftains lost to Streatham Redhawks at Streatham Ice Hockey Club on Sunday 22 November 2026. Lane Paddison scored the Chieftains' first goal in the second period. Danny Ingoldsby then struck twice early in the third to put the hosts 5-1 ahead.

Hallden Barnes-Garner pulled one back with under eight minutes left. Ollie Baldock added a late power-play goal.
