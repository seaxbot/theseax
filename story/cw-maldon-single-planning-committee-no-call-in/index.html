Maldon scraps area planning committees and councillor call-in as new national rules take effect

Maldon District Council now has one 13-member Planning Committee, with most applications decided by officers under a new national scheme of delegation.

Maldon · Thursday 12 November 2026, 06:24 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Maldon District Council agreed on Thursday 22 October 2026 to set up a single Planning Committee of 13 members from Saturday 31 October 2026. It abolished the North Western, Central and South Eastern Area Planning Committees and the District Planning Committee on the same date. Councillors were told the change was required by the Town and Country Planning (Discharge of Local Planning Authority Functions) (England) Regulations 2026, which came into force on 31 October.

Under the new National Scheme of Delegation, routine and minor applications go to officers, and larger or more sensitive ones are also delegated unless they pass a threshold known as the Gateway Test. There is no longer a process for councillors to call in applications. Councillor W Stamp said she did not consider the changes democratic and asked for her vote against to be recorded.

The new committee is due to meet every three weeks at 7pm, mostly on Wednesdays, with meetings cancelled when there is no business. A separate report to the same meeting proposed a revised public speaking protocol that would let ward councillors who do not sit on the committee speak on applications in their wards, raise speaking time from two to three minutes, and allow electronic voting.

Key points

  • One 13-member Planning Committee from 31 October 2026
  • Four previous planning committees abolished
  • No councillor call-in under the national scheme
  • Meetings every three weeks at 7pm when there is business
