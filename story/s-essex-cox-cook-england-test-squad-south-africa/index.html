Essex pair Jordan Cox and Sam Cook keep their places in England's Test squad for the South Africa tour

The two Essex players are among 15 picked by England for a three-Test series that starts in Johannesburg on 17 December.

Chelmsford · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Essex Cricket
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex batter Jordan Cox and seamer Sam Cook have both been named in England's 15-man Test squad to tour South Africa this winter, Essex Cricket has announced. The three-Test series is Stephen Fleming's first in charge as England's Test head coach, and South Africa go into it as the ICC world Test champions.

Cook will fly out early with the England Lions on 25 November for a preparation camp in Pietermaritzburg, while Cox will join after the fourth T20 against Australia in Sydney on 29 November, travelling straight to South Africa with Harry Brook and Jacob Bethell.

Key points

  • Cox and Cook named in 15-man squad captained by Joe Root
  • First Test at Johannesburg from 17 December
  • Third Test in Cape Town from 3 to 7 January
