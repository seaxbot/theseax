Braintree PC sacked after police car data showed him speeding 75 times in two months

A Braintree-based Essex Police constable has been dismissed without notice after a misconduct hearing found his driving in marked police cars amounted to gross misconduct.

Braintree · Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Misconduct999
WhatsApp Facebook X

PC Jake Lay-Flurrie went more than twice the speed limit on five occasions, according to vehicle telematics covering August to October 2025, Misconduct999 reports. Chief Constable Rachel Nolan dismissed him on Friday 6 November.

The hearing found he had switched on emergency lights without holding the qualification needed for response driving, and that he kept breaking the rules after restrictions were placed on him over earlier concerns. He first admitted gross misconduct but later argued it should be treated as misconduct, which was rejected, and the chief constable found his culpability high.

The hearing was told he was seen as a committed, proactive officer who acted with good intentions, but Chief Constable Nolan said that did not excuse a "repeated failure to follow the rules", London999 reports. His name will be added to the College of Policing barred list, which stops him returning to policing.

Key points

  • Dismissed on 6 November by Chief Constable Rachel Nolan
  • Five of the breaches were more than double the limit
  • His name goes on the College of Policing barred list
