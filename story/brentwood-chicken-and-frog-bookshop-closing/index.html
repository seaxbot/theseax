Brentwood's Chicken and Frog bookshop to shut, blaming steep rise in rates and power costs

The independent bookshop in Brentwood says sharp increases in its business rates and electricity bills have left it unable to carry on.

Brentwood · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 00:00 · Reported by BBC News (via Gateway 97.8)
WhatsApp Facebook X

The Chicken and Frog, an independent bookshop in Brentwood, is to close. The shop puts the decision down to business rates and electricity bills that it says have risen sharply, according to a BBC Essex report. No closing date has been given.

Key points

  • Independent bookshop in Brentwood is closing
  • It blames sharp rises in business rates and electricity bills
  • No closing date has been announced
