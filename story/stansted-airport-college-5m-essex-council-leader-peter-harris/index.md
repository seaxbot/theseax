# Stansted Airport College to double in size with £5m from Essex council

**Essex County Council's leader has set out why the county is backing the airport's college. He says Stansted is vital to jobs across Greater Essex.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stansted, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 10:54 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- More than 12,000 people work at the airport directly
- Greater Essex economy worth £55bn a year, says the council leader
- Blog is part of the airport's guest series on connectivity

## The story

Councillor Peter Harris, the leader of Essex County Council, used a guest blog published by London Stansted Airport on Wednesday 25 November to set out the council's £5m investment in Stansted Airport College. He said the money will more than double the college's capacity, giving more young people from across Greater Essex the chance to train for careers in aviation, engineering and aircraft maintenance.

Councillor Harris described the airport as "a vital piece of economic infrastructure". He pointed to its place as the largest single-site employer in Greater Essex and the East of England, with more than 12,000 people working there directly. He said the Greater Essex economy is worth £55 billion a year, and could produce up to £8 billion more each year if it matched productivity across the wider South East.

The blog follows the Greater Essex Government Showcase in Westminster last month, where council and business leaders made the case for investment in the area. Councillor Harris said continued investment in international links would bring in new business and better-paid jobs.
