Man, 36, to face crown court over Waltham Abbey town centre collision that left pedestrian with serious leg injuries

Arren Lewis is accused of GBH with intent, dangerous driving and failing to stop after a man in his 30s was hit in North Place on 8 October, and is due at Snaresbrook Crown Court on 19 November.

Waltham Abbey · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 10:50 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man has been charged after a pedestrian was left with life-changing injuries in a collision in Waltham Abbey town centre. Arren Lewis, 36, is accused of causing grievous bodily harm with intent, failing to stop and dangerous driving, Essex Police said.

The charges relate to an incident at about 5pm on 8 October in North Place, when a man in his 30s was reportedly struck by a grey Audi TT. He suffered significant injuries to his legs and was taken to hospital, while the driver is alleged to have left the scene.

Lewis is due to appear at Snaresbrook Crown Court on 19 November. Detectives are still appealing for anyone with doorbell or dashcam footage or other information to come forward, with Detective Constable James Refalo saying "no detail is too small".

Key points

  • Arren Lewis, 36, charged with GBH with intent
  • Also accused of failing to stop and dangerous driving
  • Victim in his 30s suffered serious leg injuries on 8 October
  • Due at Snaresbrook Crown Court on 19 November
