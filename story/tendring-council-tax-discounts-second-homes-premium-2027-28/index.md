# Tendring keeps second homes council tax premium for another year

**Second home owners in Tendring will keep paying double council tax from April. Help for struggling working-age households could also be backdated.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Clacton-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 16:52 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- New arrangements start on 1 April 2027
- Working-age support stays at up to 80% off bills
- Final decision on backdating due in January 2027

## The story

Tendring District Council has agreed to keep its council tax discounts and extra charges unchanged for 2027/28, including the 100 per cent premium on second homes. Full Council made the decision on Tuesday 24 November, and the arrangements start on 1 April 2027.

Owners of long-term empty properties will also keep paying extra charges at today's levels, and the existing exceptions stay in place. Care leavers moving towards independent living will still get help with their bills. Cabinet has widened the council's hardship policy so it can consider cases involving the extra charges on second homes and empty properties.

Council leader Councillor Mark Stephenson said the number of second homes in Tendring fell by 186 in the past year. He said the premium was about bringing properties into use as permanent homes, "not raising more council tax".

## Backdated help for working-age claimants

Councillor Stephenson has also backed plans for working-age residents to have Local Council Tax Support claims backdated by up to three months, [as The Seax reported last week](https://theseax.com/story/cw-tendring-council-tax-support-backdating-working-age/). Pension-age claimants can already do this. Eligible working-age residents get up to 80 per cent off their bills, and that level would stay the same next year. Claimants would need to show they qualified during the backdated period.

"When residents are struggling, a little help at the right time can stop things getting worse," he said. The backdating plan is still subject to consultation. A draft scheme goes to the council's cabinet in December, with a final decision expected in January 2027.
