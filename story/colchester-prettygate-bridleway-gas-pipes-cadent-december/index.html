Prettygate bridleway in Colchester to shut for gas pipe work

Prettygate, Colchester · Friday 27 November 2026, 19:44 · Reported by Cadent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Cadent is replacing 1950s gas pipes under the path to Norman Way. The path closes to walkers over Christmas.

Cadent will close the bridleway that runs from Prettygate Road to Norman Way in Colchester from Monday 7 December, while its engineers replace more than 950 metres of gas pipes laid in the 1950s. The gas network company says its permits for the work run until Monday 4 January 2027.

Cadent said the path will stay closed for the whole job to keep engineers and the public safe, and that it planned the work with Essex County Council to keep disruption down. Gas stays on throughout.

Key points

  • Pipes date from the 1950s
  • Gas supply stays on during the work
  • Homes on the old pipes get advance notice of a short cut-off
