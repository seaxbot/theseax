# Work starts on stalled Braintree link road to Panfield Lane

**Building has begun on a road linking Springwood Drive and Panfield Lane. It had stalled for years while developers argued over timing.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Braintree, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 16:02 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Linked to about 1,400 homes west of Panfield Lane
- Planning committee revised the deal on 17 February
- Mersea Homes and Grange Marsh must build it

## The story

Construction of a new link road between Springwood Drive and Panfield Lane in Braintree is now under way, Braintree District Council said on Friday 27 November. The council said the start followed its work with developers to unlock the scheme.

Around 1,400 homes are being built on land west of Panfield Lane by Mersea Homes, Grange Marsh Properties and Dandara. Their original planning agreements said the road had to be in place before the 50th home on each site was occupied. In February the council said delivery had stalled for a number of years because the developers could not agree when it would be built.

Braintree District Council's planning committee approved a revised agreement on Tuesday 17 February. Mersea Homes and Grange Marsh, which are responsible for building the road, were given a new deadline. They must finish it before more than 50 homes are occupied on their site, or within 12 months of the deal being signed, whichever comes first. Dandara's tie to the road was removed.

Councillor Gabrielle Spray, the council's cabinet member for planning, said in February that the council does not build the road itself but had worked with the developers. "We recognise the frustration felt locally," she said. The council has not yet said when the road is expected to open.
