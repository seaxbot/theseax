Tendring council tenants rate housing service above national average as tenant panel gains a bigger say

Tendring District Council says 79% of its tenants are satisfied overall with its housing service, against a 73% national average, in its first annual housing performance report.

Clacton-on-Sea, Tendring · Friday 13 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tenants across Tendring took part in 724 tenancy checks and 24 estate walkabouts during 2025/26, according to Tendring District Council's first Annual Housing Improvement, Performance and Assurance Report, published on Friday 13 November. Stock condition surveys now cover most of the council's homes.

Andy Baker, Tendring District Council's Cabinet Member for Housing and Planning, presented the report to the council's cabinet. Tenants have helped review policies, set service standards and choose contractors, working alongside the Tenant Panel chaired by Carol McDougall. "We want tenants to see that their feedback makes a difference," Cllr Baker said.

A three-year Housing Improvement and Delivery Plan will guide the work, with tighter checks on performance and risk. The council's priorities for 2026/27 are better repairs, safer and better-kept homes, quicker responses when things go wrong, more help for tenants who need it and a stronger tenant voice.

Key points

  • Tenants joined 724 tenancy checks and 24 estate walkabouts in 2025/26
  • Report covers 2025/26 and is the council's first of its kind
  • Better repairs top the council's 2026/27 priorities
