Chieftains hit Cardiff for seven to stay top

A four-goal middle period did the damage as Chelmsford Chieftains beat Cardiff Fire comfortably at Riverside to stay clear at the top of NIHL South Division 1.

Chelmsford · Saturday 31 October 2026, 21:30 · Reported by Chelmsford Chieftains
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford Chieftains beat Cardiff Fire 7-2 at Riverside Ice and Leisure on Saturday 31 October to stay top of NIHL South Division 1. The win was their fifth of the league campaign and kept them clear of their nearest challengers.

Kieran Raynor scored in the first period before the Chieftains took control in the second period, when Rio Grinell-Parke, Lane Paddison, Callum Wells and Ollie Baldock all found the net. Baldock added a second in the final period and Tom Soar completed the scoring, while Owen and Rhys Smetham replied for Cardiff. Hallden Barnes-Garner had four assists, and goaltender Brandon Stones was named man of the match after facing 25 shots as the hosts fired 47 at Morgan Parsons.

The Chieftains have 21 points from eight games, five ahead of both Slough Jets and Streatham Redhawks, who each have games in hand. This was game eight of a 27-match league season.

Key points

  • Chieftains led 5-1 after two periods
  • Ollie Baldock scored twice, once on the power play
  • Chieftains outshot Cardiff 47 to 25
  • Five wins from eight games have earned 21 points
