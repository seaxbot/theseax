Drug-driving arrests in Essex now more than double drink-driving, with 1,960 held this year

Roads policing officers made almost 2,000 drug-driving arrests between January and October, and the force is preparing its Christmas crackdown on impaired drivers.

Essex · Saturday 7 November 2026, 15:20 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police says its roads policing officers arrested 1,960 people on suspicion of drug-driving between 1 January and 31 October 2026, more than twice the number held on suspicion of drink-driving. The figures, released on Saturday, show impaired driving remains one of the biggest pressures on the county's roads as the festive season approaches.

Over the same ten months officers made 886 arrests on suspicion of drink-driving, 313 arrests for failing to provide a breath test and 515 for other drink or drug related road offences, including driving while unfit. Adam Pipe, the force's head of roads policing, said drug-driving arrests were now more than twice drink-driving arrests and warned that any impaired driver could cause a collision that changes or ends a life.

The force is now preparing its annual Christmas campaign warning drivers about getting behind the wheel after alcohol or drugs. Police say patrols will continue alongside intelligence-led operations, roadside drug testing and dedicated roads policing teams targeting repeat and high-risk offenders.

Key points

  • 1,960 drug-driving arrests from January to October 2026
  • 886 arrests for suspected drink-driving
  • 313 arrests for failing to provide a breath test
  • Christmas impaired-driving campaign being prepared
