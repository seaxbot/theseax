# Castle Point MP takes Green Belt housing worries to the Commons

**Dame Rebecca Harris leads a Commons debate on housing targets and Castle Point's Green Belt. A government minister normally replies.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Benfleet, Canvey Island, Hadleigh, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 08:43 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Politics.

## Key points

- Inspectors found the Castle Point Plan unsound in early November
- The plan proposes 6,196 homes against a government figure of 11,662
- The council has not yet said how it will respond

## The story

Castle Point's MP, Dame Rebecca Harris, leads an adjournment debate in the House of Commons on Thursday 26 November. Her subject is housing targets and protecting Green Belt land on the edges of Benfleet, Canvey Island, Hadleigh and Thundersley. It is listed in the Hansard Society's guide to the week's business in Parliament. Adjournment debates come at the end of the day's sitting, so no fixed time has been given.

## Why it matters now

The debate follows the finding by government-appointed inspectors that the Castle Point Plan is unsound as the council submitted it, [as The Seax reported on 3 November](https://theseax.com/story/castle-point-plan-unsound-inspectors/). The plan runs from 2026 to 2043 and proposes 6,196 homes. The government's standard method figure for the borough is 11,662. The inspectors want Castle Point Borough Council to look again at Green Belt sites that could bring its total closer to that number.

The council's current leaders chose to build mainly on brownfield land, rather than follow the previous administration's 2022 plan, which proposed large Green Belt releases. Deputy leader Councillor Warren Gibson has pointed to the borough's Green Belt, wildlife sites, congested roads and stretched services. The council said it would seek further advice on its next steps.

## The MP's position

Dame Rebecca, a Conservative, spoke at the inspectors' first hearings in September. She has opposed Green Belt housing schemes in the constituency, including North Benfleet Hall Farm and up to 165 homes at Glebelands, and has objected to the council's grey belt classification.

## What happens next

A minister usually answers for the government at the end of an adjournment debate. The Seax will report what was said, and the government's reply, once the official Hansard record is published.
