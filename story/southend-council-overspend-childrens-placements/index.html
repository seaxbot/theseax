Southend council still heading for a £12m overspend as one child's care placement tops £1m a year

The latest six-month forecast shows the gap narrowing by about £1.3m, but children's social care costs have climbed by around £1.5m since the summer.

Southend-on-Sea · Monday 9 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council is forecasting an overspend of £11.96m for 2026/27, according to its latest figures covering the first six months of the financial year. That is about £1.3m better than earlier forecasts, but the council says rising children's placement costs are adding fresh strain.

Children's Services costs have risen by around £1.5m since the summer. One residential placement costs more than £1m a year and seven more cost over £500,000 each, and the council says children's and adult social care now account for more than all of the remaining pressure. Improvements in adult social care contributed about £1.3m to the better forecast, and most planned savings for the year have already been delivered.

Council leader Daniel Cowan said the council was under no illusion about the scale of the challenge in Children's Services. The council says it will tighten financial controls, speed up its Transformation Programme and keep pressing the Government for changes to social care funding.

Key points

  • Overspend forecast at £11.96m, down about £1.3m
  • Seven placements cost more than £500,000 a year each
  • Most planned savings for 2026/27 already delivered
