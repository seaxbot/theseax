# Clacton Victorian Christmas market raises more than £6,000 for charities

**Tendring's biggest charity Christmas market filled Clacton Town Hall with 26 charity stalls, each keeping the money it took.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Clacton-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 15:17 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Stalls sold decorations, Fairtrade goods, toys and gifts
- Each charity kept the money from its own stall
- Prize winners get a free stall in 2027

## The story

Shoppers at Clacton Town Hall on Saturday 14 November raised more than £6,000 for charities and good causes at the annual Victorian Christmas Market run by Community Voluntary Services Together (CVST), the Tendring voluntary services charity.

The Salvation Army band played visitors in, volunteers in Victorian dress greeted them at the door, and performers included Young Minds Dementia Choir, Kaleidoscope, SingalongaPen and Princes Youth Theatre. Kevin Burns, of the Clacton branch of Parkinson's UK, won best costume and Grassroots charity won best decorated stall.
