Thurrock families could get travel budgets in place of council taxis for SEND students

Thurrock Council is consulting until 24 December on changes to home-to-school transport, and says no decisions have been made.

Grays, Thurrock · Sunday 15 November 2026, 10:20 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Under the proposals, personal travel budgets would become the standard offer for Thurrock students aged 16 to 25 with special educational needs and disabilities, with families arranging journeys themselves in most cases, Thurrock Nub News reports. The consultation also covers transport for children aged 5 to 16 and for families in temporary accommodation. Any changes would take effect from April 2027 and be brought in from September 2027, once recommendations go to the council's Cabinet.

Key points

  • An expanded travel training programme is planned for post-16 students
  • The last big overhaul, in 2024, moved bookings to a single central contractor
