Thurrock families could get travel budgets in place of council taxis for SEND students

Thurrock Council is consulting until 24 December on changes to home-to-school transport, and says no decisions have been made.

Grays, Thurrock · Sunday 15 November 2026, 10:20 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Students aged 16 to 25 in Thurrock with special educational needs and disabilities would get a personal travel budget from September 2027 in most cases, with families arranging the journeys themselves instead of using council taxis and minibuses. The budget would be worked out at 55p a mile for two return journeys a day, 10p a mile more than the council's current post-16 rate, Your Thurrock reports.

For 16 to 18-year-olds who cannot travel on their own, the budget would replace council-arranged transport, and for those aged 19 to 25 it would become the standard offer. Students aged 19 and over would first have to apply for any bursary from their school or college before asking the council for help. Councillor Milena Bingley, Thurrock Council's cabinet member for education, skills and children's services, said young people had told the council they wanted more independence.

The consultation also covers children aged 5 to 16, including families in temporary accommodation and when budgets are paid, Thurrock Nub News reports. Feedback will go into recommendations for Thurrock Council's cabinet, and any changes would be agreed from April 2027. The consultation closes at 11.59pm on 24 December.

Key points

  • Proposed rate of 55p a mile, up from 45p today
  • An expanded travel training programme is planned
  • The last big overhaul, in 2024, moved bookings to a single central contractor
