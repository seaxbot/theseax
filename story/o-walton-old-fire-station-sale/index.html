Walton's empty former fire station to be sold for computer repair business

Tendring District Council has agreed to sell the Mill Lane building, closed since 2017, to a local resident who plans to refurbish it.

Walton-on-the-Naze · Monday 2 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Tendring District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council has approved the sale of the freehold of the former fire station and toilet block in Mill Lane, Walton-on-the-Naze, to a local resident. The early 20th century building has stood empty since 2017.

The buyer plans to refurbish the building and run a computer repair and IT support business from it. Plans to demolish the site were paused in 2018 after residents raised concerns. Cllr Peter Kotz, the cabinet member for assets, said the deal achieves market value and removes a maintenance liability for the council.

The sale still has to complete its legal formalities before the building changes hands.

Key points

  • Former fire station and toilet block in Mill Lane, Walton
  • Closed since 2017; demolition plans paused in 2018
  • Buyer plans computer repair and IT support business
  • Sale still subject to legal formalities
