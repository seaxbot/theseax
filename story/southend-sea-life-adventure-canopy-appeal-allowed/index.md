# Southend Sea Life Adventure wins appeal to keep wider entrance canopy

**A planning inspector has overruled Southend councillors over the seafront attraction's canopy. They said it narrowed the pavement for disabled people.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Eastern Esplanade, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 06:19 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Property.

## Key points

- Appeal reference 6013464
- Canopy first approved in September 2025
- Highways authority did not object

## The story

Inspector J Bowyer allowed the appeal by owner Philip Miller on 10 November 2026, approving revised plans for the canopy at Sea Life Adventure on Eastern Esplanade. Southend-on-Sea City Council's planning committee had refused them on 25 February, against its officers' advice.

The canopy as built is about 0.45m wider than first approved, with an extra row of posts. Planning Geek reported that the inspector found it leaves enough room to pass and does not unacceptably block the footway.
