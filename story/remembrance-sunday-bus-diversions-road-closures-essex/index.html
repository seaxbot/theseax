Remembrance Sunday shuts town centres across Essex, with First buses diverted in 16 places

Chelmsford bus station will be out of use for much of Sunday morning and Colchester's Head Street and High Street close for more than five hours as towns gather to remember the fallen.

Chelmsford, Colchester, Brentwood, Saffron Walden · Saturday 7 November 2026, 18:45 · Reported by First Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Road closures for Remembrance Sunday parades will disrupt bus services across Essex on Sunday 8 November, with First Essex listing changes in 16 towns and villages from Chelmsford and Colchester to Leigh-on-Sea and Great Dunmow. Most closures fall between mid-morning and lunchtime around the 11am two-minute silence, so anyone travelling on Sunday morning should check their route before setting out.

In Chelmsford, city centre roads close from 10am to 12.30pm and the bus station cannot be used, so passengers are asked to board at Coval Lane, the Retail Market or Parkway instead. In Colchester, Head Street and High Street close from 8am to 1.30pm, affecting routes including the 51 to 57, 68, 75, 87 and X20. Closures also hit Brentwood High Street from 10.15am to 11.45am, Rayleigh High Street from 9.30am, Wickford's Runwell Road from 10am to 1pm, and the high streets in Maldon, Burnham-on-Crouch and Great Dunmow during the morning.

Afternoon parades bring later closures, with Halstead High Street shut from 2pm to 3pm, Witham's Newland Street from 2.30pm to 3.45pm and Leigh Broadway from 2pm to 5pm. In Saffron Walden, the town council says London Road, High Street, King Street, Market Place and Market Hill close from 9.45am to 12.30pm, with no parking in the affected spots from 5am. First Essex says drivers may also pause for the two-minute silence at 11am where it is safe to do so.

Key points

  • Changes in 16 Essex towns and villages on Sunday 8 November
  • Chelmsford bus station out of use 10am to 12.30pm
  • Colchester Head Street and High Street closed 8am to 1.30pm
  • Buses may pause for the 11am silence where safe
