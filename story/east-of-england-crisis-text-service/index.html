New 24-hour NHS crisis text line opens for people in Essex struggling with their mental health

Texting SOS to 80899, or messaging on WhatsApp or webchat, now reaches trained health and care professionals at any time of day, with no minimum age.

Essex · Thursday 5 November 2026, 08:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

People across Essex can now get round-the-clock mental health crisis support by text. The new East of England Mental Health Crisis Text Service, funded by the NHS, is aimed at anyone who finds it easier to type than to phone or talk face to face, including people coping with anxiety, loneliness or overwhelming thoughts.

Users can text SOS to 80899, send a WhatsApp message to 0800 884 0655 or use a webchat run by the provider, NXG Thrive. The service was commissioned by NHS Essex Integrated Care Board together with the NHS Central East and NHS Norfolk and Suffolk boards, and responders are registered professionals such as psychologists, counsellors, social workers and mental health nurses.

It is open to children, young people and adults alike, and the web chat offers real-time translation. As well as Essex, the service covers Bedfordshire, Cambridgeshire, Hertfordshire, Luton, Milton Keynes, Norfolk, Peterborough and Suffolk, Your Harlow reports.

Key points

  • Text SOS to 80899, any time of day
  • Also on WhatsApp and webchat
  • Staffed by registered health and care professionals
  • No minimum age
