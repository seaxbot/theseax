Essex PC dismissed after panel finds gross misconduct towards three female colleagues

A misconduct hearing chaired by Assistant Chief Constable Glen Pavelin found PC Ben Norfolk's behaviour towards colleagues amounted to gross misconduct.

Essex · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

An Essex Police officer has been dismissed after a misconduct panel found he behaved in a coercive and controlling way towards female colleagues. PC Ben Norfolk was found to have committed gross misconduct at a hearing chaired by Assistant Chief Constable Glen Pavelin.

The allegations considered at the hearing related to his behaviour towards three colleagues between 2024 and 2025, and included sending intimate images of himself in uniform and suggesting sexual activity at work. Mr Pavelin called the conduct completely unacceptable and said it showed a complete disregard for the oath the officer took.

Key points

  • Gross misconduct found at a police misconduct hearing
  • Conduct towards three female colleagues in 2024 and 2025
  • PC Ben Norfolk dismissed
