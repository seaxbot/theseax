Thurrock Council widens pest checks on Purfleet's Garrison Estate after family forced out by rodents

Thurrock Council says it is determined to find the root cause of an infestation that drove a woman, her daughter and granddaughter from their council home in Purfleet.

Purfleet · Monday 16 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

The council told Thurrock Nub News it is checking neighbouring homes, deep cleaning the property and treating it, and has surveyed Chieftain Drive while it waits for results of a wider survey of the Garrison Estate. Resident Lisa Clark had said she was disappointed by the council's response. Temporary accommodation is still on offer and compensation talks continue.

Key points

  • A full review of the case will follow once it is resolved
  • Local councillors backed the family's concerns
