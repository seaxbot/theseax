# Maldon planners set to decide on 159 homes north of West Street in Tollesbury at a third meeting

**Maldon District Council's Planning Committee returns on Wednesday 25 November 2026 to an outline plan for up to 159 homes and a new road in Tollesbury, after deferring it in August and October.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Tollesbury, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 06:20 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- Application 25/00381/OUTM: up to 159 homes on land north of West Street, Tollesbury
- Deferred at Planning Committee on 5 August and 28 October 2026
- Anglian Water headroom of about 639 homes against 558 in five Tollesbury applications
- Target decision date: 4 December 2026

## The story

Maldon District Council's Planning Committee will consider an outline application for up to 159 homes on land north of West Street, Tollesbury, at its meeting on Wednesday 25 November 2026. The scheme by Welbeck Strategic Land IV LLP also includes a road linking West Street to North Road, public open space, play space, Suitable Alternative Natural Greenspace and sustainable drainage, with only access to be settled now.

The application was first heard on 5 August 2026, when a motion to refuse it was not seconded and councillors instead deferred it so officers could test possible reasons for refusal and the applicant could answer their concerns. An update report went to the committee on 28 October 2026 to take account of the new National Planning Policy Framework published on 17 August, and the item was deferred again. The latest report by the Director of Place, Planning and Growth says Anglian Water advised that the Tollesbury Water Recycling Centre has room for about 639 more homes, against 558 homes across the five Tollesbury applications assessed together.

The report also responds to legal points raised by councillors, including on drainage, and says detailed engineering drawings are not required at the outline stage. A condition requiring a Strategic Foul Water Strategy before work starts was included at Anglian Water's request. The council's target date for a decision is 4 December 2026, and an addendum is due to be circulated before the meeting.
