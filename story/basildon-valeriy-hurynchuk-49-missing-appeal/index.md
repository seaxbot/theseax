# Missing Basildon man Valeriy Hurynchuk found, police say

**Essex Police has thanked everyone who shared its appeal to find the 49-year-old.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 18:28 (UK time). Updated: Saturday 21 November 2026, 21:50. Section: News. Topic: Police.

## The story

Valeriy Hurynchuk, who was missing from Basildon, has been located, Essex Police said on Saturday evening.

Officers had appealed on Friday for help to find him after he had not been in contact for a number of weeks. Thanking the public, the force said: "Thank you for sharing our appeal."
