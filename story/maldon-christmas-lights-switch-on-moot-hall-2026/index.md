# Maldon Christmas lights switch-on draws thousands to High Street

**Maldon Town Council's Light Up Maldon filled the High Street. Music, markets and Father Christmas drew the crowds.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Maldon, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 21:39 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Caroline Coastal Radio helped run the switch-on
- Maldon Rotary Club supported the Father Christmas grotto

## The story

Town Mayor Cllr James Burrell-Cook and Deputy Town Mayor Cllr Carlie Mayes switched on the lights from the Moot Hall balcony on Thursday 26 November, Maldon Nub News reported. Two pupils from Wentworth Primary School and Maldon Primary School joined them.

Johnny Cash and Elvis tribute acts headlined the main stage, with indoor markets at the Moot Hall, All Saints' Church, the Methodist Church and the Maeldune Heritage Centre. Winners of the shop window display contest will be named on Tuesday 1 December.
