# Danbury man, 79, fined for racially abusing Stansted Airport security

**Magistrates in Chelmsford found William Ryan guilty of two counts of racially aggravated harassment after he abused staff at a London Stansted security scanner.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stansted Airport, Danbury, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 09:23 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Fined £1,600, with £300 compensation and £1,300 costs
- Incident at a security scanner on 21 June 2025
- London Stansted says abuse of its staff will not be tolerated

## The story

Ryan, of Danbury, became abusive after security staff stopped him at a scanner at the airport on 21 June 2025, Hits Radio reports. Witnesses heard him make monkey noises at the staff.

Chelmsford Magistrates' Court convicted him on 28 October. He was fined £1,600 and told to pay £300 compensation and £1,300 costs. Inspector James Southwell of Essex Police said there was no excuse for racially abusing people doing their job.
