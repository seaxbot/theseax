Inspectors find Castle Point's local plan unsound and press for more green belt homes

The planning inspectors examining the borough's blueprint say it does not provide enough housing and want extra green belt sites looked at. The council says it is extremely disappointed.

Benfleet, Canvey Island & Hadleigh · Tuesday 3 November 2026, 17:56 · Reported by Castle Point Borough Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The government-appointed inspectors examining the Castle Point Plan have concluded that the plan, as the council submitted it, is unsound. Castle Point Borough Council published their correspondence on Tuesday evening, and the finding means the borough's long-term blueprint for where new homes go cannot be adopted in its current form, with green belt land around Benfleet, Canvey Island and Hadleigh back under the spotlight.

The letter follows the inspectors' first questions in June and a hearing in September, both of which focused on how many homes the plan proposes and whether options outside the built-up areas had been properly considered. The inspectors say more housing must be considered and want further assessment of green belt land that could take development, including extra allocations to bring the total closer to the government's standard method figure. Deputy leader Councillor Warren Gibson pointed to constraints including the green belt, wildlife sites, stretched infrastructure, congested roads and declining bus services.

Councillor Gibson said the current administration had chosen to make the most of brownfield land rather than follow the previous administration's 2022 plan, which proposed substantial green belt releases. The council says it will now seek further advice on its next steps and set out its position once that work is done, though it has given no date. Strategic planning portfolio holder Councillor Tim Copsey said the focus remained on doing what is right for the borough.

Key points

  • Inspectors say the plan as submitted is unsound
  • They want more homes and extra green belt sites considered
  • The council favoured brownfield land over green belt releases
  • No date yet for the council's response
