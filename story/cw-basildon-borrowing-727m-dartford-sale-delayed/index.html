Basildon Council borrowing stands at £727m as sale of its Dartford investment property is delayed

Basildon · Monday 16 November 2026, 06:20 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Borough Council's total borrowing stood at £727 million, including finance leases, at the end of September 2026, the council's Audit and Risk Committee heard on Wednesday 4 November 2026. The committee noted the quarter two treasury management update, which covers 1 April to 30 September 2026. The minutes say borrowing stayed within the council's approved limits throughout the period.

Councillors discussed the council's investment property in Dartford and its effect on borrowing, and were told negotiations over its sale had been delayed, although a number of interested parties remained engaged. The minutes record that the Leader of the Council said that, given current borrowing levels, he did not support any increase in the council's long-term debt and that the Dartford sale should go ahead before any further investment funded by borrowing is considered. Officers said short-term borrowing might still be needed to manage cash flow, but there was no intention to raise the council's borrowing limits.

Councillors also asked about a forecast underspend of £180,000 from ending the council's area committees, and were told no alternative scheme had been identified, though money could be found from the existing budget if area committees were brought back this financial year. The report says comments from the committee will be passed to Cabinet. The quarter two finance update is due to go to Cabinet on 26 November 2026.

Key points

  • Total borrowing £727 million, including finance leases, at 30 September 2026
  • Sale of Dartford investment property delayed by negotiations
  • Leader opposes more long-term debt until the Dartford sale is done
  • Cabinet to consider the quarter two finance update on 26 November 2026
