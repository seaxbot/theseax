Man, 32, jailed for 58 weeks for breaching restraining order in Colchester area

Shane Furner, of no fixed address, admitted breaching an order that barred him from contacting a victim and from going to an address in the Colchester area.

Colchester · Saturday 7 November 2026, 09:40 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 32-year-old man has been jailed for a total of 58 weeks after repeatedly breaching a restraining order in the Colchester area. Shane Furner, of no fixed address, was sentenced at Colchester Magistrates' Court on 26 October.

The order was imposed in June after an assault conviction and banned him from contacting the victim or attending a specific address. Police found and arrested him at that address in October, he admitted the breach, and he went back to the address again while awaiting sentence. He was also dealt with for breaching an earlier suspended sentence.

Key points

  • Sentenced at Colchester Magistrates' Court on 26 October
  • Total of 58 weeks in prison
  • Order dated from June, after an assault conviction
