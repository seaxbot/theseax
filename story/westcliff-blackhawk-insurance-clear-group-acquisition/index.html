Westcliff construction insurance specialist Blackhawk bought by Clear Group

Blackhawk Insurance Services in Westcliff-on-Sea, Southend, is now part of a Goldman Sachs-backed broking group, with the price kept private.

Westcliff-on-Sea · Friday 13 November 2026, 00:00 · Reported by Insurance Business
WhatsApp Facebook X

Blackhawk, which arranges cover for builders and construction firms from its Westcliff-on-Sea base, has joined the London-based Clear Group, the trade title Insurance Business reported on 13 November. Managing director Mark Kemp said the move would let the firm keep the personal service and industry knowledge its clients value. Clear says it will invest in the business and open up the wider group's markets to its customers.

Key points

  • Clear bought Commercial Risks and Troon Underwriting in October 2026
  • Newman Pearce & Partners joined Clear in August 2026
