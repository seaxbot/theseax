# Havens Hospices asks Essex schools to stage a Santa Run in December

**Havens Hospices, which runs Little Havens children's hospice in Thundersley, wants schools to pick a December date for pupils to run, walk or skip in festive dress to raise sponsorship.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 12:44 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Free to take part, with a downloadable pack
- Charity must raise more than £225,000 a week
