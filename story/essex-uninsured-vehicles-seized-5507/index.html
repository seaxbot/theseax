Essex Police took 5,507 uninsured vehicles off the roads in ten months as week-long insurance crackdown begins

Officers seized an average of about 550 uninsured vehicles a month between January and October, and are joining a national week of action that runs until Sunday.

Essex · Monday 9 November 2026, 17:45 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police seized 5,507 uninsured vehicles between January and October 2026, peaking at 632 in May, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reports.

The force is taking part in Operation Drive Insured with the Motor Insurers' Bureau from 9 to 15 November. Roads policing head Adam Pipe said the figures showed a continued focus on removing unsafe vehicles, and officers warn vehicles can be seized on the spot if documents are not produced.

Key points

  • 5,507 seizures from January to October
  • Busiest month was May, with 632
  • Operation Drive Insured runs 9 to 15 November
