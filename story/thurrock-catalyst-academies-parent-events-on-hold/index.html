Thurrock academy trust puts school events for parents on hold, leaving nativity plays in doubt

Catalyst Academies Trust, which runs five schools in Thurrock including Benyon in South Ockendon, has written to parents after a row over a young sibling's attendance at events.

South Ockendon · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 13:00 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Catalyst Academies Trust told parents in a letter on Monday 9 November 2026 that non-compulsory school events involving parents are on hold until further notice, Thurrock Nub News reports. Thurrock MP Jen Craft, who raised the original dispute in Parliament, called the response disproportionate. The trust says it is reviewing arrangements and aims to find a sustainable way forward.

Key points

  • Schools: Benyon, Aveley Primary, Dilkes Academy, Quarry Hill Academy and Somers Heath Primary
  • Chief executive Tony Parfett says there is no ban on siblings attending
