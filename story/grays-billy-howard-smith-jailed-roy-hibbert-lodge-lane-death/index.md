# Grays drug-driver jailed over death of man, 72, hit by reversing van

**Billy Howard-Smith, 26, has been sentenced to 40 months in prison for causing the death of Roy Hibbert in Lodge Lane, Grays.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Monday 23 November 2026, 14:20 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Banned from driving for six years and eight months
- Blood tests found a cocaine by-product over the limit
- He admitted the charges on 21 August

## The story

Mr Hibbert was on foot in Lodge Lane on the evening of Monday 21 April 2025 when Howard-Smith reversed a van into him. He died in hospital two months later, on 21 June 2025.

Howard-Smith, of Heathview Road, Grays, was arrested at the scene. Blood tests found a by-product of cocaine in his blood above the legal limit for driving. Essex Police said this showed he had taken the drug at some point before. Officers from the force's Serious Collision Investigation Unit spent more than a year on the case, and he was charged in June.

At Basildon Crown Court on Friday 21 August, he admitted causing death by careless driving while over the specified limit for a drug. He also admitted possessing a Class B drug. He was sentenced on Wednesday 18 November and banned from driving for six years and eight months. Essex Police released details of the case on Monday 23 November.

Detective Richard March, of the Serious Collision Investigation Unit, said his thoughts remained with Mr Hibbert's family. He said the case showed the danger of driving after taking drugs, "even if you think it is no longer in your system".
