Chelmsford City come from behind to beat Farnborough 2-1

Goals from Clarke and a Barham penalty turned the game around at the Dunmow Group Community Stadium after Max Ehmer had put the visitors ahead.

Chelmsford · Saturday 7 November 2026, 17:04 · Reported by Chelmsford City FC
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford City fought back from a goal down to beat Farnborough 2-1 in the National League South on Saturday. The Clarets trailed at half-time but scored twice after the break to take all three points in front of their home supporters.

Farnborough captain Max Ehmer opened the scoring on 34 minutes. Clarke levelled six minutes into the second half, finishing after a run and cross from Barham, and Barham then struck from the penalty spot on 70 minutes to put City in front.

It was an eventful afternoon for Odegah, who had an early header ruled out for offside and was later booked for simulation after going down in the box. City saw out the closing minutes to secure the win, watched by a crowd of 1,523.

Key points

  • Chelmsford City 2-1 Farnborough, National League South
  • Scorers: Clarke 51, Barham pen 70; Ehmer 34 for Farnborough
  • Attendance: 1,523 at the Dunmow Group Community Stadium
  • Tompkins and Odegah were booked for City
