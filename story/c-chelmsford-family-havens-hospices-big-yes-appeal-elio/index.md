# Chelmsford parents back Havens Hospices appeal after baby son's care

**Michael and Sarah Fragnito's son Elio spent his last two days at the Little Havens children's hospice. They now support its Christmas appeal.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Thundersley, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 15:40 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- Elio was born with a rare heart condition
- The appeal is called the Big Yes! Christmas Appeal

## The story

Elio Fragnito became critically unwell within 36 hours of his birth. Doctors at Great Ormond Street Hospital found a rare and serious heart condition that had not been picked up during pregnancy. When the family were told nothing more could be done, they chose Little Havens, the children's hospice in Thundersley run by Havens Hospices. Elio died there aged 36 days.

His parents told Havens Hospices that staff helped them bathe him for the first time, arranged family photographs and let them take him outside to feel the rain. "Grief is awful, but love comes out on top," they said.

The Chelmsford couple are backing the charity's Big Yes! Christmas Appeal, which asks the community to help fund the care its nurses give.
