# Stanway roundabout works bring more road closures into December

**Closures for the new Fiveways roundabout in Stanway run into December. Peartree Road and London Road are next.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stanway, Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 07:42 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- Dyers Road and Heath Road orders each run for 33 days
- Work is linked to 420 homes at Fiveways Fruit Farm
- Natta can be reached on 01252 851155

## The story

Drivers in Stanway face more closures in the run-up to Christmas as work on the new Fiveways roundabout goes on. Stanway Parish Council's roadworks list, updated on Friday 20 November, shows closures on Dyers Road and Heath Road that began in mid-November and run for 33 days each.

The Daily Gazette reported residents' confusion over the closures and warnings about restricted access. The parish council lists the orders under the Road Traffic Regulation Act and says they are needed for the safety of the public and the workforce.

## What is being built

Natta Building Company, working for Mersea Homes and Hills Residential, is building a four-arm roundabout at the Fiveways junction. Essex Highways says it is part of the Mersea Homes development at Fiveways Fruit Farm, where 420 homes won consent in 2021. Laurence Byrne Avenue will be extended from Warren Lane to Fiveways. The aim is to take pressure off Blackberry Road by letting traffic use the Tollgate relief road. Main works began in January and were expected to take about a year.

## Dates to know

London Road closes for two nights from Monday 30 November, 7pm to 5am, for carriageway patching by Essex County Council. Peartree Road closes for four days from 4 December for the junction work. Essex Highways says closures are used only when essential and come with signed diversions. Questions go to Natta on 01252 851155.
