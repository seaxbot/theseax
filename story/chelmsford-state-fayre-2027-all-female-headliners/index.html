State Fayre claims a UK camping festival first with an all-female headline bill at Hylands Park

The Chicks, Kacey Musgraves and Shania Twain will top the country and Americana festival in Chelmsford from 18 to 20 June 2027.

Chelmsford, Hylands Park · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 10:00 · Updated Wednesday 11 November 2026, 15:43 · Reported by Billboard (via SE7EN)
WhatsApp Facebook X

State Fayre, the country, Americana and barbecue festival that launched at Hylands Park in Chelmsford this year, has named The Chicks and Kacey Musgraves as headliners for its second edition. They join Shania Twain, announced earlier this month, completing what organisers describe as the first all-female headline bill at a major UK camping festival.

The first wave of other acts includes Billy Strings, The Corrs, Brothers Osborne, Sam Barber and Bear's Den, with more to be added. Organisers Anna-Sophie Mertens and Alex Simmonds said the three headliners had each inspired change in their own way. The 2026 debut, headlined by Kings of Leon, Neil Young and The Lumineers, drew more than 50,000 people, the festival says.

Weekend and Saturday tickets are already on sale, a presale for Friday and Sunday day tickets is running, and general sale for those days opens at 9am on Friday 13 November.

Key points

  • Billy Strings and The Corrs are in the first wave of other acts
  • Friday and Sunday day tickets go on general sale at 9am on 13 November
  • More than 50,000 attended the first State Fayre, organisers say
