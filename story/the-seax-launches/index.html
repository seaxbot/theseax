A new way to see Essex: The Seax launches to bring the whole county together

From breaking news and local sport to council decisions, community events and the stories shaping our towns, a new free online platform aims to put everything happening across Essex in one place.

Essex · Monday 9 November 2026, 07:00
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex is one of England's largest and most diverse counties. Every day, thousands of stories unfold across its towns, villages and communities. But finding out what's happening, and keeping up with it all, isn't always straightforward.

That is the idea behind The Seax, a new free online platform launching at 7am today, Monday 9 November, with an ambition to change the way people discover what's happening across Essex.

Rather than focusing on just one town, one publication or one subject, The Seax brings together news, information and resources from across the county, creating a single destination for people who want to stay connected to their local area.

From Chelmsford to Colchester, Southend to Saffron Walden, Basildon to Braintree, the aim is to make it easier to see the bigger picture of life in Essex.

More than just local news

News is at the heart of The Seax, but the ambition goes considerably further.

Visitors can explore stories from across the county, catch up on local sporting results, discover upcoming events, find community organisations and charities, and access practical information about public services.

There are dedicated areas covering everything from weather and webcams to arts, culture and what's happening in individual communities.

The idea is straightforward: people shouldn't need to visit dozens of different websites to get a better understanding of their county.

The Seax brings these different strands together, helping readers discover stories and information they might otherwise have missed.

Keeping an eye on local decisions

One of the features setting The Seax apart is Council Watch.

Decisions made by local councils can have a direct impact on people's everyday lives, influencing everything from housing and planning to roads, schools, local facilities and council spending.

Yet keeping track of what is being discussed, proposed and decided can be difficult.

Council Watch aims to make this information more accessible by highlighting developments emerging from council agendas, reports, meetings and published documents, with links to the original sources.

It is about helping residents understand what is happening behind the scenes, as well as what is already making the headlines.

Celebrating Essex and its communities

The county is home to an extraordinary range of community groups, charities, volunteers, sports clubs and local organisations.

Their stories don't always receive the attention they deserve.

The Seax aims to give readers more opportunities to discover the people and organisations making a difference, alongside coverage of grassroots sport, local events, theatre, festivals and other activities taking place throughout Essex.

Whether it is a local football club enjoying a successful weekend, a charity supporting families, a community event bringing residents together or a decision affecting a particular neighbourhood, these are all part of the bigger Essex story.

A different approach to finding information

The Seax uses technology to bring together information from a wide range of sources, making it easier to discover what's happening across the county.

Importantly, the platform is designed to help people find original reporting and information, with links directing readers to the publishers and organisations behind the stories.

It is not simply about having more information. It is about making that information easier to find, navigate and understand.

A daily briefing, Essex at 7, will also help readers start their mornings with a snapshot of the latest stories, weather, travel information and sporting results.

Just the beginning

The launch of The Seax marks the start of an ambitious project, with plenty of scope to develop its coverage, features and connections with communities across Essex.

There is no shortage of things happening in the county, and no shortage of stories worth discovering.

The challenge is bringing them together in a way that makes sense to the people who live, work and spend time here.

The Seax hopes to do exactly that.

Whether you want to know what's happening in your town, follow your local football team, understand a council decision, find something to do this weekend or simply get a better picture of the county around you, there is something to explore.

The Seax is now live, free to access and open to everyone.
