Isthmian North: Canvey Island hit five at Witham Town as Bowers & Pitsea win at Concord Rangers

It was a strong Saturday for Essex sides on the road, with Tilbury and Brightlingsea Regent also collecting away wins.

Canvey Island · Saturday 14 November 2026, 18:25 · Reported by Non League Matters
WhatsApp Facebook X

Canvey Island won 5-1 at Witham Town in the Isthmian League North Division on Saturday 14 November 2026, in front of 413 at Spa Road. In the other Essex derby, Bowers & Pitsea beat Concord Rangers 2-1 at Thames Road.

Tilbury won 4-0 at Felixstowe & Walton United and Brightlingsea Regent won 3-1 at Wroxham. Buckhurst Hill beat AFC Sudbury 2-1 at home, while Takeley drew 1-1 at Fakenham Town and Grays Athletic lost 3-1 to leaders Gorleston.

Key points

  • Gorleston lead the division on 37 points
  • Concord Rangers are seventh, the highest Essex club
  • Little Oakley host Hashtag United on Saturday 21 November
