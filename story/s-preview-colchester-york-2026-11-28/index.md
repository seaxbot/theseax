# Colchester United host fifth-placed York City in League Two

**Danny Cowley's U's are three points above the League Two drop zone and face one of the division's promotion chasers at home.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 07:20 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- York have played one more league game than Colchester
- Raggett scored Colchester's goal at Prenton Park

## The story

Colchester United play York City in League Two at the JobServe Community Stadium on Saturday 28 November, kick-off 3pm. Colchester are 21st on 15 points from 15 games, according to the Sky Sports table. York are fifth on 24 points from 16 games, nine points better off.

Form has been patchy for the U's. They ended a 10-game winless run by beating Gillingham 2-0 on 14 November. A week later they lost 2-1 at Tranmere Rovers, where Dylan Jones scored the winner in the 90th minute after Sean Raggett had equalised. York have won six, drawn six and lost four of their league games.

For team news, the latest Colchester has said in writing came before the Tranmere trip. Striker Kylian Kouassi was then still out with a hamstring injury, and Ellis Iandolo had stitches in an ear wound. Wolves loanee Leon Chiwome was back training on grass after his own hamstring problem in mid-November. The club and the Daily Gazette have both published previews, linked below, and The Seax could not read their latest injury news before publishing. The U's go to Grimsby Town next.
