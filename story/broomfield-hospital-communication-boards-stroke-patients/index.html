Picture boards help Broomfield stroke patients tell nurses where it hurts

Patients at Broomfield Hospital near Chelmsford who struggle to speak or understand staff now have communication boards on the acute stroke unit, with Basildon and Southend hospitals next.

Broomfield, Chelmsford · Monday 16 November 2026, 10:18 · Reported by Chelmsford City Hub
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust said on Monday 16 November that the boards let patients show how they feel, where they hurt and what help they need, Chelmsford City Hub reports. Mid and South Essex Hospitals Charity paid £1,700 for them, and the idea followed a deaf patient's difficulties when an interpreter was not immediately available.

Key points

  • Emergency departments, children's wards, critical care and labour wards will also get them
  • Led by Georgina Fusedale, senior sister on the acute stroke unit
