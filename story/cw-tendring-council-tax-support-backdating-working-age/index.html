Tendring plans to backdate council tax support for working-age claimants

Clacton-on-Sea · Friday 20 November 2026, 06:15 · Original reporting from council papers
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tendring District Council wants to extend a rule that now helps only pension-age claimants. Other council tax discounts and premiums in the district stay the same in 2027/28.

Tendring District Council's cabinet agreed on Friday 13 November 2026 to consult on a change to its Local Council Tax Support scheme for 2027/28. The change would let working-age claimants have their claims backdated by up to 3 months. The council must first consult the major preceptors, the other public bodies that set a share of the council tax bill.

The report to the cabinet says the scheme already lets pension-age claimants backdate claims by 3 months. It says a few recent working-age cases would have benefited from the same help where there was good cause, such as clear proof they were eligible before they applied. The rest of the scheme stays as it is, including a maximum discount of 80% for working-age claimants.

A draft scheme goes back to the cabinet on Friday 11 December 2026. The Resources and Services Overview and Scrutiny Committee will look at it before Full Council decides in January 2027. The cabinet also recommended keeping the 100% council tax premium on second homes and the current premiums on long-term empty homes.

Key points

  • Working-age claims could be backdated by up to 3 months
  • Maximum support for working-age claimants stays at 80%
  • Hardship policy can now consider cases about second home and empty home premiums
  • Full Council to decide in January 2027
