Purfleet lorry park refused by Thurrock councillors goes to appeal

Developers want the Planning Inspectorate to overturn the refusal of 24-hour HGV and container storage on the former vehicle testing site in Tank Hill Road, Purfleet-on-Thames.

Purfleet-on-Thames · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 13:28 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Thurrock Council's planning committee voted 6-2 in July to reject the part-retrospective application, against its officers' advice to approve it, Your Thurrock reports. Ward councillor Aaron Green is urging residents to send their views to the inspector, citing lorry traffic, road safety, noise and air pollution.

Key points

  • Appeal reference 6017268
  • Plans also include hardstanding repairs and acoustic fencing
