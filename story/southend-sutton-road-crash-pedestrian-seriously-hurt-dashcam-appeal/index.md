# Southend crash: police seek dashcam footage after man seriously hurt

**A pedestrian in his 40s was hit by a car in Sutton Road last Saturday. Police want witnesses and footage.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 07:53 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- Black Peugeot 2008 involved
- Near the Cromwell Road junction at about 5.30pm
- Quote incident 961 of 21 November

## The story

Police are appealing for witnesses after a man was seriously injured when he was hit by a car in Southend last weekend.

The crash happened at about 5.30pm on Saturday 21 November in Sutton Road, near its junction with Cromwell Road. It involved the pedestrian, a man in his 40s, and a black Peugeot 2008. He was taken to hospital with serious injuries, Essex Police said.

Officers want to hear from anyone who saw what happened. They are also asking drivers who were nearby at the time to check their dashcams, and anyone with CCTV or other footage to get in touch. Police have not given an update on the man's condition.

Anyone with information should quote incident 961 of 21 November. Reports can be made on the Essex Police website, through its Live Chat or by calling 101. Information can also be given anonymously to the independent charity Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
