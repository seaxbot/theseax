Chelmsford City let two-goal lead slip in 2-2 draw at Walton

Chelmsford · Saturday 21 November 2026, 20:13 · Reported by Chelmsford City FC
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mark Cooper's Chelmsford side were cruising at the break before the second-bottom club hit back in the last ten minutes in Surrey.

Chelmsford City drew 2-2 away to Walton & Hersham in the National League South on Saturday 21 November 2026, after leading 2-0 at half-time. Jack Barham scored both City goals, in the 27th and 42nd minutes.

The hosts struck twice in three minutes late on. Makise Evans scored on 80 minutes and Kleyton Dowglas De Biazze Junior levelled on 83. The crowd was 576.

Key points

  • Barham's goals came in the 27th and 42nd minutes
  • Walton & Hersham scored on 80 and 83 minutes
  • Chelmsford sit 10th in the National League South on 26 points
