Harlow police want to speak to Damien Clinton, 33, over theft inquiry

Harlow · Saturday 21 November 2026, 14:55 · Reported by Essex Police Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police wants to speak to Damien Clinton in connection with an ongoing theft investigation.

The 33-year-old is white and of medium build, with short blond or ginger hair and tattoos on the backs of his hands. He has connections to Harlow.

Anyone who sees him or knows where he is should contact Essex Police quoting reference 42/180182/26. Information can be given through the force's website or Live Chat, by calling 101, or anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.

Key points

  • Medium build, short blond or ginger hair
  • Quote reference 42/180182/26
  • Call 101 or Crimestoppers anonymously
