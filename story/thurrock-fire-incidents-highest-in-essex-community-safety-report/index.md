# Thurrock records Essex's highest level of fire incidents, report finds

**Deliberate fires rose across Thurrock last year. A council safety report names arson as an emerging risk.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Thurrock, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 14:36 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Domestic abuse incidents fell 11.6%
- Partnership budget was £41,905, with no direct council cash
- Grays Riverside named an antisocial behaviour hotspot

## The story

Your Thurrock reported on Wednesday 25 November that the Thurrock Community Safety Partnership's 2025/26 annual report puts the borough top in Essex for fire incidents. It names deliberate fire-setting and arson-related antisocial behaviour as risks needing more action.

Overall recorded crime fell slightly, and domestic abuse incidents dropped by 11.6%. The partnership ran on £41,905 last year. Councillors discuss the findings at the Place Overview and Scrutiny Committee on Thursday.
