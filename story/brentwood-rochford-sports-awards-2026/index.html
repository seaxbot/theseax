Elliott Smith crowned Sports Personality of the Year at joint Brentwood and Rochford awards

Brentwood and Rochford councils honoured athletes, coaches, clubs and volunteers at a shared ceremony in Rayleigh, with Graham Gooch helping to hand out the prizes.

Brentwood, Rayleigh · Saturday 7 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Brentwood Online
WhatsApp Facebook X

Elliott Smith was named Sports Personality of the Year at the joint Brentwood and Rochford sports awards, held on Tuesday 3 November at the Mill Arts and Events Centre in Rayleigh. Former England cricket captain Graham Gooch spoke and helped present the awards.

Brentwood Central Boxing Club was named Community Club of the Year, Isabelle Curtis-Rich took the young sports personality award and Alan Packman was coach of the year, Brentwood Online reports.

Key points

  • Ceremony held on 3 November at the Mill, Rayleigh
  • Run by Brentwood and Rochford councils, sponsored by Everyone Active
  • Sue Kite named Unsung Hero
