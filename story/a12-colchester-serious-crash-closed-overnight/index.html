A12 shut between Colchester junctions 26 and 28 after car hits pedestrian, with diversions through the town

Police and ambulance crews were called to the A12 at Colchester on Saturday evening, and National Highways said the road was expected to stay closed through the evening.

Colchester · Saturday 7 November 2026, 19:00 · Updated Saturday 7 November 2026, 21:49 · Reported by Daily Gazette
WhatsApp Facebook X

Both carriageways of the A12 at Colchester were closed on Saturday evening after a car was in collision with a pedestrian, the BBC reported. The closure, between junction 26 for Eight Ash Green and Tollgate and junction 28 for Colchester United's stadium, cut one of the county's busiest roads and left drivers queuing in both directions.

National Highways said the incident happened shortly before 5.43pm on Saturday 7 November, and Essex Police and ambulance crews attended. The Daily Gazette and the East Anglian Daily Times reported that a vehicle overturned and that the closure was likely to last through the night. Drivers caught in the stationary traffic were asked to wait for instructions from police.

Diversions were put in place through Colchester. National Highways said trapped traffic would be released first on the London-bound carriageway and then on the Ipswich-bound side. Anyone planning to use the A12 around Colchester should check for updates before setting out.

Key points

  • Closed both ways between junctions 26 and 28 at Colchester
  • Incident shortly before 5.43pm on Saturday 7 November
  • Police and ambulance crews attended
  • Diversions through Colchester; trapped traffic released London-bound first
