Man in his 20s dies after being hit by a car on the A12 at Colchester as police seek dashcam footage

The collision near junction 26 shortly before 5.30pm on Saturday closed both carriageways until shortly before 5am on Sunday.

Colchester · Saturday 7 November 2026, 19:00 · Updated Sunday 8 November 2026, 17:51 · Reported by BBC News (via Gateway 97.8)
WhatsApp Facebook X

A man in his 20s has died after being hit by a small car on the A12 near Colchester on Saturday evening, Greatest Hits Radio Essex reports. The collision happened near junction 26 shortly before 5.30pm on Saturday 7 November, and BBC News says the road stayed closed well into Sunday morning.

The man died at the scene, and his name has not been released. The East Anglian Daily Times reported that both carriageways between junction 26 at Eight Ash Green and junction 28 reopened shortly before 5am. In a separate crash the same evening, a driver was seriously hurt when his car left the road between Marks Tey and Stanway and caught fire.

Essex Police are asking anyone who saw the collision, or who has dashcam footage from the area, to come forward to help their investigation.

  • Both carriageways between junctions 26 and 28 reopened shortly before 5am on Sunday
  • Police want witnesses and dashcam footage
  • A separate crash between Marks Tey and Stanway left a driver seriously hurt
