# Brightlingsea Rotary Santa sleigh sets out its December streets

**Santa's sleigh is back on Brightlingsea's streets this Christmas. Every donation goes to local good causes.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brightlingsea, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 09:14 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- First stop: late-night shopping, 4 December
- Tours run 14 to 24 December from 6pm
- 2025 tour raised £7,000.50

## The story

Brightlingsea & District Rotary Club has published the routes for its Santa sleigh, which tours the town every evening in the run-up to Christmas. Brightlingsea Info reported the plans on Saturday 28 November, and the club's own sleigh website lists the routes for 2026.

Santa's first stop is the late-night shopping evening on Friday 4 December, when the sleigh will be parked by Spirals from 6pm to 7.15pm. Street tours begin on 14 December and run right up to Christmas Eve, with the sleigh setting off at 6pm each evening, according to the club.

## Where the sleigh goes

On the first night Santa visits Hurst Green, Mill Street, Whitegate Road, Chapel Road and the Bellfield roads. Later evenings take in the Lower Park Road and Western Road area, Manor House Way and Marennes Crescent, Regent Road and Spring Road, and Red Barn Road. On Christmas Eve he finishes the tour along the High Street, Tower Street, The Hard and Waterside.

On 15 December the route may change because of planned roadworks, so families on that run are told to check the sleigh website before heading out. Families can also follow a live tracker showing where Santa is on the night.

## Where the money goes

All the money raised supports local charities and good causes, the club says. Past beneficiaries listed for this year's appeal include Wivenhoe Scouts and Guides, Brightlingsea in Bloom, Brightlingsea Lido, the Brightlingsea Hospice Support Group and several pre-school and primary school parent groups. Donations can be made by card through the sleigh website. Its records show last year's tour raised just over £7,000.
