Grays-raised Russell Brand faces sentencing on Monday after jury convicts him of rape and three sexual assaults

A jury at Southwark Crown Court convicted the Thurrock-born actor of offences against four women between 2004 and 2009. He will be sentenced on Monday.

Grays · Thursday 5 November 2026, 15:29 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russell Brand, the comedian and actor who grew up in Grays and went to school in the town, has been found guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault after a four-week trial at Southwark Crown Court. The 51-year-old, who now lives in Oxfordshire, spent his first night in custody after the unanimous verdicts on Thursday.

The jury convicted him over offences against four women at a London bar, at a radio studio, at his London home and on a Channel 4 project, all between 2004 and 2009. He was cleared of three other counts, including a rape alleged to have happened at a Bournemouth hotel in 1999. The Metropolitan Police said officers spoke to more than 100 witnesses and took over 400 statements during the investigation, which began after allegations were reported in September 2023.

Brand is due to be sentenced at the same court on Monday 9 November. The Met says it is still investigating two further rape allegations, with files sent to the Crown Prosecution Service, and Detective Superintendent Andy Furphy urged anyone else affected to come forward on 101.

Key points

  • Unanimous guilty verdicts on one rape and three sexual assaults
  • Cleared of three other counts
  • Sentencing at Southwark Crown Court on Monday 9 November
  • Met still investigating two further allegations
