Russell Brand, who grew up in Grays, could challenge his 10-and-a-half-year rape sentence, solicitor tells the Standard

A criminal defence solicitor not involved in the case told the Evening Standard an appeal against the sentence was the most likely next step, after the comedian was jailed at Southwark Crown Court on Monday.

Grays · Thursday 5 November 2026, 15:29 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 06:46 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Russell Brand, the comedian and actor who grew up in Grays and went to school in the town, has been jailed for 10 years and six months for one rape and three sexual assaults. Mr Justice Bennathan passed sentence at Southwark Crown Court on Monday 9 November, four days after a jury found him guilty following a four-week trial.

The judge gave nine years for the rape and a further year and a half for the sexual assaults, the Evening Standard reports. Brand will serve two-thirds of the term before he can be released on licence, and he will be on the sex offenders' register for life. The offences were committed against four women between 2004 and 2009; the jury cleared him of two counts of rape and one of attempted indecent assault.

Brand, 51, had spent four nights at HMP Wandsworth since the verdicts and was expected to return there. Raj Chada, head of criminal defence at the London firm Hodge Jones & Allen, who is not involved in the case, told the Standard an appeal against the sentence was the most likely route. The Metropolitan Police praised the women's courage, resilience and determination; all four are entitled to lifelong anonymity, and the Met has said it is still investigating two further allegations, with files sent to the Crown Prosecution Service.

Key points

  • Nine years for the rape plus 18 months for three sexual assaults
  • Must serve two-thirds of the term before release on licence
  • On the sex offenders' register for life
