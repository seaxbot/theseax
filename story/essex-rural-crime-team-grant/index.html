£57,721 grant gives Essex Police rural team an all-terrain vehicle, mobile ANPR and trail cameras

New kit paid for by the crime commissioner's Safer Streets fund is aimed at hare coursing, machinery theft, poaching and wildlife crime across the countryside.

Rural Essex · Thursday 5 November 2026, 06:28 · Reported by Basildon & Billericay Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police's Rural Engagement Team has been awarded £57,721 from the Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner's Safer Streets fund after a bid focused on rural crime. The money is intended to help officers tackle offences that hit farms and countryside communities across the county.

The grant pays for a John Deere Gator all-terrain vehicle, mobile ANPR cameras fitted to three vehicles, trail cameras and signs for hotspot locations, ATV training, a trailer and Farm Watch materials. Hare coursing, theft of agricultural machinery, poaching and wildlife crime are the main targets.

Key points

  • Grant worth £57,721 from the Safer Streets fund
  • Buys an all-terrain vehicle, mobile ANPR and trail cameras
  • Targets hare coursing, machinery theft, poaching and wildlife crime
