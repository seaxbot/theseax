Colchester Zoo rated second-best day out in the East of England on Tripadvisor reviews

An agency study of reviews put only IWM Duxford ahead of the Stanway zoo in the region.

Colchester, Stanway · Sunday 8 November 2026, 13:37 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester Zoo has been ranked the second-best attraction in the East of England in an analysis of Tripadvisor reviews by One Day Agency, Your Thurrock reports. The zoo had the most reviews in the regional top ten, 6,902, with almost 73 per cent rated five stars.

IWM Duxford in Cambridgeshire took first place, while Southend Pier was the only other Essex attraction named, in 39th place.

Key points

  • Colchester Zoo scored 68.97, second in the region
  • 6,902 reviews, the most in the regional top ten
  • Southend Pier ranked 39th
