Maldon District groups can bid for Livewell health grants of up to £2,000

Voluntary groups, schools and social enterprises can apply for new projects tackling inactivity, isolation or mental health before 14 December.

Maldon · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 13:58 · Reported by Maldon District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Public Health grants of up to £2,000 per project
  • Projects must meet two of three local health priorities
  • Applications reviewed monthly, closing 14 December
  • Running costs are not covered
