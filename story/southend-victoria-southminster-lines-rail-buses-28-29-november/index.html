Southend Victoria and Elizabeth line trains replaced by buses this weekend

Wickford, Southend Victoria, Southminster, Ingatestone & Shenfield · Updated Wednesday 25 November 2026, 19:44 · First published Monday 23 November 2026, 13:38 · Reported by Network Rail
WhatsApp Facebook X

Rail passengers from Southend, Wickford and the Crouch Valley face a weekend of replacement buses as Network Rail carries out maintenance on the Southend Victoria and Southminster lines.

On Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November buses replace trains from Southend Victoria and from Ingatestone to Newbury Park, the Central line station in east London, and between Southminster and Wickford, Network Rail said. Tickets will be accepted on the Underground between Newbury Park and Stratford or Liverpool Street.

Trains from Norwich, Ipswich and Clacton will run only as far as Shenfield, with passengers changing to replacement buses via Ingatestone. The work includes general maintenance of the Southend Victoria and Southminster line and preparation on the Great Eastern main line ahead of Christmas track work at Forest Gate and Stratford.

The Elizabeth line is also shut on the same two days. Transport for London says no trains will run between Paddington and Shenfield on Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November, while engineers renew rails and crossings and maintain overhead lines. Replacement buses run from Shenfield to Chadwell Heath and Newbury Park, and from Stratford to Romford. Trish Ashton, TfL's director of rail, said replacement buses and other travel options would be available throughout.

Key points

  • Southminster to Wickford also served by buses
  • Underground tickets accepted from Newbury Park to Stratford
  • No Elizabeth line trains from Shenfield on either day
