Southend's Lazydays adds Ella Eyre, KT Tunstall and Pixie Lott for 2027

Southend, Priory Park · Thursday 26 November 2026, 10:01 · First reported by Lazydays Festival · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Sigala, Daniel Bedingfield and Celebrity Traitors winner Bash Croft are also on the biggest line-up announcement yet for the Priory Park festival, which returns in July 2027.

Southend's Lazydays Festival has named the next wave of acts for summer 2027, in what its organisers call their biggest line-up announcement yet. They join headliners Dizzee Rascal, Kaiser Chiefs and Olly Murs at Priory Park from Friday 23 to Sunday 25 July 2027. Madness open the week with a separate concert on the Thursday.

The festival expects more than 22,000 attendances across the four days. About 8,000 people went in 2025 and more than 12,000 in 2026, Your Southend has reported.

Who is coming

BRIT Award winner Ella Eyre brings her full band and her UK number one Waiting All Night. Grammy-nominated Scottish singer KT Tunstall adds Suddenly I See and Black Horse and the Cherry Tree.

Pixie Lott joins her fellow Essex-raised pop star Olly Murs for what the festival calls a homecoming Sunday.

For dance fans, Sigala plays a set of anthems and Jaguar Skills takes over The Big Hop Stage. Lottery Winners arrive fresh from supporting Robbie Williams on his stadium tour, and Daniel Bedingfield brings Gotta Get Thru This.

Lazydays says it is the first UK festival to book Bash Croft for 2027. The Heartstopper actor won BBC One's The Celebrity Traitors and released his debut EP, The Great Forever, in November. The Amy Winehouse Band, made up of musicians who played with the late singer, is also on the bill. Issey Cross brings drum and bass to the festival for the first time.

Room for local talent

Rising artists Girl in the Year Above, The Bracknall, Grace Gachot and Abby T are booked, alongside homegrown acts Cuckoo Corner and Nemesis. The Breakwater Stage will again showcase new talent.

Madness on the opening night

The Madness concert on Thursday 22 July is ticketed separately. Peter Hook and The Light, The Selecter, The Undertones, Hak Baker, David Rodigan, Dub Pistols, Ed Rome, Gentleman's Dub Club and The Skints support. Grapevine reported in November that 90% of tickets had already gone.

How it started

Three friends from Southend launched Lazydays in 2024: Andy Williamson, Elliott Waters and Mike Forster-Bromfield. David Rodigan headlined that first year. The 2026 headliners were Tinie Tempah, Feeder and Sister Sledge.

"This is where people really start to see the scale of what we're creating for 2027," said Elliott Waters, co-founder and artistic director. "Lazydays has never just been about the music. It's the atmosphere, the food and drink, the unexpected moments."

What else is new

For the first time there will be an off-site campsite, with shuttle buses to Priory Park. The Thirsty Moose festival pub, silent disco, Bangers Bingo, vintage rides and VIP garden all return, with comedy, street theatre and local street food. Children under six get in free.

Tickets

Weekend tickets start at £149.95, weekend camping tickets at £194.45 and day tickets at £54.95, all plus booking fees. Payment plans start at £35 a month for a weekend ticket and £10 a month for a day ticket. More acts and attractions are due to be announced in the coming months, at lazydaysfestival.com.

Key points

  • Headliners Dizzee Rascal, Kaiser Chiefs and Olly Murs
  • More than 22,000 attendances expected over four days
  • First off-site campsite, with shuttles to the park
  • Under-sixes go free
