Kids Inspire opens Christmas hamper drop-off for Essex families

The Great Baddow children's charity is collecting gifts and complete food hampers for nominated children and families across Essex, an appeal it has run every year since 2009.

Great Baddow · Sunday 1 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Kids Inspire
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Hamper drop-off at Hargrave House, Molrams Lane runs from Monday 2 November to Wednesday 9 December, weekdays 10am to 4pm
  • New, unwrapped gifts for ages 0 to 18 worth about £10 to £20 are wanted
  • Gift tags can also be picked from the tree at Dunelm in Chelmsford
  • Partial hampers, clothing and preloved items cannot be accepted
