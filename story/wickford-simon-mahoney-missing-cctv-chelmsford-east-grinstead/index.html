Missing Wickford man Simon Mahoney pictured on CCTV in Chelmsford shops on Friday

Essex Police say they are extremely concerned for the 54-year-old and think he went by train through London. Anyone who sees him should call 101, or 999 in an emergency.

Wickford · Sunday 15 November 2026, 06:04 · Updated Sunday 15 November 2026, 13:45 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police have released CCTV pictures of Simon Mahoney taken in shops in Chelmsford on Friday 13 November, as the search for the missing Wickford man widens beyond the county.

Officers believe he may have travelled by train to London and then on to East Grinstead, West Sussex, and they are working with other police forces to trace him. They say they are extremely concerned for his welfare and want to find him as quickly as possible.

Simon is 6ft tall, of medium build and has brown hair. In the CCTV pictures he is wearing glasses and a dark jacket with pale reflective bands around the cuffs.

Anyone who has seen him, or knows where he is, should contact Essex Police straight away on 101, or 999 in an emergency.
