# Gilston's first 276 homes by Harlow: what the new village will include

**Plans for the first homes in the Gilston expansion next to Harlow are now approved. Here is what the new village will include.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Gilston, Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 25 November 2026, 16:29 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Property.

## Key points

- Approval announced by Savills on 29 October
- 39 homes for affordable rent, 26 shared ownership
- More than 1,000 cycle parking spaces planned

## The story

The first 276 homes in Village 7 of the Gilston Area, just north of Harlow across the River Stort, have detailed planning approval from East Herts Council. Savills, which handled the application for the builder, announced the approval on 29 October. The Harlow and Gilston Garden Town partnership set out on Wednesday 25 November what the village will include.

Of the 276 homes, 65 are affordable: 39 for affordable rent and 26 for shared ownership, in a mix of one to five bedrooms. Taylor Wimpey is building the village, which East Herts Council says will have 1,500 homes in all. Every home will be linked to a shared heating and hot water network, and the Community Heat Hub behind it must be working before the first residents move in.

## What the village will have

Walking and cycling routes will run separately through Church Lane Green, with links to a planned public transport corridor and more than 1,000 cycle parking spaces. Residents will get raised beds for growing food, a foraging trail of fruit plants, a sensory garden and a farm-themed play area, plus five smaller play spots along everyday routes. The village masterplan, approved in April, also includes a football hub and the restoration of listed barns for offices and community use.

Councillor Maura Connolly, East Herts Council's executive member for planning and growth, called it "a major moment for Gilston". Jonathan Locke, head of planning at Taylor Wimpey North Thames, said work could now start on what will become the gateway to the new community.

## Why it matters for Harlow

Gilston sits in Hertfordshire, but the garden town brings it together with Harlow's plans for homes, jobs and transport on both sides of the county border. [Planners heard last week that the whole Gilston scheme is a 30-year job](https://theseax.com/story/o-gilston-area-10000-homes-30-year-vision-east-herts/), with schools, green space and other infrastructure to follow the homes.
