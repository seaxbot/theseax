Thurrock MPs demand talks with c2c and Network Rail bosses after signal failure strands commuters

A signalling fault between Stanford-le-Hope and Grays hit Wednesday's morning rush, while Purfleet stays shut after the Barking freight derailment and four MPs call the disruption unacceptable.

Grays, Stanford-le-Hope, Tilbury & Purfleet · Saturday 31 October 2026, 12:43 · Updated Wednesday 4 November 2026, 11:09 · Reported by National Rail
WhatsApp Facebook X

c2c passengers in Thurrock faced another disrupted morning on Wednesday when a signalling failure between Stanford-le-Hope and Grays stopped trains, leaving commuters stranded at Stanford-le-Hope, East Tilbury, Tilbury and Grays, Thurrock Nub News reports. Purfleet station remains closed after the freight train derailment near Barking on 23 October, with repairs expected to run until at least the week of 9 November.

Thurrock MP Jen Craft is coordinating a joint response and has asked to meet c2c managing director Rob Mullen and Ellie Borrows, managing director of Network Rail's Eastern region. In her letter she describes the disruption as far too regular in recent months, pointing to overcrowded trains, cancellations, delays and extra travel costs, and calls c2c "an essential lifeline" for many communities. MPs Margaret Mullane, James Asser and Bayo Alaba have backed the call.

c2c said on Wednesday morning that engineers were working on the signalling fault and expected disruption until about 10am. Rail replacement buses continue between Upminster and Grays via Rainham and Purfleet while the Barking track, signalling and cabling are rebuilt, and passengers delayed may be able to claim Delay Repay.
