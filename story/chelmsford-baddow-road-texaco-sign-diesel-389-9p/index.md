# Chelmsford forecourt sign shows diesel at 389.9p a litre

**Drivers on Baddow Road did a double take on Saturday morning. A fuel price app listed the real price at 189.9p.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 28 November 2026, 08:48 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Unleaded on the same sign was 157.9p
- A 50-litre fill at the sign's price would cost £194.95
- Nearby forecourts were listed within about 2p of each other

## The story

The price board at the Texaco and Co-op forecourt on Baddow Road was showing diesel at 389.9p a litre, in a photo shared in a Chelmsford community group at about 8.20am. Unleaded, on the line above, was 157.9p.

"Blimey had a shock when I drove past this morning," wrote the driver who posted the picture. He said it was obviously a mistake.

It looks like one wrong digit. A fuel price comparison app listed the station's price at 189.9p a litre on Saturday morning, exactly £2 less than the board. The BP forecourt on the same road was listed at the same price.

There is nothing to suggest anyone was charged the higher figure. The Seax has seen the photo of the board but has not checked the pumps.

## What the sign's price would cost

At 389.9p a litre, putting 50 litres in the tank would cost £194.95. At 189.9p it is £94.95, a difference of £100.

The same app listed Asda in Chelmsford at 187.7p and Sainsbury's at Springfield at 188.9p.

If a price board and a pump ever disagree, check the pump display before you fill up.
