# Missing Harlow man found, police say

**Essex Police has ended its appeal for a 45-year-old man reported missing from Harlow on Friday.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 20:15 (UK time). Updated: Friday 27 November 2026, 22:20. Section: News. Topic: Police.

## The story

A man reported missing from Harlow on Friday 27 November has been found, Essex Police said on Friday night.

Officers from the force's Harlow district said they were no longer looking for Stuart Buckland, 45, and thanked everyone who shared their appeal.

Police gave no further details. The Seax has removed his photograph and the description from the earlier appeal now that he has been found.
