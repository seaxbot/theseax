HAVENS Hospices opens Light up a Life dedications for Christmas

Supporters can dedicate a light on the hospices' Christmas trees in memory of a loved one by Sunday 15 November.

Southend-on-Sea, Prittlewell & Canvey Island · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Dedication deadline Sunday 15 November
  • Names shown in Books of Remembrance through December
  • Books at Canvey Methodist Church, Southend Crematorium, St Mary's Prittlewell and Fair Havens
  • Sponsored this year by S. Stibbards & Sons
