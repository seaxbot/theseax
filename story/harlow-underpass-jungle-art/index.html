Harlow underpass between Katherines and Sumners turned into a jungle

Colourful jungle artwork is the latest stage of the council's programme to transform the town's subways, with the rest to follow in the new year.

Harlow · Friday 6 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

The underpass linking the Katherines and Sumners neighbourhoods of Harlow has been covered in bright jungle-themed artwork. Called Welcome to the Jungle, it is the newest addition to Harlow Council's programme of underpass makeovers, and its designs draw on ideas residents gave in a public consultation.

The work was carried out with Harlow street art company MurWalls as part of the council's Building Harlow's Future plan for renewing neighbourhoods. Council leader Dan Swords called it another incredible transformation and urged people to try Painting Harlow's Future, a free art trail app made with MurWalls and Japeto.

The council says the remaining underpasses will be transformed in the new year, as the weather has pushed the work back. Councillor Swords said every remaining underpass will still get a makeover.

Key points

  • New artwork links Katherines and Sumners
  • Designs reflect residents' consultation ideas
  • Free art trail app available
  • Remaining underpasses due in the new year
