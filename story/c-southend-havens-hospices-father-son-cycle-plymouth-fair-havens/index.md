# Havens Hospices cyclists ride 500km from Devon to Southend

**A father and son rode from Devon to Fair Havens hospice in Southend. They raised more than £4,000 for the hospice that cared for family.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Westcliff-on-Sea, Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 11:13 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Charity.

## Key points

- James is 45 and Ted is 17
- Ted's first long-distance ride
- Family moved to Devon from Leigh-on-Sea

## The story

James Warne and his son Ted cycled from Noss Mayo, near Plymouth, to Fair Havens adults' hospice in Southend, arriving on Saturday 31 October after six days on the road. They rode in memory of Ted's grandfather, Paul Wellsteed, who was cared for at Fair Havens before he died in June.

The pair covered about 80km a day through rain and mist, and took a day off to rest when both fell ill. The family moved to Devon from Leigh-on-Sea eight years ago.
