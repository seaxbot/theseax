Harlow man, 37, charged with stalking a woman and damaging her letterbox

Police say the alleged campaign of unwanted calls, voicemails and visits ran for more than three weeks over the summer.

Harlow · Wednesday 4 November 2026, 07:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

A 37-year-old Harlow man has been charged with stalking and criminal damage after a woman reported unwanted contact over the summer. Lee Exley, of Edinburgh Gate, Harlow, has not been convicted of any offence and the case will now go through the courts.

The stalking charge alleges that between 13 July and 6 August 2026 he pursued a course of conduct including unwanted phone calls, voicemails and turning up at the woman's address, causing her on at least two occasions to fear violence would be used against her. A separate charge of criminal damage relates to a letterbox allegedly damaged on 6 August.

He was released on conditional bail with a wide exclusion zone in Harlow and is due to appear before Chelmsford Magistrates' Court, Your Harlow reports.

Key points

  • Charged with stalking and criminal damage
  • Alleged conduct ran from 13 July to 6 August
  • Bailed with an exclusion zone in Harlow
  • Case listed at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court
