# Clacton Leisure Centre hosts free NHS health checks on Monday

**Clacton residents can get blood pressure checks and flu jabs at a free NHS event. Researchers will also show studies people can join.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Clacton-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 16:34 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Health.

## Key points

- Runs 12.30pm to 3.30pm on 30 November
- Parkinson's study uses augmented reality headsets
- Children aged two and three eligible for flu vaccine

## The story

NHS Essex is running a free research, health and wellbeing event at Clacton Leisure Centre in Vista Road on Monday 30 November, from 12.30pm to 3.30pm. NHS services, researchers and local community groups will be under one roof.

Visitors can have free blood pressure and cholesterol checks and talk to health professionals about support near them. Eligible people can get a free flu vaccination at a drop-in, including children aged two and three. Local organisations will give advice on healthy living, mental health and community support.

Research is a big part of the afternoon. Professor Andrew Bateman of the University of Essex will show a Parkinson's study that uses augmented reality headsets. People can also sign up to projects including Be Part of Research, Improving Black Health Outcomes and Genes & Health.

Parents and carers can find out about NHS Essex's Super Bodies campaign on protecting children from winter illnesses. NHS Essex says places can be booked through the event page on its website.
