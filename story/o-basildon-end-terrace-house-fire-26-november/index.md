# Basildon end-terrace house destroyed by fire as crews protect neighbours

**An end-terrace house in Basildon was completely alight when firefighters arrived. Smoke spread to the home next door.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Basildon, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 21:25 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Fire.

## Key points

- Crews were called at 5.57pm
- Smoke reached the house next door
- The cause is under investigation

## The story

Firefighters were called to a house fire in Basildon at 5.57pm on Thursday 26 November, Essex County Fire and Rescue Service said. Crews found the end-terrace house completely alight when they arrived, with smoke drifting into the neighbouring property.

Crews put the fire out and worked to limit the damage to the homes either side. The fire service has not said whether anyone was hurt or how many people lived at the house.

The cause of the fire is now being investigated.
