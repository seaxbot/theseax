# Stansted Airport checks stop 3,000 unsafe travel adaptors

**Essex County Council Trading Standards has stopped a shipment of travel adaptors at Stansted Airport. Lab tests found faults that raise the risk of shocks and fires.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stansted Airport, Stansted, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 09:47 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- The adaptors also failed high-voltage safety testing
- They were flagged during routine import safety checks

## The story

Independent laboratory tests found no fuse inside the adaptors, too little insulation between parts and plug pins that did not fit sockets properly, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reported on Friday 27 November. The whole consignment has been permanently detained and will be destroyed or recycled.

A Trading Standards spokesperson said the faults could lead to electric shock, overheating and fire in normal use. Shoppers are advised to buy from reputable UK sellers and to be wary of unusually cheap products.
