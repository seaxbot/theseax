Maldon Mud Race sponsor gives Basildon Hospital two Xbox gaming trolleys for young patients

A £3,060 gift from JR Revell Ltd pays for portable consoles that can be wheeled to children's and teenagers' bedsides.

Basildon · Thursday 5 November 2026, 08:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Hospital now has two portable Xbox gaming trolleys for children and teenagers, paid for by a £3,060 donation from JR Revell Ltd, main sponsor of the Maldon Mud Race. The trolleys can be wheeled to bedsides, so they also reach young patients too unwell to leave their rooms or who must isolate.

The firm chose Basildon because the partner of its operations manager, Ronnie Baker, works on two of the hospital's children's wards. This year's Mud Race raised more than £50,000 for local charities, and JR Revell entered its own team to add to the total.

One trolley will go in a new teen room being refurbished on the ward, and the other will move between the children's wards. Southend and Broomfield hospitals already have similar trolleys, which the trust says have proved popular.

Key points

  • £3,060 donation from JR Revell Ltd
  • Two portable Xbox trolleys for children's wards
  • Mud Race raised more than £50,000 for charities
