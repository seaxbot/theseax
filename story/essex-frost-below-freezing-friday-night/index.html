Another frosty night ahead for Essex, with Chelmsford forecast to fall to -2C

Temperatures will plunge again once the sun goes down after a bright but cold day, with milder, wetter weather moving in on Sunday.

Essex · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 20:48 · Updated Friday 13 November 2026, 08:17 · Reported by Met Office
WhatsApp Facebook X

Friday 13 November should stay dry and sunny, but Chelmsford will reach only 7C, according to the Met Office. Under clear skies it is expected to drop below freezing by late evening, so anyone heading out early on Saturday should allow time to clear windscreens and watch for icy patches on untreated roads and pavements.

Saturday 14 November brings more sunshine and a high of 8C. Sunday is set to be the mildest day of the week at 11C, though rain is likely from the morning, with the chance peaking at 70% in the early afternoon.

Monday turns colder again at 7C with sunny intervals, and light rain is forecast early on Tuesday 17 November. No Met Office weather warnings were in force for Essex when the forecast was checked on Friday morning.

Key points

  • Friday high of just 7C
  • Sunny Saturday, up to 8C
  • No Met Office warnings in force
