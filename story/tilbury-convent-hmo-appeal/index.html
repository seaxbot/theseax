Developer appeals after Thurrock councillors refuse 20-bed HMO in former Tilbury convent beside primary school

The Planning Inspectorate will hold a hearing into plans for the former Convent of Mercy on Malta Road, which the planning committee rejected against officers' advice.

Tilbury · Saturday 7 November 2026, 12:13 · Reported by Thurrock Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

The applicant behind plans to turn the former Convent of Mercy on Malta Road, Tilbury, into a 20-bedroom house in multiple occupation has appealed after Thurrock Council's planning committee refused the scheme. The two-and-a-half storey building sits directly beside St Mary's Roman Catholic Primary and Junior School, and residents had objected that an HMO of that size was wrong for the spot.

Planning officers had recommended approval, but no committee member would move that recommendation and none supported the application. The applicant, Dole Nunn Investments, has now taken the case to appeal. Four Reform UK councillors for Tilbury Riverside and Tilbury St Chads, Shirley Bartlett, Harley Pyman-Brickwood, Bailey Scott and David Hollenbach, say they will keep opposing it, arguing the scale and intensity are wrong for the site while accepting the need for housing. Jo Seymour, chief executive of the Christus Catholic Trust, which runs the school, said the trust does not object to reusing the building in principle but that the plans did not address the impact on the school next door.

The Planning Inspectorate will hold a hearing to reconsider the case; no date has been given. Thurrock Nub News notes that under new government planning rules in force since 1 November, most decisions of this kind now pass to officers rather than councillors.

Key points

  • Plan: 20-bed HMO in former convent on Malta Road
  • Officers backed it; committee refused
  • Applicant Dole Nunn Investments has appealed
  • Planning Inspectorate hearing, date not yet set
