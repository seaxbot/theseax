# Tendring council runs £3.9m under budget at half-year, mainly on timing

**Tendring District Council says the gap mainly reflects when money is spent and received across several budgets.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Tendring, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- General Fund £3.918m under profiled budget at end of September 2026
- Budget changes approved for 2026/27 and 2027/28
- Financial forecast now runs to 2029/30
- Government update on Greater Essex reorganisation expected by early 2027

## The story

Tendring District Council's General Fund was £3.918m under its profiled budget at the end of September 2026, a report to the cabinet on Friday 13 November 2026 showed. The cabinet noted the position and approved a small number of budget changes for 2026/27 and 2027/28. Councillors were told no new major issues had emerged in the first six months of the year.

The report warns that other spending or income trends may still emerge later in the year. It found no other major issues in council housing finances, building projects, tax collection or the management of the council's cash. Officers will review any longer-term effect of the budget changes from 2027/28.

The cabinet also noted an updated financial forecast running to 2029/30. The council is planning for four years in case it still exists beyond April 2028. The report says the government expects to give an update on reorganising councils in Greater Essex by early 2027. The cabinet asked the Resources and Services Overview and Scrutiny Committee for its views.
