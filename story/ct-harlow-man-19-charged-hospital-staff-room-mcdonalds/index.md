# 19-year-old Harlow man charged over hospital & McDonald's trespass

**A Harlow man has been charged with entering a staff room at the town's hospital and an area of a McDonald's restaurant as a trespasser with intent to steal, Your Harlow reports.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Friday 20 November 2026, 11:21 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Both charges relate to 9 November
- He is due back in court on 14 January 2027

## The story

Tommy Maughan, 19, of Hodings Road, Harlow, appeared at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court charged with two offences said to have happened in Harlow on 9 November 2026, Your Harlow reports from court records. He was given conditional bail not to enter Princess Alexandra Hospital, except for treatment, or any McDonald's in Harlow. His next hearing is on 14 January 2027.
