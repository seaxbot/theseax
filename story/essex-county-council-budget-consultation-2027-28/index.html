County Hall finance chief warns of 'existential threat' as Essex budget gap could reach £476m by 2030

Essex County Council's cabinet member for finance, Bo Davis, says the gap between spending and income will keep widening without action, with social care driving most of the pressure, as a public budget consultation runs until 6 December.

Chelmsford · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Updated Tuesday 10 November 2026, 11:44 · Reported by Essex County Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex County Council faces a budget gap of nearly £170 million in 2027/28 if no further action is taken, rising to £280 million the year after, £384 million in 2029/30 and £476 million by 2030/31, according to figures reported by the Local Democracy Reporting Service. Cabinet member for finance Bo Davis, of Reform UK, described it as the biggest threat the council had faced in a long time, and the council is asking residents where money should go in a consultation on its 2027/28 budget.

About 70 per cent of next year's pressure is in social care, with extra spending expected of £45 million on adult social care, £64 million on children's care, £32 million on children's placements and £15 million on home-to-school transport. The council's 2026/27 net cost of services came to about £1,437 million, roughly £56 million more than budgeted. Council leader Peter Harris has argued that the government's funding settlement has not been fair to Essex taxpayers.

Mr Davis said it was too early to discuss council tax, currently £1,641.96 for a Band D home, and that no decisions had been made; reserves of around £600 million would be used only for transformation or bridging where absolutely necessary. He has contacted the Liberal Democrat, Conservative and independent group leaders seeking cross-party work on savings. The consultation closes on 6 December and the cabinet is due to consider budget proposals on 15 January 2027.

Key points

  • Gap of nearly £170m in 2027/28, rising to £476m by 2030/31 without action
  • Social care accounts for about 70% of next year's pressure
  • Band D council tax is £1,641.96; no decision on any change
