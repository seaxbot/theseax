Fastwarm founders join Little Havens hospice's corporate supporters

Thundersley · Updated Thursday 19 November 2026, 07:40 · First published Tuesday 17 November 2026, 13:02 · Reported by Havens Hospices
WhatsApp Facebook X

Neil Cohen, Stephen Lewis and Michael Lewis have backed Little Havens in Thundersley since 2008, first through The Underfloor Heating Store and since 2020 through Fastwarm, raising more than £150,000 for the children's hospice, Havens Hospices said on Tuesday 17 November. They have now joined the charity's Corporate Membership programme at Platinum level, each pledging £300 a month.

Fastwarm has sponsored the Race4Business event in Chelmsford, run Christmas jumper days and given 10p from every online order, a scheme that has raised more than £3,000 in just over a year.

Key points

  • Joint pledge: £10,800 a year
  • Fastwarm's 10p-an-order scheme has raised more than £3,000
