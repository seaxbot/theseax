# Beat the Street players in eastern Thurrock walk 35,201 miles

**Schools and teams from Stanford-le-Hope, Corringham and nearby villages were honoured after the six-week walking game.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Stanford-le-Hope, Corringham, East Tilbury, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 14:43 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- St Clere's School won the secondary prize
- Mayor of Thurrock's charity: Diabetes UK

## The story

Winners of Beat the Street Eastern Thurrock were honoured at Arthur Bugler Primary School, Stanford-le-Hope, on Monday 23 November, Your Thurrock reported. More than 4,979 people played between 23 September and 4 November, tapping Beat Boxes as they walked, cycled and ran.

Giffards Primary School topped the schools table and Corringham Library led the community teams. The game was funded by Thurrock Council, Active Essex, Sport England and NHS Essex.
