Braintree driver banned after roof load smashed into armed police car on A120

A panel flew off his Audi near Stansted Airport, crossed the central barrier and shattered a police car's windscreen.

Little Canfield · Wednesday 28 October 2026, 15:30 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

A Braintree driver whose unsecured roof load smashed into an Essex Police armed response car on the A120 near Stansted Airport has been banned from driving for a year. The panel flew off his Audi, cleared the central barrier and shattered the police car's windscreen, showering both officers with glass.

Glen Lillie, 34, of Park Drive, Braintree, pleaded guilty to three charges and was given a 12-month community order with 140 hours of unpaid work at Colchester Magistrates' Court on 22 October. The collision happened at about 1.30pm on 19 December 2025 near Little Canfield as officers drove to an emergency on blue lights. Lillie did not stop or report it, but police traced him through images of a car carrying a large flat item on a roof rack.

Police said the officer brought the damaged Volvo to a controlled stop, and the dual carriageway was closed while debris was cleared. The car was off the road for several months. PC Simon Bailey said the outcome could have been devastating and urged drivers to secure their loads.

Key points

  • 12-month driving ban and 140 hours unpaid work
  • Crash was near Little Canfield in December 2025
  • Police car out of action for months
