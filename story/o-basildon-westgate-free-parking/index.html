Basildon's Westgate car park to offer 90 minutes free from December as Aldi prepares to open

The council has hired a new operator with number plate cameras at its Westgate Shopping Park, where Aldi's first Basildon town centre store opens on 12 November.

Basildon · Sunday 1 November 2026, 09:00 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Shoppers at Basildon's Westgate Shopping Park are set to get 90 minutes of free parking from December under a new contract agreed by Basildon Borough Council. The council, which owns the shopping park, says the free period is meant to bring more people into the town centre and support its shops and leisure venues.

The council chose Sagoss Ltd to run parking at Westgate after a tender, with the decision taken on 26 October and published four days later. Number plate recognition cameras will manage the car parks and aim to cut unauthorised and long-stay parking. Basildon Today reports it is not yet clear whether the free time covers the multi-storey, the surface car park or both, or how overstays will be charged.

The scheme is expected to start in December once the contract and set-up are complete, so drivers should pay as normal until a start date is announced. Aldi's new store in Units 3 and 4 at Westgate Park opens on Thursday 12 November, and regeneration cabinet member Cllr Alex Myers said the council looked forward to welcoming it.

Key points

  • 90 minutes free alongside existing tariffs
  • Sagoss Ltd appointed with ANPR cameras
  • Start expected in December, date not yet set
  • Aldi opens at Westgate on 12 November
