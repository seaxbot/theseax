Saffron Walden recycling centre stays shut after worker is airlifted following serious injury

The Thaxted Road site has been closed since a medical emergency on 31 October and will not reopen before Wednesday 11 November.

Saffron Walden · Monday 9 November 2026, 10:16 · Updated Monday 9 November 2026, 12:13 · Reported by Bishop's Stortford Independent
WhatsApp Facebook X

Saffron Walden Recycling Centre on Thaxted Road remains closed after a member of staff was seriously injured on Saturday 31 October, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reports. The ambulance service and the Essex and Herts Air Ambulance attended and a man was taken to Addenbrooke's Hospital.

Essex County Council said bookings that cannot be rearranged will be honoured at its other recycling centres, which are expected to be busier.

Key points

  • Closed since 31 October
  • Reopening no earlier than Wednesday 11 November
  • Bookings honoured at other Essex sites
