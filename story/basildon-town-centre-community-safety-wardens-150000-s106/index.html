Basildon sets aside £150,000 for two years of town centre safety wardens

Basildon Council wants a contractor patrolling the town centre by February 2027, paid for with money from developers.

Basildon · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 08:11 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

The wardens would tackle anti-social behaviour, support vulnerable people and work with police and housing providers in Basildon town centre and the new Orwell homes, Basildon Today reports from a council report. Underpass cleaning and better lighting make up the rest of a one-off package of up to £260,000 from Section 106 developer payments.

Basildon Council's Regeneration Scrutiny Committee discusses the plan at 7pm on Tuesday 24 November.

Key points

  • The council's S106 board approved the money on 5 November
  • Lighting work could cost £60,000 to £80,000
  • Keeping the wardens after the funded period would need new money
