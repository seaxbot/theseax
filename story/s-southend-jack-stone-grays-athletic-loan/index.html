Southend United send Jack Stone back to Grays Athletic for a month

Southend-on-Sea, Grays · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 18:00 · Reported by Southend United
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend United have let Jack Stone return to Isthmian League North side Grays Athletic.

The deal, confirmed by Southend United on Tuesday 17 November, runs for one month. Stone already knows the club well after spending three months on loan at Grays last season.
