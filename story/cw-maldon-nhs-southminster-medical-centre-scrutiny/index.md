# NHS agrees to face Maldon councillors over Southminster health hub

**Councillors are worried about slow progress on a planned medical centre in Southminster. The local NHS has agreed to answer their questions.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southminster, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 26 November 2026, 06:15 (UK time). Section: Council Watch. Topic: Council Watch.

## Key points

- NHS Essex Integrated Care Board agrees to attend; date to be fixed
- Anglian Water due before the committee on 4 February 2027
- Update goes to the Overview and Scrutiny Committee on 3 December
- The council uses more than 1,000 standard letters

## The story

NHS Essex Integrated Care Board has agreed to come before councillors in Maldon over a planned medical centre in Southminster. A report to Maldon District Council's Overview and Scrutiny Committee, which meets on Thursday 3 December, says a date is still being worked out. The committee will be asked to agree the further questions and actions the group has identified.

Concern came from the committee's working group, which met on 12 October and 3 November. Members discussed the lack of progress on the proposed Southminster Medical Centre and hub, and the absence of any update from the NHS. They asked for the NHS to be invited to a meeting, and the integrated care board has said yes.

Water is on the list too. Anglian Water has agreed to attend the committee on Thursday 4 February 2027, and the working group wants to invite Essex and Suffolk Water and the Environment Agency too. Councillors plan to settle their questions in advance so that full answers can be given.

Essex County Council's highways team has offered to brief councillors, after members raised concerns about the highway advice they get and how the team sets priorities. Council letters will now be signed by a named person rather than a team, and debt recovery letters are being reviewed. The group has paused its look at the Prentice Hall planning application because of a possible judicial review.
