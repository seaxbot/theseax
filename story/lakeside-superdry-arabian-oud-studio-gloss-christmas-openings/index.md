# Lakeside gets bigger Superdry and new perfume shop before Christmas

**West Thurrock's Lakeside Shopping Centre is adding shops and festive events. Superdry has moved to a larger unit opposite Boots.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: West Thurrock, Lakeside, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 06:55 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Business.

## Key points

- Studio Gloss uses AI scanning to print nail art
- Lakeside's Santa Express train ride returns

## The story

Essex Magazine reported that Superdry's bigger store opens on Friday 27 November, after fragrance shop Arabian Oud opened beside Lefties on Wednesday. A Studio Gloss nail kiosk and a refurbished Jewells store are also arriving.

Author Cressida Cowell visits Waterstones on Sunday at 2.30pm, and a Charlotte Tilbury stand runs outside H Beauty until then. The centre says more festive activities will follow.
