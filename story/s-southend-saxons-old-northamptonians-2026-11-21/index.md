# Southend Saxons beat Old Northamptonians 40-33 for first win

**Fly half Tom Ramsey was named man of the match as the Southend-on-Sea rugby side recovered from conceding a converted try inside the first minute.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 18:10 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Rugby.

## Key points

- Two yellow cards briefly left Southend with 13 men
- Prop Syd Noad went off injured after 35 minutes
- Southend led for the first time at 33-26 on 57 minutes

## The story

Southend Saxons beat Old Northamptonians 40-33 at Warner's Park on Saturday 21 November 2026, their first win of the season. The visitors started the day joint third. It was 19-19 at half-time and 33-33 with minutes left, before a mauled try and a conversion settled it.

Southend stay bottom of the league but now have 8 points. Westcliff are the next visitors, on Saturday 28 November.
