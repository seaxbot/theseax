# Brightlingsea U3A book traces 150 years of the town's shops

**Members of Brightlingsea U3A have published Brightlingsea Shops and Shopping 1880s to 2020s, on sale for £8 at Brightlingsea Museum.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Brightlingsea, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:00 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- The 60-page book is the group's 13th heritage title
- The town once had about 175 shops
- It profiles family firms such as Pannell's and Osborn's
