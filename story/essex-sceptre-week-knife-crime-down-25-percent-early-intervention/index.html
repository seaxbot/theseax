Essex knife crime down more than a quarter since 2019, violence unit says

Essex's Violence and Vulnerability Unit is pointing to youth projects in Harlow, Chelmsford, Thurrock and south Essex schools as national knife crime week runs.

Essex · Tuesday 17 November 2026, 08:30 · Updated Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:40 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Hospital admissions of young people with knife injuries in Essex have more than halved since 2019/20, the Essex Violence and Vulnerability Unit said on Tuesday 17 November, Your Harlow reports.

Its projects include sport-based workshops in Chelmsford, Castle Point, Rochford and Southend schools, a carpentry course run by Rainbow Services in Harlow and mentoring at North Avenue Youth Centre in Chelmsford. Police, Fire and Crime Commissioner Roger Hirst said there was more to do.

Key points

  • Thurrock's EmpowerHer project works with vulnerable girls
  • ReRoute supports young people who have already offended
