Southend opens final consultation on Local Plan it was ordered to publish by ministers

Residents have until 21 December to comment on the draft blueprint for homes, jobs and infrastructure, which the city council published only after the housing minister intervened.

Southend-on-Sea · Friday 6 November 2026, 11:39 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council has opened the last public consultation on its draft Local Plan, the document that will decide where new homes, workplaces, transport links and community facilities can go across the city. The plan has been published because the Government formally directed the council to do so, after it signalled it would strip out housing sites and delay this stage.

Housing and Planning Minister Matthew Pennycook wrote to council leader Daniel Cowan on 23 October ordering publication by 6 November, submission for examination by 31 March 2027 and monthly progress reports, Local Government Lawyer reported. The minister pointed to a local plan that is 19 years old and a Housing Delivery Test score that fell to 20% in 2025. Cabinet agreed unanimously on 5 November to publish the draft, and planning portfolio holder Kevin Robinson said: "We have been told quite clearly to publish this plan by the Government".

The Regulation 19 consultation runs from 6 November to 21 December, with documents on the council's Local Plan website and in libraries and the Civic Centre. Comments should address whether the plan is legally compliant and sound. All responses then go to a Planning Inspector for independent examination, and full council will look at the final plan in early 2027.

Key points

  • Consultation runs 6 November to 21 December 2026
  • Published under a direction from the housing minister
  • Plan must be submitted for examination by 31 March 2027
  • Council says publishing does not mean the plan is adopted
