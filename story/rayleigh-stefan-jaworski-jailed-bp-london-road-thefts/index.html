Rayleigh shoplifter jailed for five months after BP garage thefts

Rayleigh, London Road · Thursday 26 November 2026, 14:35 · Reported by Essex Police
WhatsApp Facebook X

Stefan Jaworski, 44, stole £260 of meat, fish, chocolate and alcohol. A court order bans him from every BP in England and Wales.

A Rayleigh man has been jailed for five months for stealing from a petrol station he was banned from, Essex Police said.

Stefan Jaworski, 44, of Francis Walk, appeared at Basildon Crown Court on Wednesday 25 November. He admitted two thefts from the BP service station in London Road, Rayleigh, on 19 September. The goods were worth £260 in total.

He also admitted two counts of breaching his criminal behaviour order, which bans him from entering any BP service station in England and Wales. He was arrested by officers in Southend.

The order runs until 3 February 2031. It also bars him from every M&S store in England and Wales, the Sainsbury's Local in West Street, Rochford, and the Co-op in Spa Road, Hockley.

Key points

  • Sentenced at Basildon Crown Court
  • Thefts on 19 September
  • Order runs until February 2031
