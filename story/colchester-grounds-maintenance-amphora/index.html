Colchester's parks and green spaces brought back under council-owned Amphora

About 40 grounds staff have moved from contractor Idverde to the council's own company, which now looks after almost 13,000 assets across 870 hectares.

Colchester · Friday 6 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Colchester City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester City Council's grounds maintenance service transferred to Amphora, the company the council wholly owns, on 1 November. The service looks after parks, sports grounds, open spaces, housing land, cemeteries and the crematorium across the city.

Around 40 former Idverde employees moved across, and council and Amphora staff marked the change with volunteering days at Castle Park on 2 and 3 November, clearing leaves and planting hundreds of spring bulbs. The service covers almost 13,000 assets over more than 870 hectares. Cllr Martin Goss, portfolio holder for waste, neighbourhoods and leisure, welcomed the new colleagues into the council family.

The council says the arrangement gives it more flexibility to respond to local priorities and to find new ways of improving parks and green spaces, while keeping investment in the local area.

Key points

  • Transfer took effect on 1 November
  • About 40 staff moved from Idverde
  • Almost 13,000 assets over 870 hectares
  • Amphora is wholly council-owned
