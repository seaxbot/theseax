New national planning rules end call-ins for smaller cases in Rochford and Castle Point

From 31 October, householder and minor applications must be decided by officers, and larger schemes go to committee only if strict national criteria are met.

Rochford & Benfleet · Friday 30 October 2026, 10:00 · Reported by Rochford District Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • About 96% of Rochford applications already avoid committee
  • Rochford councillors and parishes can ask for a gateway assessment within 21 days
  • Castle Point says the rules were imposed nationally and fears less local democratic say
  • Residents' comments are still considered in both districts
