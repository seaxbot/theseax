Plans emerge for 1GW solar and battery energy park on the Dengie Peninsula

Developer Low Carbon has started the national planning process for a scheme it says could power 250,000 homes, and Maldon District Council has until 24 November to say what studies it needs.

Bradwell-on-Sea, Dengie & Burnham-on-Crouch · Friday 6 November 2026, 14:16 · Reported by Maldon Nub News
WhatsApp Facebook X

A renewable energy company has begun the national planning process for a solar and energy storage park of up to 1 gigawatt on the Dengie Peninsula. The Dengie Peninsula Energy Park would sit on land near Bradwell-on-Sea, Dengie and Burnham-on-Crouch, and its developer Low Carbon says it could power around 250,000 homes.

Because of its size the scheme is a Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project, so it would be examined by the Planning Inspectorate and decided by the Secretary of State rather than Maldon District Council.

Key points

  • Up to 1GW of solar and battery storage
  • Developer estimates power for 250,000 homes
  • Council comments due to the Planning Inspectorate by 24 November
