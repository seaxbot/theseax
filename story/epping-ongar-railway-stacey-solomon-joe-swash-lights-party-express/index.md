# Stacey Solomon and Joe Swash film Christmas show on Epping Ongar Railway

**The couple rode the heritage line's festive light train for their BBC One series. The railway says their visit added extra sparkle.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: North Weald, Ongar, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 17:31 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: What's On.

## Key points

- Runs on selected weekend evenings until 2 January
- Santa trains start on Saturday 28 November

## The story

Stacey Solomon and Joe Swash travelled on the Epping Ongar Railway's Lights Party Express while recording Stacey & Joe's Crafty Christmas for BBC One, RailAdvent reported on Friday 27 November.

The light and music train runs on selected Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings until 2 January. Rob Bentley, the railway's operations manager, called the visit "an absolute pleasure". Santa Special trains also return from 28 November to 24 December.
