Essex's Tracy Lawson and Reinhard Pekrun on world's most-cited list

Wivenhoe, Colchester · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 20:45 · First published Saturday 21 November 2026, 17:00 · First reported by Clarivate · Additional reporting by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

A plant scientist working on crops that cope with drought and a psychologist who studies how feelings shape learning have been recognised for the global reach of their work.

Two professors at the University of Essex's Colchester campus at Wivenhoe Park are on Clarivate's Highly Cited Researchers 2026 list: the plant biologist Tracy Lawson and the psychologist Reinhard Pekrun. The list picks out scientists and social scientists whose papers rank in the top 1% by citations in their field over the past decade.

Clarivate named 6,802 researchers from more than 1,200 universities and institutes in 62 countries and regions this year. It says roughly one in every 1,000 of the world's scientists and social scientists makes the cut.

Crops for a changing climate

Professor Lawson, from the School of Life Sciences, has been on the list for seven years in a row, the Daily Gazette reports. Her research looks at photosynthesis and at how the tiny pores on leaves open and close. She also studies how efficiently plants use water, with the aim of breeding crops that hold up better in drought and heat.

In August she won the International Society of Photosynthesis Research's Innovation Award for technology that has changed how scientists measure those processes. In 2024 she oversaw the opening of the £3m STEPS lab at Essex, where crops are grown in conditions set up to mimic a future climate.

Why students feel what they feel

Professor Pekrun, from the Department of Psychology, studies how emotions such as enjoyment, anxiety and boredom affect students' motivation and results. He devised the control-value theory of achievement emotions and the Achievement Emotions Questionnaire, which researchers use to measure feelings such as test anxiety.

He first joined Professor Lawson on the list in 2024, when the university said he had published more than 350 books, articles and chapters. He told the Gazette this year's recognition was down to teamwork and the support of colleagues, students and staff at Essex.

Key points

  • Clarivate's 2026 list was published on 18 November
  • Professor Lawson has now been named seven years running
  • 520 of this year's 6,802 names are based in the UK
