Broomfield, Southend and Basildon hospital patients told to keep appointments as Microsoft email recovery continues

Microsoft says its email service is approaching normal levels after the global outage, but some messages are still arriving late.

Chelmsford, Southend-on-Sea, Basildon · Friday 13 November 2026, 13:18 · Updated Friday 13 November 2026, 17:39 · Reported by Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust
WhatsApp Facebook X

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust, which runs Broomfield Hospital in Chelmsford, Southend University Hospital and Basildon University Hospital, was still telling patients on Friday evening that it cannot send or receive emails. Its hospital systems are working normally, patients should carry on attending unless they are contacted, and its phone lines are open as usual.

Microsoft says traffic is gradually being restored and that mailbox access and the processing of delayed email are improving, although it warns that some users may still see intermittent problems or late messages. To stop mail stuck in the backlog from being lost, it has extended the time it holds undelivered email to 48 hours.

Hospitals, GP surgeries and police forces across the UK were caught up in the outage, PA reports, and NHS Essex has apologised for slow email replies. The National Pharmacy Association warned that the disruption affects pharmacies' ability to contact GP practices and some patients, which could hold up prescriptions.

Key points

  • Microsoft has extended how long it holds delayed email to 48 hours
  • Outlook and Teams are among the Microsoft services hit
