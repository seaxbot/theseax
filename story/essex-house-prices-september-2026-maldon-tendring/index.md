# Maldon house prices up 5% in a year as Tendring's fall 2.2%

**Official September figures show a county split in two. Prices rose in nine Essex areas and fell in five.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Published: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 16:50 (UK time). Updated: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 17:27. Section: News. Topic: Property.

## Key points

- Epping Forest averages £536,719, the county's highest
- Detached homes in Epping Forest average over £1m
- Flats cost less than England's average in 12 of 14 areas
- Essex County Council area up 0.5%, England 0.8%

## The story

The UK House Price Index is the official monthly measure of what homes sell for. It is published by HM Land Registry with the Office for National Statistics, and its figures for September came out in November. The Seax has gone through them for all 14 Essex council areas.

Maldon district came out on top. The average home there cost £406,165, up 5% on a year earlier. That is more than £19,000 extra, and a rise more than six times England's 0.8%.

Tendring went the other way. Prices there fell 2.2% to £258,932, more than £5,500 less than a year before. It is now the only part of Essex where the average home costs less than the England average of £292,998.

## The risers

Harlow had the second biggest rise, at 3.9%, taking its average to £317,113. Epping Forest rose 2.1%, Southend-on-Sea 1.8% and Rochford 1.3%. Colchester, Basildon, Braintree and Brentwood also went up, by between 0.2% and 0.9%. Of those four, only Colchester beat England's rise.

## The fallers

Prices fell in five areas. As well as Tendring, they slipped 0.8% in Chelmsford, 0.7% in Castle Point and Thurrock, and 0.5% in Uttlesford. Chelmsford's average is now £378,412, and Uttlesford's £465,802.

## Where homes cost most

Epping Forest is still the most expensive part of the county, at £536,719, with Brentwood close behind at £520,447. Uttlesford is third. Maldon is now fifth, £2,718 behind Rochford.

The gap between a flat and a detached house is widest in Brentwood and Epping Forest, at more than £700,000. The average detached house in Epping Forest costs £1,022,897, the only Essex area above £1m. Brentwood's is just under, at £996,190. In Tendring the figure is £366,896.

## Flats and first-time buyers

Flats are a different picture. In 12 of the 14 areas, the average flat or maisonette costs less than the England average of £215,760. Only Epping Forest, at £296,026, and Brentwood, at £264,292, are higher. The cheapest are in Tendring, at £130,581.

First-time buyers pay most in Epping Forest, an average of £415,027, and least in Tendring, at £220,732. The England figure for a first-time buyer is £245,052. Colchester, at £249,711, is the only Essex area within £5,000 of it.

## A jumpy month

Single months swing more. Castle Point rose 2.5% between August and September and Epping Forest 2%, while Braintree fell 1.8%. The change over a whole year is the better guide.

## Essex against England

Across the Essex County Council area, which leaves out Southend and Thurrock, the average home cost £360,791 in September. That is up 0.5%, a slower rise than England's, but the average home there still costs £67,793 more than across England.

Earlier this month Basildon Today reported that Basildon's September average of £367,920 was a record for the borough, [as The Seax reported](https://theseax.com/story/basildon-house-prices-record-367920-september-2026/). Our figures match that number.
