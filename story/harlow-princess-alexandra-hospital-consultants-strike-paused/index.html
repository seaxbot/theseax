Senior doctors' walkout that threatened Harlow hospital appointments is paused for pay talks

Consultants at Princess Alexandra Hospital in Harlow and across England will no longer strike later this month after the British Medical Association paused action to negotiate with the government.

Harlow · Thursday 12 November 2026, 00:00 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Patients at Princess Alexandra Hospital had been warned to expect disruption to appointments and planned operations from 6.59am on Wednesday 25 November, when consultants were due to walk out for two days and provide emergency cover only. The hospital had asked people to keep their appointments unless contacted, Your Harlow reported.

The BMA's consultants committee, led by co-chairs Dr Helen Neary and Dr Shanu Datta, called off the action on 11 November to open talks with the government, NewsWire Explorer reports. Consultants received a 3.5% rise this year; the union wants the standard week cut from 40 to 35 hours, better pay for out-of-hours work and changes to pensions.

Health Secretary Yvette Cooper called it welcome news for patients and the wider NHS. Formal negotiations could begin in the week of 12 November, though ministers say a bigger pay rise this year is not on offer. In the strike ballot, 76% of consultants who voted backed action, on a turnout of just over half.

Key points

  • The action would have run from 6.59am on Wednesday 25 November to 6.59am on Friday 27 November
  • Consultants want the standard week cut from 40 to 35 hours
  • Health Secretary Yvette Cooper called the pause welcome news for patients
