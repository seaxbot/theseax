Basildon Council offers tenants £1,500 and free removals to move to a smaller home

Basildon council tenants with spare bedrooms can claim a cash payment if they move to a smaller property, with the council covering removals, decorating and carpets.

Basildon · Saturday 14 November 2026, 14:06 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Council tenants moving from a two or three-bedroom home to a one-bedroom property, or from four bedrooms or more to one or two, can qualify for the downsizing payment, Your Thurrock reports. Tenants who move into sheltered housing get an extra £300.

Tenants choose where they move, and applying does not commit them to going. Councillor Phil Turner, cabinet member for housing, said tenants should feel supported, not pressured.

Key points

  • Details on the basildonchoice.org.uk website
  • A council officer guides each tenant through the move
