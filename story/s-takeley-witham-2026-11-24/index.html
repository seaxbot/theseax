Witham Town win 2-0 at Takeley to climb to sixth in Isthmian North

Witham · Wednesday 25 November 2026, 18:15 · Reported by Non League Matters
WhatsApp Facebook X

Witham Town are now in the Isthmian North play-off places. Brightlingsea Regent had a tough night at the new leaders.

Witham Town won 2-0 away at Takeley in the Isthmian League North Division on Tuesday 24 November. It was their second away win in four days, after a 3-1 victory at AFC Sudbury on the Saturday. Witham now sit sixth with 29 points from 17 games. Takeley slip to 18th, one place above the relegation zone.

Brightlingsea Regent lost 4-1 at Cambridge City on the same evening. The Cambridge Independent reported that Fletcher Toll opened the scoring after 31 minutes. Charlie Royle added two more and Freddie Hockey got the other. City manager Mickey Spillane said Regent scored from their only shot, a breakaway. The win took City top, while Regent stay 21st on 15 points.

There were Essex winners in the Velocity Cup too. Brentwood Town beat Bowers & Pitsea 4-2 at home, and Maldon & Tiptree won 4-3 at Buckhurst Hill. In the Isthmian Premier Division, Aveley lost 3-1 at Eastbourne Borough. Witham are at home to Gorleston on Saturday, and Brightlingsea go to Mulbarton Wanderers.

Key points

  • Crowds: 101 at Takeley, 381 at Cambridge City
  • Toll has 13 league goals, the most in the division
  • Takeley host Bowers & Pitsea in an Essex derby on Saturday 28 November
