Air ambulance charity draws more than 150 women to North Weald event on careers in emergency flight medicine

Essex & Herts Air Ambulance says women already make up about a third of its doctors and paramedics, well above the UK picture, and wants to go further.

North Weald · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 10:00 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Nationally 20% of HEMS doctors and 24% of HEMS paramedics are women
  • EHAAT figures: 35% of its doctors, 36% of its paramedics
