Southend seafront to get new public toilets in £750,000 plan

Southend-on-Sea · Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:14 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Southend-on-Sea City Council's cabinet has backed the spending, which comes from outside the council, after a review of the city's public toilets.

A large new toilet block is planned for Marine Parade in Southend, with a second facility serving the eastern seafront, under plans the cabinet approved on Monday 16 November. Southend-on-Sea City Council announced the decision on Wednesday 18 November.

The plans follow a city-wide review of public toilets and feedback from nearly 500 residents and visitors, 94% of whom said new seafront toilets were important or very important. Money will also go on improving existing toilets, their upkeep and accessibility, and the council says several other seafront sites are being considered. Its announcement does not name the funder.

Councillor Cheryl Nevin, cabinet member for civic pride, said clean and accessible toilets make 'a real difference to people's day-to-day lives'. Detailed design and delivery plans come next, and no start date has been given.

Key points

  • Nearly 500 people gave feedback in the toilet review
  • 94% rated new seafront toilets important or very important
  • No start date yet: detailed design comes next
