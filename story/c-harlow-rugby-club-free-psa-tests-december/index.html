Harlow Rugby Club to host free prostate cancer blood tests for 96 men in December

The club is teaming up with men's health charity CHAPS and the Matchroom Charitable Foundation for a screening day at Latton Park.

Harlow · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 07:49 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Harlow Rugby Club will host a free prostate cancer screening session on Saturday 12 December, offering 96 PSA blood tests, Your Harlow reports. It runs from 10am to 2pm at the club's Latton Park ground, organised with CHAPS Men's Health Charity and the Matchroom Charitable Foundation. Places must be booked in advance through the charity, as walk-ins will not be taken.

Key points

  • Session runs 10am to 2pm
  • No walk-ins: booking is essential
