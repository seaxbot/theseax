Wivenhoe gas works: Cadent holds drop-in ahead of January start

Wivenhoe · Monday 23 November 2026, 05:03 · Reported by Essex-TV
WhatsApp Facebook X

The drop-in runs from 11am to 1pm on Thursday 26 November on the green at Grasby Close and Broome Grove, and no booking is needed, Essex-TV reports. Work starts on Monday 25 January 2027, with permits for 25 weeks and closures planned on roads including Spring Lane, Tower Road, Heath Road and the High Street. Gas stays on for most homes throughout.

Key points

  • Permits in place for 25 weeks
  • Temporary lights planned on The Avenue and Vanessa Drive
