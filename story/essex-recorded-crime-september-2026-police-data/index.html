Essex anti-social behaviour reports up 76% as robbery falls 19%

A Seax analysis of police.uk data shows anti-social behaviour reports rose in 50 of Essex Police's 56 neighbourhood areas, with Basildon recording the most.

Essex · Friday 20 November 2026, 15:15 · Updated Friday 20 November 2026, 16:12 · Reported by data.police.uk (Essex Police)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Police in Essex logged 2,042 reports of anti-social behaviour in September 2026, up from 1,162 in September 2025, according to the street-level records published on police.uk. Recorded crime rose more slowly, to 12,530 from 11,563. Both figures were lower than in August.

Some of the rise in anti-social behaviour may come from changes in how police record it, rather than from more incidents. The figures do not show how much.

Violence and sexual offences remain by far the largest category of crime. Police recorded 5,800 of them in September, up 15% from 5,045 a year earlier, and they made up almost half of all recorded crime in the county.

Thefts rose too. Shoplifting went up 22% to 1,265 offences, theft from the person rose by half to 126, and other theft climbed 20% to 1,008.

Most other types of crime fell. Robbery dropped 19%, from 132 to 107, and bicycle theft fell 12% to 94. Criminal damage and arson went down 8% to 981, vehicle crime 6% to 851 and burglary 3% to 376. Drug offences, public order offences and possession of weapons were also slightly lower.

Anti-social behaviour reports rose in 50 of Essex Police's 56 neighbourhood areas. Basildon had the most, with 151 reports against 76 a year earlier, followed by Colchester Town West with 112 and Southend Central with 110.

Among the 10 areas with the most reports, three more than doubled their totals: Chelmsford Town, from 38 to 86; Chafford Hundred and Grays, from 51 to 107; and Colchester Town East, from 36 to 75. Only one of the 10, Aveley and Ockendon, saw a fall, from 57 to 54.

Taking crime and anti-social behaviour together, records rose in 45 neighbourhoods and fell in 11. Clacton had the biggest rise, with 151 more records than a year earlier, followed by Westcliff (129 more), Basildon (118 more) and Chelmsford South (115 more). In Westcliff, shoplifting nearly trebled.

The biggest falls were in Wakering, with 21 fewer records, Thundersley, with 19 fewer, and Canvey, with 18 fewer.

Police.uk's anti-social behaviour category covers personal, environmental and nuisance anti-social behaviour. It is recorded separately from crime and is not part of the official recorded crime statistics.

Anti-social behaviour and crimes that are not emergencies can be reported to Essex Police online at essex.police.uk or by calling 101. Call 999 if a crime is happening now or someone is in danger.

Police.uk places each record at an approximate location rather than an exact address. The Seax counted every record inside the boundaries of the 56 neighbourhood areas. A few records sit exactly on the line between two neighbourhoods and appear in both neighbourhoods' figures, but each is counted once in the county totals. A further 216 crimes in September had no location and are not included. Totals may differ slightly from Essex Police's own figures.

The figures cover the whole Essex Police area, including Southend and Thurrock, and can be checked street by street on police.uk.

Key points

  • Recorded crime up 8% to 12,530
  • Violence and sexual offences: 5,800 crimes, up 15%
  • Shoplifting up 22% to 1,265
