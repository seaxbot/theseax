Essex crime up 8% on last September as anti-social behaviour jumps 76%

Police in Essex logged 12,530 crimes in September 2026, against 11,563 a year earlier, a Seax analysis of police.uk street-level data shows.

Essex · Friday 20 November 2026, 15:06 · Reported by data.police.uk (Essex Police)
WhatsApp Facebook X

Reports of anti-social behaviour rose faster still, to 2,042 from 1,162. Taken together, crimes and anti-social behaviour incidents came to 14,572, up 15% on September 2025. Both measures were lower than in August, when there were 15,853 records in all, 2,776 of them anti-social behaviour.

Violence and sexual offences, the largest category, accounted for 5,800 crimes, up 15% on a year earlier. Shoplifting rose 22% to 1,265 and other theft 20% to 1,008, while thefts from the person climbed from 84 to 126. Several crimes fell: criminal damage and arson dropped 8% to 981, vehicle crime 6% to 851 and burglary 3% to 376, and robberies fell from 132 to 107.

Crime and anti-social behaviour rose in 45 of Essex Police's 56 neighbourhood areas and fell in 11. The Basildon neighbourhood recorded the most, 878, ahead of Colchester Town West (800), Southend Central (794) and Clacton (720), which had the largest rise in numbers, 151 more than a year earlier.

Among neighbourhoods with at least 100 records a year earlier, the sharpest rises were in Wickford, up 58% to 228, Chelmsford South, up 52% to 338, and Westcliff, up 48% to 397. Shoplifting in Westcliff nearly trebled, from 38 to 102, and violence and sexual offences in Chelmsford South went from 94 to 145. The biggest falls were in Wakering, Thundersley and Canvey.

The jump in anti-social behaviour is large enough that it may reflect changes in how incidents are logged as well as more incidents, and the figures alone do not show which. Police.uk places each record at an approximate location rather than an exact address. The Seax counted every record inside the boundaries of the 56 neighbourhood areas, each one once. A further 216 crimes in September, and 205 a year earlier, had no location and are not included. Totals may differ slightly from Essex Police's own figures and from the official recorded crime statistics, which leave out anti-social behaviour.

The figures cover the whole Essex Police area, including Southend and Thurrock, and can be checked street by street on police.uk.

Key points

  • Violence and sexual offences: 5,800, up 15%
  • Shoplifting: 1,265, up 22%
  • Burglary, robbery and vehicle crime all down on a year earlier
