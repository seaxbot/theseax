Police hunt two cars after motorcyclist knocked off in suspected Chelmsford hit-and-run

Officers want footage of two cars said to have been driven erratically before they hit a motorbike at the Melbourne Avenue and Chignal Road junction and drove off.

Chelmsford · Thursday 5 November 2026, 15:25 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police are investigating a suspected hit-and-run in Chelmsford after a motorcyclist was knocked off their bike at the junction of Melbourne Avenue and Chignal Road. Two cars involved in the collision left the scene, and officers are appealing for anyone who saw what happened or has footage to come forward.

The collision happened at around 5.30pm on Monday 2 November and involved two cars and a motorbike. Witnesses reported that the cars were being driven erratically before they collided with the motorbike, and the rider was not seriously hurt. Police say no descriptions of the cars are available.

Essex Police are asking anyone with CCTV, dash cam or other footage to get in touch, quoting incident 965 of 2 November. No arrests have been reported.

Key points

  • Motorbike rider knocked off at Melbourne Avenue and Chignal Road
  • Two cars left the scene after the crash on Monday 2 November
  • Rider not seriously injured
  • Quote incident 965 of 2 November
