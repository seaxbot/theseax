# Colchester's Provide Community asked to pilot UK nursing public health framework

**The Colchester-based community health provider has been invited to help test new guidance on how nurses, midwives and nursing associates prevent illness and tackle health inequalities.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Thursday 19 November 2026, 15:53 (UK time). Section: Community. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Framework published on 16 November
- Florence Nightingale Foundation backs the pilot
- Work could start in January 2027

## The story

The Nursing and Midwifery Public Health Practice Framework, published on Monday 16 November 2026 and endorsed by the chief nursing officers of all four UK nations, sets out a shared approach to five areas: promoting wellbeing, protecting people from harm, preventing ill health, reducing inequalities through partnership and environmentally sustainable care. Provide Community announced its part in the pilot on Thursday 19 November.

Professor Lucy Wightman, chief executive of Provide Health and group chief nurse, said a routine appointment can be the first time someone admits they are struggling to manage a condition at home.
