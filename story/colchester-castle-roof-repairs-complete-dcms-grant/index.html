Colchester Castle roof repairs finished after £1.3m project

Colchester · Friday 20 November 2026, 11:32 · Reported by BBC News
WhatsApp Facebook X

Repairs to the roof of Colchester Castle, the largest Norman keep in Europe, have been completed, BBC News reported on Friday 20 November. The Grade I listed building had been letting in water as it aged.

Surveys found the north-west tower was the most badly damaged part. Work began early in 2026 under the eye of Historic England, paid for mainly by a grant of £1,293,625 from the Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport.

Key points

  • Work overseen by Historic England
  • North-west tower was worst affected
  • Councillor Darius Laws leads on heritage for Colchester City Council
