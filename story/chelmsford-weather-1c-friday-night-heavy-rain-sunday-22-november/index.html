Frosty start to Monday for inland Essex, then light rain from Tuesday

Essex · Updated Sunday 22 November 2026, 16:39 · First published Thursday 19 November 2026, 00:45 · Reported by Met Office
WhatsApp Facebook X

Chelmsford is forecast to drop to about 0C on the night of Sunday 22 November, after a day of sunny intervals and a high of 9C, the Met Office says. Its East of England forecast earlier warned of frost in inland areas.

Monday 23 November should stay sunny, with a high of 8C and gusts of no more than 17mph. Cloud and light rain move in on Tuesday 24 November, when the high is 7C and gusts reach 29mph.

More light rain is forecast on Wednesday 25 November, then mist on Thursday 26 November with a high of 6C. The weekend looks cloudy and a little milder, with a high of 9C in Chelmsford on Saturday 28 November.

Key points

  • Monday 23 November: sunny, high of 8C
  • Tuesday 24 November: light rain, gusts up to 29mph
  • Saturday 28 November: cloudy, high of 9C
