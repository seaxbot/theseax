Havens Hospices asks Essex to back its Big Yes! Christmas appeal

The charity that runs Fair Havens and Little Havens Children's Hospice says £31 could pay for an hour of specialist nursing care over Christmas.

Essex · Monday 16 November 2026, 13:32 · Reported by Havens Hospices
WhatsApp Facebook X

Key points

  • Schools, businesses and clubs are asked to hold festive fundraisers such as jumper days and bake sales
  • Gifts can be made at havenshospices.org.uk/christmas or on 01702 220 305
