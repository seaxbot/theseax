# Broomfield children's ward Pokémon appeal seeks Christmas gifts

**Harry Fusedale wants every child spending Christmas in hospital in Chelmsford to have something to unwrap, and he is asking the public to help.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Broomfield, Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Sunday 22 November 2026, 21:05 (UK time). Updated: Sunday 22 November 2026, 21:16. Section: News. Topic: Community.

## Key points

- Pokémon cards, packs, tins, figures and toys wanted
- Cash donations buy new, sealed gifts
- Sharing the appeal helps too

## The story

A fundraiser is gathering Pokémon cards, booster packs, tins, figures and toys for children who will spend Christmas on Phoenix Ward, the children's ward at Broomfield Hospital. Organiser Harry Fusedale is also raising money online to buy brand-new, sealed Pokémon products and unused toys for the ward's young patients. He says he has been in contact with the ward so that everything donated reaches the children.

Harry says Pokémon products can be hard to come by. Opening a pack or finding a favourite character, he says, can mean the world to a child stuck in a hospital bed over the festive period. His aim is to bring "a little Christmas magic" to children and families who will be on the ward rather than at home.

Phoenix Ward, in the East Wing of Broomfield Hospital, gives specialised care to children and is run by Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust. Harry is asking anyone with spare Pokémon items to get in touch, and anyone who cannot give to share the appeal. Donations can be made through Harry's GoFundMe site and there's a clickable link at the bottom of this page.
