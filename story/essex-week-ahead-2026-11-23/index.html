Week ahead in Essex: Army and Navy inquiry opens on Tuesday

Essex · Monday 23 November 2026, 06:24 · Reported by The Seax
WhatsApp Facebook X

Across Essex, the coming days bring a public inquiry into Chelmsford's biggest road scheme, council votes on savings and rents, and a run of Christmas lights switch-ons.

The biggest moment comes at 10am on Tuesday 24 November, when a public inquiry opens at County Hall, Chelmsford, into the compulsory purchase and side roads orders for the £81m Army and Navy roundabout scheme. On the same day London Stansted holds a full-scale emergency exercise with about 400 people from the airport and the county's emergency services. More emergency vehicles than usual will be seen near the airport.

Council business fills the rest of the week. Colchester's Local Plan Committee decides on Monday 23 November whether to recommend sending the draft plan to the Planning Inspectorate after 1,800 responses. Thurrock Council's Corporate Scrutiny Committee discusses £27.3m of proposed savings on Tuesday 24 November. That evening Basildon Council's Regeneration Scrutiny Committee looks at £150,000 for town centre safety wardens, underpass cleaning and lighting. Maldon District Council opens a four-week consultation on the scope of its new Local Plan on Wednesday 25 November. On Thursday 26 November Basildon's cabinet decides on a rent policy adding up to £1 a week for most council tenants from April 2027.

Christmas arrives in earnest on Thursday 26 November. Billericay's Late Night Light Up brings the town's first official lights switch-on since before the pandemic at 5.30pm, and Grays switches on its tree lights from 4.30pm. Light Up Leigh-on-Sea follows from 4pm on Friday 27 November, the day Cinderella opens at Chelmsford Theatre. B&M opens its first Colchester city centre store at St John's Walk on Saturday 28 November, and Mind in West Essex holds its Merry Minds Festive Fun Run at Harlow's Water Gardens on Sunday 29 November. Full bids for UK Town of Culture 2028 are due on Monday 30 November, with Basildon one of 15 towns shortlisted.

Key points

  • Basildon cabinet votes on council rents on Thursday 26 November
  • Maldon Local Plan scoping consultation runs to 22 December
  • Town of Culture 2028 bids due on Monday 30 November
