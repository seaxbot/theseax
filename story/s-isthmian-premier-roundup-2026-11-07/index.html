Isthmian Premier: Maldon & Tiptree win 3-1 at Wingate & Finchley as Aveley are beaten 4-0 at Ramsgate

A mixed Saturday for the Essex clubs in the Isthmian Premier Division, with one away win, two draws and a heavy defeat on the Kent coast.

Aveley · Sunday 8 November 2026, 15:48 · Reported by Your Thurrock
WhatsApp Facebook X

Maldon & Tiptree produced the best Essex result of the day in the Isthmian Premier Division, winning 3-1 away at Wingate & Finchley on Saturday. Brentwood Town came away from Three Bridges with a 1-1 draw, while Stanway Rovers and Whitehawk finished goalless.

Aveley had a much harder afternoon, losing 4-0 at promotion-chasing Ramsgate in front of a crowd of 1,444. Billy Munday headed the hosts in front inside two minutes from an Alfie Paxman corner, and Rowan Liburd made it two on 15 minutes after Aveley goalkeeper Jamie Grossart dropped the ball at his feet. Paxman added a third on 77 minutes and Latrell Lewis struck in the 90th, while Ramsgate keeper Jonny Henly also saved a penalty from Malachi Napa.

The defeat leaves Aveley fifteenth in the table. Their next game is away at Hutton in the Essex Senior Cup on Wednesday 11 November, and they return to league action at Carshalton on Saturday 21 November.

Key points

  • Ramsgate keeper Jonny Henly saved a Malachi Napa penalty
  • Brentwood Town drew 1-1 at Three Bridges and Stanway Rovers drew 0-0 with Whitehawk
  • Ramsgate beat Aveley 4-0 in front of 1,444
  • Aveley next visit Hutton in the Essex Senior Cup on 11 November
