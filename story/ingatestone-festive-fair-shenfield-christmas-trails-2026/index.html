Ingatestone Festive Fair brings 30 stalls to the High Street on Sunday

Ingatestone, Shenfield, Brentwood · Friday 27 November 2026, 13:57 · Reported by Brentwood Borough Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Brentwood's Christmas lights are now on. The borough's free festive events move on to Ingatestone and Shenfield.

Ingatestone Festive Fair runs from 12pm to 5pm on Sunday 29 November. Brentwood Borough Council says 30 traditional market stalls will sell food, gifts and handmade goods, with live music and walkabout acts along the High Street. Entry is free.

It is the next date in the Christmas programme run by Brentwood Connected, the town centre Business Improvement District (BID), with the council. Christmas Toy Trails open in Ingatestone and Shenfield on the same day, with prizes to be won across all the trails. Festive Shenfield follows on Sunday 6 December, also from 12pm to 5pm, with funfair rides, market stalls and a Santa's grotto in Hutton Road.

How Brentwood lit up

The season began on Saturday 21 November with Lighting Up Brentwood. A Santa Parade led by Razzamataz Theatre School made its way through the High Street. Later the Mayor of Brentwood, Councillor Nicky Cuthbert, switched on the lights, helped by the young winners of her Christmas card and poem competitions.

Sarah Walmsley, manager of Brentwood Connected, said the event was "all about bringing people together". A Christmas Window Trail is open too, with windows painted by local artists at businesses away from the High Street, including Levain Bakehouse and Tulip Café. A giant Christmas arch at the Chapel Ruins, paid for by town centre businesses, stays up through the festive season.

Key points

  • Ingatestone Festive Fair: Sunday 29 November, 12pm to 5pm
  • Festive Shenfield: Sunday 6 December in Hutton Road
  • Christmas Toy Trails offer prizes in both villages
  • Every event is free to attend
