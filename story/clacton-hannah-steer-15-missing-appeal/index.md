# Police concerned for missing Clacton girl Hannah Steer, 15

**She has not been seen since about 9.30pm on Sunday, and Essex Police says officers need to find her to make sure she is ok.**

## The story

Essex Police has appealed for help to find Hannah Steer, a 15-year-old girl who is missing from Clacton.

Hannah was last seen at about 9.30pm yesterday (Sunday 15 November). The force describes her as slim and short with black hair, and has released a photograph of her wearing dark-rimmed glasses.

Anyone who has seen Hannah, is with her, or knows where she might be is asked to call 999 straight away, quoting incident 180 of 16 November.
