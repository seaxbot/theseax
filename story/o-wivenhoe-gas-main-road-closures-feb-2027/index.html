Gas main works to shut five Wivenhoe roads for six weeks from February

Tower Road, Spring Lane, Broome Grove, Valfreda Way and Heath Road in Wivenhoe are all on the list, Wivenhoe Town Council says, while Cadent replaces gas mains.

Wivenhoe · Thursday 12 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Wivenhoe Town Council

Key points
