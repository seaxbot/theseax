Gas main works to shut five Wivenhoe roads for six weeks from February

Wivenhoe · Thursday 12 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Wivenhoe Town Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

Tower Road, Spring Lane, Broome Grove, Valfreda Way and Heath Road in Wivenhoe are all on the list, Wivenhoe Town Council says, while Cadent replaces gas mains.

Key points

  • Closures run from 1 February to 15 March 2027
  • Full details on one.network under reference GB153203875
  • Daily hours and diversion routes have not yet been published

What happens next

  • Monday 1 February: Gas main works begin, closing five Wivenhoe roads for six weeks until 15 March
