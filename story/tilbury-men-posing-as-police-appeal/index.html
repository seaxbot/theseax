Dash cam appeal as police link three pre-dawn Tilbury incidents involving men falsely claiming to be officers

Officers believe the incidents near St Chads Road and Calcutta Road between 4am and 4.45am on Saturday are connected, and say genuine police can always show a warrant card.

Tilbury · Saturday 7 November 2026, 12:56 · Reported by Greatest Hits Radio Essex
WhatsApp Facebook X

Essex Police is appealing for help after three men reportedly claimed to be police officers while approaching members of the public in Tilbury in the early hours of Saturday 7 November. Officers believe three incidents around St Chads Road and Calcutta Road between 4am and 4.45am are connected, and are warning residents that genuine officers can always show a warrant card.

Police said a white car flashed its headlights at a taxi driver before stopping in front of the taxi on St Chads Road, where one man tried and failed to open the taxi door. Near the Tesco Express on Calcutta Road, two men got out of a white car and stopped a man, taking his wallet and keys while a third stayed in the vehicle, and the group then tried to take another man's wallet and phone. Nobody was hurt, and police say the men wore no uniforms and showed no identification.

The men are described as a group of three in a large white SUV-style car, one in a high-visibility jacket and two in dark tracksuits. PC Jack Osborne said officers would be in the area on Saturday and asked anyone who was driving nearby at the time to check their dash cam footage. Police say anyone unsure whether a caller is a real officer is entitled to ask to see their warrant card.

Key points

  • Three linked incidents between 4am and 4.45am on Saturday
  • St Chads Road and Calcutta Road, Tilbury
  • Wallet and keys taken from one man; nobody injured
  • Suspects in a large white SUV-style car
