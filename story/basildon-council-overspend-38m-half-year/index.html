Temporary housing for homeless families drives Basildon Council towards a £3.8m overspend this year

A half-year report warns reserves of £41.1m could be largely used up by 2029/30 if gaps keep being covered from savings, though it calls that a scenario rather than a forecast.

Basildon · Tuesday 10 November 2026, 18:34 · Updated Wednesday 11 November 2026, 17:48 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Basildon Council's general fund is forecast to finish 2026/27 £3.8m over budget, up £0.4m since June, according to a half-year report reported by Basildon Today. Housing accounts for £2.4m of the gap, £1.5m of it temporary accommodation, while the council housing account is £3.2m over.

Councillors on the finance scrutiny committee discuss the report on Monday 16 November before it goes to cabinet on 26 November.

Key points

  • Temporary accommodation forecast at £7.6m against a £6.1m budget
  • Council housing account £3.2m over, improved from £4.5m in June
  • Scrutiny committee meets on 16 November, cabinet on 26 November
