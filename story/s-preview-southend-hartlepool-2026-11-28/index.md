# Southend United seek back-to-back wins at 17th-placed Hartlepool

**Kieron Dyer's Southend side go to the North East after a 2-0 win at Forest Green, with the kick-off pushed back to 5.30pm.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Southend-on-Sea, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 16:51 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Jamie Robinson is the referee
- Southend wear their yellow away kit
- Dyer has called the late kick-off a 'shambles' for travelling fans

## The story

Southend United play Hartlepool United in the National League at the Suit Direct Stadium on Saturday 28 November, kick-off 5.30pm. The Shrimpers are fourth on 35 points, three behind second-placed Harrogate Town. Hartlepool sit 17th on 22 points, so a win would keep Southend firmly in the play-off places.

Southend arrive in better heart after Tuesday's 2-0 win at Forest Green Rovers, when Harry Cardwell and Kyle McAdam scored. That followed a 3-2 home defeat by leaders Boreham Wood. Hartlepool, now coached by Pete Wild after his appointment on 1 October, have won their last two, beating Solihull Moors 2-0 and winning 2-1 at Gateshead. When the clubs last met at Victoria Road in April, Hartlepool won a seven-goal game 4-3.

On team news, defender Ben Goodliffe missed the Forest Green game with an injury, and the club has not said whether he is fit. Omari Patrick, on loan from Forest Green, was not allowed to face his parent club but is eligible again. McAdam has four goals and nine assists this season. Dyer said there was "no better confidence" than Tuesday's win. The game is on DAZN outside the UK and Ireland, and BBC Essex will carry radio commentary online through the BBC Sport website.
