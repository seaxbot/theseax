# Harlow man, 41, sent to crown court on strangulation charge

**A Harlow man has been charged over an alleged offence in the town on 7 November. He is on bail until a crown court hearing.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 09:19 (UK time). Section: Courts. Topic: Court.

## Key points

- Released on conditional bail
- Next hearing: Chelmsford Crown Court

## The story

Martin Barnsley, 41, of Lower Meadow, Harlow, is charged with intentionally strangling a woman in Harlow on Saturday 7 November, Your Harlow reported. The charge is brought under section 75A of the Serious Crime Act 2015.

He appeared at Chelmsford Magistrates' Court and was released on conditional bail. His next appearance is at Chelmsford Crown Court on 8 December.
