Southend council still heading for an almost £12m overspend as children's care placements cost more

A handful of very expensive residential placements for children in care are now the main drain on Southend-on-Sea City Council's budget.

Southend-on-Sea · Monday 9 November 2026, 12:00 · Reported by Southend-on-Sea City Council
WhatsApp Facebook X

The forecast for April to the end of September 2026 puts this year's overspend at £11.96m, about £1.3m better than earlier estimates, the council said on Monday 9 November. Most of its services are within budget or close to it, and the remaining pressure sits almost entirely in children's and adult social care.

Children's services costs have climbed by about £1.5m since the summer. One residential placement costs more than £1m a year and seven others more than £500,000 each, which the council puts down to more complex needs and a national shortage of suitable placements pushing prices up. Adult social care costs have improved over the same period.

Council leader Daniel Cowan said most areas were coming under control, but argued local taxpayers could not keep carrying the cost of care markets he described as broken, and said the council would keep pressing the Government for change. The council plans tighter spending controls, recovery plans for each directorate and faster transformation work, and says most of the savings planned for 2026/27 have already been delivered.

Key points

  • Covers the first six months of 2026/27
  • Children's services costs up about £1.5m since the summer
  • Most planned 2026/27 savings already made, the council says
