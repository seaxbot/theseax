# Shoeburyness East Beach could get dunes and step-free boardwalk

**Southend City Council plans to replace worn sea defences at East Beach with replanted dunes. Work could start in spring 2027.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Shoeburyness, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 07:55 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Environment.

## Key points

- Boardwalk and paths likely by late 2027
- Rock armour would be reused from Three Shells Lagoon
- Surveys found sea holly and rare burrowing bees

## The story

Council papers reported by Southend Today show the scheme at East Beach, Shoeburyness, would swap the gabion sea defences for reshaped dunes, fencing and a step-free boardwalk onto the sand. It would cost about £900,000, with £576,000 secured from flood defence funding, subject to a final business case.

Councillors on the Place Scrutiny Committee get a verbal update on Thursday 3 December. The plan still needs planning permission and a marine licence.
