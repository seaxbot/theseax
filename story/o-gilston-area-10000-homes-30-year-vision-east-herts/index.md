# Gilston 10,000-home scheme by Harlow is a 30-year job, planners hear

**East Herts Council officers leading the Gilston Area beside Harlow shared lessons on trust, governance and long memories with planners from across the East of England.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Harlow, Gilston, Essex, England. Published: Monday 16 November 2026, 16:08 (UK time). Section: Public Services. Topic: Council.

## Key points

- Seven villages planned in the Gilston Area
- Gilston team leader has worked on the scheme since 2012
- Talk given to the Planning Officers Society East of England network

## The story

Officers running the Gilston Area, the 10,000-home expansion on Harlow's northern edge, spoke at Epping Forest District Council at a Planning Officers Society East of England event, the Harlow and Gilston Garden Town partnership said on Monday 16 November 2026. The scheme covers seven new villages with schools, transport links, community buildings and green space.

Martin Plummer, East Herts Council's Assistant Director for Place Delivery, said several developers work across the area, so the council built a single vision alongside two planning permissions and a long-term regulatory system.
