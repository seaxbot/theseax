Basildon house prices hit record average of £367,920

Basildon · Thursday 19 November 2026, 07:21 · Reported by Basildon Today
WhatsApp Facebook X

Land Registry figures for September put the Basildon borough's average just £11 above its November 2022 peak, Basildon Today reports.

Prices in the Basildon borough rose 0.8% over the year, against an Essex average of £360,791. The index came out on 18 November. Flats were the only type of home to fall in value over the year. The latest months are often revised as late sales are registered, so the record could slip.

Key points

  • First-time buyers paid an average of £307,721
  • Castle Point's average is now £271 above Basildon's
  • Flats fell 1.6% over the year
