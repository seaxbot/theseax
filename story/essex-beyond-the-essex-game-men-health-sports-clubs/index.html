Essex football and cricket clubs to lead year-long push on men's mental health

Essex County Council is funding the Colchester United, Southend United and West Ham United community foundations, with cricket partners, to reach men through sport across the county.

Essex · Friday 13 November 2026, 07:51 · Reported by Your Harlow
WhatsApp Facebook X

Beyond the Essex Game, a 12-month pilot run by Active Essex for Essex County Council's public health team, was launched at Essex County Cricket Club with Peter and Steph Shilton among the guests, Your Harlow reports.

The clubs will work on stigma and asking for help, addiction to alcohol, drugs and gambling, and loneliness. Clubs are "trusted voices in their communities", said Sarah Muckle, Essex's director of public health.

Key points

  • Shiltons' Silver Lining works alongside the Essex Cricket Foundation
  • Amounts awarded have not been disclosed
