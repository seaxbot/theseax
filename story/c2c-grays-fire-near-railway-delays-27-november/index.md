# c2c lines reopen after fire beside Grays railway

**A fire next to the railway at Grays slowed c2c trains on every route. Cancellations ran into Friday evening.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Grays, Essex, England. Published: Friday 27 November 2026, 16:08 (UK time). Section: News. Topic: Transport.

## Key points

- c2c has not said what caught fire
- Delay Repay applies to delays of 15 minutes or more

## The story

c2c trains were cancelled and delayed on every route on the afternoon of Friday 27 November, after a fire on property next to the railway at Grays forced trains to slow down on all lines.

By 4.08pm c2c said all lines had reopened, but warned trains could still be cancelled or changed while services recover. It said disruption was expected to last until about 6pm. The operator has not said what caught fire. The line's troubles follow [an extra £2m reliability plan](https://theseax.com/story/c2c-gbr-anglia-2m-reliability-plan-december-works/) announced on Thursday.
