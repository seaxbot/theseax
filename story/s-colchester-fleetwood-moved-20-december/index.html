Colchester United's home game with Fleetwood Town switched to Sunday 20 December

The League Two match moves back 24 hours so Fleetwood can recover from their Carabao Cup quarter-final against Everton.

Colchester · Wednesday 11 November 2026, 15:13 · Reported by Yahoo Sports
WhatsApp Facebook X

Colchester United have moved their League Two home match against Fleetwood Town to Sunday 20 December, with a 1pm kick-off, the Daily Gazette reports via Yahoo Sports. Fleetwood play Premier League Everton in the Carabao Cup quarter-finals on the evening of Thursday 17 December. It will be the U's last home game before the Christmas fixtures.

Key points

  • Tickets bought for the original date remain valid
  • The game stays on Sky Sports' live schedule
