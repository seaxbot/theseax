# Colchester United's Fleetwood Town game moves to Sunday 20 December

**The League Two match moves back 24 hours so Fleetwood can recover from their Carabao Cup quarter-final against Everton.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Colchester, Essex, England. Published: Wednesday 11 November 2026, 15:13 (UK time). Section: Sport. Topic: Football.

## Key points

- Tickets bought for the original date remain valid
- The game stays on Sky Sports' live schedule

## The story

Kick-off is at 1pm, the Daily Gazette reports via Yahoo Sports. Fleetwood play Premier League Everton in the Carabao Cup quarter-finals on the evening of Thursday 17 December. It will be the U's last home game before the Christmas fixtures.
