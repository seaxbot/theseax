# Chelmsford police urgently search for Johnny Coyle, 37, who may be hurt

**Anyone who sees him should not approach him but call 999, quoting incident 892 of 20 November.**

Published by The Seax (https://theseax.com/), independent Essex news. Place: Chelmsford, Essex, England. Published: Saturday 21 November 2026, 08:11 (UK time). Updated: Saturday 21 November 2026, 12:30. Section: News. Topic: Police.

## Key points

- Not seen for a number of hours on Friday
- Police are concerned for his welfare

## The story

Essex Police are urgently trying to find Johnny Coyle, who is missing from Chelmsford. The force released his picture on Friday night.

Officers said the 37-year-old had not been seen for a number of hours when the appeal went out on Friday 20 November, and that they are concerned for his welfare. He may have been injured that day, the force added.

Police ask anyone who sees him not to go up to him. Instead, call 999 straight away and quote incident 892 of 20 November.
