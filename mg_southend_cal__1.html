35.

Local Government Reorganisation and Transformation Programme Delivery PDF 2 MB

Additional documents:

  • Webcast for 35.

Minutes:

The Committee received a report regarding the costs associated with the Local Government Reorganisation (LGR) programme and its impact on delivery of the Council's Transformation Programme. The report outlined the various phases of the LGR programme together with direct expenditure incurred by the Council and the relationship between LGR activity and the Council's transformation work.

 

Members discussed the costs incurred by the Council, including direct expenditure and the wider impact of officer time devoted to the programme. Questions were raised regarding the methodology used to calculate costs, the work being undertaken across Greater Essex to identify system-wide expenditure, and the extent to which officer resources had been diverted from other priorities.

 

The Committee was advised that additional work was being undertaken to estimate the level of senior officer time committed to the programme and that further information could be provided once that exercise had been completed. It was also noted that work was continuing across Greater Essex to identify broader programme costs.

 

Members discussed lessons learned from the LGR process, opportunities for future collaboration with neighbouring authorities, and the impact of Government's decision to pause the programme. The Committee also explored the effect on the Council's Transformation Programme and received reassurance that a number of projects previously aligned to LGR had either resumed or continued under revised arrangements.

 

The Leader of the Council and officers responded to questions regarding future Government intentions, engagement with partner authorities, and the potential implications should LGR proposals be revisited at a later date.

 

Members requested that further information relating to the senior leadership ‘opportunity’ costs related to Local Government Reorganisation and the Greater Essex ‘system’ programme costs be provided before Christmas. The Chair advised that he would invite the Chief Executive to attend a future meeting of the Committee to provide an update on Local Government Reorganisation and associated matters.

 

Resolved:

 

That the report be noted.

 

36.

Call-In - Vecteo Fleet Procurement Framework PDF 274 KB

  • View the background to item 36.

Additional documents:

  • Webcast for 36.

Minutes:

In accordance with paragraph 3.16 of the Call-In Procedure Rules set out in Part 5, Section 3 of the Council's Constitution, the Committee considered a report of the Cabinet Member for Finance and Transport relating to the Vecteo Fleet Procurement Framework. The Chair outlined the reasons for the call-in and explained the procedure to be followed by the Committee in considering the matter.

The Committee noted that the decision had been called in by Councillor J O'Rourke (Lead Call-In Member), together with Councillors M Heaver and T Cox. The call-in documentation had been published and circulated to Committee Members as a supplementary agenda pack prior to the meeting. The Monitoring Officer had determined that the call-in request met the requirements of the Call-In Procedure Rules and was therefore valid for consideration by the Committee.

 

The Lead Call-In Member outlined the reasons for the call-in and requested further scrutiny of the proposed procurement arrangements and the selection of the preferred supplier.

 

The Cabinet Member for Finance and Transport explained the procurement process and the reasons for the recommendation. The Committee was advised that five suppliers had submitted tenders through the relevant procurement framework and that the recommended supplier had achieved the highest overall score following evaluation against the published price and quality criteria.

 

In discussing the matter, Members sought clarification regarding the Council's previous relationship with the supplier, the financial implications of the proposal, future fleet replacement arrangements, the reasons for determining the matter through an Individual Cabinet Member Decision, contract management arrangements, vehicle maintenance responsibilities, opportunities to increase the use of electric vehicles and the procurement timetable.

 

In accordance with paragraph 3.21 (b) of the Call-In Procedure Rules (Part 5, Section 3 of the Council's Constitution), a motion was proposed that the decision be referred to Cabinet for reconsideration. Upon being put to the vote, the motion was lost, with 2 votes in favour, 5 votes against and 1 abstention.

 

As a result, the Committee took no further action in respect of the Individual Cabinet Member Decision. The Chair advised that, in accordance with paragraph 3.21(a) of the Call-In Procedure Rules (Part 5, Section 3 of the Council's Constitution), the decision could therefore take immediate effect.

 

Resolved:

 

1.    That the call-in request be closed.

2.    That the Committee takes no further action in respect of the Individual Cabinet Member Decision.

3.    That, in accordance with paragraph 3.21 of the Call-In Procedure Rules (Part 5, Section 3 of the Council's Constitution), the Individual Cabinet Member Decision be noted and the decision be permitted to take immediate effect.
