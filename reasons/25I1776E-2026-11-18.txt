Decision and reasons on interim order

The panel decided to make an interim conditions of practice order for a period of 18
months.

The panel has determined that the following conditions are proportionate and appropriate:

   ‘For the purposes of these conditions, ‘employment’ and ‘work’ mean any paid or
   unpaid post in a nursing, midwifery or nursing associate role. Also, ‘course of
   study’ and ‘course’ mean any course of educational study connected to nursing,
   midwifery or nursing associates.’

      1. You must limit your midwifery practice to a single substantive employer.
          This must not be as an agency or bank midwife.

      2. You must not be the midwife in charge of any shift or the sole midwife
          on duty.

      3. You must ensure that you are directly supervised by another registered
          midwife any time you are working, until you are signed off by a band 6
          midwife or above as competent in the following areas:
          a. Medicines management
          b. Patient assessment
          c. Obtaining informed consent
          d. Record keeping

      4. In relation to condition 3, when you have been signed off for each
          competence, you must send your case officer at the NMC a copy of the
          individual competency assessment document.

      5. When condition 3 does not apply, you must be indirectly supervised at
          all other times by another registered midwife. This supervision must

6. You must meet with your line manager or supervisor on a fortnightly
   basis to discuss the following:
      a. Medicines management
      b. Patient assessment
      c. Obtaining informed consent
      d. Record keeping
      e. Communication

   relation to:
      a. Medicines management
      b. Patient assessment
      c. Obtaining informed consent
      d. Record keeping
      e. Communication
      f. Your overall clinical performance

8. You must keep the NMC informed about anywhere you are working by:
       a)    Telling your case officer within seven days of
             accepting or leaving any employment.
       b)    Giving your case officer your employer’s contact
             details.

9. You must keep the NMC informed about anywhere you are studying by:
       a)    Telling your case officer within seven days of

that may be relevant to the interim order.
