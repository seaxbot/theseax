Shenfield · Essex rail passengers face buses to the Tube for a week over New Year. Trains also finish early on Christmas Eve.

Grays, Chelmsford · Oksana Antanaitiene, 52, a maths tutor at HMP Chelmsford, used a false name and a secret phone to keep in touch with him.

ARU installs forensic scientist Sue Black as chancellor in Chelmsford

Chelmsford · One of the world's leading forensic scientists, named Anglia Ruskin University's chancellor in September, has been formally invested at its Chelmsford graduations.

Tendring keeps 100% council tax premium on second homes

Wickford man jailed over £30m cocaine pick-up from the sea

Wickford · Cameron Weedon helped fishermen fetch 374kg of cocaine dumped in the Channel. A gang leader from Rainham was also jailed.

One in three Essex takeaways misses top food hygiene rating
