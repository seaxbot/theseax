The stories that matter

Essex oyster farmers could win back lost EU trade under new deal

West Mersea, Blackwater, Colne, Crouch and Roach · Since 2021 the EU has refused oysters from most Essex waters unless they are purified first, a Seax analysis shows. The new UK-EU deal is expected to end that from March 2028.

Brentwood School coach Gareth Stafford Bull likely abused pupils, review finds

Brentwood · Former pupils who raised concerns years later were brushed off, the report says, and British Fencing is now setting up its own inquiry into what was known.

Spitting Image co-creator Peter Fluck, who taught art in Colchester, dies at 85

Colchester · The artist helped create one of Britain's most iconic television programmes, but before achieving worldwide fame he taught at Colchester School of Art.

Firefighters pump floodwater away from five Warley homes

Warley, Brentwood · A burst water main sent water towards houses in a Brentwood street on Saturday morning.

Maldon Salt labelling questions passed to Essex Trading Standards

Maldon · Maldon District Council says it has no concerns about Maldon Salt's sites, the Maldon Standard reports, after an Observer investigation found the firm mixes German rock salt into its sea salt.

Former Ravens store in Southend could become 17-room shared house

Southend-on-Sea, Clifftown Road · The developer wants to drop a students-only rule on rooms above the old Clifftown Road store, opposite Southend Central station.

Dengie solar farm: wider public consultation planned for early 2027

Burnham-on-Crouch, Bradwell-on-Sea, Tillingham & Southminster · Residents across the Dengie Peninsula will get a fuller say on Low Carbon's seven-site, 4,200-acre plan before the firm applies to the government for consent.

Colchester leaders rule out Leisure World rebuild ahead of cabinet vote

Colchester, Leisure World · Upgrading the ageing Cowdray Avenue centre is the only practical way to protect public swimming in the city, Colchester City Council says.
