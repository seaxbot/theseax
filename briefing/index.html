The stories that matter

c2c gets £2m plan to fix delays, with night and weekend works in December

Southend-on-Sea, Basildon, Upminster · Rail bosses are spending more to fix the Southend line after months of delays. Some December trains will finish early or be replaced by buses.

Frosty start across Essex before a raw Friday and milder weekend

Essex · Clear spells tonight bring a frost by dawn. Friday stays cold and grey, before rain and milder air arrive on Saturday.

Tube strike: Essex commuters face a late start and limited lines on Friday

Essex · The Tube drivers' walkout runs until midday on Friday. Central line users in Epping Forest face another disrupted journey.

Harlow Council tells government to drop asylum plan for private homes

Harlow · The Home Office's contractor wants to rent homes across Harlow for asylum seekers. The council's leader wants the plan dropped.

Southend's Lazydays adds Ella Eyre, KT Tunstall and Pixie Lott for 2027

Southend, Priory Park · Sigala, Daniel Bedingfield and Celebrity Traitors winner Bash Croft are also on the biggest line-up announcement yet for the Priory Park festival, which returns in July 2027.

Cocaine kingpin who sent couriers to Harwich and Southend jailed for 18 years

Harwich · Bradley Stainer ran a network that moved more than a tonne of cocaine. His couriers were paid more than £1,000 a day.

Great Waltham concert to fund generators for Ukraine's winter

Great Waltham · Ukrainian accordionist Igor Saienko plays the village church on 9 December. Entry is free, with donations going to a Felsted charity which helps fund generators for Ukraine.

Southend United legend Frankie Banks given Freedom of the City

Southend-on-Sea · The former Shrimpers full-back started the club's community work in 1991. The club says his impact goes far beyond the pitch.

From Council Watch

Braintree plans £7m for leisure centres and £600,000 on homelessness

Braintree District Council · Braintree's first budget plans for 2027-28 promise no cuts to services. A gap of £542,000 still has to be closed.

NHS agrees to face Maldon councillors over Southminster health hub

Maldon District Council · Councillors are worried about slow progress on a planned medical centre in Southminster. The local NHS has agreed to answer their questions.

Maldon drafts guide to curb dust and noise from building sites

Maldon District Council · Councillors want builders to plan better for dust, noise and debris. A new guide goes to a committee on 3 December.
