The stories that matter

Inquest opens into death of Chelmsford boy, 2, who fell from Cyprus hotel window

Chelmsford · Jaxon Foster died from multiple severe injuries on the first day of a family holiday in July, Essex Coroner's Court has heard.

Anglia Ruskin University seeks voluntary redundancies to cut staff bill by 10%

Chelmsford · The university, which has campuses in Chelmsford plus Cambridge and Peterborough, spends about £150m a year on its workforce, the BBC reports.

Thurrock Council plans to save £995,000 as fewer families wait in emergency housing

Grays, Thurrock · Thurrock Council says the number of households it has placed in temporary accommodation is now falling by about 15 a month, after three years of steady rises.

Almost half of Stansted Airport passengers now arrive by train, bus or coach

Stansted · More than 13.5 million people used public transport to reach London Stansted Airport over the past 12 months, the Bishop's Stortford Independent reports.

Broomfield, Southend and Basildon hospital patients told to keep appointments as Microsoft email recovery continues

Chelmsford, Southend-on-Sea, Basildon · Microsoft says its email service is approaching normal levels after the global outage, but some messages are still arriving late.

Former PC who saw cyclist heading into traffic before fatal A130 crash would have been sacked, hearing rules

Chelmsford · A misconduct hearing chaired by Essex Police Chief Constable Rachel Nolan found Nicholas Davies did not stop, turn round or report the rider he had seen in Chelmsford.

Essex football and cricket clubs to lead year-long push on men's mental health

Essex · Essex County Council is funding the Colchester United, Southend United and West Ham United community foundations, with cricket partners, to reach men through sport across the county.

Maldon MP warns Hammonds Farm garden community could clog the A414 into town

Maldon, Heybridge · Sir John Whittingdale has written to the planning inspector examining Chelmsford's Local Plan, saying the 3,000-home scheme would leave Maldon and Heybridge with worse congestion and few other routes.
