The stories that matter

Essex falls silent for Remembrance Sunday as parades close town centres from Colchester to Leigh-on-Sea

Chelmsford, Colchester, Brentwood, Saffron Walden · Services at war memorials across the county mark the 11am two-minute silence today, with First Essex changing buses in 16 towns and villages while roads are closed for parades.

A12 shut between Colchester junctions 26 and 28 after car hits pedestrian, with diversions through the town

Colchester · Police and ambulance crews were called to the A12 at Colchester on Saturday evening, and National Highways said the road was expected to stay closed through the evening.

Dash cam appeal as police link three pre-dawn Tilbury incidents involving men falsely claiming to be officers

Tilbury · Officers believe the incidents near St Chads Road and Calcutta Road between 4am and 4.45am on Saturday are connected, and say genuine police can always show a warrant card.

Essex commissioner brands regional 'mega force' plan not fit for purpose as merger review awaits publication

Essex · Roger Hirst told the BBC there is no evidence bigger forces perform better, a day after the Met chief called for the 43 forces in England and Wales to shrink to as few as eight.

Essex drivers warned before Christmas crackdown as drug-driving arrests reach 1,960 in ten months

Essex · Roads policing officers held more than twice as many people on suspicion of drug-driving as drink-driving between January and October, and the force is now planning its festive campaign.

South Basildon MP James McMurdock raises alarm over how few refused asylum claims end in returns

Basildon, Thurrock · Home Office figures show only around one in ten asylum claims refused from 2025 has so far led to a recorded return, and the MP for South Basildon and East Thurrock wants the Government to publish clearer data.

Parking at Princess Alexandra Hospital in Harlow to cost more from 17 November

Harlow · The trust says the rise is a small inflation-linked increase, with the first 20 minutes staying free and existing exemptions kept.

E-bike pods at Harwich station and on the harbour ferry aim to keep coastal visitors moving

Harwich · Greater Anglia and Freeport East have launched the Harwich Haven Eezybike Connect network, with five pods at stations and on the Harwich Harbour Ferry.

Tendring District Council · With Whitehall reviewing its plan to replace Essex's councils, Tendring's cabinet has agreed to plan four years ahead, and backed money for toilets, playgrounds and housing staff.

Thurrock's special needs funding gap keeps growing as Treetops School plans 110 more places

Thurrock Council · Children's services are on course to underspend by about £0.5m, but the grant that pays for special educational needs is heading deeper into deficit, councillors heard.

Thurrock picked as one of seven national pilots helping children stay with their wider family

Thurrock Council · The Kinship Zones pilot will give practical, emotional and financial support to relatives raising children, as Thurrock reshapes its children's social care.
