The stories that matter

Essex police commissioner vows to fight plan that could fold Essex Police into a regional super force

Essex · Roger Hirst says there is no public appetite for change in Essex, as a Government-commissioned review is reported to recommend cutting England's 39 forces to as few as nine.

Essex County Council asks residents to help close a £170m gap in one of its toughest budgets this century

Chelmsford · The county council has opened a month-long consultation on its 2027/28 budget, saying government funding changes will cost it more than £50m a year.

Plans emerge for 1GW solar and battery energy park on the Dengie Peninsula

Bradwell-on-Sea, Dengie & Burnham-on-Crouch · Developer Low Carbon has started the national planning process for a scheme it says could power 250,000 homes, and Maldon District Council has until 24 November to say what studies it needs.

A130 southbound shut all weekend between Howe Green and the Rayleigh Spur for maintenance

Howe Green & Rayleigh · Drivers heading south from Chelmsford towards Basildon and Southend face a signed diversion via the A12 and A127 until early on Monday.

Southend opens final consultation on Local Plan it was ordered to publish by ministers

Southend-on-Sea · Residents have until 21 December to comment on the draft blueprint for homes, jobs and infrastructure, which the city council published only after the housing minister intervened.

Basildon councillors refuse 910-home Foots Farm scheme near Billericay's Sun Corner

Billericay · The planning committee turned down the green belt application off Southend Road, but the developers' appeal is already heading to a public inquiry next May.

More than 50,000 still waiting at Southend Hospital as trust backlog falls by 14,000

Southend-on-Sea · Hospital chief executive Fiona Ryan told councillors there is still a long way to go, although waiting lists across the Mid and South Essex trust are starting to come down.

New website launched for 7,750-home Tendring Colchester Borders Garden Community

Colchester & Tendring borders · Three councils have opened a single online hub for the planned new town east of Colchester, with a decision on its planning application expected in summer 2027.
