23.

Change in membership of the Committee

Minutes:

The Democratic Services Manager (Ian Ford) informed the Committee that, on 25 September 2026, in accordance with the expressed wishes of the Leader of the Reform UK Group, and the authority delegated to him, the Chief Executive had appointed Councillor Aimee Keteca to serve on the Planning Committee in place of Councillor Jeff Bray.

 

Councillor Keteca had not yet been able to receive the required mandatory training and therefore was unable to sit as a member of the Committee at the present time.

Temporary structure for a term of 7 years. A robust structure to act as a temporary boat hall has been proposed to allow protection for the Shannon class lifeboat and tractor from the elements due to the exposed location, as well as a measure of security.

Minutes:

Earlier on in the meeting, as detailed under minute 26 above, Councillor Alexander had declared for the public record, in relation to this application that he was one of the Ward Members, and he had confirmed that he was not pre-determined on this application.

 

The Committee was informed that this application sought planning permission for a temporary boat hall for a period of seven years to accommodate the RNLI's Shannon-class lifeboat and associated launch tractor. The existing lifeboat station could not accommodate the vessel due to the size and configuration of the current boat hall.

 

Members were advised that the proposal was considered by Officers to be acceptable in principle and that it would provide significant public benefits through the continued operation of a vital emergency service. No objections had been raised by the Environment Agency, Essex Highways, Place Services Ecology or the Council's Tree and Landscape Officer. Whilst there would be a temporary loss of part of the overflow parking area, this harm was outweighed by the operational and public benefits of the development. Subject to conditions, the application was recommended by Officers for approval.

 

The Committee had before it the published Officer report containing the key planning issues, relevant planning policies, planning history, any response from consultees, written representations received and a recommendation of approval.

 

At the meeting, an oral presentation was made by the Council’s Planning Officer (AP) in respect of the application.

 

No updates had been circulated to Members in relation to this application.

 

No member of the public had registered to speak on this application.

Application for Permission in Principle - One self-build dwelling.

Minutes:

Earlier on in the meeting, as detailed under minute 26 above, Councillor Wiggins had declared for the public record, in relation to this application that she was one of the Ward Members, and she had confirmed that she was not pre-determined on this application.

 

Councillor Scott, who was present in the public gallery, had also declared for the public record, in relation to this application that he was one of the Ward Members.

 

It was reported that this application sought ‘Permission in Principle’ for the erection of one self-build dwelling. The scope of consideration was limited to the location, land use and amount of development proposed.

 

The Committee was informed that the application site was located within the defined Settlement Development Boundary of Alresford, where residential development was supported in principle by the adopted Tendring District Local Plan and the Alresford Neighbourhood Plan. Having regard to the site's sustainable location and the scale of development proposed the principle of development was considered by Officers to be acceptable.

 

The proposal was considered by Officers to accord with Policies SP3, SPL1, SPL2 and LP7 of the Tendring District Local Plan 2013-2033 and Beyond, together with Policy ALRES1 of the Alresford Neighbourhood Plan. Officers had therefore recommended the application for approval.

 

The Committee had before it the published Officer report containing the key planning issues, relevant planning policies, planning history, any response from consultees, written representations received and a recommendation of approval.

 

At the meeting, an oral presentation was made by the Council’s Planning Officer (CC) in respect of the application.

 

No updates had been circulated to Members in relation to this application.

 

No member of the public had registered to speak on this application.

Report of Corporate Director (Planning and Community) - A.3 - Planning Application 26/00883/FUL - Clacton Leisure Centre, Vista Road, Clacton-on-Sea, CO15 6DB PDF 429 KB

  • View the background to item 31.

Construction of a community ride and play facility comprising, pump track, learn to ride area, play area, facilities building, tennis courts, padel courts with canopies, fencing and floodlighting, and including associated hard and soft landscaping works, SuDS and infrastructure.

Minutes:

The Committee was informed that the proposed redevelopment of part of Clacton Leisure Centre would deliver a significantly enhanced and more diverse range of leisure, sport and recreational facilities, including new play areas, cycling facilities, tennis and padel courts, a pavilion and associated landscaping. The scheme would provide substantial community, health and wellbeing benefits, achieve biodiversity net gain, and was supported by statutory consultees, subject to conditions.

 

The proposal was considered by Officers to accord with the NPPF and adopted Local Plan policies, with any potential impacts capable of being suitably mitigated through planning conditions. Accordingly, it was recommended by Officers that planning permission be granted, subject to the conditions set out in the Officer report (A.3).

 

The Committee had before it the published Officer report containing the key planning issues, relevant planning policies, planning history, any response from consultees, written representations received and a recommendation of refusal.

 

At the meeting, an oral presentation was made by the Council’s Principal Planning Officer (AL) in respect of the application.

 

No updates had been circulated to Members in relation to this application.

 

Lee Heley, representing the applicant (Tendring District Council), spoke in favour of this application.

 

Matters raised by Members of the Committee:-

Officer’s response thereto:-

I note that there has been only one objection received, what was that regarding?

That objection is summarised within Section 6.2 of the Officer report (A.3).

 

After the debate, it was moved by Councillor Goldman, seconded by Councillor Fowlerand unanimously:-

 

RESOLVED that:-

 

(1)     the Head of Planning and Building Control be authorised to grant planning permission, subject to the conditions as stated at paragraph 9.2 of the Officer report (A.3), or varied as is necessary to ensure the wording is enforceable, precise, and reasonable in all other respects, including appropriate updates, so long as the principle of the conditions as referenced is retained; and

 

(2)     informative notes be sent to the applicant, as may be deemed necessary, by the Head of Planning and Building Control.
