How Council Watch works

Essex has fifteen principal councils: Essex County Council, Southend, Thurrock and twelve district, borough and city councils. Each publishes agendas, reports, decisions and minutes that few people have time to read. Council Watch reads them for you.

Twice a day an automated collector visits each council's public meetings website, politely and within the rules each site publishes, and lists the meetings, agenda items and reports. It ranks items by how much they are likely to matter to residents: money, services, homes, planning, closures, consultations and decisions. Our desk then writes short stories from the most important papers, with the help of AI, and links every story to the exact documents and pages it comes from.

Council Watch only reports what the council papers say. It does not report private or exempt business, and it does not name members of the public who appear in council papers. Where a council's website limits automated reading, our desk checks it by hand instead.

Spotted something in your council's papers, or work for an Essex council and want to point us at something? Email news@theseax.com. Corrections to corrections@theseax.com.
